Episode 71

I brought a neatly rolled piece of tamagoyaki to my mouth.\
Today's bento from Fujishiro was delicious too.

She'd said it was enough for me just to go shopping with her, but I thought I really ought to give her something in return sometime, as I turned it over in my head.

"So, are you really not dating the girl who was with you the other day?"

At Arai-san's words my chopsticks stopped dead.

I looked across the table, and there sat a woman smiling brightly, though I couldn't tell what she found so fun.

"We're not dating."\
"Even though you two were so lovey-dovey?"\
"Lovey-dovey..."

Was that how it looked when Fujishiro and I were together?\
I'd spent a lot of time with her without thinking about it, but now I suddenly wondered how we'd looked to the people around us.

"Then, are you friends?"\
"Friends..."

Is Fujishiro a friend?\
If someone called her that, maybe she was. The thought only struck me now.

"Ha ha. You've gone all stilted on me."

Arai-san clutched her stomach laughing. Without my noticing she had slipped into a casual way of speaking, and she leaned in to look at my face.

"I think it's very youthful of you."\
"What is?"\
"It's hard to say what exactly, but watching you, Morishita-san, reminds me of my own high school days."\
"Watching me?"\
"Yeah. You're so alike."

Arai-san?\
I held back from saying it aloud.

Because Arai-san, unusually, had a pained look on her face.

I'd always assumed she lived in a world opposite to mine. Someone loved by many people, like Fujishiro, someone gifted, someone who sparkled in my eyes.

But the hurt expression she wore was one I'd seen in the mirror.

"You seem oblivious to your own feelings, Morishita-san."\
"Oblivious... you're making fun of me, aren't you?"\
"Yeah. A little."

As I'd thought, this person was a lot like Fujishiro. A little tactless, and perfectly happy to say so out loud.

Not knowing my gloomy thoughts, Arai-san pressed on.

"What's that girl's name?"\
"Fujishiro Nanoha."\
"Nanoha-chan, huh. Why don't the three of us, me included, go out sometime?"\
"Fujishiro too?"\
"Yes. She's cute, and I thought I'd like to be friends."

This time she wasn't laughing with her mouth wide open as before, but with her eyes narrowed in a smile.

Suddenly, deep in my chest, something squeezed with pain.

I thought Fujishiro was someone anyone would find cute, someone anyone would want to be friends with, and the thought left me with an unpleasant feeling.

"That's a bit..."\
"Well, look at that. You do get jealous, Morishita-san."\
"Huh?"

Arai-san burst into amused laughter.\
It felt like I was being shown up by grown-up composure, and it annoyed me.

And yet, strangely, she was also someone I felt like leaning on.

"It's just... I don't know."\
"You don't know?"\
"I don't really know what I feel. I don't have many wants, like what I'd like to do. So I don't really understand jealousy either."\
"Then why not try making an effort to find out?"\
"Find out?"

I leaned in eagerly without meaning to.\
Arai-san looked a little exasperated this time, but something seemed to come to her, and she pressed her hands together in front of her face.

"You don't seem good with hearing things in words, Morishita-san, so why not just touch Nanoha-chan a lot?"\
"Touch her a lot..."\
"Exactly. If you're close, you could do sleepovers and spend more time together."\
"Is that so..."

I answered, but my head was in a tangle.\
The other day Fujishiro had invited me to stay over and I'd turned her down. Because I thought there was no need to stay over when I had a bed and a bath next door.

After that Arai-san kept chattering happily about one thing and another, but I was mostly somewhere else.

—

When work finished, I had a bit more energy than usual, so I decided to stop at the supermarket.

Cooking for myself... well, I couldn't manage anything from scratch, so I bought some vegetables and one of those toss-in sauces sold under the Cook Day label, and headed home.

When I reached the apartment, I couldn't help thinking of Fujishiro.

I'm only going to return the washed bento box.\
Telling myself that, I rang her doorbell.

A cheerful voice from inside answered as always, and Fujishiro appeared in her loungewear.

"Morishita, good work today."\
"Yeah. Thanks for the bento."\
"You're welcome. Oh, did you go shopping?"\
"Yeah. I'm cooking for myself."\
"Want me to help?"\
"No."\
"Oh, okay."

The conversation was over and I should have left her flat, but Arai-san's words wouldn't leave my head and I stayed rooted to the spot.

"Morishita, what's wrong?"

Fujishiro stepped closer.\
Her face was so near I could have touched it, and my heart began to thud.

I'd always touched her without thinking, and yet suddenly I couldn't remember how I'd done it.

It was a distance I could cross by reaching out, and yet it felt incredibly hard.

"It's nothing. See you."\
"Eh, okay? ...See you?"

I turned my back on Fujishiro and hurried into the flat next door.
