Episode 74

"Don't look so grumpy."\
"I'm not."\
"What happened?"

Arai-san seemed determined to play innocent.\
I thought she was a very bad person.

"I touched Fujishiro a lot, but I still couldn't understand."\
"Pfft!"\
"Why are you laughing?"\
"Well, I've never met her, but I can't help feeling sorry for Nanoha-chan."\
"Why is Fujishiro the one you feel sorry for?"

To stop myself from saying anything even more cutting, I decided to shove one of the octopus-shaped sausages Fujishiro had made into my mouth.

The savoury juices burst across my tongue, and I hurriedly followed it with a mouthful of white rice.

"Anyway, please don't get angry. Let's look for another approach, shall we? I'll help however I can."

I felt as though she was somehow talking me round and getting me to do exactly what she wanted, but my desire to understand Fujishiro was stronger, so I resigned myself to it.

"Then, can I ask you something?"\
"Ask me anything!"\
"What sort of things make girls happy to receive?"\
"But you're a girl yourself, Morishita-san."\
"I don't really have anything I particularly want, so I don't know."\
"I see. Well, I don't really know what kind of person Nanoha-chan is."\
"I never said I was going to give Fujishiro anything."\
"But you are going to give something to Nanoha-chan, aren't you?"\
"......"

I couldn't say anything.\
It seemed Arai-san could see right through me, which I found thoroughly unpleasant.

Sigh...\
"Please don't sigh so conspicuously. Now, what was it you wanted to ask?"\
"...Her birthday is coming up."\
"Ah, that's a difficult one. But I'm sure she'd be happy with anything you gave her, Morishita-san. Why not give her yourself as a present? I think that would make her happiest of all."\
"What sort of suggestion is that?"

Fujishiro had said something rather similar, but there was no way anyone would be happy to receive someone like me.\
I could already see a future in which my very existence would eventually become a burden to her.

My thoughts kept turning negative, and I felt even more miserable when I realised that there wasn't a single person around me who could give me a straight answer.

"Well, shall we look for something after work?"\
"Huh? You don't mind?"\
"Of course not."

Arai-san wore a confident expression, as though to say, Leave it to me.\
She looked like someone with a good eye for these things, and I thought having her help me choose might actually be the best option.

I reconsidered my rude attitude up to that point and bowed to her.

"Please. I'd really appreciate your help."\
"All right, then. See you this evening."

I nodded deeply, and our lunch break came to an end.

*

When work finished, I looked over at Arai-san's desk and saw that she'd already shut down her computer and clocked off. I hurriedly did the same, followed her into the lift, and found her waiting for me in the ground-floor entrance hall, fiddling with her phone.

"Oh, Morishita-san! All done?"\
"Yes. Thank you for putting up with my selfish request."\
"Don't mention it. I enjoy this sort of thing."\
"Is that so?"

Perhaps she was only saying it to be considerate of me, but her words made me feel a little reassured.\
Arai-san looked at my face, nodded firmly once, and set off. I hurried to catch up, walking alongside her.

"So, we're looking for a birthday present for Nanoha-chan, right?"\
"Yes."\
"If she's around your age, she'd probably appreciate make-up or something. Do you know what she likes?"\
"Dogs..."\
"Dogs? Anything else?"\
"............"

I didn't want to admit that I didn't know.\
Despite spending so much time with Fujishiro, I felt pathetic for knowing practically nothing about the things she liked.

I'd simply spent time beside her without giving it much thought.\
That had been easy, and I supposed that was why I'd chosen to stay with her. But now I regretted the way I'd behaved in the past.

"Then what about things you've given her before?"\
"Things like a choker, I suppose."\
"Morishita-san, you're rather naughty for someone who doesn't look it."\
"Huh?"

I looked up, completely caught off guard by the unexpected remark.\
Arai-san was clearly enjoying herself, her expression one of pure amusement at my expense.

"Well, a choker is practically a collar, isn't it? A lot of gifts people give each other carry some sort of meaning."\
"I didn't think about any of that when I gave it to her."\
"So you're the type who gets people hopelessly hooked without even realising it."\
"I don't understand what you're saying."\
"Come on, don't get so worked up. I think you're fine just the way you are, Morishita-san."

I wasn't remotely convinced, but Arai-san seemed perfectly satisfied as she continued walking through the streets. I wasn't happy about it, but with my complete lack of taste, all I could do was follow her lead.

Gifts have meanings...\
If I could communicate something through an object rather than words, what would I want to tell Fujishiro?

"For example, what about perfume?"\
"What would that mean?"\
"There's something rather sexy about making someone smell the way you like, isn't there?"\
"Your head really is full of nothing but lewd thoughts, Arai-san."\
"That's so mean!"

Despite my rather harsh comment, Arai-san didn't seem remotely bothered. She simply carried on walking ahead of me.

"Also, it doesn't have to be an object."\
"What do you mean?"\
"For example, taking her out to dinner somewhere beautiful, with a view of the night skyline."\
"Would that make someone happy?"\
"It would make some people happy!"

Would Fujishiro enjoy that?\
If it would make her happy, I thought it might be worth considering.

"Or, if you're having so much trouble deciding, you could bring her along and let her choose something she wants."\
"Fujishiro's the sort of person who'd hesitate to ask for anything like that."\
"Well, that's where you get to show what you're made of, Morishita-san. You have to make sure she doesn't hold back."\
"Show what I'm made of..."

I looked down at my own arms.\
They seemed rather frail and unreliable, hardly the sort of thing I could count on.

"Choosing a present is difficult."\
"From my perspective, I think it's lovely to have the time to agonise over something for someone else. I'm actually a little envious."

Arai-san said this with a wistful expression.

I wondered whether I ought to ask her more about it, but I wasn't particularly interested in her personal affairs, and prying into them might come back to bite me later. So I decided against it.

In the end, despite consulting Arai-san, we never actually got as far as buying a present.

Instead, I listened to a seemingly endless stream of advice, much of which I wasn't sure would be useful at all. Sometimes she'd make silly remarks, and when I played along and teased her back, laughter naturally began to spring up between us.

I'd been worried about whether I'd fit in at work, but I was glad I'd managed to find someone who felt something like a friend.

"This is fine for me. Sorry for keeping you out when we both have work again tomorrow."\
"Not at all! I had a lovely time, so don't worry about it!"

Just as we were saying goodbye, someone came rushing towards us.\
That person grabbed my arm firmly and pushed herself between Arai-san and me.

"Fujishiro?"

I called out to her, but Fujishiro was looking at Arai-san. I couldn't see her expression from where I stood. Unusually, she didn't say a word or move a muscle.

As for Arai-san, she gave an awkward smile and took a step backwards.

"Well, see you tomorrow, Morishita-san!"\
"Ah, yes. Thank you for today."

I'd have liked to thank her more properly, but I could do that tomorrow. I gave Arai-san a small bow.

Once that was settled, I turned my attention to Fujishiro, whose strange behaviour was beginning to concern me. She was still looking down, doing nothing but clutching my arm.

"Fujishiro, what's wrong?"\
"Were you on a date?"\
"A date...? We were just shopping."\
"Then go on an evening date with me, too."\
"I told you, it wasn't a date."\
"Really?"

Fujishiro finally raised her head.\
But her eyes were glistening with tears, and she looked as though she might burst into tears at any moment.

I had no idea what had made her so sad, and a dull ache began to spread through my chest.

"It's true."\
"Then spend the evening with me, too."

Normally, I would have brushed aside a request like that without much thought. But this time, it suited me perfectly.

I'd been wanting to spend time with Fujishiro on her birthday, which was only two days away.

"Then let's go out the day after tomorrow."\
"Okay."\
"I'll head to the station after work, so around six..."\
"I'll wait at the station until you finish, as soon as my university classes are over."\
"No, you don't have to wait..."\
"I'll be waiting."

Fujishiro cut me off insistently and pulled me along by the hand.\
She gripped my arm tightly all the way home, and it hurt, but the atmosphere was such that I couldn't bring myself to tell her.

◆

I'd ended up with a troublesome girl as my colleague.\
Or perhaps troublesome wasn't quite the right word. More like someone I couldn't help looking out for.\
She was so oblivious to the feelings of the people around her that I couldn't help getting involved.

Looking back, I realised I'd been sticking my nose where it didn't belong.\
But I couldn't help it. Morishita-san reminded me so much of my former self.

I once had a childhood friend whom I loved dearly.\
Someone more precious to me than anyone else, someone I couldn't bear to lose.

Someone who was always by my side, someone I could tell anything to, someone who was practically family.\
We always held hands, played together endlessly at home, and even kissed.

But I had been far too oblivious.\
I'd realised both her feelings and my own far too late.

"What's it that you want to be with me, Maho?"

When my childhood friend asked me that, I couldn't give her the right answer.\
All I wanted was for us to remain just as we'd always been. I wanted us to stay the people closest to each other, the people whose hearts were more in tune than anyone else's. I didn't want anything more than that. I simply didn't want her to leave me.

"I want us to stay close childhood friends. Just like we've always been."\
"I see. Then we want different things, so we can't stay together."\
"What...?"

At first, I hadn't taken her words seriously.\
I thought we'd be able to carry on as usual the next day.

But our familiar daily life never returned.

From the following day onwards, my childhood friend started getting close to another girl in our class. And she began avoiding me.

I had no idea what was happening, and all I could do was endure a school life in which I was hurt over and over again.

It wasn't until we were university students that I learnt my childhood friend had been in love with me romantically.

She told me that she'd found a partner with whom she intended to spend the rest of her life, and along with the news, she revealed that she'd been in love with me back in high school.

Only then did I realise that I had loved her.

But by the time I understood my true feelings, it was already too late.

Regret had filled every part of me, leaving a wound in my heart that had never healed, even to this day.

*

"Oh dear. I'm definitely being seen as her rival."

Nanoha-chan had been glaring sharply at me right up until she turned away.

It wasn't as though I had the slightest intention of stealing Morishita-san away. I simply didn't want someone as oblivious to her own feelings as Morishita-san to suffer the same pain I had.

But...

"I'm so jealous of their youth."

Morishita-san and Nanoha-chan, who weren't even that much younger than me, seemed so radiant. I couldn't help feeling envious.\
The two of them were still together, still able to stay by each other's side.

Morishita-san ought to be a little more grateful that Nanoha-chan hadn't left her. I thought that rather presumptuously to myself.

*

I turned my back on the two of them and started walking.

Perhaps even now, it wasn't too late to tell my beloved childhood friend everything I'd kept inside.

I'd thought I was helping Morishita-san find the courage to take a step forward, but somewhere along the way, it seemed she'd ended up giving me the push I needed instead.
