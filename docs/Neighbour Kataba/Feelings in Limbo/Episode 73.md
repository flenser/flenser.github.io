Episode 73

A bright light shone into my eyes.\
My alarm hadn't gone off yet, so it probably wasn't time to get up.

As I gradually came to, the first thing I became aware of was a soft sensation.

Something was gently cradling my head.

When I looked up, I saw Fujishiro gazing down at me, her drooping eyes only half open.

Realising she was stroking my head, I felt embarrassed and caught her hand to make her stop.

"Morning, Morishita."\
"...Morning. What's with that hand?"\
"Morishita, you've got one of those faces that makes me want to pet you."

She squeezed me in a tight hug, leaving me unable to resist. She pulled me so close that my nose was practically squashed, making it difficult to breathe. But the soft fragrance that drifted into my nose wasn't unpleasant.

"Let go."\
"What if I don't want to?"\
"...Then I won't come here again."\
"You're so mean, Morishita."

Fujishiro puffed out her cheeks, but eventually released me.\
However, she didn't move away. She continued gazing at me with her usual cheerful smile.

Actually, rather than looking at me, she seemed to be staring at my collarbone.\
I glanced down, wondering what she was looking at, but there didn't seem to be anything wrong with my clothes.

"What?"\
"Hmm?"

Even when I asked, Fujishiro simply kept looking at me with a happy expression. I grew tired of it and tried to close my eyes again.\
But Fujishiro suddenly got out of bed, and I wondered what she was doing as I sat up.

"Fujishiro? Are you getting up?"\
"Yeah. I need to get ready."\
"Already?"\
"Yep."\
"Then I'll get up too."\
"Don't you have work, Morishita? You can keep sleeping. I'll wake you up when it's time."\
"No, I'm getting up."

I stood and stretched.\
Following Fujishiro, I went to the bathroom and stood beside her at the washbasin.

It had been a peaceful morning, but when I caught sight of my neck, I could no longer feel quite so peaceful.

"What's this?"\
"Hmm? You mean the love bite?"\
"No, a love bite...?"

Now I understood why Fujishiro had bitten my collarbone last night. She'd touched me specifically to leave this mark.

I couldn't help frowning as I stared at her.

"Why did you do that?"\
"Who knows?"\
"How do I get rid of it?"\
"It won't disappear until some time has passed. Your clothes will cover it, so it's fine, right?"\
"You're an idiot, Fujishiro."

I reflexively pinched her cheek. But Fujishiro merely let out a delighted little "Ow, that hurts!"

I really had no idea what to do with her sudden, incomprehensible antics. I sighed, but it seemed to have no effect on Fujishiro.

"Morishita, do you want to wash your face first?"\
"No, you go ahead."\
"Then I'll go first."

With that, she began splashing water over her face.

When she'd finished washing, she dried herself with a towel and looked at me with a refreshed expression.

"All yours."\
"Okay. Thanks."

I splashed cold water over my own face.\
Washing up cleared away the little sleepiness that remained, along with my irritation towards Fujishiro. I felt much more refreshed.

When we went into the living room, Fujishiro immediately began preparing breakfast.

I casually moved to stand beside her as she cooked.

Did she really get up this early every day to prepare everything?

"Do you always get up this early?"\
"Yeah. I'm still not used to cooking, you see."\
"You don't have to make my bento."\
"Don't say something so sad."

Fujishiro looked at me with a melancholy expression.\
I'd only said it because I didn't want to inconvenience her, but it seemed I'd ended up hurting her instead.

It was only now that I was beginning to appreciate just how difficult it could be to choose the right words.\
In that case... I decided to ask her what I could do, as a way of making up for having upset her.

"Then is there anything I can help with?"\
"Hmm. I'd like you to peel some potatoes."\
"Okay."

We set to work quietly, each concentrating on our own task.

As I peeled the potatoes, I casually glanced at the calendar in the room.

17 April.

I was surprised to realise that half a month had already passed since I'd started working.

And, rather inconveniently, Fujishiro's birthday was only a few days away.

I felt as though I'd struggled with what to give her last year, too.

It would probably be quicker to ask Fujishiro directly than agonise over it myself. I followed her instructions as I worked, waiting for a chance to bring it up.

"Hey, Fujishiro. Is there anything you want at the moment?"\
"Something I want? I'd love as much affection from Morishita as I could get."\
"................"\
"I'm joking! As for actual things, there's nothing in particular."

Fujishiro answered with a laugh, but her expression resembled the one she wore when she was hurt.

Seeing that expression left a small knot of unease in my chest.

I wanted to do something for Fujishiro in return, but not knowing what to do was becoming painful.

"Hey, hey, more importantly. Don't you think we look like newlyweds?"\
"Huh?"\
"Getting up in the morning and making breakfast together like this. We're so lovey-dovey!"\
"You don't have to be newlyweds to make breakfast together."\
"Ahaha, that's true."

Even as she chatted, Fujishiro continued preparing everything with practised efficiency. I hurried to keep up with her.

Although I'd only been helping, the ingredients were soon packed into the bento boxes. On top of that, she'd prepared a colourful breakfast.

There was fish in front of me, and the twice-cooked pork I'd made in front of Fujishiro.

"Twice-cooked pork for breakfast? You're certainly energetic."\
"You made it, so I wanted to try it as soon as possible."\
"Oh."\
"Mmm! This is delicious! Morishita, you're really good at cooking!"\
"I just bought some sauce and mixed it in."\
"Even so, it's really tasty. Thank you."

Fujishiro smiled with her usual carefree, sunny expression.\
Her innocence was something I envied.

I thought Fujishiro had a talent for making people happy so effortlessly.

If I actually complimented her on it, she'd probably get carried away, so I would never say it aloud.

I took a bite of the fish Fujishiro had grilled for me.

After breakfast, I accepted my bento from Fujishiro and prepared to return to my own flat.

"Thanks for letting me stay over. And for the bento, too."\
"You're welcome. Morishita, come here for a moment."\
"Hm?"

I felt her pull my arm, and before I knew it, Fujishiro was holding me in a tight embrace.

A faint citrus fragrance drifted from her hair.

"Come and stay over again sometime."\
"I have somewhere to sleep right next door."\
"When you're here, my nights are more fun."

My nights are more fun...

I wanted to ask what kind of reason that was, but Fujishiro's voice seemed to tremble, so I decided against it.

I hadn't felt lonely last night either, so I supposed it had been a rather nice evening.

Still, I had plenty of complaints I'd like to make to Arai-san. I'd touched Fujishiro so much, but all it had done was leave me with even more things I didn't understand.

After a while, Fujishiro gently released me.\
I thanked her once more, then left her flat.

◆

I changed into my work clothes, got ready, and checked that Fujishiro's bento was in my bag before leaving home.

Usually, the first thing I did when I arrived at work was turn on my computer. But today, there was something I needed to do first, so I headed over to Arai-san, who had arrived before me.

"Um, would you like to have lunch together today?"\
"I don't think anyone's ever invited me to lunch in quite such a strained voice before."

Arai-san gave an awkward smile. I hadn't meant to sound that way, but I regretted letting my unpleasant attitude show, even a little.

"I'm sorry..."\
"It's all right. Let's have lunch together."\
"Thank you."

I gave her a little bow, then returned to work.
