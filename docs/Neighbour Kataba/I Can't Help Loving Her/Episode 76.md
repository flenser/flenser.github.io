Episode 76

I had no reason to hurry, and yet I rushed home.

I touched up my make-up, curled my hair again, and changed into one of my favourite outfits, something I'd bought recently.

Of course, I didn't forget to put on the choker Morishita had given me. Whenever I wore it, Morishita seemed to spend a little longer looking at me than usual.

Finally, since the perfume Yudzuki had given me smelled so lovely, I gave myself a single spray. In an instant, I was surrounded by its pleasant fragrance.

There was still time, but I decided to head for the station anyway.

With my legs practically bursting with energy, I set off.

The sun was beginning to set, and the streets were crowded with people out shopping.

To avoid being swallowed up by the crowds, I stood in an open area where I could keep an eye on the station.\
I put on my earphones and watched short videos to pass the time.

Perhaps because I'd been standing for so long, my legs began to tire. I'd lost track of how many times I'd swiped through videos when someone leaned in to look at my face.

The soft, soapy scent of Morishita.\
I knew it was her without even looking up, and the corners of my mouth naturally curled into a smile.

"Good work today."\
"Sorry. Have you been waiting long?"\
"Not at all. I only just got here."\
"That's good."

I looked Morishita up and down, taking my time.

I saw her in her suit every morning when I handed over her bento, but no matter how often I saw her, I still thought she looked both cool and cute.

It had only been a month since she'd been wearing a school uniform, yet now I thought a suit suited her better than her uniform ever had.

"Why are you grinning at me like that?"\
"Oh, sorry, sorry. I was just admiring how wonderful you look."\
"I don't know what you're talking about. Come on, let's go."\
"Huh? Oh, okay..."

Morishita grabbed my wrist and started walking.\
I'd assumed she'd simply be accompanying me on my usual shopping trip, so I was left struggling to catch up with what was happening.

I hurried to get alongside her and asked,

"Where are we going?"\
"...Somewhere."\
"Ehh...?"\
"Fujishiro, are you hungry?"\
"Well, yeah, a little."\
"Right."

Morishita pressed her lips together in a sulk.\
I couldn't tell whether she didn't feel like talking to me or was simply unsure what to say. Either way, I decided it was probably best not to keep asking questions, so I fell silent.

The place she brought me to was a stylish Western-style restaurant that had recently become popular on social media. It was particularly famous for its omurice, and I'd been wanting to try it for ages.

But I couldn't help finding it strange that Morishita had brought me somewhere like this.

"Morishita, do you want to eat omurice?"\
"Not particularly."\
"Why don't we go somewhere you want to eat?"\
"Don't you like this place?"\
"No, I'm happy to come here, but..."\
"Then we'll eat here."\
"O-okay...?"

Since Morishita didn't seem inclined to change her mind, we went inside.

Morishita held out the menu for me.

"Have you decided what you want?"\
"Yeah."\
"That was quick."

She'd decided so quickly, almost as if she'd known from the beginning what she was going to order.

I didn't want to keep her waiting, so I hurriedly scanned the menu.

I'd barely settled on what I wanted when Morishita took the opportunity to call over a waiter.

"May I take your order?"\
"One regular omurice, please. Fujishiro?"\
"I'll have the mushroom cream omurice."\
"Certainly. The soup and salad are self-service, so please help yourselves."

The waiter said this in a cool, businesslike manner before walking away. As though following after them, Morishita stood up.

"I'll get yours too, so you can stay here."\
"Huh? But..."\
"It's fine. Just wait here."

With that, Morishita headed over to the self-service counter.

Left alone at the table, I found all sorts of thoughts running through my head.

Morishita wasn't acting like herself.\
She was being much more proactive than usual.

Come to think of it, wasn't it practically a miracle for Morishita to take me out somewhere herself?

It felt as though all sorts of inexplicable things were happening, and I couldn't quite settle down.

After a little while, Morishita came back, carefully carrying a glass in each hand, walking slowly to keep them level. Worried she might spill something, I stood up and took one from her.

"Thanks."\
"Mm. I'll get the salad too."\
"I want to come with you."

I said it out of consideration, feeling that leaving Morishita to manage on her own might be a little risky. But she looked thoroughly displeased.

Even so, she didn't tell me not to come, so I went with her to get the salad, and we returned to our seats.

"Morishita, what's got into you all of a sudden?"\
"What do you mean?"\
"Well... um..."

It seemed rather rude to say that it was unusual for her to bring me somewhere like this, so I faltered.

There were plenty of things I wanted to ask, but I felt that simply enjoying this time together was probably the best thing we could do right now. So I decided not to say anything else that might spoil the moment.

About ten minutes later, our delicious-looking omurice arrived, and we put our hands together before starting to eat.

The fluffy egg melted in my mouth, and happiness spread across my palate.

I couldn't have been happier to be sitting here, eating out with Morishita like this.\
It was hard to stop myself from grinning too much.

"It's delicious, isn't it?"\
"Mm."\
"How was work today, Morishita?"\
"Same as always. How about you?"\
"I had my usual classes too."\
"Are there lots of people at university?"\
"Yeah. So many more than there were at high school."\
"I see."\
"Would you like to come and visit sometime?"\
"Huh?"\
"They don't take attendance individually, so nobody would notice if you sat in on a lecture."\
"Really?"\
"Yeah. If you can get a day off, you should come."\
"O-okay..."

It was a hesitant answer, but somehow I felt she was considering it seriously.

After that, I continued telling her little, everyday stories about what had happened at university, and before I knew it, dinner was over.

"I'm so full!"\
"Did you like this place?"\
"Like it...? Yeah. The food was really delicious."\
"Good."

Perhaps I was imagining it, but I thought the corners of Morishita's mouth lifted just a little. However, she immediately stood up and headed for the till, so I quickly checked that I hadn't left anything behind before following her.

The other people in the restaurant were full of energy.\
There were couples among them, too.

I'd found myself gazing enviously at the couples happily flirting with each other, but I shook off that feeling and went to stand beside Morishita.

"Let's go."\
"Wait, I'll pay for my share."\
"I've already paid."\
"Excuse me?"

Morishita had an awkward, uncomfortable look on her face.\
I desperately tried to get my thoughts moving again, which had ground to a halt in surprise.

"How much was it? I'll pay you back."\
"You don't need to."\
"Huh?"\
"I got paid."\
"That's not what I mean..."

As I tried to say something more, Morishita took hold of my face, pressing both my cheeks inwards. My lips were puckered like a fish's, and I was definitely not looking cute right now.

I hated the thought of her seeing me with such a ridiculous expression, but when I saw Morishita smiling at me, I stopped caring.

"Ahaha. What a funny face."\
"That's rude."\
"You're even more of an idiot than usual."\
"Don't say it like I'm always an idiot!"\
"Your face always looks a bit silly."\
"Wha...!"

Just as I was about to protest, Morishita took my hand and tugged me along.

Perhaps she'd grown impatient because I'd been standing there instead of walking, or perhaps there was somewhere she wanted to go in a hurry.

I didn't know the reason, but Morishita rarely reached out for me like that of her own accord. It was enough to make me feel as though I might be getting ahead of myself.

Morishita really was a crafty person.\
And because she did these things without even realising it, she was all the more wicked.

Still, I felt so overwhelmingly happy in response to her teasing that I began to wonder whether there was something loose in my head.
