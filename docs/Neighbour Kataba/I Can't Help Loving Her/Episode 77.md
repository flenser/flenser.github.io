Episode 77

Morishita kept holding my hand the whole time.

Usually, I was the one dragging her around by the hand and making her accompany me on shopping trips, so having things this way round felt wonderfully novel.

"Morishita, is there somewhere you'd like to go?"\
"Not particularly."

Despite saying that, she walked with a firm, purposeful stride, as though she already knew exactly where she was going.

As we walked side by side, Morishita suddenly brought her face close to me and began sniffing.

"What!? Do I smell bad!?"\
"No. You smell different from usual. Are you wearing something?"

I wanted to retort, What are you, a dog!?\
It had been a while since I'd put on the perfume, and the fragrance had faded, so I'd completely forgotten about it.

"I'm wearing perfume."\
"Hmm. Did you buy it?"\
"No. Yudzuki gave it to me."\
"That's... the one about wanting to make it your own scent?"\
"Huh?"

I tilted my head at Morishita's incomprehensible remark, but she merely frowned and started walking again.

After a while, we entered a rather stylish shop inside the shopping centre, almost as though we'd been drawn inside.\
Morishita slowed her pace once we were in there.

I had no idea what she was planning, but asking seemed somehow tactless, so I looked over the displayed goods alongside her.

We reached the accessories section, where a necklace caught my eye, glinting beautifully. The necklace on the mannequin looked almost like a cute little collar, and my heart began to race.

As I browsed the accessories, I unconsciously touched the choker around my neck.

If only this were a collar Morishita had put on me...

How many times had I wished for that?

Of course, that was just my own interpretation. I was sure Morishita had given it to me simply because she thought it suited me, or something along those lines.

But now, the choker alone wasn't enough for me.

My neck, my hands, my feet.\
I wished she would bind every part of me in chains.

I wanted Morishita to claim me as her own.

I knew she wasn't the sort of person who would ever do something like that.\
Perhaps it was precisely because I knew it could never happen that I wanted it so desperately.

I tried on a chain bracelet. Set into its centre was a deep crimson natural stone.

Despite its delicate chain, it felt strangely conspicuous once fastened around my wrist, an accessory that drew my attention to my hand.

"Hello. That bracelet is lovely, isn't it? It has a garnet set in it, which I think gives it a really striking appearance."\
"I thought it was very beautiful."

The shop assistant had spoken to me, and I felt a little guilty for trying it on when I had no intention of buying it. I was just about to take it off and put it back where I'd found it when Morishita caught hold of my wrist.

"Excuse me, we'd like to buy this."\
"Huh? Morishita...?"\
"Fujishiro, weren't you wearing it because you wanted it?"

It wasn't as though I absolutely had to have it, but I certainly had been drawn to it.

While I fumbled for an answer, Morishita calmly completed the purchase with the shop assistant.

I stood there in a daze until Morishita approached me, looking rather displeased.

She took me by the arm, led me outside, and immediately took the bracelet out of its box and fastened it around my wrist.

My heart raced at having Morishita do something like that for me.

"Why...?"\
"I don't know."

You don't know, huh?\
That was so like Morishita.

She must have had some reason for doing it, but I didn't need to know what it was right now. Just the fact that Morishita had given me something was enough to make me happy.

I couldn't take my eyes off the bracelet dangling from my wrist.

"Thank you, Morishita."\
"Mm."\
"Thank you."\
"I heard you."

Morishita turned her face away, looking embarrassed, and started walking. I hurried after her.

Happy moments always seemed to pass in the blink of an eye. It was already approaching nine o'clock.

I had university tomorrow, and Morishita had work.

Her feet were carrying us towards our apartment.

I knew it couldn't be helped, and that there were things I simply had to accept, but I'd become greedy for more time with Morishita.

"That omurice was delicious."\
"Mm."\
"And I'm really happy about the bracelet. Thank you."\
"Mm."\
"Let's go out for a meal together again sometime. Next time, let me treat you. I'm going to start a part-time job, so once I get my first pay cheque, let's go."\
"...Mm. But I'll pay for my own share."\
"Morishita, you're so stubborn about things like that."\
"Stubborn..."

Morishita was making that troubled expression again.\
Perhaps she found it difficult and frustrating to wrestle with her own thoughts, but I loved even that expression of hers.

We reached the apartment building, and I unlocked the door to my flat.\
Morishita went next door and unlocked hers, too.

I tried to suppress my longing and open my door, but then I noticed Morishita standing right beside me. I nearly jumped. She was looking straight into my eyes.

"What's wrong?"\
"Um..."\
"Yes?"\
"Happy birthday."

The gaze she'd been holding so steadily began to waver, and she lowered her eyes, suddenly looking vulnerable.

I was so surprised that I couldn't even speak.\
It took me a long time to understand what was happening, but then all of Morishita's unusual behaviour that evening suddenly fell into place.

I'd told myself over and over again not to get my hopes up, but how could I possibly stop myself from feeling giddy with happiness after she'd done something like this?

And yet Morishita didn't seem inclined to give me even a moment to show how happy I was.

She quickly turned away and started to head back inside.

I immediately wrapped my arms around her from behind.

A throbbing warmth spread around my eyes, and the back of my nose began to sting.

"What?"\
"Can I come over to your place tonight?"\
"Why?"\
"I want to spend more time with you..."

I couldn't think of any clever words just then. No smooth explanation, no plausible excuse came to mind.

All I could do was pour my overflowing feelings straight into my words and give them to Morishita.

I swallowed hard and waited for her answer.

Morishita began to fidget, then turned around and gave my shoulders a firm push.

Her action made my heart clench painfully.

"Ten minutes."\
"Hm?"\
"Give me ten minutes. I'll tidy up."\
"Eh? You mean... I can?"\
"Yeah."

Morishita nodded firmly and went inside.

I took one deep breath in and let it out, then went into my own flat to fetch my pyjamas.
