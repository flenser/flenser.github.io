Episode 79

Little by little, I was getting used to Morishita's home. Perhaps that was why the tension had finally begun to ease, and sleepiness was creeping over me.

As I started yawning more and more often, Morishita considerately dimmed the lights in the room.

Hm...?

“Shall we go to sleep soon?”\
“Is it all right if I stay the night?”\
“If you go out now, you'll get cold. Come over here.”\
“Eh...?”

Usually, Morishita would make a fuss about sleeping on the floor or not wanting to share a bed with me. But today was different.

She had climbed into bed before me and was lying there, looking in my direction.

“Aren't you coming?”\
“Er, is that really all right?”\
“Where else are you going to sleep?”

She said it as though I were the one being unreasonable, but she had no idea just how happy it made me to have her not turn me away.

Trying to contain my excitement, I lay down beside her.

I settled in cautiously, taking care not to get too close to Morishita. Surrounded by her scent, my head began to spin.

“You'll fall off if you don't come closer.”\
“Y-yes...”

As she had instructed, I edged towards her, little by little.\
Once I had settled comfortably, I lifted my head. Morishita was right there, so close that my heart leapt.

I knew this would happen, yet my body hadn't caught up with that knowledge. My breath stopped without me even realising it.

And then, before I knew it, the words were coming out of my mouth.

“I'm sorry.”\
“Hmm?”\
“Because you're so important to me, Morishita, I want to kiss you.”\
“...What?”

It was a contradictory thing to say.\
If Morishita really was that important to me, then I should never say anything that might make her uncomfortable.

I'd decided to let her fall for me little by little.

She had a heart so delicate that I could scarcely comprehend it. I'd promised myself that I would approach her slowly, without rushing her feelings or hurting her.

But I could no longer stop myself.

I caught her collar and pulled her towards me, closing the distance between us.\
When our lips were just about to touch, her hand covered my mouth.

The pain of being rejected by the person I loved was beyond anything I could have imagined.\
It felt as though my heart had been pierced by knives from every direction.

And yet, I think Morishita looked even more distressed than I did. Her trembling lips slowly parted.

“One day...”\
“Hmm? One day?”\
“One day, you won't need me any more...”\
“Won't need you?”\
“Yeah.”

Her voice was shaking, rough and hoarse.\
She seemed terribly sad, as though she were frightened of something.

I wished I had the strength to wrap up every last one of Morishita's anxieties and hold them close. The realisation of how powerless I was weighed me down. But this was no time to lament my own helplessness.

I softened my eyes and gazed at her.

“You mean you think you'll stop being important to me?”\
“That's right. I won't be important to you any more.”\
“Morishita will always be someone important to me. Someone I love.”\
“That's just pretty words. I can't believe in ‘always’.”

Her voice was so sorrowful that I could feel the pain in it.

Perhaps she was right.

Even people who were supposed to be family couldn't always cherish one another.\
They couldn't even love one another.

If that was true of family, then expecting it of two complete strangers seemed all the more impossible.

But I thought that nobody could know what the future held.

“Then I'll prove it to you properly.”\
“Prove it?”\
“I'll love you until the day I die, Morishita.”\
“That's...”

I took hold of Morishita's wrist and pressed my lips against hers, silencing the words she couldn't seem to stop herself from saying.\
At first, carried away by the force of the moment, I brought our lips together rather clumsily. After that, though, I pressed them gently against hers, again and again.

If she didn't want this, I wanted her to refuse me.\
I pressed my lips to hers softly, over and over, giving her time to push me away if she wished.

I had been prepared for rejection. But Morishita merely gazed at me with vacant eyes. That only stirred my desire, until I was using my tongue to prise her mouth open.

I didn't want to force a kiss on her and make her hate me. And yet, even more than that, I couldn't stop wanting to touch her. I drove her into a corner, leaving her no escape from my heat.

Morishita's body gradually melted into mine, matching my own feverish warmth. We became so entwined that the boundary between our breaths grew indistinct.

It was far too late by the time I realised how completely I'd surrendered myself to desire.

When I finally pulled away from Morishita, her breathing was terribly ragged. She looked as though she was struggling for air.

“I-I'm sorry...”\
“...It's all right.”

An awkward silence settled between us.\
There was no point in staying awake like this, so I quietly lay down beside her.

Morishita turned towards the wall and wouldn't look at me.

Her breathing was steady now, her shoulders rising and falling slowly.

“Morishita...”\
“...Hmm?”\
“Are you angry?”\
“Not really.”\
“Then, could you turn this way?”

Her movements stopped dead. I gave her back a gentle poke, and Morishita eventually turned to face me.

But the expression on her face was one I could never have imagined. A chill ran down my spine.

Beautiful droplets were streaming in torrents from Morishita's eyes.

I was confronted with just how awful what I'd done had been, and I had no idea what to do.

“I'm really sorry. I'm sorry I did something you didn't want.”\
“I told you, I'm not angry.”\
“I'm sorry...”

Apologising was all I could do.\
Perhaps I should have left right then and there.

Or perhaps the right thing would have been to let Morishita hit me until she'd had enough.

But the words caught in my throat, and nothing came out.

As I turned things over and over in my head, her forehead gently bumped against mine.

Morishita's arms had come around my body. We were as close as we'd been when I kissed her, and my heart began pounding wildly all over again.

“Stay here until I fall asleep.”\
“Eh?”

Morishita said that, then closed her eyes.

Had she somehow sensed from my manner that I was about to leave, even though I hadn't said a word?

No. It was probably just a coincidence.

Whatever her reason, the fact that she had allowed me to stay close to her, even after I'd done something so awful, was the one thing I could cling to.

I stroked Morishita's cheek, gently wiping away the tears still clinging to it. At first, her face tightened, but gradually the tension left her features and they softened.

As I watched Morishita sleeping, I too drifted off before I knew it.
