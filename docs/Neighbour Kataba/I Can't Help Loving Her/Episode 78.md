Episode 78

**What should I do?**\
I was so nervous that I could hardly breathe.

Back in high school, I had never once gone to Morishita's house, so this would be the first time I stayed over.

It was also the first time I'd ever stayed in the room of someone I was in love with.

Thinking about it calmly, Morishita had only agreed to let me into her home. I hadn't even asked whether I could stay the night. And yet here I was, getting ready to bring my pyjamas. Would that really be all right?

So many things had happened all at once that I felt as though my head might burst.

At any rate, I stuffed my pyjamas and the little bottles of shampoo I'd prepared into my bag.

Since we lived next door, I could easily go back for anything I'd forgotten. But once I was inside Morishita's home, I wanted to avoid leaving again if at all possible, so I checked my things over and over.

By the time I was finally satisfied that I had everything, ten minutes had passed. I hurried over to Morishita's and rang the doorbell.

I heard the lock click, and Morishita appeared, her expression perfectly neutral.

"Come in."\
"Excuse me."

The layout was exactly the same as my own flat, and yet I felt the same excitement as when entering somewhere completely new for the first time.

I took my time looking around. I'd thought the same thing when I'd briefly been inside before, but the place was still immaculately clean. It was a simple room, just as I'd expect from Morishita.

Her faint, familiar scent lingered throughout the flat. I took several little breaths, one after another.

Even her scent was something I loved. Honestly, I was hopelessly smitten.

"You can use the bath first."\
"Then I'll take you up on that. Oh... want to get in together?"

It was only a joke.\
I'd made the flippant suggestion to distract myself from my own nerves.

"I don't mind."\
"What!?"\
"Heh. What a funny face."

Morishita looked at me and smiled.\
It was such a gentle, peaceful expression. I didn't think I'd seen her look like that very often when we were in high school.

And then I realised that she was playing me like a fiddle, and I tried to compose my expression.

She must have known how I felt about her when she made that remark. If she'd said it fully expecting me to pull a ridiculous face, then she really was a wicked girl.

"You're so mean, Morishita."\
"Yes, yes. Take your time and warm up properly."\
"Th-thanks..."

Her reply was so unruffled that my embarrassment reached its absolute peak. I fled towards the bathroom as though making my escape.

I was in the middle of taking off all my clothes when the changing-room door slid open.

I immediately covered myself with a towel.

"Morishita!?"\
"Oh, sorry... I bought some bath salts, and I thought I'd like you to use them."

Morishita handed them to me without looking in my direction.

I snatched them from her and, though I knew it was rude, immediately closed the changing-room door.

How much of me had Morishita seen just now?

We'd bathed together before.\
We'd seen each other's bodies naked.\
She'd even touched me quite a lot the other day.

There shouldn't have been any reason to feel embarrassed at this point. And yet, being seen by the person I loved suddenly felt terribly shameful.

Still flushed all over, I got into the bath.\
The little tub had already been filled with clear water.

Realising she'd gone to the trouble of filling it for me left me feeling both guilty and happy.

I sank into the water, my thoughts filling entirely with Morishita, until I felt as though I might overheat.

Realising how dangerous that was, I quickly washed myself and got out.

I was so carried away, rushing around in a fluster. It was embarrassing.

I needed to pull myself together. I patted both cheeks a few times, then slipped into my pyjamas. Putting my choker on now would feel unnatural, but I thought there was nothing strange about wearing the bracelet, so I fastened it around my wrist.

"Morishita, thanks for the bath."\
"Mm."

As soon as I came out, Morishita headed straight for the bathroom.\
She'd left a hairdryer ready on the table. It really brought home just how considerate Morishita was.

She might seem matter-of-fact, but she paid close attention to the people around her and always chose what she thought was the best thing to do.

I didn't know whether that was because of the environment she'd grown up in or simply her own nature. But I wished she could relax a little more when she was with me.

After drying my hair, I found myself stroking the bracelet dangling from my wrist.

Time passed so easily as I simply sat there admiring the present Morishita had given me. When she came out of the bath, she sat down beside me.

"Morishita, thanks for the hairdryer."\
"Don't mention it. Can I borrow it?"\
"I'll dry your hair for you."\
"I can do it myself."

Morishita rather abruptly took the hairdryer from me.

Perhaps my ulterior motive, wanting even the slightest excuse to touch her, had been all too obvious.

Chastising myself for it, I waited beside her until she'd finished drying her hair.

The noisy whir of the hairdryer finally stopped. Morishita quickly put it away, then came straight back to sit beside me. She leaned against me, resting her head on me.

It was so unusual for Morishita to act affectionately like that that my heart felt as though it might twist itself apart.

She stayed there, reaching out to touch the bracelet on my arm.

"Did you really want this?"\
"Hm? Yes, I'm happy with it."\
"I see."\
"What's wrong?"\
"I'm sorry I didn't know what you wanted."\
"Morishita, did something happen? What's the matter?"

Her behaviour was so strange that I couldn't help leaning over to look at her face. Her brows were furrowed.

I asked her again and again what was wrong, but she had completely shut herself off.

How could I understand what Morishita was feeling?\
How could I make even a little of the weight she carried inside her feel lighter?

I thought and thought, with the limited brain I'd been given.

"Morishita."\
"...Hm?"

I called her name as gently as I could. She lifted her head, which had been hanging down uncertainly.

Once I was sure she was looking at me, I pressed my lips to the bracelet she'd given me.

What I really wanted was to kiss Morishita.\
Because she was precious to me, because I loved her so much, I wanted to kiss her.

This bracelet was precious to me, too.\
I acted in the hope that somehow, this feeling would reach her.

Morishita, who had been watching me, blushed faintly and looked away.

"What are you doing?"\
"I kissed it because it's precious to me."\
"Right..."\
"It's my treasure. Thank you. I'll cherish it for the rest of my life."

She didn't reply, but the tension had faded from Morishita's face.
