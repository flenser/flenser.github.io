Episode 75

I woke up early.

I felt a little embarrassed by how ridiculously simple I was.\
My plans with Morishita weren't until the evening. First, I had to go to university.

I rolled over and gently placed my hand against the wall.

I wonder what Morishita is doing right now.\
She's probably still asleep.

Suddenly, I was reminded of what had happened the other day, when she'd stayed over at my place. A gentle warmth began to spread from deep within my body.

What had Morishita been thinking when she'd touched me so much?

Whatever her reasons, she knew perfectly well that I was in love with her, and yet she'd done all that. It was a little mean of her.

I'd been so desperate not to let any strange sounds escape that I'd bitten down hard on my lip. I could almost taste the iron.

Just a little...

I wanted her to forgive me for using Morishita to satisfy the desire that was welling up inside me.

Just imagining Morishita's gentle hands wandering over my body was enough to make my thoughts drift away from reality.

I wanted her to touch every part of me.

Even the deepest places, places I didn't know existed myself...

How happy would I be if Morishita loved me?\
How good would it feel?

Making that fantasy real seemed about as difficult as grasping a cloud, but even the imagining alone was enough to leave me drowning in pleasure...

*

At some point, I must have fallen asleep, because I suddenly jerked upright.

I checked the clock. It was the same time I usually woke up, and I was relieved I hadn't overslept.

As usual, I prepared breakfast and two packed lunches, then rang the doorbell at the house next door.

Morishita always accepts her lunch with a rather troubled expression. There have been times when I've wondered whether I'm imposing on her, but she eats it properly and brings the empty lunchbox back, so I've continued handing them over, taking advantage of her kindness.

Our eyes met, and I suddenly remembered that morning's thoughts. My face grew hot.

"Th-then, see you this evening."\
"Hm?"\
"Good luck at work."\
"Mm. Thanks."

Once I saw Morishita nod, I turned my back to her.

"Um, Fujishiro!"\
"Hm?"

Morishita, who was usually so quiet, called after me in an unusually loud voice. I turned around in surprise. Her brow was still furrowed, as ever.

"Good luck at university."\
"That's all..."

I was about to say, That's all? but managed to hold it back.

The anxious look on Morishita's face, the unfamiliar tone of her voice, the way she carried herself...

I could feel just how hard she'd tried to say those words.

Morishita, who even needed time to work up the courage to say something as simple as good luck, was so unbearably dear to me.

"Thank you. I'll do my best."\
"Mm."

I planted my feet firmly on the ground and made my way back inside, resisting the urge to skip.

Once I was alone, I was practically floating with delight.

I felt as though Morishita and I were growing a little closer, bit by bit.\
That made me happier than I could say.

Still giddy, I left for university several dozen minutes earlier than usual.

The streets seemed to sparkle.\
I was amazed by how bright and cheerful the world could look when something wonderful was waiting for me at the end of the day.

I was walking along my usual route when someone tapped me on the shoulder from behind.

"Morning, Naoha."\
"Oh, Yudzuki. Morning. You're up early."\
"If I don't get here early, it's hard to get a good seat."\
"Thanks for always doing that."

I hadn't realised Yudzuki came to university this early.\
I'd always thought she was good at securing the best spots in the lecture theatre, but I hadn't imagined she came in ahead of time just to choose our seats.

As expected of such a model student.

Yudzuki cautiously reached towards the ends of my hair. I subtly moved out of reach, and she gave me a sad look.

"Naoha, did something good happen?"\
"Huh? Why?"\
"You're smiling so much. Did something happen with Mei-chan?"\
"..."

How did she know?\
Was my face somehow displaying the words Something good happened with Morishita?

I was so painfully transparent to Yudzuki that I couldn't help feeling uncomfortable, absurd as that worry was.

"You live near each other now, don't you?"\
"Yeah. More or less."\
"Do you see each other often?"\
"Well, yeah."\
"Do you go to each other's places?"\
"Why are you so interested?"\
"I am interested."

Yudzuki looked so serious that, despite having been ready to stand my ground, I found myself faltering.

"Well... sometimes."\
"Hmm. Then can I come over to your place sometime?"\
"Huh? Why?"\
"Because I want to go to your place, Naoha."\
"Well, I suppose that's fine..."

I had no particular reason to refuse, so I agreed. Still, I couldn't help wondering what Yudzuki would even do at my place.

There was a certain tension between us, and my cheerful mood was beginning to settle back down.

Yudzuki stayed quiet for a while, but then she suddenly seemed to remember something. Her eyes widened, and she rummaged through her bag.

Eventually, she pulled out a red bag and handed it to me. I tilted my head, looking at it, while Yudzuki glanced away with an awkward expression.

"Happy birthday."\
"Oh! Right..."\
"Don't tell me you'd forgotten your own birthday?"\
"I'd completely forgotten."\
"How does that even happen?"

Yudzuki looked exasperated, but I was even more exasperated with myself for having forgotten my own birthday.

"Ahaha. Thank you. Can I see what's inside?"\
"Of course."

I opened the bag and was surprised to find a bottle of perfume from a fairly well-known brand.

"Wait, this is expensive!"\
"It's not that expensive. I could afford it with the money I earned from my part-time job."\
"You really didn't have to work just to buy something for me."\
"It's something I wanted to do for you, Naoha."

There was a sharpness to Yudzuki's voice.

Lately, I'd been thinking she seemed a little childish, or perhaps more quick-tempered than before.\
There was no way I could say that to her, though. For now, I ought to accept her feelings sincerely.

I took the cap off and gave myself a single spray of perfume.

It was a citrus scent, not too strong, and it made me feel relaxed.

"I really love this smell."\
"I thought you would."\
"Thank you. I'll treasure it."\
"Mm. I'm glad."

By the time I'd put Yudzuki's present away in my bag, we'd arrived at the lecture theatre.

Once I sat down, my thoughts returned to Morishita.

Morishita's birthday was in June.\
If I started now, I could get a part-time job and have plenty of time to choose a present for her.

I ought to thank Yudzuki for reminding me to think about Morishita's birthday, I thought.

I was so excited about what lay ahead that I couldn't concentrate on the lecture.
