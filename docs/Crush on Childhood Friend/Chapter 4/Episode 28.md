## Episode 28: Everyday Life With My Childhood-Friend Girlfriend Is Just Too Convenient!

\
Out of nowhere, I've started thinking that my life — Sukue Yotsuka's life — is way too convenient. Like, there's a limit.

...Ah, wait, hold on. Don't get the wrong idea.

I don't mean anything vulgar like my parents' stocks suddenly skyrocketing and letting me live without ever working again, or getting scouted off the street and rocketing straight down the idol-career path, or regaining memories from a past life just in time to dodge becoming a villainess. Nothing like that.

I mean, well, how do I put this.

...After years of one-sided love for my childhood friend, we're now officially, happily, dating!!!!!!

◇◆◇◆◇

I used to wonder what would actually change, now that we're dating.\
Turns out, quite a lot has, actually.

Kisa-san figured it out almost instantly. Just from watching us walk home together after our shift, she got this delighted, knowing grin and said, "You two — that's the 'finally' face if I've ever seen one."\
Then she slapped me on the shoulder, shouting "On the house, on the house!" with a face permanently stuck in a smirk.

A few days later, at my next shift, she slid over a mountain of chāshū pork and bean sprouts — a full "mashi-mashi" order I definitely never asked for.

"Dating takes a lot of energy, doesn't it? Eat up properly!"

I can't eat that much, and what exactly do you think I'm using all that "energy" for?!

The seasons slipped by fast, and at year's end, the two of us went to the local shrine together for the first shrine visit of the new year.\
Standing side by side, hands pressed together, neither of us said what we wished for.\
Probably wished for the same thing... I hope.

For Valentine's Day, I attempted handmade chocolate for the very first time in my life.\
Until now I'd just handed out store-bought stuff as casual "friend chocolate" — but this time it meant something different. This was the real thing.

Result: an absolute, spectacular disaster.\
No matter how many times I tried, I just kept producing charcoal.\
What I finally, somehow managed to hand over was a batch of wildly misshapen chocolate that, at the very least, probably tasted fine.

"Interesting shape."\
"Shut up, it's about the taste, not the looks!"\
"Fufu... delicious. Tastes like you, Yotsuka."\
"Wh— what does 'tastes like me' even mean?! Stop saying weird stuff like that!"

Makina's chocolate had been beautiful and genuinely delicious, so I felt a little defeated by comparison.\
Well — I figured, if I'm the only one who gets to eat this, that's fine by me.

The days slowly grew longer again, and as the wind turned warm, the "something" between us kept quietly, steadily changing too.\
Take, for instance, an unremarkable afternoon off, like this one—

◇◆◇◆◇

"Hey, Yotsuka, can you grab the AC remote for me?"

Makina asks, sprawled lazily across the sofa, glancing my way.

"Ah, sure, sure... wait, it's right there next to you."\
"Too sleepy to move..."\
"Lazy!"

Fine, fine, hard work being a princess, I guess.\
I lean over toward the far end of the sofa, near Makina's head, to grab the remote.

"Whoa!"

Because Makina's face is right there.

Her eyes, half-lidded and hazy.\
Her long black hair, usually so neatly kept, mussed slightly from being pressed against the sofa cushion.\
The soft, warm feeling of her body, caught between my knees.

"...Um, Makina?"\
"What?"\
"This is a pretty compromising position, don't you think...?"\
"Now that you mention it."

"Should probably get off, huh~..."\
"Yeah, probably..."

And yet, I didn't get off.

"Yotsuka, you're making a really weird face right now."\

"Rude!"

A soft laugh slipped out between us.\
That laughter slowly settled.

Like it was a signal, understood between us without words.\

Only her eyes, watching me steadily, gave the answer.

As I got closer, Makina's eyes slowly began to fall shut.

Lightly, at first.\
Like testing the water.

Changing the angle, over and over again.

"Yotsuka..."

I was losing track of how to stop.

From her cheek down to the nape of her neck.\

From her shoulder, down to her back.

Seriously, isn't this way too convenient?

This time, much more slowly.

If a convenient little stretch of time like this could just go on forever, that'd be fine by me too.
