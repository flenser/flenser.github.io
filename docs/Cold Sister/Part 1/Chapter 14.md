## Chapter 14 – A Sweet Tooth?

\
The teacher's hoarse voice and the sound of chalk tracing across the blackboard echoed through the otherwise silent classroom. Mechanically, I copied the classical Japanese text into my notebook.

In the corner of my vision, beyond the notebook filling my field of view, the fresh green leaves of the cherry trees swayed and shimmered in the refreshing May breeze.

Yet the thoughts slipping into my mind between the scenery and my schoolwork were all about Ren. I suspected one reason was that I hadn't had a chance to talk to Tomoka-chan about her before morning homeroom. After all, Ren's recent behaviour was extraordinarily difficult to discuss with anyone. Climbing onto my lap, asking me to feed her... Compared with before, she'd suddenly become much more physically affectionate. And yet her cold attitude and curt way of speaking hadn't changed at all. That inconsistency was driving me to distraction.

Of course, it wasn't that I disliked it. Being able to eat together, watch television together, and simply share the same space as we used to made me feel both bashful and happy. Being thanked was so wonderful that I could hardly stand it.

At the same time, though, whenever I remembered how quickly my heart had beaten when I touched Ren's skin, or some of her inexplicable words and actions, questions inevitably arose. And so, I spent the entire time thinking about her.

I was just like a girl in love from a novel.

I scoffed at myself for the comparison that had popped into my head. In love with my little sister, of all things.

While I was thinking about that, the bell rang. I hurriedly caught up with the notes I'd fallen behind on, then stood and bowed along with the class at the class representative's instruction.

When lunchtime arrived, the atmosphere in the classroom relaxed in an instant. Taking advantage of the change, Tomoka-chan came over to my desk.

“I'm exhausted. Classical Japanese is seriously so sleepy, isn't it?”\
“The teacher has such a gentle voice.”\
“Exactly! I reckon they'd conquer the world if they released a sleep-inducing ASMR recording.”\
“What on earth?”

We chatted lightly as we got our lunches ready. Though, in our case, that simply meant taking sweet buns out of our bags and wiping our hands.

Tomoka-chan wasn't particularly interested in food. I wasn't especially particular about my own meals either. Shortly after Ren started high school, I'd once tried making her a packed lunch, but she'd firmly refused it. Ever since then, I'd been eating convenience-store sweet buns and savoury bread for lunch.

Before I knew it, I was thinking about Ren again. Or perhaps it was more accurate to say that she'd always occupied my thoughts, and her recent behaviour had simply made me aware of just how much. After all, one of the things that had brought Tomoka-chan and me closer in the first place was talking about Ren.

As I was thinking about this, the classroom door suddenly rattled open with an unusually loud clatter. The atmosphere in the room stirred with a wave of murmuring.

“Isn't that one of the basketball club girls?”\
“She's so tall and cool-looking!”

I instinctively looked towards the door. And there, standing just as I'd been imagining her over and over, was Ren.

“Oh, it's your little sister!”

Tomoka-chan exclaimed, too. Ren was quietly looking around the classroom.

I stood up and hurried over to her. I could feel the eyes of everyone around us gathering on me.

“Ren, what's wrong?”

When she recognised me, I thought her tense expression softened a little.

“I want to eat with you.”

She said it as though it were the most ordinary thing in the world.

Just those few words made my heart ripple. It was probably because of her injury, but I was happy that Ren had come to me as she used to, wanting to spend time together.

“Okay. Just a moment.”

I turned on my heel and hurried back to Tomoka-chan.

“Um, Ren says she wants to have lunch together.”\
“Oh! That's a huge step forward. I'm happy for you! Go on, then.”\
“Huh? What about you, Tomoka-chan?”\
“Come on, don't be silly. I'd have to be pretty insensitive to intrude on some rare quality time between sisters. Don't mind me. Take your time and enjoy yourselves.”\
“Okay.”\
“Oh, and if you're eating outside, I recommend the rooftop. Around this time of year, the first-years are starting to get bored of it, so it's about when the place begins to empty out.”

Tomoka-chan winked as she said this. The gesture came naturally to her, yet there was something comical about it. I smiled and pressed my palms together.

“Thank you. I'll be off, then.”\
“Have fun!”

Tomoka-chan waved her hand. Then, with only her face peeking around the classroom door, she gave Ren a little signal, having been watching us. Ren responded with a slight bow.

With the attention of the entire murmuring classroom fixed on me, I fell into step beside Ren and walked away, leaving those curious gazes behind us as we headed for the rooftop.

◇◇◇

The blue sky stretched overhead, meeting the grey of the concrete. It seemed the two of us would have the place entirely to ourselves. Just as Tomoka-chan had said, there wasn't anyone else on the rooftop.

It was already the latter half of May, and perhaps the increasingly strong sunlight was one reason the place had emptied out. We settled down in a conveniently shaded spot near the entrance.

“Feed me again today.”

It was no longer a question, but an instruction. Her cool expression and flat tone might easily have come across as arrogant, but somehow I found even that manner endearing. I suppose the genetic programming of being an older sister was a terrible thing. When it came to my little sister, I wanted to grant any request she made, no matter what it was.

“Of course.”

I smiled and nodded. Ren had already begun moving before she'd even heard my answer. She took a bag of sweet buns she'd brought with her and pulled out a packet of four doughnuts, the sort you often see in supermarkets. She also took out a carton of strawberry milk from the vending machines on the school grounds and placed it beside her feet.

Her unevenly arranged skirt, the doughnuts and the carton lay scattered across the concrete.

“Here.”

As though it were perfectly natural, Ren handed me the doughnuts, still in their packaging, and prompted me to feed her.

For a moment, I wondered whether she couldn't eat them herself with her uninjured left hand. But I was so happy to be spending time with her that I deliberately looked away from that question.

I peeled open the packaging and took out an orange-coloured doughnut. It was coated in sugar and had a glossy sheen. It looked awfully sweet, I thought, as I held it out to Ren.

She leaned towards me and took a bite. It was a surprisingly small mouthful for someone with such a large frame. Her body was tilted towards me, leaning against my side as she chewed.

“Is it good?”

Ren nodded, her mouth still moving.

“You really like sweet things, don't you?”

At that, she shook her head. Apparently embarrassed at being thought of as someone with a sweet tooth, she denied it with all her might. But the doughnut took a long time to disappear from her mouth, and she couldn't get any words out.

It was true that swallowing such a dense, doughy thing was going to take quite some time. While she was occupied with it, I took out my own yakisoba bun and bit into it. Ren shot me a reproachful look.

“Pay attention to me.”

With that, like a capricious cat, she bumped her shoulder against mine. The impact made me stagger slightly, whereupon Ren put an arm around my shoulders. My heart leapt reflexively.

“Thanks.”

I said it with a hot face, before realising that Ren herself had caused me to stumble.

“It's fine.”

Ren nodded coolly, still holding my shoulder. I couldn't imagine a better example of shamelessness. Today, Ren was somehow so carefree and wilful. It was as though her usual self had been a lie. She was even more so than when she was little.

And it wasn't just today. Ren had been acting strangely lately. The cold tone of her voice and her unsociable attitude remained unchanged, but her words, actions and sense of personal distance kept shifting from one moment to the next. Sometimes too close, and sometimes even closer.

And yet, I found myself closing my eyes to whatever was causing this change, content simply to accept things as they were. Because when Ren was close to me, I didn't feel lonely.

I tried to twist my body around to pick up the doughnut again. Ren was so close that our skirts were overlapping, making it difficult to move freely.

“Um, it's hard to move like this.”\
“No. And I don't want the doughnut. I want the strawberry milk.”

She rejected my request immediately. For some reason, she apparently had no intention of letting me move away from her. And now she wanted the strawberry milk instead of the doughnut.

“All right, all right.”

I put a straw into the carton and held it out to Ren. Resting her head on my shoulder, she drew on the straw with a little slurp.

I laughed at the surreal sight. I was cramming all sorts of things into the word *surreal*: how close we were, how inexplicable it all was. It felt as though I were using laughter to cover up those things, pretending not to notice even the reality right in front of me.

On the rooftop, with only the two of us for company, the wind blew through, but neither of our skirts fluttered.
