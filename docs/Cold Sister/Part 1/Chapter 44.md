## Chapter 44 – Thank You

\
My room somehow felt rather spacious.

Recently, Ren had been spending practically all her time in here, not just when we went to bed. I wasn't used to being alone any more. I couldn't concentrate on my schoolwork, and whenever I tried reading a novel, the words simply slid past my eyes. Apart from doing the housework, I'd almost forgotten how to spend time by myself.

And all the while, I kept finding myself distracted by something I rarely paid much attention to: my phone.

It was lying on my desk, its screen still dark, without a single notification sound. I picked it up almost absent-mindedly and opened the app, but, just as before, there were no messages.

*She'll probably be practising until late. And even if she's already back at the accommodation, she'll be with her friends, so she probably doesn't have much time to get in touch.*

I tried to convince myself of that.

The evening sun pierced my lowered cheek through a gap in the curtains. Slowly, I got to my feet and left my room to prepare dinner.

While I was cooking, Mum came home. Apparently, she'd finished work early because it was Saturday. So, for the first time in a while, the two of us cooked together and sat down to eat at the same table.

The teriyaki chicken, rice bowls and miso soup laid out before us were all for two people.

I was happy to have dinner with Mum, but at the same time, Ren's absence felt all the more keenly.

As though trying to blow away something creeping into my heart, I called out *Itadakimasu!* in a loud voice. Mum's voice joined mine. Then I took a big bite of the teriyaki chicken. The beautifully browned meat, coated in its sweet and savoury sauce, was delicious.

*Ren likes this flavour too, doesn't she?*

It was only then that I realised I'd unconsciously adjusted the seasoning to suit her tastes.

Being apart from her had made me realise all over again just how important Ren was to me. Even more so than when she'd distanced herself from me during her rebellious phase.

Of course, Ren had always been my precious little sister. But now, there seemed to be something more added to that.

As though her presence had gained weight, sinking deeper and deeper into me. As though I were falling into some profound depth, sinking and sinking, diving further down.

I wondered what might lie at the bottom.

As I sat there, gazing down absent-mindedly, Mum's voice suddenly reached me.

“Are you lonely without Ren?”\
“...Yes.”\
“I thought so.”

Mum wore an innocent, mischievous smile, rather like a little girl playing a prank. That smile really did resemble Ren's, a smile I hadn't seen in quite some time.

“How did you know?”\
“Something I'm about to tell you has to stay between us, all right? Don't tell Ren.”

Mum raised an index finger to her lips. Taken aback by this sudden secret, I nodded.

With a gentle smile, Mum quietly began:

“When Ren was going through her rebellious phase and being all prickly towards you, Ai, she used to make much the same expression.”

My eyes widened at the little secret she'd offered me.

Mum continued, gazing into the distance.

“She was at a sensitive age, so I deliberately didn't say anything. But I could tell straight away that Ren was lonely inside. She was always looking at you when she thought nobody would notice, and I'd often catch her lost in thought, too. From where I stood, even though she was cold towards you in what she said, she was still just the same little girl who adored her big sister.”\
“I see...”

That really was something Ren must never hear. I felt a little guilty about hearing such things behind her back, but I was also happy, and a little embarrassed.

“That's why I'm so happy to see you two getting along again lately. Ren's expression might still be rather stiff, but she's completely different from how she was during her rebellious phase. She's enjoying herself every day.”

Mum spoke with an expression of genuine happiness. Then, still smiling, she said something that seemed to wrap itself around my loneliness.

“Ai, thank you for everything. For always cherishing Ren. For staying by her side all this time, even more than I could. You're such a kind girl, and you always work so hard. So, when you're feeling lonely, it's all right to let someone take care of you, too.”

Mum's hand gently touched my head. Her warm, broad palm stroked my hair.

“Thank you.”

I murmured the words like a little child.

What filled my chest was a mixture of relief, happiness, embarrassment, and guilt. There was another kind of guilt, too, separate from the secret I'd shared with Mum, yet somehow connected to it.

Pretending not to notice the identity of the darker feelings lurking beneath all those soft, floating emotions, I embraced the warmth touching me.

Before long, Mum withdrew her hand. Perhaps she'd felt embarrassed after speaking so earnestly for so long. As though trying to hide it, she returned to our usual conversation.

She told me about work, I told her about school, and time passed pleasantly and peacefully amid our lively chatter.

After dinner, I returned to my room. In the darkness, the screen of my phone was glowing.

I hurriedly switched on the light and rushed over to my desk.

Then, when I saw what was displayed on the screen, I couldn't help but doubt my eyes.
