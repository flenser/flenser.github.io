## Chapter 41 – Senpai

\
Rain poured down, beating against the classroom windows. The rainy season had arrived a little late, but now seemed intent on making up for lost time, growing ever heavier.

Amid the silence heightened by countless little sounds, the chalk tracing the blackboard, the teacher's hoarse voice, I gazed resentfully at the world beyond the window.

With rain like this, we wouldn't be able to use our usual spot on the rooftop, which meant I probably wouldn't be able to eat lunch with Ren.

Just thinking about it made my spirits sink.

To think I could get so miserable over missing out on just one day.

I hurriedly took hold of my feelings and tried to rein them in. It was obvious, really. I'd see Ren every day at home, and we even slept together at night. Surely missing out on that tiny bit of time during lunch wasn't such a big deal.

I managed to pull myself out of my gloom, only to sink back into it again. I repeated the process several times. By the time I'd finally decided to blame all of it on the rain, the bell rang.

As I sat absent-mindedly at my desk, Tomoka-chan called out to me.

“Good work! Lessons before lunch are sleepy as ever, aren't they? Though, I suppose lessons after lunch are sleepy too.”\
“That's true. Tomoka-chan, aren't you eating lunch with Shimamoto-san today?”\
“Oh, right. We usually eat in the courtyard, but with this weather, I sent her a message saying we'd give it a miss today. Are you eating in the classroom too, Ai?”\
“I think so. With this weather, Ren will probably eat in her own classroom.”\
“Then let's have lunch together, just the two of us, for a change!”\
“Okay.”

After that exchange, Tomoka-chan took the seat in front of mine.

There were various groups in our class, and people moved around to sit together with their friends during lunch. Quite a few students also ate in the cafeteria, so there were plenty of empty seats.

The person sitting beside me and the one diagonally in front of me had both gone to eat somewhere else.

It was a welcome return to normality.

As we chatted and began getting our lunches ready, the classroom door slid open with a rattle.

And then came an unexpected return to normality.

Ren was standing at the front of the classroom, looking our way. Just as usual, a stir went through the room.

“Oh? Isn't that your little sister?”\
“Yeah. I'll just go and see what she wants.”

I hurried over to Ren.

“Ren, what's up?”\
“I thought I'd come and have lunch with you.”\
“With all this rain, I thought you wouldn't come today.”\
“Well, I thought Big Sis might be lonely without me... Was that not the case?”

Ren lowered her eyes and asked, looking a little awkward.

Seeing her like that, I found my honest feelings slipping out.

“No, I was lonely. I'm happy you came. Shall we eat together, then? Tomoka-chan is here today too. Is that all right?”\
“That's fine. I wanted to say hello to her anyway.”

Once I'd made sure Ren was happy with that, I returned to my seat. Ren followed behind me, scattering her natural beauty everywhere she went. I felt as though the commotion in the classroom had grown even louder.

Tomoka-chan greeted me with a smile as I returned, bringing Ren along with me.

“Ren wants to eat lunch with us. Is that okay?”\
“Of course! Come on, sit down, sit down!”

At her invitation, Ren sat down beside me.

Perhaps because she was naturally shy around people, she looked a little uncomfortable. Tomoka-chan spoke to her gently.

“Come to think of it, is this the first time we've actually talked? I'm Takatsuki Tomoka, Ai's friend. Nice to meet you!”\
“It's nice to meet you too, Senpai Takatsuki. Thank you for always taking such good care of *my* Big Sis.”

Tomoka-chan gave an awkward smile at the strangely emphasised word.

I felt strangely restless watching Tomoka-chan and Ren talking to each other. It was somehow fascinating to see the two of them exchanging words.

There was something else making me feel unsettled, though, for an entirely different reason. Before I knew it, the thought had slipped out of my mouth.

“Being called Senpai is nice...”\
“What do you mean?”

Tomoka-chan tilted her head at my sudden remark. Ren stared at me expressionlessly.

“Well, I just think it's nice and feels so fresh, the way Ren calls you Senpai. She's only ever called me Big Sis. Hey, Ren. Why don't you try calling me Senpai too?”\
“No.”

She rejected the idea immediately.

“Please?”

I don't usually act this clingy, but perhaps I was feeling a little strange today. Thinking that, I looked up at her pleadingly.

Ren swallowed hard and let her gaze wander. Even so, she shook her head.

“I can't. Because you're my Big Sis.”\
“I know, but...”\
“Seriously, you two are so lovey-dovey...”

Tomoka-chan muttered under her breath.

“That's not true!”\
“We're not!”

We hurriedly denied it, both speaking at exactly the same time, as though we'd rehearsed it.

“Ha ha ha. You're perfectly in sync, too. Just what you'd expect from sisters.”

Tomoka-chan smiled as she said that.

Both Ren and I turned bright red and were utterly defeated.

Just then, the classroom door opened once more.

The moment Tomoka-chan saw the unexpected visitor, her previously relaxed demeanour changed completely. Her eyes widened, she stood up, and hurried straight towards the doorway.

The person who had appeared was Shimamoto-san.
