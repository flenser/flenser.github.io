## Chapter 28 - A Date!

\
My consciousness was hazy, my vision blurred. I blinked several times, and Ren's face appeared right in front of me.

“Ren…”

I smiled sleepily. Ren looked away, perhaps embarrassed. Her movements were brisk and alert, making it clear she'd been awake for some time. She usually woke up before me. I suppose that's only to be expected of someone who has morning basketball practice every day.

Come to think of it, didn't she have practice this morning…? I wondered, before looking at the clock. The date displayed there reminded me that it was Sunday, the day Ren and I were going on our date.

“Sorry I slept in.”\
“It's fine.”

After that brief exchange, we both got out of bed.

“I'm going to get ready.”

With that, Ren hurried out of the room. I watched her go, then began getting ready myself.

Once I'd finished my morning preparations, I finally opened the wardrobe and took out the pure white dress. I let out a deep breath, then steeled myself and slipped my arms through its smooth, silky fabric. Unlike the cool-toned clothes I usually wore, I was now enveloped in pure white. I checked myself in the mirror, making sure my hair and everything else looked all right.

I wasn't confident it suited me, but at least nothing looked out of place.

Taking my bag, I steeled myself once more and left my room.

And there, in the hallway, I unexpectedly came face to face with Ren.

We both froze at the unexpected encounter. Ren's gaze travelled slowly down me, from head to toe. My heart felt as though it might explode under that gaze. I was so worried about whether the dress suited me that I wanted to run away right then and there. But Ren stood frozen like a stone, saying nothing.

The silence seemed endless. My heart thudded wildly, until Ren's quiet murmur suddenly brushed against my ears.

“Cute.”

She said it so softly that I could barely hear her, one hand covering her mouth. That gesture told me she wasn't simply offering a polite compliment, and I breathed a sigh of relief. Then, gradually, joy welled up from deep within me, surging through my whole body.

“Thank you. Your clothes are cute too, Ren. And stylish. They suit you so well.”

Ren was wearing a houndstooth blouse and a black skirt. The simple monochrome outfit accentuated her lovely figure and well-proportioned features. Her subtle make-up also brought out the best in each of her features. There was nothing I could fault. Honestly, seeing Ren dressed up to go out for the first time in ages, she was so cute and beautiful that I felt a little intimidated. And yet, simply receiving a compliment from her was enough to brighten my mood.

“Don't praise me so much. It's embarrassing.”

Ren turned her face away as she said it. That was cute too. The contrast with her otherwise mature style made my heart ache with affection. Now that the day had finally arrived, I realised just how excited I was to be going out with Ren again after such a long time.

“Well then, shall we go?”

With my heart still beating excitedly, I started down the hallway.

As we passed through the kitchen into the living room, Mum was cleaning.

“Oh? Why are you two all dressed up today?”

She looked at us in surprise as she spoke.

“I'm going on a date with Ren today!”

Caught up in the pure excitement of the moment, the words slipped out before I could stop them.

“Oh, how unusual. Have a lovely time, and take care!”

Mum smiled as she said it.
