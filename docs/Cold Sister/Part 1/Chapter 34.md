## Chapter 34: A Question

\
Monday's classroom was filled with a peculiar atmosphere, a mixture of the excitement left over from the weekend and the lethargy of facing the week ahead. I was firmly in the former camp, still riding the high of my wonderful weekend.

As I cheerfully sorted out my textbooks, Tomoka-chan came over to my desk.

"Morning."\
"Morning. You look a little sleepy."\
"Yeah. There was so much going on that I couldn't sleep."\
"So much going on?"\
"Well, I'll tell you about it eventually."

Tomoka-chan gazed off into the distance. Dark circles had settled beneath her eyes. It was unusual for someone as normally carefree as her. If something was troubling her, I wanted to offer a sympathetic ear.

"If there's anything I can do, I'm happy to listen."\
"...Thanks. But it's fine. This is more of a personal problem, or rather, a whole jumble of things concerning my favourite person. Anyway, I'm fine for now. More importantly, how was your outing with your little sister? Did you enjoy it?"\
"I did! Thanks to you picking out my clothes. Thank you so much."\
"You're welcome. It's an honour to have been of assistance to the adorable Ai."

Tomoka-chan said this with a playful smile. Seeing her acting just as she always did, I quietly breathed a sigh of relief.

"What did you get up to over the weekend, Tomoka-chan?"\
"Oh, well, I was with Shimamoto-san."\
"Really? Where did you two go?"\
"...We just went for a little walk around, I suppose."\
"I see! You two have become friends without me even noticing."\
"Well, friends... Sort of. There were a few things."

I smiled at her somewhat embarrassed answer. Although Tomoka-chan was sociable, she tended to keep a certain distance from everyone except me. So I was rather happy that she'd found someone she could spend her weekends with.

And the fact that it was Shimamoto-san, a friend and teammate of Ren's from her club, made me think about how small the world could be. Though, of course, Shimamoto-san was in love with Ren. But that was another matter. For now, I simply wanted to be happy that Tomoka-chan had made a new friend.

As I was thinking about this, an idea suddenly occurred to me.

"Tomoka-chan, are you free this weekend?"\
"I am. Why?"\
"Actually, Ren asked me to come and watch one of her club matches. Would you like to come too? Shimamoto-san will probably be there."\
"Maybe I will."

Tomoka-chan seemed to hesitate for a moment at my invitation, but eventually nodded.

"Yay! I've never been to one of Ren's club matches before, so I was a little worried, but I'll feel much better if you're there, Tomoka-chan."\
"...Ai, you've become really close to your little sister lately, haven't you? Between that date the other day and now going to cheer her on at a match."\
"Huh? Oh, y-yes, I suppose so. It seems she's mostly got over her rebellious phase."

For some reason, the word *date* coming from Tomoka-chan's mouth made me stumble over my words. It wasn't as though I'd done anything wrong.

Besides, there was something a little strange about Tomoka-chan's manner, too. Her tone was rather strained, almost as though she were pressing me for an answer. Was she still troubled by something?

Then another question came from her, cutting straight across my thoughts.

"This might sound ridiculous, but, Ai, do you have feelings for your little sister...?"

Before she could finish asking, the bell rang. Tomoka-chan seemed to come back to her senses at the sound and shook her head.

"Sorry! Forget I said anything! Just ignore that."

She said this with her usual cheerful smile, then returned to her seat.

For some reason, I felt relieved that she hadn't finished her question.

After that, Tomoka-chan remained her usual bright and kind self, and in the end, she never brought that question up again.
