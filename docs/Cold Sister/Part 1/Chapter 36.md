## Chapter 36: Do Your Best

\
In the morning, I saw Ren off as she left the house ahead of me. Dressed in her completely black tracksuit for club activities, she looked every bit the sporty girl, giving her a slightly different impression from usual. But among all that black, she was wearing the white scrunchie we'd bought together so carefully on her wrist. The sight made me indescribably happy.

"Have a good day. Do your best!"\
"Mm. See you."

Ren replied in her usual calm, matter-of-fact manner. She seemed about to step out through the front door. But just before she did, she turned on her heel and walked back towards me.

Ren's body enveloped me in a tight embrace. In that brief moment, she whispered into my ear.

"I'll do my best, so watch me."

At those words, the warmth of her cheek against mine the previous night seemed to return.

◇◇◇

"Wow, it's huge! So this is where Ren plays her matches."\
"Ah, over there. That's your little sister, and Shimamoto-san too."

We were in the spectator stands above the main floor of the municipal gymnasium, looking out over the courts spread before us. As soon as we opened the door directly opposite Court A, where Ren's team would be playing, Tomoka-chan spotted Ren and Shimamoto-san.

The players were warming up before the match, taking turns shooting towards the basket.

Ren received a pass from Shimamoto-san and took her shot. Her movements were fluid, and the ball left her fingertips in a beautiful arc before slipping cleanly through the hoop. Even to an amateur like me, it was obvious from the elegance of her technique that Ren was good.

"That's Kawai-san, the one who's been selected for the representative team, isn't it?"\
"She's so cool."

I heard voices like these from students at other schools sitting nearby, all wearing their team uniforms. I'd known Ren was impressive, but I hadn't realised she was talked about enough to attract that sort of attention.

As her older sister, I felt a quiet pride welling up inside me.

Then, just after finishing her shot, Ren happened to look up towards us. She found me almost instantly, her gaze moving with astonishing speed until our eyes met.

Excited cheers rose from the people around us.

Ren paid them no attention. Instead, she lightly tapped the wrist where I was wearing the black scrunchie, as though pointing it out. She couldn't wear hers while playing, of course, so there was nothing on her own wrist. Even so, that little gesture made me feel as though it were a secret connection between just the two of us. It made me happy.

I clasped the scrunchie around my own wrist and silently mouthed an encouragement.

*Do your best.*

Perhaps my message reached her, because Ren gave a small nod before returning to her team.

"Did I just make eye contact with Kawai-san?"\
"Wasn't Kawai-san looking over here a lot just now?"

The people around us were getting excited.

"Your little sister is really popular, isn't she?"\
"Yes, she is."

I exchanged those words with Tomoka-chan.

Before long, a whistle sounded, and the players from both teams gathered at their benches. They took off the tracksuits they'd been wearing over their uniforms. Ren's basketball kit showed off her excellent figure to its fullest. She looked cool and beautiful. The number seven on her black uniform, outlined in white, seemed to shine.

Another whistle sounded. Both teams lined up and entered the court. They shook hands, formed a huddle, and finally the match was about to begin.

The jump ball was just like the ones I'd seen in physical education lessons. Players from each team gathered in the centre of the court. It seemed that Shimamoto-san, wearing number eight for Ren's team, would be taking the jump.

The referee tossed the ball into the air, and the game began. Shimamoto-san reached it first, and Ren collected the ball.

A series of passes followed, beginning with Ren. The ball went through Shimamoto-san, who was positioned beneath the basket, before returning to Ren. She dribbled past an opponent and leapt into the air.

Ren's movements were beautiful, just as I'd expected. Her shot slipped perfectly through the net. Three points went to her team.

From then on, the match moved at a dizzying pace. Basketball involved such rapid changes between attack and defence that I could barely follow the action with my eyes. To be honest, I didn't really understand the difference between scoring two points and three. But there was one thing even an amateur like me could tell: Ren was the centre of her team. I repeatedly saw Shimamoto-san collect the ball beneath the basket before Ren took the shot. And it wasn't just Shimamoto-san. Pass after pass was directed towards Ren, and she sent practically every one of them sinking through the hoop.

*Watch only me.*

Ren's words from the previous evening came back to me.

Even without those words, my eyes were fixed on her. Every player giving their all on the court shone in their own way, but Ren stood out more brightly than anyone else. Even someone like me, who knew nothing about basketball, could sense it instinctively.

"She's amazing..."

The words slipped out before I realised it.

"She really is."

Tomoka-chan nodded in agreement.

I didn't understand the rules of basketball. I simply kept watching Ren. She moved across the court with such energy, as though her usual awkwardness had never existed. She ran, bounced the ball, and leapt, scattering beauty with every movement.

To think that I lived under the same roof as such an incredible girl, that she sought affection from me and slept beside me. Even as her older sister, I could hardly believe it.

Ren's performance stirred something deep within me. More than the physical distance between the court and the stands, Ren seemed far away.

On the court, she was an athlete in her own right.

And that made me happy.

It made me proud.

And it made me lonely.

With something like a prayer in my heart, I watched the match. I watched Ren.

The score remained close even as the buzzer sounded several times, interrupting the match.

"These are the final ten minutes."

Tomoka-chan explained.

After that, both teams continued scoring at an even pace, seesawing back and forth. The scoreboard read 84–85. Five seconds remained. Ren's team was behind, and this would be their final play.

The ball was passed to Ren.

Cheers erupted around the gymnasium. The excitement of the entire venue focused on her alone.

Touching the scrunchie on my wrist, I whispered:

"Do your best."

Ren leapt, her movements impossibly beautiful.

The buzzer rang out with a deafening sound. My own heart pounded in time with it. Then, cutting through those sounds, the ball she had released traced a beautiful arc through the air and quietly stirred the net.

In that instant, Ren stole everything from my sight, and even from my heart.
