## Chapter 9 – Seeking Advice?

\
“Did you manage to buy the new book by Souji-sensei?”

“I did, and I’ve already read it three times just yesterday... It’s a series about a love triangle involving two siblings and their friend, and things take a dramatic turn at a breathtaking pace... Wait, never mind my BL rambling! Ai, what are you going to do about that thing we talked about?”

Tomoka-chan whispered, her voice blending discreetly into the classroom chatter before homeroom.

“Yeah. About that... I think I’m going to turn her down.”

I lowered my voice to match hers.

“I thought so.”

I’d expected her to be surprised, so her reaction actually caught me off guard.

“Well, yesterday I got so excited on my own because someone had recognised how wonderful you are. But you didn’t seem all that interested, did you? So afterward, I regretted getting carried away a little. I was so caught up in my excitement that I’d completely ignored how you felt. I’m sorry.”\
“No, it made me happy. You were celebrating as if it were your own good news, and it showed me how much you care about me. So it’s okay. And I’m sorry too, after you were so happy for me. I still don’t really understand what it means to go out with someone.”\
“No, really. That’s completely fine. What matters most is how you feel. If anything, I’m the one who should apologise for making you feel like you had to be considerate of me.”

Tomoka-chan said this and drooped her shoulders.

Usually, her cheerful personality seemed to veil this side of her, but I liked the sincerity she carried deep down when it came to other people.

“Anyway, how about you let me make it up to you by asking for your advice?”\
“If it’s something I can help with, absolutely! So, aside from deciding what to do, what else is bothering you?”\
“Well... If she really does confess to me, I’m still going to turn her down. But I’m happy that she has feelings for me. I don’t want to hurt her any more than I have to. What should I do?”

Tomoka-chan folded her arms and groaned as she considered my question. Then, she cautiously began to speak.

“I think all you can do is be honest about how you feel, without trying to dodge the issue. Just as only the person receiving a confession can decide whether to accept it, only the person making the confession can decide how to take the answer. So even if the result is hurt or pain, I think being honest is several times better for them than trying to gloss things over.”

Tomoka-chan spoke while looking straight into my eyes. Her words reached my heart directly, without the slightest resistance.

“I understand. I’ll try to tell her honestly how I feel.”\
“Yeah. Good luck.”

Tomoka-chan smiled and nodded at my answer.

Thanks to her advice, it felt as though a fog had lifted. But with my vision now clear, I ran into another problem.

Once I noticed it, I inwardly clutched my head. After hesitating for a while, I cautiously broached the subject with Tomoka-chan.

“There’s one more thing I wanted to ask you about...”\
“What is it?”\
“Well, yesterday, Ren was acting kind of strange.”\
“Your little sister? In what way?”

Tomoka-chan leaned toward me, her expression a mixture of curiosity and concern.

“Her words and attitude were cold, more or less as usual, but... she seemed strangely affectionate toward me.”

I couldn't bring myself to mention that Ren had climbed onto my lap. For her dignity's sake—and for various other reasons—I had to be a little vague.

Tomoka-chan rested a hand on her chin and thought for a moment.

“Did you tell her about being confessed to?”\
“Not directly. She heard about it through Mum. Why?”

I tilted my head. Tomoka-chan clutched hers.

“Your little sister is just too devoted...”

I was about to ask what she meant by that, but the bell rang, cutting her off, and the teacher appeared in the classroom.

“Crap!”

With that muttered exclamation, Tomoka-chan gave me a quick wave and hurried back to her seat.

And so, with my vision clearer but a lingering sense that something had been left unresolved, the day began.
