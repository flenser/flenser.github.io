## Chapter 37: More

\
It had been a while since the three of us had sat down together for a meal. Curry was laid out on the table, and Mum asked with a cheerful smile:

"Ren, how was your match today?"\
"We won."

Her answer was so matter-of-fact that I hurriedly added:

"Ren was amazing! She just kept scoring, and she even scored the winning basket right at the end!"

I was so carried away by the excitement of the moment that my vocabulary failed me. Mum nodded, her eyes crinkling.

"I see. I'm hopeless when it comes to sports, so I'm proud as can be. Still, it's unusual for Ai to go and watch one of Ren's matches."

Mum tilted her head. Just as I was trying to think of some suitable explanation...

"I wanted her to watch me."

Ren stated this plainly, then went back to eating her curry as though nothing had happened.

For some reason, my cheeks grew hot.

"You two have been awfully close lately, haven't you? Whatever happened to Ren's rebellious phase?"\
"I was never going through a rebellious phase."\
"...Well, you were always stuck to your big sister when you were little, so perhaps you've just gone back to how things used to be."

Mum smiled, looking half exasperated but genuinely pleased.

For some reason, her words filled me with guilt.

◇◇◇

"How was the match today?"\
"It was amazing... You were so cool."\
"Really?"\
"Really."\
"...Then give me a reward?"

As evening slowly drew its curtain over the day, Ren came to my room as though it were the most natural thing in the world. Sitting beside me on the bed, she made that request with her usual expressionless face.

"A reward?"\
"Mm. I worked hard for Big Sis today."

Ren said it casually, her voice utterly devoid of inflection. My heartbeat, however, was anything but calm.

"Then..."

Hesitantly, I reached out and stroked Ren's head.

"You're such a good girl, Ren. You were really cool today."

I spoke in the soothing tone I'd used when she was little. Even so, Ren seemed quite content, leaning her body against me and closing her eyes. The gesture was so childlike, quite at odds with her sophisticated appearance, that it was hard to imagine the same girl I'd watched playing so brilliantly in today's match.

"I worked hard. Because I wanted Big Sis to look only at me."

Such sweet words were impossible to reconcile with the Ren I knew. Desperately maintaining my composure as her older sister, I continued stroking her head.

"You were so amazing that I couldn't see anyone but you. I could hardly believe you were really my little sister."

Yes, so amazing that it was almost unbelievable. Ren was shining so brightly that it was hard to believe she would seek affection from someone like me.

I wished that, from now on, I could be the only one who ever saw this side of Ren. The thought of her seeking affection from someone other than me sent a crack through my heart.

Wanting to monopolise Ren, to close the distance I'd felt between us from the stands today, I struggled against the thought. I hurriedly tried to drive away the image of myself as such a selfish person.

I wanted to protect Ren. I didn't want to stand in the way of her efforts towards her future. I wanted to be somewhere she could rest and feel at ease, not a shackle that held her captive.

As I stroked her head, the soft, slightly damp texture of her hair, fresh from her bath, brushed against my palm. I wrestled with my thoughts, hoping that whatever was stirring inside me would follow the right course.

Then...

"More reward."

Ren looked straight at me and said this.

"More?"\
"Mm. I worked hard today. More."

With that, Ren spread both arms wide.

As though gently picking up the one choice that was, in practice, all I had left, I drew Ren into my arms.

Her warm body pressed against mine.

"You're such a good girl, Ren."

The words I managed to squeeze out trembled. Ren was reaching for me, our body heat meeting. Somehow, I felt as though I was the one receiving a reward.

The everyday life in which Ren was by my side, once something I had taken for granted, had fallen apart, only to be joined together again like this.

It should have been nothing more than a return to how things used to be. And yet, so many things had changed. Ren, who had once been so small, was now big enough to envelop me. Her innocent smile was hidden beneath a veil of ice. The girl who used to cry all the time now shone so brightly that she drew everyone's attention.

"I'm happy."

Her voice was as flat as ever. But those simple, sincere words were just as they had been when she was little.

That's right. No matter how sophisticated her appearance had become, no matter how much she'd accomplished, Ren was still Ren. My one and only, adorable little sister.

And so I had to stop this heartbeat. I had to return to the person I'd once been, the girl whose heart had been filled with nothing but pure, innocent affection.

"Hey, Big Sis."

And yet Ren's honeyed whisper wouldn't let me.

"What would happen if I asked for even more?"
