## Chapter 21 - Happiness?

\
Today was a day when Mum would be coming home early, so Tomoka-chan and I were spending time together in the clubroom.

The calls of the sports clubs drifting over from the playing fields and gymnasium, the sounds of instruments from the wind ensemble, and, tucked quietly between them, the faint rustling of turning pages. Amid these familiar, repeated sounds, everyday life continued to unfold at its own leisurely pace.

However, something about Tomoka-chan was a little different today. Usually, when she read her BL novels, she'd smile happily, squirm with excitement, or let out little sounds that weren't quite words. But today, she was turning the pages mechanically, wearing an unusually troubled expression. Wondering whether she'd finally started reading ordinary fiction, I peered at the cover. It showed two men with similar faces, naked and facing one another, just like her usual books. I hurriedly looked away.

“What's wrong?”

Tomoka-chan asked, noticing me fidgeting by myself.

“Nothing. I just thought you seemed a little different from usual while reading.”\
“Do I?”\
“You normally look happier, I suppose. You smile more.”\
“What!? Is it really that obvious!?”

I nodded, unable to suppress a wry smile at her exaggerated reaction.

“Seriously? I'll have to be careful.”

Saying that, Tomoka-chan put a hand to her face and tried to arrange her expression into something more composed. She was quite pretty herself, but there was something comical about the gesture. It reminded me once again just how remarkable Ren was, managing to keep her expression as immovable as a statue.

As I recalled that the title I'd glimpsed on the cover had looked familiar, I asked:

“Is that the new book by Souji-sensei you mentioned before?”\
“Yeah. There's something that's been bothering me, so I'm reading it again... Oh, and just to protect Sensei's reputation, it's not that I don't find it interesting!”

I laughed at how desperately Tomoka-chan was defending her favourite author.

Despite that little episode, club activities came to an uneventful end.

We left school when the dismissal bell rang. It was unusual for us to stay this late, and I gazed at the sunset spreading out before us with a feeling that was somehow both unfamiliar and nostalgic. Was the sky this colour when Ren and I used to walk home together after after-school care? I wondered.

Exchanging idle conversation with Tomoka-chan as she walked beside me, another ordinary day quietly became part of our lives. I'd thought those days would go on forever, ever since we were in primary school. But each one had ended, one after another, and brought us to where we were now. And today, too, we were one step closer to the future as we arrived at the T-junction where Tomoka-chan and I went our separate ways.

“See you tomorrow.”\
“See you tomorrow.”

We said goodbye, and I continued walking. A little further on, I reached home and opened the sliding door with a familiar hand.

“I'm home.”

Just as Mum had drummed into me when I was little, I always greeted the house whether anyone was there or not.

“Welcome home!”

Hearing a reply always made me happy. I thought there was something wonderful about having a voice welcome you home after parting ways with your friends.

Drawn along by the faint smell of cooking drifting into the hallway, I made my way towards the kitchen.

When I reached it, I called out to the figure standing with her back to me at the cooker.

“Are we having curry tonight?”\
“Correct. How did you know?”\
“I could smell it.”\
“That's what comes of all the cooking you do. Dinner will be ready soon, so could you help me by getting the plates ready?”\
“Of course!”

I went to the bathroom to wash my hands, put my bag in my room, and changed out of my uniform into something comfortable. Or rather, I put on an old T-shirt of Ren's. I felt a little embarrassed wearing my younger sister's hand-me-downs as her big sister, but it seemed a waste to throw it away, so I'd adopted it as loungewear.

After that, I returned to the kitchen and helped prepare dinner by taking out the plates and carrying the dishes to the table.

As we were doing so, the sliding door rattled, and Ren appeared in the living room.

“I'm home.”\
“Welcome back. Dinner's nearly ready, so go and get changed.”

Ren nodded and disappeared into her room. I watched her go with a peaceful feeling. Sharing a meal as the three of us always filled me with joy. Lately, Ren had been eating with us, so I hadn't felt lonely, but precisely because the time we spent together as a family was precious, I wanted to treasure it.

As I continued preparing dinner, everything was eventually laid out on the table, and Mum and I took our seats. Before long, Ren appeared in her loungewear and sat down. Not in the place that had been set for her, but beside me. It seemed that, somewhere along the way, this had become her usual seat. Ren casually moved her tableware into position, while Mum watched her with a broad smile.

“Let's eat.”

One of us said it, and dinner began. I scooped up some curry and ate. It was the same curry as always, full of chicken and vegetables.

“Is it good?”\
“It's delicious. Mum's curry really is the most comforting.”

At my words, Mum's face softened into a smile. Beside me, Ren was nodding repeatedly as well.

It was such a warm moment. Then, as though gently rolling a pebble into the middle of it, Mum asked:

“By the way, what happened with that confession you told me about the other day, Ai?”

Perhaps she enjoyed chatting with her daughter about romance, because Mum was smiling broadly. Beside me, Ren was drinking water with an impassive expression. Then, as though it had gone down the wrong way, she began coughing violently.

“Are you all right!?”

I had a sense of déjà vu, as though this had happened before. I rubbed Ren's back and answered Mum.

“I turned her down, but at least I managed to avoid hurting her.”

There were so many complicated circumstances involved, and the person whose back I was rubbing right now was the very person at the centre of them. Naturally, my answer came out sounding rather cryptic. As expected, Mum looked puzzled. Then she gave me a reassuring smile and said:

“I see. There are some parts I don't quite understand, but if that's what you chose because it was true to your own feelings, Ai, then I think that's all right.”\
“Thank you.”

I smiled. Mum smiled back. Then she let out a small breath and continued.

“There was something I've been meaning to tell you and Ren once you were both a little older. Since the timing seems right, I'll tell you now.”

Her tone was gentle, but the sudden seriousness of her words made me straighten my back. Ren, too, looked at Mum intently. I quietly withdrew my hand from her back.

“You know, I don't regret marrying your father at all. Because, thanks to that marriage, I got to meet my two adorable daughters, the most precious treasures in the world to me. So, although we ended up separating because of various misunderstandings and things not quite falling into place, I don't hate your father. I'm grateful to him.”

Until now, we'd somehow always avoided bringing up our father. Mum deliberately touched upon the subject and looked firmly from one of us to the other.

“So, Ai, Ren. Don't be afraid of falling in love, or of finding happiness. Spend your lives making yourselves happy. That's something I've always wanted to tell you both, someday.”

Mum smiled, but there was a serious conviction in her eyes. She had devoted most of her own life to us, always living for our happiness.

“Anyway, I know this is rather sudden. The curry's getting cold, so go on, eat up!”

Watching Mum deliver those words so brightly and gently, I found myself thinking:

I want to become strong and kind, just like her.
