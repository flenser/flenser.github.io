## Chapter 29 - Lovers

\
The café was naturally quite busy on a Sunday, but we still managed to find a table for two without much trouble. Fortunately, it was a shop specialising in sweets, so there were fewer people coming in for lunch than at the food court or the other restaurants.

We already knew what we wanted, so after a quick glance at the menu, I called over a member of staff.

A beautiful female server came over.

“May I take your order?”\
“Um, could we have one of these ‘Couples-Only Tropical Parfait’, please?”

I pointed to the item on the menu, my heart pounding with anxiety that she might find something suspicious about our order.

“Certainly. One Couples-Only Tropical Parfait. Would you like anything else?”\
“No, that's all.”\
“Very well. I'll be right back.”

The server took our order with a smile throughout, then left. I breathed a sigh of relief.

*Couples-only.*

The words were still reverberating around inside my head. My cheeks felt unbearably hot, so I took a sip of water. Then Ren spoke, as though delivering a finishing blow.

“Today, we're a couple.”\
“Eh?”\
“We ordered a couples-only parfait, so today we're lovers.”\
“That's true. It would be a problem if people found out we're actually sisters.”

That's how I interpreted Ren's words. But she frowned slightly, looking dissatisfied, then placed her hand over mine, which was resting on the table.

“R-Ren!?”

I couldn't help raising my voice. Ren paid no attention, instead threading her fingers through mine. Her long fingers slipped between my own, and her broad palm completely enveloped my hand.

“If we're lovers, this much is normal.”

Ren said it without a hint of hesitation. She squeezed my hand tightly, leaving me nowhere to escape.

I was so embarrassed that my face burned and I couldn't stop sweating. I was painfully conscious of the people around us. For some reason, I felt as though everyone was looking at us.

I wondered how we appeared to them. Sisters? Friends? Lovers? Even if they saw us as a couple, that would be embarrassing. But if they didn't, that would be a problem in its own way.

“Um, this is a little embarrassing…”\
“But we're lovers.”

Ren was utterly immovable on that point.

“Or do you not want to be lovers with me?”\
“It's not that I don't…”

Had I ever managed to refuse one of Ren's questions? I'd always known I was a big sister who spoiled her little sister, but lately, as the distance between us had grown smaller, that tendency seemed to be becoming more pronounced. Honestly, what sort of request would I actually turn down? It felt as though I might agree to absolutely anything, without limit. And the thought of just how far that could go frightened me.

“I'm glad.”

Ren murmured without changing her expression. Yet her grip tightened, as though expressing her delight. That peculiar way she had of showing her feelings stirred my big-sister instincts, washing away all my regret and hesitation. And so, I thought, I'd probably end up spoiling her again next time, too.

While I struggled with my embarrassment and emotions, the same server as before arrived carrying an enormous parfait.

“Thank you for waiting. One Couples-Only Tropical Parfait.”

The parfait was placed between us. It was topped with colourful fruit and whipped cream, with layer upon layer of ingredients beneath, and came with two spoons. Thankfully, there were no heart-shaped drinking straws or anything of that sort. Apart from its generous size, it wasn't much different from an ordinary parfait.

“Well then, shall we eat?”\
“Mm.”

We said *itadakimasu* together and picked up our spoons. Carefully, so as not to disturb its shape, we scooped up some parfait. Just as I was about to bring my spoon to my mouth, another spoon interrupted me.

“Here.”\
“Eh?”\
“Open your mouth.”\
“We're out in public!”\
“But we're lovers.”

Ren was sticking to that one point and wouldn't budge. I finally gave in, opened my mouth, and accepted the spoonful she offered. Sweetness touched my tongue, while heat rushed to my cheeks.

Someone was definitely watching us. I could feel their eyes. Of course they were. Feeding someone with an *aah* in public was surely going too far.

I was nowhere near able to appreciate the taste of the parfait when another trial presented itself.

“Me too.”

Ren leaned towards me as she spoke. Following her prompt, I offered her a spoonful of parfait. Her beautiful face came close, and she whisked the sweet treat away. Looking carefully, I noticed her cheeks were faintly flushed. So Ren wasn't entirely unembarrassed either.

She was willing to put herself through all this embarrassment, even pretending to be lovers, just to eat this parfait. I had to admire her devotion to sweet things.

And so, in response to Ren's determination, we continued playing at being lovers.

◇◇◇

Feeling both full and exhausted, we walked through the shopping centre.

I turned to Ren, who was beside me.

“What shall we do now?”\
“I want to spend a little more time with you.”\
“...It's still early afternoon. Since we're here, shall we go and have some fun?”

That was close. My heart had given an involuntary leap at Ren's choice of words. She looked particularly beautiful today, so honestly, she needed to stop saying things like that. Perhaps because I was so excited myself, I was overreacting to her usual affectionate behaviour. I had to pull myself together. I was the big sister, after all.

Just as I made that resolution, a familiar warmth enveloped my hand. The same sensation as before. Ren's fingers slipped between mine.

Walking along hand in hand like that, there was absolutely no way to disguise it. We were holding hands like lovers.

“Ren…?”\
“Because today we're lovers.”

*But wasn't that just an act for the couples-only parfait…?*

I thought so, but if I pointed that out, she'd probably ask me again whether I didn't want to. So I quietly nodded instead.

Besides, I didn't actually dislike holding hands. Quite the opposite: I was happy about it. So there wasn't really a problem. Although I had a feeling that if I started examining why it made me so happy, that might become a problem. But, carried away by my excitement, I ignored my better judgement.

“That's right. Since it's our date, let's enjoy ourselves.”

And so, connected by each other's warmth, we walked through the shopping centre.
