## Chapter 26 - The White Flag of Love - POV Shimamoto

\
I think I began to find the evening sky beautiful around the same time of year that I met Ren.

Ever since Ren appeared before me, the world had gained a higher resolution. My eyes had opened wide, unwilling to miss even a fleeting moment, and everything before me had come into sharper focus. The weight of everything I received from the world had grown. And, of course, that included not only beauty, but pain and sorrow as well.

After club activities, we walked for a few minutes from the main school gate to the crossroads. I burned the sight of Ren beside me into my memory. Despite having just got through a gruelling practice session, Ren seemed unusually cheerful today. Her steps were light, and she was even humming to herself.

For some reason, the sight gave me a bad feeling. My heart began preparing itself for a small-scale despair. I laughed bitterly at the twisted state of my own mind. Since meeting Ren, the world had become more beautiful, but I felt that I myself was growing uglier by the day.

And, even though I knew I was better off not asking, I spoke to her.

“You seem happy.”\
“Not particularly.”

When I asked, Ren stopped humming and answered in her usual composed manner. But her eyes, shining with the reflection of the sunset, were still coloured by joy. Their beauty pierced my heart.

The days I'd spent watching Ren had made me sensitive to even the smallest changes in her. That very sensitivity robbed me of any place to hide.

“Is that so?”\
“Oh, but… a friend of mine is apparently going on a date with someone they're interested in on Sunday. If I had to pick one thing, I suppose that's what I'm happiest about.”

Ren smiled as she said it.

That unguarded smile was proof that she felt at ease with me. It was also proof that I didn't even stand a chance of entering the competition.

Drawn in, then pierced. I went round and round through that cycle of joy and pain.

“I see. That's lovely for them. By the way, where are they going on their date?”\
“The shopping centre nearby! There's a parfait on the couples-only menu that they want to try, so apparently they used that as an excuse to invite the other person.”

Ren was more talkative than usual. She had no idea that every single word was a blade to me.

“But there's one thing they're worried about. What should they wear? What do you wear when you want to make an effort for a date? They don't usually go out much apart from club activities, and it's been a really long time since they've gone anywhere with that person. Oh, I mean, apparently it's been a long time.”

I had no desire to exploit that little slip in her words.

Instead, as a last act of resistance, I considered recommending something completely lacking in romantic appeal. In my mind, I began dressing Ren up like a paper doll.

For example, a pair of jeans that played down her femininity, with a casual shirt that didn't look remotely like something you'd wear on a date.

…No, that wouldn't work. Trousers would emphasise Ren's long legs, and a casual shirt would suit her boyish hairstyle so well that she'd end up looking even more handsome. Put her beside a short, girlish older sister, and they'd look even more like a couple.

Then, the opposite approach: a short dress, completely unlike Ren's usual image.

…No, that wouldn't work either. The contrast between her cool expression and the feminine dress would be so adorable that I could tell just by imagining it.

Ren's looks were simply too powerful. I couldn't compete. No matter what I dressed her in, she'd look good.

I raised the white flag in my heart and answered.

“I think whatever outfit that person chooses will probably be the right one. If they wear something they want to wear, I'm sure the other person will be happy too.”\
“Do you think so? But if Rira says that, I suppose you're right! Thank you!”

Seeing her smile, I thought: *I love her.*

A smile from the person you love is magic. It makes you recklessly believe that this alone is all your world needs.

And such blind happiness is always a catalyst for obsession.

I'd raised the white flag when it came to Ren's charms, but this love… I still wasn't ready to give up on it.

After parting from Ren at our usual crossroads, I took my phone from my uniform pocket.

Then I sent a message to the girl who was in the same predicament as me.

“Are you free on Sunday?”
