## Chapter 6 – Out of Control?

\
"Just as I thought, Mum's nikujaga is delicious!"\
"I'm glad you like it. What about you, Ren? Is it good?"\
"Ah, yeah. It's delicious."

The small dining table was a little cramped with three people's worth of food spread across it: nikujaga, miso soup, rice and salad. Nevertheless, I found something endearing about that cosiness.

Food always tasted several times better when shared with someone else. Ren normally wouldn't eat meals with us, but whenever Mum was home, she'd join us at the table. That alone made me incredibly happy.

Ren herself, however, seemed to have her mind somewhere else.

She was as quiet as usual, but her replies lacked their usual strength, and food kept slipping from between her chopsticks and falling back onto her plate. Seeing this, Mum finally asked her:

"Hey, Ren, are you all right?"\
"I-I'm fine."

No sooner had she answered than her chopsticks slipped from her fingers and rolled across the table.

I wondered what on earth was wrong. This was so unlike Ren, who was normally so graceful and composed in her movements and mannerisms.

Unable to contain my concern, I asked her too.

"Are you okay? Are you feeling unwell or something?"\
"It's none of your business, Big Sis."

She answered with her usual coldness.

She was still as distant towards me as ever. And yet, the fact that she'd called me *Big Sis* for the first time in ages made me ridiculously happy, even in response to such a cold remark.

The imbalance between the intensity of our feelings was almost enough to make me cry.

Perhaps I'm a little too indulgent towards Ren. No, actually, quite a lot too indulgent.

"Ren, you should be a little more careful about how you speak to people."

Mum gently admonished her.

"I'm sorry."

Ren obediently apologised.

"Well, Ren has always loved Ai so much. It's just her way of hiding her embarrassment, isn't it?"\
"T-That's not true!"

Ren suddenly raised her voice, louder than I'd heard her speak in a very long time.

*THUD!*

A dull sound rang out, and the dining table jolted.

It seemed she'd knocked her knee against it. Ren's face had turned bright red, and she wore an expression of considerable pain.

"A-Are you okay?"\
"I'm fine. Just be quiet, Big Sis."

As soon as I asked, Ren quickly regained her composure and replied in her usual firm, dignified manner.

Mum watched the two of us with an amused smile. Then she murmured thoughtfully:

"Come to think of it, Ai has grown up enough to be receiving love confessions now. When I think about it, you're already old enough to get married if you wanted to. Perhaps I should start preparing myself to let my daughters go..."

*Cough! Cough!*

Ren suddenly began choking rather spectacularly.

Even Mum was taken aback by the sight and hurriedly asked:

"Hey, Ren! Are you really all right?! You were coughing so violently I thought you were going to bring up blood!"\
"I'm fine. It's nothing."

With those words, Ren quickly devoured the rest of her meal at an astonishing speed.

"I'm finished. I'm going back to my room."

And with that, she left the dining table.

Ren, who was normally as calm as a still lake, had just departed like a raging storm.

"Honestly, what's gotten into Ren?"\
"I have no idea..."

Mum and I exchanged glances and tilted our heads in confusion.
