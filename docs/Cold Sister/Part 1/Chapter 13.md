## Chapter 13 – Red?

\
Having finished the smaller household chores, I returned to the kitchen and began reheating the food in the microwave, one dish at a time. As each item was ready, I removed its cling film and arranged it on the dining table, far too spacious for one person sitting alone.

Once everything was prepared, I put my hands together and quietly said, “Itadakimasu.”

My faint voice echoed through the room.

The pork and tomato stew had turned out just as it usually did, and I was relieved that I hadn't made a mistake. I made a conscious effort to chew thoroughly as I ate, when Ren suddenly poked her head into the room.

“Are you having dinner too, Ren?”

I swallowed before asking.

“Yeah. There's something I wanted to ask you, Big Sis.”

Ren murmured, turning her face away. Her cheeks, still flushed from her bath, were illuminated by the overhead light.

“What is it?”\
“I hurt my wrist during club activities. So... I want you to feed me.”

She said this, timidly raising her right hand. Sure enough, her wrist was wrapped in a bandage.

“Oh, you really are hurt. Are you all right?”

I instinctively stood up and reached out to take her hand.

“I-I'm fine!”\
“S-Sorry.”

Ren recoiled sharply. Apparently, I'd closed the distance between us too suddenly. After what had happened yesterday, I'd thought perhaps her rebellious phase was beginning to ease a little, but it seemed the wall between us was still quite high.

And yet, her injury must have been bad enough for her to ask me to feed her. I was terribly worried.

“So, are you going to feed me or not?”

Ren asked in an even voice.

“Of course. You sit down, Ren. I'll get everything ready.”

I encouraged her to take a seat at the table. She did as I asked, sitting in the chair beside mine. I'd assumed she would sit opposite me, but if I was going to feed her, this arrangement certainly made more sense.

I reheated each dish in turn, removed the cling film and set everything out in front of Ren. She obediently waited for me to finish.

As I served the rice and poured barley tea into a glass, I couldn't help glancing nervously at the place setting appearing beside my own. It had been such a long time since Ren and I had eaten together. I felt a little ashamed of myself for being happy about this unexpected stroke of good fortune, brought about by her injury.

While I quietly turned those conflicting feelings over in my mind, everything was finally ready. I took my seat with an air of formality. I was a little nervous about performing such an unfamiliar task.

“Please.”

Ren said this, giving me a slight bow.

I nodded and picked up my chopsticks.

“What would you like to eat first?”\
“The meat.”

Sitting right beside me, Ren looked straight at me. The contrast between her mature, composed gaze and what I was about to do made me feel rather self-conscious.

Putting that awkwardness aside, I picked up a piece of pork as requested, held my left hand beneath it to catch any drips, and brought it to Ren's mouth.

She accepted it without difficulty and began chewing.

“Is it good?”\
“Yeah.”

Ren nodded honestly, her mouth still full.

I rarely received any comments about my cooking, so even that simple answer made me happy. I took turns offering her rice and salad, watching her as she ate.

The scene might have looked like something between lovers, but what filled my heart was the tender affection of a mother bird feeding her chick. Whatever the outward appearance, I was simply happy that Ren was eating the food I'd made, and that we were sharing this time together.

“You're staring too much.”

Ren gently admonished me.

“Sorry.”

I hurriedly looked away and began eating my own meal to fill the silence. But the warmth in my heart was reflected in my expression, and I couldn't stop the corners of my mouth from turning upwards.

“Why are you smiling?”

She asked in a displeased voice.

I answered honestly.

“Because I'm happy you're eating the food I've made.”\
“What's that supposed to mean?”

Ren looked bewildered. Then she lowered her head, apparently lost in thought. Finally, as though she'd made up her mind, she opened her mouth.

“Big Sis.”\
“Hmm?”

A moment of silence passed. Then Ren's words gently stirred my eardrums.

“Thank you for always making our meals. And... I'm sorry for leaving all the cooking and housework to you.”

She was sitting right beside me, her frame larger than mine, her features as perfectly composed as a model's. And yet, the atmosphere surrounding Ren hadn't changed at all from when she was little. Her eyes, like those of a small child, gazed at me imploringly.

“Don't worry about that at all. You're working hard at club activities, getting up early every morning. But I'm really happy you said thank you. And thank you, too, for telling me so honestly how you feel.”

With that, I gently stroked Ren's head.

Embarrassed, she turned her face away. At that moment, the same shade of red as the tomatoes caught my eye, making me feel strangely ticklish inside.

I'd grown so accustomed to Ren's rebellious phase that I hadn't yet learned how to deal with the pure affection I was now receiving so freely, or the feelings it stirred up in me.

Apparently, Ren was in much the same position.

“I wish you'd hurry up and feed me.”

She pouted as she said this.

“Here you go.”

Unable to suppress a grin at her adorable attempt to hide her embarrassment, I was met with Ren's silent, displeased pressure. Shrugging my shoulders, I brought another piece of pork to her mouth.

The atmosphere remained soft and unsettled, and we continued eating in silence. Trying to find something to talk about, I found myself looking at the wrist she'd injured during club activities. That reminded me of something I wanted to ask.

“By the way, Ren, do you know someone called Shimamoto Rira?”

Ren looked puzzled by my question.

“She was my teammate in middle school. We're friends. Why?”

I sensed a certain pressure in her response. Thinking that explaining everything that had happened today might complicate matters, I decided to gloss over it.

“Oh, nothing. I ran into her in the corridor the other day and had a little chat with her because I thought she was someone who spent time with you. That's all.”\
“Hmm.”

Ren muttered, her voice sounding just a little displeased at my rather unconvincing answer.
