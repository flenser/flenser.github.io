## Chapter 23 - Payback

\
What roused me from my sleep wasn't the sound of my alarm, but the presence of someone nearby.

I slowly opened my eyes. Through my blurred vision, I saw Ren's eyes directly in front of me. The moment our gazes met, she hurriedly looked away.

As though chasing after her, I murmured:

“Rennn...”\
“B-Big Sis!?”

Just as I always did, I wrapped my arms tightly around Ren's body. For some reason, she was startled and went completely stiff.

“Rennn...”\
“Big Sis...”

Ren sounded bewildered. Paying no attention to her confusion, I hugged her tightly, both seeking her affection and wanting to pamper her. Our body heat mingled as we pressed against one another.

I loved this time of day. Those soft, hazy mornings, when Ren and I would snuggle together in a sleepy daze.

Her smooth, soft skin was pleasant to the touch, and her body was warm. I could happily stay like this forever.

Come to think of it, Ren felt particularly warm today. More than warm, actually. She was positively hot. Her face was inexplicably bright red, and she had her eyes squeezed shut as though embarrassed to be held by me. Her behaviour was strange, too. Normally, she'd happily hug me back, and the two of us would playfully wrestle together. That was our usual routine. But today she was unusually quiet.

And even the way she felt in my arms was different. Normally, she'd fit much more snugly against my body...

It was around that point, as I considered this, that the fog slowly lifted from my mind and the truth became clear.

Ren was sleeping with me because she'd come to my room yesterday. And we weren't children anymore. We were high school students.

My consciousness snapped fully awake.

“W-Wah! Ren, I'm sorry! I was still half asleep!”

I hurriedly pulled away and sat up.

Ren sat up as well. She stared at me, bewildered by my sudden return to my senses, then murmured:

“Big Sis, you're not a morning person, after all.”\
“I'm really, really sorry!”

I apologised profusely to the expressionless face before me. Her usual poker face seemed frightening. Her face was higher than mine, and its extraordinary beauty only made her more intimidating. The thought that I'd been hugging someone this beautiful as though she were a little child made me want to bury my head in my hands.

Of course she hadn't enjoyed being treated like that, had she?

I nervously watched Ren's reaction.

She was looking down, apparently deep in thought. Her expression was serious, and I braced myself for whatever angry words she might throw at me.

After a little while, Ren seemed to make up her mind.

“It's fine. In exchange...”

As though filling the silence between those words, Ren leaned towards me.

In an instant, our bodies became entangled, and we fell back onto the bed together. Ren wrapped her arms around me, enveloping me with her larger frame. She took away all my freedom of movement. I could only surrender myself to her, desperately enduring her soft, gentle violence.

She brought her mouth close to my ear, one of the parts of me now under her control, and whispered:

“Payback.”

Those two innocent little syllables made my head feel as though it were boiling.

It should have been just like the playful tussles we'd shared as children. And yet, no matter how hard I searched through my memories, I couldn't find anything that had ever made me feel like this.

I desperately tried to process the heat and weight of Ren pressing against me.

It was the beginning of a morning that was very different from our usual ones, and decidedly hard on my heart.
