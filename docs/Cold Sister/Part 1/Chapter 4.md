## Chapter 4 – A Confession?

\
On our way from the shoe lockers to the school gate, Tomoka-chan rattled on at a rapid pace.

"Listen, Ai! Being summoned behind the school building is practically always a confession. The whole 'I thought it was going to be a confession, but it turned out they wanted something completely different!' kind of scenario only happens in fiction."\
"Is that how it works...? But judging by the handwriting, I think it might be from a girl."\
"These days, it doesn't matter whether it's a boy or a girl. Besides, honestly, I can totally see girls falling for you, Ai. You're kind and considerate, but you're also tiny and a little airheaded, with that sort of charm that makes people want to protect you. I can see how you'd bring out a girl's maternal instincts."\
"I'm not entirely sure whether that's a compliment..."\
"It is, it is! And personally, I'd actually feel safer if it were a girl. You're just so kind, Ai. I can easily imagine some absolute scumbag of a guy taking advantage of you. You seem like the sort of person who'd end up showering some gambling-addicted, chain-smoking band guy with money. At least I can't imagine a girl doing something like that to you."\
"Somehow, I still feel like you're insulting me a little..."

I asked with an exasperated voice, thoroughly bewildered by Tomoka-chan's unrestrained commentary.

"Ah, sorry. It's just that this is the first time I've ever heard anything like this about you, Ai, so I'm getting a little carried away."\
"Honestly... You never tell me anything about your own love life, either."\
"Sorry about that. But seriously, I'm so happy! It's like someone has finally recognised how wonderful you are, or the world has finally discovered just how cute you are. All these feelings are rushing over me like an avalanche. I mean, I practically consider myself the world's second-oldest hardcore Ai fan!"\
"Second? Then who's number one?"\
"...That's exactly what makes you so adorably oblivious! You're absolutely worth supporting!"\
"What on earth are you talking about?"

I laughed as I said that. Her words were rather frivolous, but Tomoka-chan genuinely seemed happy for me. I watched her with an expression that said, 'Well, I suppose I can let her have this,' and continued walking.

Before long, we reached the school gate.

Normally, Tomoka-chan and I headed in the same direction when going home, so we'd walk together for part of the journey. But today was the day she was going to Animate. Since we'd have to take a train from the nearest station in the opposite direction from our usual route home, this was where we had to part ways.

"You're going to Animate today, right, Tomoka-chan?"\
"Well, honestly, I'd love nothing more than to hear more about you, Ai, but I absolutely have to get my hands on Souji-sensei's new book on release day! So, as for that little matter we were discussing, let's hold a strategy meeting tomorrow morning!"

Tomoka-chan grabbed my hand firmly and declared this with great enthusiasm. Faced with her overwhelming intensity, I could only smile wryly and nod.

"Okay. I'd be happy if you could help me figure things out tomorrow."\
"Leave it to me! I hate talking about my own romantic experiences, but I absolutely love hearing about other people's!"

Leaving me with that somewhat terrible declaration, Tomoka-chan waved goodbye and walked away.

"Don't forget our promise!"

She made sure to remind me one last time.

I watched her retreating figure for a moment before setting off along my usual route home.

One step, then another.

The scenery, which had once been rice fields, now retained only faint traces of its former appearance. The narrow paths between the fields where Ren and I used to run around had been replaced by paved concrete, and houses now lined the streets. It was a landscape that was half countryside, half residential neighbourhood.

I watched the skirts of school uniforms sway as cyclists overtook me, pedalling while standing up. I followed the movement of their schoolbags hanging from their shoulders, and the little straps dangling from them.

In the silence that had settled after Tomoka-chan left, the reality of the situation, along with my questions about it, gradually grew stronger.

*I'd received a love letter...*

*Someone might confess their feelings to me tomorrow.*

Somehow, it all seemed like something far beyond anything I could have imagined.
