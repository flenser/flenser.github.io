## Chapter 2 – Delusions?

\
*Sigh...*\
"What's wrong? Why such a big sigh? Is it your little sister again?"\
"That's right. She wouldn't even talk to me this morning. What should I do?"\
"Sounds like she's going through puberty. There's probably nothing you can do about it."\
"But she's been like this ever since middle school. Isn't that an awfully long rebellious phase?"\
"Maybe since you barely had a rebellious phase yourself, she's making up for it?"\
"Ehh? I did have a rebellious phase, you know! Back in middle school, I used to think things like doing housework was such a pain."\
"You're really too much of a good girl... There, there. Well done."

As she said that, Tomoka-chan gently patted my head.

Tomoka-chan was a fellow member of the literature club, whom I'd known since middle school. She was cheerful and energetic, and for someone like me, who was quiet and had hardly any friends, she was practically my one and only best friend.

And talking to her about Ren had become something of a daily routine before homeroom.

"Thanks. But I'm not really that great."\
"Ai, you really are. I mean, you looked after your little sister all throughout elementary school, didn't you? And even after she suddenly started treating you coldly in middle school, all you ever do is worry about her. You never get angry with her or anything."\
"Well, it's not like I went out of my way to look after her. Ren was just so cute, always clinging to me wherever I went. I couldn't help loving it."\
"Your love really does live up to your name, doesn't it?"\
"Though it doesn't seem to be getting me anywhere..."\
"Rather than your love going to waste, I'd say the one who's really missing the mark is... Well, personally, I think the pairing of a gentle, easygoing older sister and her cool, talented, beautiful younger sister who acts all prickly towards her is absolutely wonderful! And just imagine if it were a younger brother who's usually all prickly but actually has a secret submissive side, while his gentle older brother seems like a timid, passive type, only to reveal his true nature in bed... Ahh! My BL fantasies are running wild!"

Tomoka-chan suddenly launched into her speech at lightning speed, attracting the attention of everyone around us. I hurriedly tried to calm her down.

"Okay, I get it! Just keep your voice down..."

The only flaw in Tomoka-chan's otherwise cheerful and kind personality was that she was an extreme fujoshi.

Not only did she have a wonderful personality, but she was also quite beautiful, so she frequently received love confessions. However, apparently, she'd turned every single one of them down, saying she had no interest in anything other than romance between men.

That's what I'd heard through the grapevine, anyway. She rarely talked about herself, so I didn't know the details firsthand.

I often heard classmates remark that it was such a waste, considering how pretty she was. Those comments usually came up alongside the rumours about her.

Still, I thought there was something wonderful about how wholeheartedly she pursued her own interests.

Sometimes I couldn't quite understand what she was talking about, and occasionally she'd suddenly raise her voice, but I found those sides of her amusing and endearing too.

"Oops, sorry about that. Anyway, this is just my intuition after reading every kind of BL story involving siblings under the sun, but I think your little sister probably just has trouble being honest with herself. Deep down, she still loves you just as much as ever, Ai."\
"Thanks. I hope that's true..."

I gave a rather feeble smile at Tomoka-chan's reassuring words.

Just then, the school bell rang. She waved casually at me before returning to her own seat.
