## Chapter 25 - Cute

\
Amid the familiar noises of the classroom, I quietly replayed Ren's words from earlier in my head.

*Date.*

What did she mean by deliberately using that word? Had she just said it without thinking? Or perhaps…

No, no. She probably meant it in the same way that old married couples make an ordinary outing sound like some grand occasion. What else could it possibly mean?

My thoughts swung between extremes, growing feverish one moment and cooling the next. It was just like the distance between Ren and me. One moment she seemed so far away that I could barely see her, then the next she came so close that my entire field of vision was filled with her eyes and her warmth. Either way, I couldn't see anything else. I couldn't work out what the right distance between us was, or where to place my feelings. It was a case of not seeing what was right under my nose.

Or perhaps the light Ren gave off was so dazzling that it forced everything beneath it into shadow.

And even now, I had lost sight of where my thoughts were taking me. A lighthouse shining from too close at hand doesn't illuminate the way ahead. Its brightness only blinds you.

“Phew…”\
“What's with the sigh?”

Tomoka-chan turned round from the desk in front of mine.

“Actually, Ren and I are going out together on Sunday.”

I avoided words that might give the wrong impression, like *date* or *couples-only parfait*.

“Oh, that's great! Sounds like you're finally becoming proper close sisters. But if that's the case, why the sigh? Aren't you looking forward to it?”\
“Of course I am.”

And I was. There was no doubt about that. But I was also troubled by how ridiculously flustered I'd become over Ren using the word *date*, along with a few other rather ordinary concerns.

I picked out one of those concerns that I could talk to Tomoka-chan about.

“I don't have anything to wear.”\
“Huh? All the clothes you usually wear are perfectly cute, Ai.”\
“But when I imagine walking alongside Ren, I don't have a single thing I'd feel comfortable wearing.”

With her blessed looks and figure, Ren could wear anything and look like a fashion model. And I didn't own a single outfit that would let me walk proudly beside her as her big sister.

“Don't you think you're overthinking it?”\
“Maybe, but…”

Even so.

It had been such a long time since we'd gone out together. I didn't want Ren to be disappointed in me. As her big sister, I didn't want to embarrass her. I was shorter than Ren, and I wasn't cute like she was. So at the very least, I wanted to get my clothes right.

As I lowered my head, Tomoka-chan grabbed me firmly by the shoulders.

“Listen. You might not realise it, but you're just as cute as your little sister. So have a little more confidence in yourself… Though I don't think that low self-esteem of yours is going to change overnight. Are you free on Saturday afternoon?”

Taken aback by the sudden question, I nodded.

“Let's go find you an outfit together, something that'll let you walk beside your little sister with confidence!”
