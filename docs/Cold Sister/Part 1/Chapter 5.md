## Chapter 5 – The Right Answer?

\
It took a little over ten minutes to walk home from school. Leaving the main road lined with newly built houses, I turned into a narrow street crowded with older homes. A little further along was my house.

I slid open the well-worn front door with a rattling sound.

"I'm home!"

My voice echoed down the hallway.

"Welcome home!"

A voice answered from beyond the door. Hearing it made me happy, and I took off my shoes before walking down the hallway.

I opened the door and passed through the living room. In the kitchen, Mum was cooking while humming to herself. I greeted her again, looking at her cheerful back.

"I'm home. What are you making?"\
"Welcome home. Today I'm making nikujaga, which you two love so much. It's not every day I get a day off, so I have to make something my daughters like and remind them of their mother's authority!"

With that, Mum burst into hearty laughter.

Mum was beautiful, cute, and wonderfully strong-minded. Having raised two daughters on her own, she was straightforward and decisive in everything she said and did. She was tall and slender, almost like a fashion model, and always walked with her back straight. She worked from morning until night as the manager of the pharmacy near the station, and was highly trusted by the people in our neighbourhood.

Our family might be a little different from other families, but I loved Mum very much.

"Yay! I'm looking forward to it. Is there anything I can help with?"\
"Thank you. The cooking will be finished soon, so could you wash the dishes for me?"\
"Sure!"

I washed my hands and rinsed my mouth in the washroom next door, then put my schoolbag in my room before joining Mum in the kitchen.

"You're already helping me with the cooking most days, and now I'm asking you to help again. I'm sorry, and thank you."\
"It's okay. Besides, I like helping out. It gives me a chance to talk with you like this."\
"Ai really is such a good girl... And Ren works so hard at her club activities, too, despite being my daughter. I never imagined my daughter would be so athletic, considering how hopeless I am at sports. Having such adorable, hardworking daughters and being able to spend time with them... I'm truly a lucky mother."

Mum murmured those words with heartfelt affection.

"Oh, Mum, you're exaggerating. You work so hard at your job, too. You're amazing. Thank you for everything you do for us."\
"Honestly, I'd like to spend more time with you girls instead of just working... I'm sorry I've made you feel lonely ever since you were little."\
"I'm sorry I've made you feel lonely."

Those were Mum's habitual words. Whenever she said them, her normally confident expression would become just a little uncertain. Her usually straight posture would seem to slump.

Every time I saw that expression, I felt the same way. I didn't want Mum to feel anxious or guilty. That was why I had to be a good girl. And someday, I wanted to become an adult who could protect both Mum and Ren.

"It's okay. Thanks to how hard you work, we have everything we need and can live comfortably."

I smiled as I said that.

"Thank you."

Mum smiled as she dissolved the miso paste into a pot.

Her smile looked so much like Ren's.

Ren's smile... a smile I hadn't seen in quite some time.

As I scrubbed the dishes with a sponge, I savoured the sight of Mum's smile for a little while longer.

"So, Ai, how are things at school lately? Are you enjoying yourself?"\
"I am. Tomoka-chan is still being as friendly as ever."\
"That's good. I'm glad to hear it. Is there anything troubling you? I'm worried about Ren, since she hasn't been talking much lately, but I'm also worried about you, Ai. You're almost too good a girl."\
"There's nothing wrong! I'm always having fun!"

As I said that, I remembered what had happened at the shoe lockers earlier that day. Something unusual, something that had never happened to me before: a single letter sitting inside my schoolbag.

"Although... I suppose there is one thing that's bothering me. Um, I think I might have received a love letter..."

This was the first time I'd ever talked to Mum about something like this. As I spoke, I became embarrassed and stumbled over my words. Feeling restless, I scrubbed a bowl rather obsessively with the sponge. I couldn't bring myself to look at Mum's face.

"Really? That's wonderful! Of course someone would fall for you. You're a good girl, you're cute, and above all, you're my daughter! It would be strange if you weren't popular. So, what kind of person is it?"

When I glanced sideways, Mum was smiling broadly and peering at me.

It was an almost girlish gesture. Yet it seemed perfectly natural coming from her, because Mum really was that beautiful.

"Well, there wasn't a name written on the letter, so I don't really know. But judging by the handwriting, I think it might be from a girl rather than a boy."\\
"Oh, how lovely! I used to receive confessions from girls quite often when I was a student, too. Being popular with other girls is proof that you're a truly wonderful woman!"\\
"I see. I guess I've still got a long way to go before I can match you, Mum. But... I'm a little happy to hear that."

I smiled as I said that.

I really was happy. But at the same time, my worries were going round and round inside my head, like water swirling down a drain.

Perhaps Mum noticed how I was feeling, because she spoke to me gently.

"Well, I think it would be rather nosy of me to ask what you're going to say in response to a confession, so I'll just tell you one thing. Whether you decide to go out with that person or not, as long as it's a decision you've thought about and made for yourself, I'll consider it the right choice. Whether you end up dating a boy, dating a girl, or deciding not to date anyone at all, no matter what happens, there's one thing you can always be certain of. I'll always love you, Ai."

As she said that, Mum wrapped her arms around me.

She was careful to hold me gently, making sure that her hands, which she'd been using to prepare dinner, didn't touch my skin.

Even in something as small as the way she held me, I could feel how much she loved me. My heart filled with a sense of security.

Just being surrounded by her warmth somehow made me feel that everything would be all right.

Nothing had been resolved, and I still didn't know what to do.

But somehow, I felt that everything would be okay.

"Thank you. I love you, Mum."\
"I love you too, Ai!"

As we exchanged those words, I heard the rattling sound of the front door opening.

A moment later, I heard a set of quiet footsteps. Then Ren appeared in the kitchen doorway.

"I'm home. What are you doing?"

She asked in her usual flat, emotionless voice.

Mum released me from her embrace and happily announced:

"Welcome home! Hey, listen to this! Ai received a love letter! And it's from a girl, too!"

Her voice was full of excitement, as though she were the one who had received the confession herself.

Feeling rather embarrassed, I lowered my eyes and glanced towards Ren.

"Huh?"

*Thud.*

Ren's schoolbag slipped off her shoulder and fell to the floor.

Her face had gone completely pale.
