## Chapter 38: Teasing

\
"What would happen if I asked for even more?"

Ren whispered something close to my ear. Her breath, brushing against my earlobe, was hot and sweet. My brain felt as though it might melt under the weight of the words and the intensity that washes over me. My feverish thoughts struggled desperately to process Ren’s words.

Any more. More.

Even now, we were this close, with Ren’s body heat, her words and her smooth skin sending my heart racing wildly, and yet, ‘more’?

Even in my muddled state of mind, I found myself looking at Ren's face. At her lips.

Wait. No. Why was I looking there? Startled by my own unconscious thoughts, I hurriedly pushed them away. Then, in the end, I offered Ren a completely incoherent response.

"I-I don't know."\
"I see."

Her words carried a trace of resignation. Ren moved away from me. Although I felt a little lonely, I also breathed a sigh of relief. At least now I could escape these unbearably noisy thoughts and my racing heartbeat.

But Ren wouldn't let things remain so ambiguous. Her whisper came again.

"Then I'll stay like this until you understand, Big Sis."

With that, Ren hugged me even tighter. It was a movement almost like that of a predator. Ren’s body pressed close against mine, as if entwining itself around my petite frame. My heartbeat, which I’d let my guard down about, began to race. If this carried on, Ren would hear it. She was so close that I found myself thinking just that.

Whenever Ren touched me, all my senses seemed to become sharper. As though I couldn't bear to miss a single thing she gave me. That sensitivity seemed to grow more acute by the day.

And then I noticed something I hadn't before.

A heartbeat. One very much like my own.

Ren's heartbeat.

Her heart was beating just as rapidly as mine, perhaps even faster. Behind her composed expression, Ren's heart was in just as much turmoil as mine.

The moment I realised this, something sweet seemed to make my head go numb. Then, like a child who'd thought of a mischievous trick to play on someone they liked, I felt an urge to take advantage of Ren's vulnerability.

I couldn't stop the thought once it had entered my mind.

I leaned towards Ren and whispered, just as she had done to me.

"Then what would you like me to do, Ren?"

The moment I spoke, Ren's body gave a little start.

"...I don't... know."

Ren's normally even voice wavered. Her weak, strained reply seemed to kindle something inside me. The girl who had looked so cool during her match was now so flustered by a mere whisper from me.

The thought sent a strange shiver down my spine.

I pressed her further, one question following another.

"You asked me, even though you don't know yourself?"

Ren squirmed, apparently embarrassed. I held her close so she couldn't get away, and continued.

"Come on, Ren. What do you really want me to do?"

Ren let out a soft little sound. Her breath was sweet. Where my words had landed, the lobe of Ren’s ear was flushed a deep red. Captivated by the beauty of that colour, I gazed at it for a moment. I felt as though I might be drawn right in. Then, as I slowly lowered my gaze, I saw that her cheeks were tinged with the same beauty; every part of her body I touched was warm, and her lips were trembling. From there, the words came.

"Big Sis... you're mean."

Those childish words nearly split my mind in two. There was one part of me that wanted to continue, and another that was beginning to come to its senses.

I was her older sister. I had to protect Ren. I shouldn't tease her like this.

The two conflicting feelings fought inside me, but in the end, Ren's childishness brought the older-sister side of me back into control. Or rather, I was always Ren's big sister, no matter what. An older sister had to be kind to her little sister.

By the time I recognised that simple truth, my head had returned to normal.

"S-sorry! I think some strange switch got flipped inside me."

I hurriedly moved away from Ren. We sat side by side on the bed, leaving an appropriate distance between us.

Ren lowered her head at my apology, then murmured:

"It's not that... I didn't like it, though."\
"Huh?"

I couldn't help asking her to repeat herself. What did she mean, she hadn't disliked it...?

As though putting a lid on my questions, Ren made another selfish request.

"Well, anyway, if you hug me tightly while we're sleeping, I'll forgive you."

That small, simple request swept away all the heat and confusion from a moment earlier. A smile escaped me, and with it, the words:

"You're adorable."\
"Shut up."

Ren rolled over onto the bed, hiding her bright-red face.
