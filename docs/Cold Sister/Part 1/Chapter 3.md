## Chapter 3 – An Encounter?

\
"Souji-sensei's BL novels really are something else. They're not just erotic, they're emotional, too. The balance between those two elements is absolutely perfect. It's truly the work of a master."\
"Is that so? That's amazing."\
"Ai, would you like to read it?"\
"I'm not really comfortable with books that have quite so much skin showing..."

I replied while glancing sideways at the rather provocative cover of the novel Tomoka-chan was holding out towards me.

The only reason we could have this sort of conversation was that there was nobody else in the literature club's room besides the two of us. It was a secluded room on the second floor of the club building. Books left behind by former club members were scattered in every direction, overflowing from the bookshelves.

From a distance, we could hear the shouts of the sports clubs and the music of the brass band. From the adjoining gymnasium came the shrill squeaking of shoes against the floor. Ren was surely among those making all that noise.

And while Ren was working hard at practice in the neighbouring gymnasium, I was listening to Tomoka-chan talk about BL.

On days when Mum came home early from work, I didn't need to prepare dinner, so I could spend time doing literature club activities with Tomoka-chan like this.

That said, there were only two members in the club, so we simply read whatever books we liked, talked about our favourite novels, or got carried away chatting about completely unrelated things. It was a very relaxed club.

"Argh! My losing streak in trying to convert Ai to the wonders of BL just keeps getting longer!"\
"But you don't read the books I recommend either, do you, Tomoka-chan?"\
"That's because the novels you like don't have any male-male romance or erotic scenes... If a book doesn't have at least one of those elements, I get sleepy and can't read it."\
"So we're even, then."

At those words, we naturally looked at each other and burst out laughing.

Just then, Tomoka-chan suddenly raised her voice, looking as though she'd just remembered something terribly important.

"Ah! Today is the release date for Souji-sensei's new book!"\
"Really? That's quite something."\
"So, I'm sorry, but could we call it a day and stop by Animate on the way home? The local bookshop sells it too, but Animate has a special bonus leaflet that comes with a short story!"\
"That's fine. I want to get home early and help Mum with things anyway."\
"Thank you! I really love you, Ai!"

With that, Tomoka-chan suddenly threw her arms around me.

"If that's supposed to be a pun, it isn't funny."\
"What was that?!"

As we continued fooling around, her embrace eventually came to an end. We quickly packed up to leave. Unlike the sports clubs, we didn't need to change clothes or anything. We simply put the books we'd been reading into our bags, straightened the chairs, checked that the room was locked, and headed out.

We descended the creaking aluminium staircase and left the club building.

There was a covered walkway extending from the gymnasium, with a lone water cooler standing beside it.

We walked from the club building grounds towards the entrance to the covered walkway. There, we spotted a group of girls gathered between the gymnasium and the water cooler. They were all wearing short-sleeved shirts, shorts, and black practice uniforms. Every one of them was tall and slender.

And among them was a face I knew very well.

"Hey, isn't that your little sister?"

Tomoka-chan had noticed her too.

"Yeah."

I glanced over discreetly. It seemed Ren had noticed us as well, because our eyes met.

Just as I used to do when we were younger, I smiled brightly and gave her a little wave.

But Ren simply turned her flushed cheeks away with a huff. Perhaps it was because she'd been practising so hard, but her face was bright red. She didn't return my greeting.

Her teammates seemed to have noticed the exchange, too. They playfully tapped Ren on the head and ruffled her hair rather roughly, laughing and fooling around with her like close friends.

Ren brushed their hands away, looking thoroughly annoyed.

Among that group, however, there was one girl who was staring intently in our direction.

I wondered if she didn't know that Ren and I were sisters. When I smiled back at her, as though acknowledging her gaze, she gave me a small nod before looking away.

"Your little sister really is as unresponsive as ever, isn't she?"\
"But I'm glad she's having fun with everyone in her club."\
"Just how nice a person are you, anyway?!"

Tomoka-chan threw her arms around me again.

As I simply let her do as she pleased and accepted all that energy of hers, I happened to notice Ren staring intently at us, her eyes wide open.

I tilted my head slightly, wondering what was wrong.

But Ren immediately looked away and hurried over to the water cooler.

With a question mark floating above my head, I managed to escape from Tomoka-chan's embrace. We passed alongside the girls' basketball team and headed towards the shoe lockers.

"Still, your little sister really has grown beautiful, hasn't she? Of course, she was cute back in middle school too. When she first started there, she even had the same hairstyle as you, and she was so friendly and outgoing. Sometimes she'd come over to see you during breaks. Compared to those days, she's become such a cool, intelligent beauty now."\
"That's true. She's so beautiful that sometimes I wonder whether we really have the same blood running through our veins."\
"You're cute in your own soft, gentle way too, Ai, so don't worry! Beautiful sisters are the best! Seriously, just looking at the two of you makes my understanding of BL stories about siblings so much more vivid!"\
"You really do manage to relate everything to BL..."

While we continued chatting and joking around, we eventually arrived at the shoe lockers.

My surname was Kawai, while Tomoka-chan's was Takatsuki, so our lockers happened to be conveniently positioned next to each other.

I stretched up slightly and hooked my fingers around the handle of the topmost steel locker.

With a rusty creak, the door opened, and something fell out.

Puzzled, I picked it up.

It was a letter with a cute pattern on the stationery. As I looked at the writing, the words seemed to leap straight into my mind.

"What's that?"

Tomoka-chan asked, peering into my hand.

"Wait, isn't that a love letter?!"

She immediately shouted the words without a moment's hesitation.

"Is it?"

I tilted my head in confusion.

Written on the stationery in adorable handwriting were the words:

**'Please come to the back of the gymnasium tomorrow.'**
