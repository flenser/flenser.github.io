## Chapter 43 – Attachment

\
As the rainy season began to give way to summer, the days grew warmer. It was an otherwise unremarkable day, with the weekend just around the corner. But Ren's various actions gradually transformed it into something quite different.

Ren was acting strangely today.

For instance, when I was standing in front of the frying pan, making stir-fried vegetables for dinner, Ren came home after basketball practice and hurried straight over to me. Without even saying she was home, she wrapped her arms around me.

Then, holding me from behind, she whispered:

“Big Sis.”\
“What brought this on all of a sudden!?”\
“Nothing in particular.”

Her voice was as cold as ever. Yet, in complete contrast to that tone, Ren buried her face in the nape of my neck from behind. Almost as though she were trying to breathe me in. She took several deeper breaths than usual, and her warm breath brushed against my ear.

“Um, Ren. That's a little ticklish.”\
“Bear with it.”

She said this without the slightest hesitation, tightening her embrace.

As though screaming in my place, the vegetables in the frying pan writhed in the heat, sizzling.

Enveloped by Ren's body heat, my heart throbbed as though desperately asserting its presence. Each beat was almost painful, while the places where Ren touched me felt soft and warm. I fought down the urge to surrender myself to that sensation forever and told her:

“Dinner will be ready soon, so go and get changed.”\
“...All right.”

About ten seconds passed after her reply before Ren finally let go.

I watched her back as she left the kitchen, tilting my head at her unusual behaviour.

She was acting strangely even while we ate dinner. She had moved her chair even closer than usual, until our bodies were practically touching.

“Don't you think you're a little close...?”\
“I'm perfectly normal.”

Ren said that, but she was clearly behaving differently from usual. Throughout the meal, her body remained right beside mine. I was so conscious of the outline of her warmth against my skin that I couldn't taste the stir-fried vegetables at all.

That closeness continued even after dinner. Whether I was washing the dishes or folding the laundry, Ren stuck close behind me, never wanting to leave my side for even a moment. She stayed beside me, helping with the chores or suddenly wrapping her arms around me.

It was almost as though she'd returned to being a little girl. I remembered how the younger Ren used to toddle along behind me wherever I went.

But what enveloped me now wasn't the adorable innocence of a small child. It was the overwhelming force of her refined beauty. The contrast between her childish behaviour and the beautiful girl touching me left my head spinning.

And even now, after we'd finished our chores and I'd settled into the floor chair in the living room, Ren had naturally made herself comfortable on my lap.

I still wasn't accustomed to the sensation of her smooth skin or her body heat. My heart continued to race, and I felt as though I could hardly breathe. Happiness taken to excess felt remarkably similar to suffering. As though trying to escape that sensation, I spoke to Ren.

“You're being particularly clingy today, aren't you?”\
“Because I'm going to training camp tomorrow. I need to replenish my Big Sis supply, or I'll die.”\
“Isn't that a little exaggerated...?”\
“It's not exaggerated.”

Ren stated this firmly. Then, just as she'd done once before, she turned herself around so that she was facing me.

Her eyes captured only me. My field of vision contained only Ren.

Just like that, a world belonging to the two of us came into being so effortlessly. Ren's words sounded sweet.

“I don't want to be apart from you for even a single moment any more, Big Sis.”

Her straightforward attachment to me, expressed in her usual emotionless voice, made my head feel as though it were boiling. Her words took on almost exactly the same shape as the promise we'd made long ago, and my heart tightened with happiness.

I was so happy. Surely all I needed to do was tell her, *I feel the same way.*

That was all I wanted to convey.

But there was something else rising inside me, too.

*Then why did you leave me in the first place?*

A lingering, twisted attachment, so pathetic and distorted that it took the form of a question.

I was her big sister. I should be able to forgive her for something like that.

But once the thought had entered my mind, it began slipping through the gaps between the heartbeats overflowing with happiness, threatening to escape my lips.

Just before those words could take shape, Ren's sweet, gentle voice placed a lid over them.

“Then, once I get to the training camp, can I message you?”

She said this and buried her face against my chest, as though trying to hide her embarrassment.

The adorable way Ren behaved, and the affection overflowing inside me, made that ugly question vanish in an instant. My twisted attachment was transformed into affection, just like that. I answered, as though wrapping Ren in my tenderness:

“Of course. I'll be waiting.”

At my reply, Ren rubbed her cheek against my chest, as though expressing her happiness. She was just like a kitten seeking affection.

I smiled and stroked her head.
