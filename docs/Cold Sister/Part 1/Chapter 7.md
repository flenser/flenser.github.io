## Chapter 7 – Mine?

\
After finishing dinner, helping Mum with the washing-up, and successfully completing my school assignments, I was now sitting back in a reclining floor chair in the living room, absent-mindedly watching television.

Mum had to get up early for work again tomorrow, so she'd already gone to bed.

I was thinking that I should probably take a bath soon. Just the thought of drying my hair and going through all the trouble of moisturising my skin afterwards made me feel a little reluctant to get up.

Just then, I heard footsteps approaching the living room.

I turned to look, and before long, Ren appeared in the doorway.

It was quite unusual for Ren to come into the living room for anything other than meals. Normally, she stayed shut away in her own room.

I was a little surprised and hesitated, wondering whether I should say something to her.

But Ren, keeping her head lowered, walked straight towards me without hesitation and, with a sudden burst of momentum...

Settled herself right down on my lap.

The television instantly disappeared from view.

My body rocked, and so did my heart. Ren's sudden action had delivered quite a shock to both my mind and body.

"U-Um, Ren... What's this?"\
"We used to do this all the time."\
"Well, that's true, but..."

It was certainly true that when we were little, Ren used to sit on my lap like this while we watched anime together.

But things were different now. Our bodies, our relationship, practically everything had changed.

Back then, Ren's little body had fitted perfectly into my lap. Now, she'd grown bigger than me. I couldn't even see the television past her back, and she seemed unsure what to do with her long arms and legs.

"If you don't like it, I'll stop."\
"I don't dislike it."

I was more bewildered than anything else. It wasn't that I disliked it.

Although Ren was bigger than me now, she was slender and toned, so she wasn't particularly heavy or uncomfortable to have sitting on my lap.

"Then I'll stay like this."

With that, Ren leaned her body against mine.

Her movements were different from the smooth, graceful motions she normally made. They were stiff and awkward.

Whatever had brought this on?

Questions were flying around inside my head. My blood seemed to be rushing through my entire body.

Ren wasn't heavy, but for some reason, my heart was pounding so loudly that it was the only thing I could think about. It was making it difficult to breathe.

"Ren, what brought this on all of a sudden?"

I asked, hoping to ease that uncomfortable feeling, if only a little.

"Nothing. Nothing in particular."

Her voice was just as cold as always.

And yet, the distance between us was completely different. We were far, far too close.

"I see..."

That was about all I could manage to say between the incessant thumping of my heart.

"More importantly, your hands.\
"My hands?"\
"I don't think... they used to be like this."

Ren murmured in a rather accusatory tone.

"Sorry, I don't really understand what you mean..."\
"Never mind."

As soon as she said that, Ren took my hands, guided them underneath her arms, and clasped them together in front of her body.

"Ren...?"

I couldn't help voicing my confusion.

"This is how it used to be."

Now that she mentioned it, when we were little, I'd sit with Ren nestled between my legs, holding her in my arms.

But things were different now.

We'd both grown up, and rather than the sweet, innocent picture we'd once made, the position we were in now was much closer to an embrace or a back hug.

"If you don't like it, I'll stop."

Her words were just as cold as before, as though she were speaking in her usual detached manner.

But unlike earlier, I thought I could detect a little uncertainty in Ren's voice.

Instinctively, I wanted to reassure her.

"It's okay."

I smiled, even though she probably couldn't see my face.

Then, just as I used to when we were younger, I gently stroked her head.

Her hair, which was now much shorter than it used to be, was slightly damp, perhaps because she'd just come out of the bath. Each time I ran my fingers through it, the scent of shampoo wafted around us.

We were supposed to be using the same shampoo, but somehow, her hair smelled unusually sweet.

Perhaps she was enjoying it, because the tension gradually left Ren's previously rigid body.

It wasn't the most comfortable position, but that wasn't really a problem.

The person having difficulty was me.

Despite what I'd just told her, I was anything but okay.

The degree of physical contact between us had increased considerably. The warmth of her body and the smoothness of her skin against mine were almost overwhelming.

The light from the ceiling seemed to flicker before my eyes.

I couldn't hear a thing coming from the television.

And then, as though to give my already frantic heartbeat another push, Ren murmured:

"More..."

Her voice was utterly unlike her usual tone. It was soft, sweet, and pleading.

Hearing it made me feel as though I were doing something terribly improper.

If I thought about it rationally, we were simply two sisters enjoying some affectionate time together, sharing a reclining chair.

And yet, my heartbeat was far too fast, and Ren's behaviour was far too sweet.

Perhaps it was simply the contrast that was affecting me. Ren, who was normally as cold as ice, was behaving unusually affectionately, and the sudden change in temperature was making me feel this way.

I constructed this little explanation in my head and convinced myself it made sense.

It was just that Ren was acting differently from usual, and her behaviour was making me feel strange too.

My racing heart, my flushed face, even the unusually sweet scent...

All of it was perfectly normal.

Having reached that conclusion, I finally felt a little more at ease. And, for the first time in a while, I felt like saying something in the manner of a proper big sister.

"Goodness, Ren, you've grown so big, haven't you?"\
"Not particularly."

Ren replied curtly, just as she always did.

I continued speaking in my best big-sister voice.

"But even though you've grown so much, you're being awfully clingy today, aren't you?"

I wondered whether I'd perhaps gotten a little carried away, and glanced at Ren to see how she would react.

"I'm not being clingy. This is normal."

Ren delivered her usual cold response.

And yet, she continued leaning against me, even slightly lowering her head so that it would be easier for me to stroke her hair.

Her behaviour was simply so heartwarming and adorable that, before I knew it, the racing heartbeat that had felt like some kind of fever had disappeared.

I was simply enjoying some long-overdue time alone with my little sister.

Ren sat on my lap, and I stroked her head, which was now positioned considerably higher than it used to be, while holding her in my arms.

A warm, rather peculiar moment passed between us.

There was something so endearing about the whole situation.

No matter how much taller she'd grown, or how beautiful she'd become, Ren was still my little sister.

My tiny, tiny little sister, whom her big sister was supposed to protect.

I was basking in that reassuring sense of being her older sister when...

"Did you get a love letter?"

Ren asked in a completely emotionless voice.

"Y-Yeah. Although it might just be some kind of mistake."\
"I see."

Those quiet words were swallowed up by the background noise of the television.

And then, shortly afterwards...

My field of vision suddenly changed.

Ren, who had been sitting on my lap, abruptly turned around to face me.

She repositioned herself so that I was pressed back against the reclining chair, with her facing me directly.

Her larger body leaned over mine, almost completely covering me.

I looked up at Ren's face as it suddenly approached mine.

Her large, cat-like eyes. Her long eyelashes. Her delicate lips.

My entire field of vision was filled with her.

I found myself staring at her, thinking how incredibly beautiful she'd become.

It was a rather inappropriate thing to be thinking at that moment, but I couldn't help being captivated.

And then she spoke.

"You're not allowed to go out with anyone."

Her words were cold and firm.

But everything about Ren, whose face filled my vision, seemed to convey a desperate sincerity.

Her eyes, and the gaze directed at me from them, were burning with an almost scorching intensity.

"Not allowed...? What do you mean...?"

I couldn't understand what she was trying to say.

Ren's face and body were so close to mine that my heart was pounding even harder, making it increasingly difficult to think clearly.

"Because Big Sis... is my big sister."

The way she said *Big Sis* didn't match the tone of her voice at all.

I still didn't understand anything.

As I sat there completely bewildered, Ren suddenly moved away from me, as though everything that had just happened had been a lie.

The pressure against my chest disappeared, and I could breathe more easily again.

Although, strangely enough, I found myself missing the warmth of her body and her sweet scent just a little.

"I'm going to bed."\
"Oh, I see. Goodnight."

I replied rather blankly, still unable to process everything that had just happened.

"Also, forget what I just said."

Leaving those words behind, Ren walked out of the living room.
