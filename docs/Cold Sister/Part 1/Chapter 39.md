## Chapter 39: Marriage

\
The sensation of a warmth that wasn't my own brought me vaguely back to consciousness.

In my hazy, half-awake state, I realised that the source of that warmth was Ren. She usually woke up before me without fail, but perhaps yesterday's match had left her exhausted. Today, she was sleeping soundly, clinging to my body.

Ren's sleeping face looked so young, in stark contrast to her cool demeanour during the day. Her peaceful expression hadn't changed at all since she was a child.

"Big Sis..."

Ren's lips moved faintly.

Apparently, I was making an appearance in her dreams. The thought made me feel strangely ticklish inside. Without thinking, I stroked her head, just as I used to.

In the hazy light of morning, my sleepy mind sometimes carried me back into the past. Bathed in the pale blue light of dawn, Ren's familiar warmth drew me into memories of days gone by.

◇◇◇

"Big Sis!"

My younger sister, Ren, was always following close behind me. She was tiny, a cry-baby, innocent and shy around strangers. Without me, she couldn't do anything.

I thought she was unbearably adorable. Whenever Ren came to me for affection, I'd always wrap her in a tight hug and stroke her head. Whenever some bully made her cry, I'd rush over and teach that boy a lesson.

"That was scary, wasn't it? It's all right now."\
"...Mm."\
"Whenever you're in trouble, Big Sis will always protect you."\
"Thank you, Big Sis. I love you!"

With her face still a mess of tears, Ren would smile.

My little sister. Tiny, weak, adorable, and such a cry-baby. I would protect her, absolutely, no matter what.

We were always together when we were little. Reading picture books, playing house, going to the park. Everything was something we did together.

"When I grow up, I'm going to marry Big Sis!"

At some point, Ren began saying those words frequently.

And she didn't just say them directly to me. She'd announce it at the dinner table, too.

"Mum, Dad! Guess what? When Ren grows up, she's going to marry Big Sis!"

She would say it excitedly, almost bursting with enthusiasm.

"I see. I can't wait for the wedding!"

Dad would laugh and play along. He was a cheerful, easy-going man, tall and athletic, and he often gave us piggyback rides on his shoulders. I loved the view from so high above the ground, so much higher than I could ever reach myself. He was kind and funny, a father I was proud of. Mum would smile beside him.

I loved scenes and moments like those. I loved Mum and Dad. And, of course, I loved our whole family, Ren included.

I was older than Ren, so I knew that sisters couldn't marry each other. But I sometimes thought that, if it were possible, perhaps that wouldn't be so bad. After all, if Ren and I got married, the four of us could live together forever as a family. Surely that would be wonderful. For our present happiness to continue for ever... What more could anyone ask for?

Those were the innocent thoughts that occupied my little mind.
