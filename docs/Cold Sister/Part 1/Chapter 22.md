## Chapter 22 - I'll Do My Best

\
After finishing the washing-up from dinner, taking a bath, and seeing to everything else I needed to do, I lay down on my bed.

Usually, I'd read a little before going to sleep, but for some reason I wasn't in the mood tonight. I left the light on and closed my eyes.

I thought about what Mum had said earlier.

Mum is strong and kind. And perhaps because of that, she's always carried a sense of guilt towards us. She's an exceptionally responsible person, so I think it pains her that she hasn't been able to give us even a little of what might be considered an ordinary family life.

But we're already perfectly happy as things are.

It frustrates me a little that she doesn't seem to understand that. And yet, I'm also happy that she thinks so seriously about our happiness. I respect her for that.

I really must become strong enough to protect them. Mum and Ren. So that we can always continue living together happily as a family.

In the end, that's the resolution I always arrive at. And it's why I want to grow up as quickly as possible.

As I renewed that determination, someone knocked on my bedroom door.

It must be Mum. I wondered whether she needed something.

“Come in!”

I jumped off the bed and went to the door. When I slowly opened it, the head standing before me was considerably higher than I'd expected.

It was Ren.

Taken aback by this unexpected visitor, I asked:

“Eh? What's wrong?”

At my question, Ren lowered her gaze and quietly let the words fall from her lips.

“Can I sleep with you?”

Her tone was flat, but her cheeks were flushed from her bath. The way she tilted her head made her seem to be looking up at me, even though she was taller than I was. I was weak to this side of Ren.

“...Of course.”

I'd agreed without even asking why. But if Ren, of all people, was asking to sleep together, she must have been feeling particularly lonely, or perhaps there was some other reason. I decided it would be tactless to ask.

“Then, excuse me.”

Ren said this and entered my room rather tentatively. I closed the door behind me, walked past her, and lay down on the bed. Then, indicating the empty space beside me, I called out to her as though inviting her over.

“Come here, Ren.”

For some reason, Ren's body stiffened for a moment when I called her. Then she approached and lay down beside me.

Or so I thought.

Instead, in a strangely awkward movement, she fell forwards as though tumbling onto me, covering my body.

“Ren...?”

The sudden sensation of her body heat, and the overwhelming feeling of her weight, made me let out a bewildered sound.

She was close. So close that I felt she might be able to hear my rapidly accelerating heartbeat. And imagining that she could hear it made me embarrassed.

Why? Why was I embarrassed? I couldn't identify any clear reason, and yet my face was growing hot.

Ren's large hands, her long, slender fingers, were right beside my face, sinking into the mattress.

Actually, her face was close too. So close that I could almost see my own reflection in her eyes.

“Big Sis.”

Ren whispered beside my ear.

Her beautiful voice vibrated against my eardrum, raced through my entire body, and made my blood boil.

This distance was no good. The already unusual distance between us was in danger of becoming genuinely unbearable. I'd only just reflexively stroked her head at lunchtime today, too.

As all sorts of thoughts raced through my mind—

“Sorry. I got it wrong.”

Ren said this, broke away from me, and lay down beside me instead.

It felt as though a cold wind had blown through the space in my heart.

Even though she was still close enough, I was feeling that way. It seemed my senses really were becoming thoroughly distorted. So much for having any dignity as an older sister.

Wanting to calm myself down, I turned around and lay with my head facing away from Ren, taking a deep breath.

Then—

“Face this way.”

Ren's voice came from behind me. Unusually, she sounded sulky.

Without thinking, I rolled over and turned to face her.

Her face was right in front of me. Her legs were slightly bent, following the contours of my body. Even so, her toes extended beyond mine, reminding me just how well-proportioned she was.

Then there was the scent tickling my nostrils. The same shampoo I used. And yet, Ren's hair somehow smelled sweeter.

Trying to understand why, I first looked into the eyes right in front of me.

Normally, her mature atmosphere and refined movements gave me the impression that she was cool and composed. But looking at her from the same eye level, she seemed almost adorable. Her eyes were like those of a kitten, irresistibly cute.

That part of her had never changed.

Finding something about Ren that was just as it had been when we were younger somehow calmed my heart.

In contrast, Ren's gaze wandered restlessly, as though her eyes were spinning around. It was almost as if she were trying to escape my gaze.

But there was nowhere to run in the narrow bed. Eventually, as though resigned to her fate, she looked at me and murmured:

“Too close.”\
“Once you're in high school, there's not much room for two people to sleep together, is there?”

I said with a smile, gently stroking Ren's head.

Ren closed her eyes and let me do as I pleased. For a while, we lay there together in silence, gently drifting on the bed. Our bodies were close enough for the outlines of our warmth to brush lightly against one another.

It was a very gentle moment.

I reached towards the pillow and pressed the switch on the light remote.

Darkness enveloped the room.

Illuminated only by the faint night-light, Ren shifted her body closer to mine. Trying to preserve the peaceful atmosphere, I asked in my older-sister voice:

“What happened today?”

I asked gently, tracing only the outer edges of her feelings, careful not to touch anything too close to the heart of the matter.

Ren remained still for a moment, as though thinking. Then she slowly answered, weighing each word.

“I've decided to do my best. I've decided to stop hesitating about something I've been uncertain about until now.”

Whether she understood my intention or not, Ren's answer was just as vague. I assumed she wanted some encouragement to give her the strength to work towards whatever it was she wanted to do.

If she'd chosen me as her source of encouragement, I felt honoured.

Pride and loneliness mingled inside me.

The reason I felt lonely was that whatever Ren wanted to work hard at, whether it was club activities, studying, or romance, I imagined it would be something separate from my own world.

Now that her rebellious phase had subsided and we'd grown closer, I found myself thinking that perhaps the distance between sisters was supposed to gradually increase.

We'd joined different clubs, stopped travelling to and from school together, and moved into separate rooms. Little by little, more and more of Ren had become unknown to me.

Just as people like Shimamoto-san, who harboured feelings for Ren and wanted to walk through life together with her, would surely appear in abundance from now on.

The bond of family would never disappear. But precisely because that bond existed, people went on to expand their own worlds. They built places for themselves apart from their families.

Home was ultimately a place to return to, not a destination in itself. I thought very few people were like me, with their family at the very centre of their world.

And so, the world we'd once shared would gradually separate, and Ren would spread her wings beneath a different sky from mine.

Even so, I wanted to become a place she could lean on when she was tired, or when she felt a little lonely, as she did now. I wanted to build a place she could always return to with peace of mind.

I wanted to be an older sister who could protect Ren whenever she was hurting.

“I see. I'll support you. Big Sis will do her best, too.”

I put that wish into my words: the wish to remain part of Ren's world, even if only a little.

At my words, Ren nodded with a sad expression.

Before I could find out whether she really was sad, my consciousness was swallowed by sleepiness.

In my hazy state of awareness, I thought I heard Ren murmur something, but I couldn't make out the words.

◇◇◇

“I really am going to do my best. So be prepared, Big Sis.”
