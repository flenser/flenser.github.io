## Chapter 35: Look at Me

\
I'd finished the housework, had my bath, dried my hair, washed and moisturised my face. All that remained was to go to bed. Friday nights like this always filled me with a sense of accomplishment and relief at having made it through another week.

Besides, tomorrow was the day I'd be going to watch Ren's basketball match. I was terribly excited, and so happy she'd invited me. But I was just as nervous. I couldn't settle down. I had a feeling I wouldn't fall asleep easily tonight. The match itself didn't start until the afternoon, so there was no particular reason I needed to go to bed just yet.

Unlike me, however, Ren apparently had to be at the venue from early morning to attend the opening ceremony.

Since we couldn't travel there together, I checked the match's starting time and location once more, as Ren had told me. City Gymnasium, Court A, two o'clock in the afternoon. I'd already passed the details on to Tomoka-chan, but I had to make sure I didn't get them wrong.

Come to think of it, Ren hadn't come to my room tonight, which was unusual. Perhaps she'd already gone to bed, since she had an early start tomorrow. I understood that she'd want to spend the night alone before such an important match, but I couldn't help feeling terribly lonely.

Not so long ago, we hardly even spoke to each other, let alone slept together. And yet, before I'd realised it, a familiar everyday life in which Ren was always by my side had begun to take shape again. It made me happy, but it also frightened me a little.

When the day came for Ren to leave my side, would I be able to see her off with a smile? Would I be able to let go of my little sister properly? For instance, if Ren found a lover, or decided to live with that person...

A murky feeling began to gather in my heart. I hurriedly brushed it aside. Perhaps I was simply a little oversensitive because of how nervous I was. Ren was the one who had to play tomorrow, after all. I made an effort to recover my usual cheerfulness. Ren had gone to the trouble of inviting me, so the least I could do was cheer her on with all my heart.

As I was telling myself this, a knock sounded at the door.

I forced down the hope that was bubbling up inside me and made my way towards it.

When I turned the handle, Ren was standing there, just as I'd hoped.

"I've decided I'll sleep with you again tonight."

Ren declared this as though it were the most natural thing in the world, then stepped into my room.

We sat on the bed together, and Ren casually leaned against me, her body heat just barely touching mine. Then she leaned further, until she was lying across my lap. A lap pillow, in other words. Her long legs stretched towards the edge of the bed, and her silky hair spread across my knees.

Ren rubbed her cheek against my lap and closed her eyes contentedly. Her kitten-like gesture made my expression soften. My heart felt full.

Lately, whenever Ren sought affection from me, I found myself feeling a sense of security. It came from somewhere different from the protective instinct I felt as her older sister. Its very precariousness made me want to look away, to smother the feeling beneath my usual behaviour as a big sister. I gently stroked the head before me, then covered it with words.

"You're especially clingy today, aren't you?"\
"Not particularly."\
"Is it because you have a match tomorrow? Are you too nervous to sleep?"\
"I'm not nervous. It's just..."

Her voice remained as calm and flat as ever. With her face buried in my lap and her eyes still closed, Ren continued.

"I was planning to sleep alone because I can't fall asleep easily when I'm with you, Big Sis. But I couldn't sleep unless I was with you."

Her words were like a riddle. Her cheek against my lap, her breathing, even her voice, all seemed unusually warm. I felt that heat might easily spread to me as well, and hurriedly tried to smother it with words.

"W-what do you mean?"

When I asked, Ren went still. A moment later, she suddenly sat up and placed a hand against my cheek.

Ren's beauty, appearing so suddenly before me, bared its fangs.

"What do you think I mean?"

Her face was overwhelming. She was so beautiful, and so accustomed to behaving like a girl in a romance manga, that even my heartbeat seemed to belong in one.

And the fact that the cause of those gestures, and of that heartbeat, was my own little sister was enough to make me feel as though I were losing my mind. No, perhaps I already was, considering how hot I felt, as though I were about to boil over.

Ren's eyes held me captive. I felt as though I might be swallowed up by them. My reason could offer only one desperate instruction: I had to get away.

Somehow, I managed to squeeze out a response.

"I don't know."

Ren didn't seem satisfied with my answer. She continued looking at me.

As that gaze lingered, I felt as though Ren were beginning to invade every one of my senses, not just my sight. Her scent, her touch... I wanted to feel more of her.

Just as those desires were about to take shape, Ren moved away from me.

"I see. I suppose that's only natural."

The words she left behind carried a faint sadness. Instinctively, I wanted to reach after her. But just before I could extend my hand, I noticed something.

Ren's gaze was still fixed on me, and it was still filled with heat.

Then, carried along that intense, beautiful gaze, her words reached me.

"Tomorrow, watch me. Watch only me."
