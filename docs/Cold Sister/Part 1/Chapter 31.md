## Chapter 31: Matching

\
“Can we stop by the bookshop?”\
“Sure.”

We left the arcade and walked a little further, until I spotted a familiar place. I asked Ren's permission and headed towards it.

As though it were an unspoken condition, our hands were joined again. My heart gave a little flutter at the contact, but I pretended it was nothing and stepped into the rows of books.

Whenever I came across a bookshop, I couldn't help wandering inside, even if there wasn't anything in particular I wanted to buy. I could easily spend an hour or two just browsing the titles on the spines and picking out books that looked interesting. I had to be careful today, what with Ren accompanying me. With that in mind, I made my way through the paperback section a little faster than usual.

Oh, a hardback I'd been interested in had come out in paperback.

I tucked my bag under one arm, took the book from the shelf with my free hand, and read the synopsis on the back. It did look interesting after all. I decided to buy it immediately.

“Big Sis, you really like books, don't you?”\
“I do. I am in the literature club, after all.”

Although the other member only read books I could never let Ren see. I gave a wry smile to myself at the thought.

“Do you have any recommendations?”\
“Eh? Ren, you want to read something too?”

Ren nodded.

It made me happy when someone showed an interest in something I liked. And since the genres Tomoka-chan and I enjoyed were completely different, I was also delighted at the prospect of recommending one of my favourite books to someone.

“Is there a particular genre you'd like to read?”\
“...Romance, perhaps.”\
“Romance?”

That was a little unexpected. Even so, I looked over the shelves.

“Everything by this author is really good.”\
“Then I'll take this one.”

Ren picked up one of the books as she spoke.

“Oh, that one! It's had really good reviews, and I've been meaning to read it too. Could you lend it to me once you've finished?”\
“Of course. And next time, let me borrow one of the books you own that you'd recommend.”

With that, Ren headed towards the till.

I followed after her, smiling broadly at the thought that Ren and I would be able to lend books to each other. My heart was still bubbling with happiness, and I used the excuse that it would be strange not to hold hands again when we'd just been doing so. This time, I was the one who reached out and took her hand.

◇◇◇

Our hands were still joined. I'd grown accustomed to it by now. Even my constantly pounding, noisy heartbeat had become familiar.

It was almost evening, and I was wondering whether it was about time we headed home when Ren suddenly stopped.

There was a little accessories shop in front of us. Ren's gaze was fixed on the hair accessories displayed inside.

“This is cute.”

She picked up a black scrunchie decorated with lace.

“That's lovely! It'll definitely suit you, Ren.”

I said so honestly. The black scrunchie, with its combination of mature elegance and cuteness, seemed absolutely perfect for her.

But Ren shook her head.

“It's cute, so I want Big Sis to wear it.”\
“Eh?”

Ren slipped the scrunchie around my wrist.

“See? It suits you.”

She nodded several times, looking satisfied.

“D-do you think so?”

The contrast between the black scrunchie and my pure white dress was certainly pretty and cute. But I wasn't sure I could really bring out its full charm, and I tilted my head uncertainly. Ren, as though trying to convince me, repeated herself.

“It's cute.”

She said it, then calmly stroked my head. With the difference in our heights, it almost felt as though Ren were the older sister.

I felt as though she'd stolen my usual role, and the fact that my heart was so easily sent racing made me rather frustrated. I turned my gaze away from her to hide my embarrassment. Then something caught my eye, and I picked it up.

“Wouldn't this suit you too, Ren?”

In retaliation for what she'd just done, I slipped a white scrunchie, the same design as mine but in a different colour, around Ren's wrist.

“See? Cute.”

I said it and reached out to stroke her head… or at least, I tried to. Standing upright, Ren's head was too far away. I stretched up desperately on my tiptoes, but even then I could barely reach.

Seeing my predicament, Ren took pity on me. She bent down slightly and looked straight into my face.

I managed to stroke her head, but more than that, her face was so close. We were positioned as though gazing into each other's eyes. Her sparkling eyes, long eyelashes and delicate lips all filled my field of vision. Even the way she blinked was beautiful.

“I'm happy we're matching.”

Ren said it with an innocent, almost smiling expression. In truth, her face was still expressionless, but the tone of her voice and the way her eyes trembled conveyed her feelings.

If this continued any longer, my heart wouldn't survive.

“Well then, shall we go to the till?”

I said, and hurried away as though making my escape.

Somehow, I felt as though I'd lost in the end.
