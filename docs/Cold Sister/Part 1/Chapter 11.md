## Chapter 11 – A Secret?

\
After school, I always felt a sense of liberation once I stepped out through the main gate. It was as though the entire landscape opened up around the cloudless blue sky. But today, something was weighing on my mind, preventing me from enjoying that familiar feeling of freedom.

Tomoka-chan seemed to pick up on it and asked:

“So, how did the confession go? Were you able to give her an answer?”\
“Yeah. Thanks to you, I managed to tell her how I really felt. Thank you for listening to all my worries.”\
“Oh, that's wonderful! And well done, Ai. You did a great job!”

With that, Tomoka-chan began stroking my head. Her hands were large and warm. I let myself enjoy the pleasant sensation for a little while before cautiously bringing up what had happened.

“But I might have hurt her feelings.”\
“When you turn someone down, that's more or less inevitable. Being honest about how you feel is the greatest sincerity you can show the other person, so you shouldn't worry too much about it.”

Tomoka-chan offered those words of comfort.

“Well, it's more that... she seemed desperate, rather than simply hurt. And unless I misheard her, she muttered Ren's name as she was leaving.”

Tomoka-chan's expression immediately hardened at my words.

“Why would your little sister come into it?”

Her voice was colder than I'd ever heard it before. Startled, I hurriedly tried to explain.

“Well, the girl who confessed to me seemed to be one of Ren's teammates. I remember seeing her among the group of basketball club members when I passed Ren near the water fountains yesterday. So it's not as though she and Ren are complete strangers, but...”

Tomoka-chan listened intently to my rather muddled explanation, then folded her arms and began thinking.

Silence settled between us. The only sound was the rhythm of our footsteps, two sets echoing in my ears. By the time we reached the T-junction where we always parted ways, she finally spoke.

“If my guess is right, I don't like that girl. Actually, I can't forgive her. After all, you agonised over this so seriously, Ai.”

Tomoka-chan's voice trembled with anger.

I'd never seen her show her emotions so openly before, nor had I ever seen her direct her anger at another person. I didn't know what to do, and the words died in my throat.

“Tomoka-chan...?”

At my almost pleading question, Tomoka-chan seemed to snap back to her senses. Then, in her usual gentle voice, she said:

“Sorry. I must have scared you. It's all right. You haven't done anything wrong, Ai.”

She gently patted my head, as though soothing a child.

She wasn't quite as tall as Ren, but she was taller than me, and there was something reassuring about her dependable manner. Sometimes, I thought of her as an older sister. A far more capable and reliable older sister than I was.

“Anyway, Ai. What's that girl's name?”\
“Um, I think it was Shimamoto Rira.”\
“Right. Shimamoto-san.”

Tomoka-chan quietly repeated the name, almost rolling it around in her mouth.

“Ai. If that girl says anything else to you from now on, come and talk to me. I have a few suspicions about what's going on. And I'll see if I can do something about her myself.”\
“Um, please don't get angry with her. She seemed like a nice girl, and she's Ren's friend, after all.”\
“You really are kind, Ai. Don't worry. It's not as though I'm going to eat her alive. But if my guess is right, that girl will almost certainly show up in front of you again.”\
“Your guess...?”\
“That's a secret.”

Tomoka-chan smiled as she said it. With that smile, we arrived at the T-junction where we always said goodbye. An orange traffic mirror, standing in the middle of the junction, watched over us.

“Bye-bye. Thanks for everything.”\
“Don't worry about it. I'd do anything to protect you, Ai. See you tomorrow.”

Tomoka-chan said it in a teasingly light-hearted way, then disappeared down the road in the opposite direction.

*To protect me.*

Her parting words resonated deep within my heart. I needed to become a more dependable big sister, too, so that I could protect Ren.

My determination swelled. I clenched my fists tightly in front of my chest, then set off down the road home.

For now, I needed to get back and make dinner for Ren.
