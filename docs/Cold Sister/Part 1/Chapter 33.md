## Chapter 33: Points and Lines - POV Tomoka

\
"You've got to be kidding me."

At the spot just beside the shopping centre entrance, I was rendered speechless when I saw Shimamoto-san arrive at our meeting place.

She came striding towards me, proudly showing off her height. Wearing sunglasses.

"What's that supposed to mean? We've only just met."\
"Ah, honestly, I'd rather you didn't come anywhere near me. I don't want people thinking I'm suspicious too."\
"Suspicious? But if I want to keep people from recognising my face, I need something like this, don't I? After all, we're going to be following someone today."\
"Forget your face! With that stature and those sunglasses, you're drawing so much attention that you'll be spotted immediately!"

I clutched my head. After talking with her a few times, I'd come to realise that Shimamoto-san, despite her prim and proper appearance, was actually rather... well, hopeless.

"N-no, that's not true!"\
"Just take them off!"\
"But I went to the trouble of buying them..."

I snatched the sunglasses away without giving her a chance to argue. A thoroughly displeased face appeared before me.

"It was a perfect disguise..."

Shimamoto-san continued grumbling under her breath.

I pressed my fingers against my temples, feeling a headache coming on.

Our day off was off to a thoroughly inauspicious start.

◇◇◇

"Two hot coffees."\
"Thank you."

Steam rose from the coffee poured into our cups.

It was lunchtime, and we'd somehow managed to secure a seat in the café from which Ai and the others couldn't see us. We watched the table in the distance intently.

"Why are they holding hands...?!"

The person sitting opposite me muttered those words with such intensity that she seemed ready to stab them to death with her gaze. She didn't so much as glance at the coffee that had just been brought to us.

I watched Shimamoto-san as I picked up my cup.

"Hey, don't you have anything to say about that?"

She asked in a voice tinged with frustration. I blew on my coffee and carefully took a sip. It was still piping hot.

Oh, right. I was supposed to be in love with Ai.

"Well, I mean, behaving so shamelessly in public... It's quite disgraceful, isn't it? Unforgivable, really."

I put my cup down and improvised an appropriate response. Had I been a little too casual? I was beginning to worry that I'd made a poor job of it when...

"Exactly! Honestly!"

The person opposite me nodded repeatedly, looking satisfied.

She really was an endearingly foolish person. Yes.

Love really does make people lose their minds. How terrifying.

Still, I had to admit that even I was a little taken aback. The distance between Ai and her little sister was much closer than I'd imagined. They were holding hands as though it were the most natural thing in the world. I'd heard they'd been getting along lately, but I hadn't expected things to have progressed this far.

The two of them occasionally looked a little embarrassed as their hands rested together. At times, they looked so much like an actual couple that my heart gave a start.

While I was privately torn between astonishment and sympathy, something happened at their table.

A waitress approached Ai and the others, placed an enormous parfait on their table, and left.

I was sure that was... I lowered my eyes to the menu lying on our table.

"Ah, I see."\
"What do you see?"\
"I have good news. That parfait is only available to couples."\
"What's good about that?"\
"It means the sisters are deliberately pretending to be a couple so they can eat that parfait."\
"I see!"

Shimamoto-san's face lit up. Then, almost immediately, it clouded over again.

"But isn't that taking things a little too far...?"

I looked over. Ai and her little sister were feeding each other the parfait, taking turns.

"You're right..."

Well, judging from what Ai had told me, I could more or less tell that her little sister was rather awkward when it came to these things. Perhaps her brakes had simply stopped working altogether. Probably.

I tried to convince myself of that, then asked Shimamoto-san a question to lighten the mood.

"Shall we order a couples-only parfait too?"\
"No, thank you!"

She shot the suggestion down in a tone that was rather more serious than necessary.

After that, Shimamoto-san kept staring at the little sister. The coffee in the cup before her had gone completely cold.

◇◇◇

"Seriously, why are those two holding hands the whole time...?"\
"How should I know?"

Shimamoto-san was still on edge. As I tried to calm her down, I found myself privately perplexed.

Was that really how sisters behaved with each other? Their hands were firmly interlaced in the way lovers held hands, and combined with the difference in their heights, they looked like a genuine couple.

"Shimamoto-san, you said the other day that you have an older sister, didn't you?"\
"I did. What about it?"\
"Well, I'm an only child, so I don't really know what it's like, but... Is that how close sisters usually are?"\
"Of course not. I talk to my older sister about all sorts of things, and it's not as though we don't get along, but we're certainly not all over each other like that."

That's exactly the problem.

Shimamoto-san muttered those words.

From what Ai had told me, it was easy enough to imagine that her little sister was actually very fond of her, but unable to be honest about it. However, wasn't the direction of that affection a little different from what I'd originally thought...?

I recalled a conversation I'd had with Shimamoto-san before.

'Her little sister's feelings towards Ai might be rather excessive for a younger sister's feelings towards her older sister, but surely they aren't enough to get in the way of your love...'\
'You really don't understand anything, do you?'

No, surely not.

I tried to convince myself otherwise. For one thing, I hadn't sensed anything from Ai to suggest that she felt anything other than sisterly affection towards her little sister. That sort of thing was surely impossible.

I really must have been reading too many BL novels. As a form of self-discipline, I forcibly erased the suspicion that had entered my mind. Or rather, the delusion.

Even as they walked a tightrope over the top of my delusions, Ai and her little sister kept holding hands all the way from the café, through the arcade, and until they disappeared into a purikura booth.

I couldn't see what was happening inside the booth. But beneath the curtain, the uneven heights of Ai and her little sister's feet occasionally drew remarkably close together. Just what sort of pictures were they taking?

To distract myself, I spoke to Shimamoto-san, who was grinding her teeth.

"Shall we hold hands too?"\
"Why on earth would we do that? What's with you all of a sudden?"\
"Nothing in particular."\
"Don't tell me... Since your chances with the older sister are looking slim, you're switching your affections to me...?"\
"No, I'm not!"\
"Sorry, but I'm devoted to Ren..."\
"That's not what I meant! Will you listen to me?!"

While we were bickering like that, the two of them emerged from the purikura booth. After spending some time enthusiastically decorating their pictures beside the machine, they took them out, cut them apart with scissors, and started walking again.

Holding hands as naturally as ever.

"Tch."

A click of the tongue came from beside me.

How uncivilised. Frightening, really.

Still, I could understand her feelings a little. Their interactions were so sickeningly sweet, far more than I'd anticipated, that I was beginning to feel as though I'd had too much sugar myself. Something unpleasant was quietly accumulating deep in my chest.

The sisters, scattering sugar wherever they went, left the arcade and walked a little further before slipping into a nearby bookshop. They headed straight for the paperback section near the entrance.

We took a wide detour to avoid being spotted and hid in a quiet corner where few people were around.

By sheer coincidence, we'd ended up in the BL section. It was a surprisingly well-stocked selection for a local shopping centre. Souji-sensei's books were there too, of course.

My gaze was drawn to them before I knew it. For some reason, Shimamoto-san was looking in exactly the same direction.

Then she murmured:

"Oh, that's one of my older sister's books."\
"...What?"\
"I said that this person, Souji Terako, is my older sister."

The bizarre actions Shimamoto-san had taken towards Ai.

The reason I'd been able to see through Shimamoto-san's intentions so quickly.

It was because the whole sequence of events resembled the plot of Souji-sensei's latest novel, a BL love triangle involving brothers.

The dots had connected, forming a single line.

And then, with that line now complete, the shock of the revelation blew everything away.
