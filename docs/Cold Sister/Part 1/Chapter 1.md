## Chapter 1 – A Siscon?

\
"When I grow up, I'm going to marry Big Sis!"

The words sounded so terribly nostalgic. A memory from the distant past gently brushed against the back of my mind.

For example, the park in our neighbourhood, the elementary school playground, the shopping arcade... Wherever I went, Ren would follow me.

"My, my! Ren-chan really does love her big sister, doesn't she?"

My teachers and the ladies in the shopping arcade would all say the same thing. Every time I heard those words, I felt proud. My little sister, who clung to me and never wanted to leave my side, was simply too adorable.

I often heard classmates with siblings complain about them, but I couldn't understand how they felt.

After all, how could anyone not find an adorable little sister cute? She was a year younger than me, and would trot along right behind me, always looking for an opportunity to hold my hand.

Our hands clasped tightly together, the warmth of her soft little body... Back then, I couldn't imagine a future in which any of that would ever come undone. I thought we would always remain close, loving sisters.

*Bzzzz! Bzzzz! Bzzzz!*

My phone alarm rang, pulling me back to reality.\
I slowly opened my eyes and stretched out with a great big yawn. After checking the time, I leapt out of bed, resisting the temptation to go back to sleep.

I left my room and, still half-asleep, staggered towards the bathroom. And there, right before my eyes, was the very face I'd just seen in my dream.

"Good morning, Ren."

I directed my greeting towards a face now positioned considerably higher than the one in my dream. However, there was no reply. Ren simply turned her face away with a huff, walked past me, and headed back to her room on the second floor.

Ren had changed.

For starters, she'd grown taller. As if mocking me for having the title of *big sister*, she'd even surpassed me in height. And, as though following suit, her face had grown beautiful too. She'd chopped off the long hair we'd once kept matching, and now gave off a rather cold, distant air.

It wasn't just her appearance, either. Her personality had changed as well.

She used to be so clingy and affectionate, but now she wouldn't even talk to me. I couldn't remember exactly when things had become this way. It was probably around the time Ren started middle school. Little by little, she began putting distance between us.

We used to share a room and even sleep together in the same bed at night. Then she said she wanted a room of her own and moved into the room our father had once used.

There was a time when she couldn't go anywhere without me. Not just on the way to and from school, but even during elementary school recess, she'd come looking for me every single time. And when I graduated from elementary school a year ahead of her, she'd cried and thrown a tantrum.

But once she entered middle school, everything changed. While I joined the literary club, she went in the opposite direction and joined the basketball team. She became so good that she was selected for the city's representative team. I saw her receive awards at school assemblies countless times.

Because of her morning basketball practice, our journeys to and from school almost never coincided. Even when I spoke to her at school, she'd act as though she hadn't noticed me. And when I graduated from middle school a year ahead of her, she didn't shed a single tear.

Now, even though we attend the same high school, nothing has changed. Just a moment ago, unlike me in my pyjamas, she was already dressed in her school uniform. She was probably heading off to morning practice.

As her older sister, I'm proud to see how wonderfully my little sister has grown up. At the same time, though, I feel a little lonely.

I suppose I should be happy that she's grown independent of her big sister. But the truth is, I'm the one who hasn't managed to let go of my little sister.

After all, no matter how much taller she's become or how mature her personality is, the Ren I see is still an extension of that little girl who used to come running to me in tears, calling out "Big Sis!" whenever something happened.

She doesn't have to cling to me quite as much as she used to. But I do wish she'd at least talk to me.

I wonder...

Is this what they call a *siscon*?

With that thought lingering in my mind, I slowly went about getting ready for the day.
