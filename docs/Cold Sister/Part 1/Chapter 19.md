## Chapter 19 - A Bandage?

\
“I actually love Ren.”

Shimamoto-san's words kept circling through my mind. As though trying to scrape them out of my head, I stirred the contents of the frying pan with a pair of cooking chopsticks. The smell of ginger filled the kitchen. The chicken breast sizzled and spat in the pan. I went through these familiar motions mechanically, trying to empty my mind. But whenever my concentration slipped, my thoughts were dragged away, and before I knew it, I was trapped in that same dead end again.

It wasn't as though Ren being popular was anything new. She was good at sports, beautiful, and bound to attract admirers. And yet, simply learning that fact first-hand had left me shaken. Not understanding why I was so unsettled, or even what this vague feeling inside me was, only made my agitation worse.

It was wonderful that Ren was loved by so many people. As her big sister, I ought to feel proud of her. So why couldn't I simply be happy about it?

I shook the frying pan, as though trying to dispel those doubts.

I'm her big sister. I need to be more sensible.

I steeled myself once again.

Just then, as though responding to my state of mind, I heard the rattling of the front door. After a few light footsteps, Ren appeared in the kitchen.

“Welcome home, Ren.”\
“I'm back... Um, listen.”

Ren lowered her head as though she wanted to say something, then moved closer to my side.

“Feed me dinner again today.”

She made a point of drawing attention to the bandage wrapped around her right wrist as she spoke. Her voice and face were so mature, yet this particular gesture was so childish. The incongruity caught my attention.

“Okay.”

As I looked at Ren, the words slipped out of my mouth without conscious thought. I'd been thinking this quite often lately. I was probably far too indulgent towards Ren whenever she acted affectionate.

“Thank you.”

Ren thanked me with her usual expressionless face, her beauty almost too perfect. I found myself thinking something strange: I wished I could see that expression crack, even just a little. A smile, or anything at all. I could no longer remember clearly what Ren's face had looked like when she was younger, when her emotions had been so much more openly expressed.

While I was gazing vaguely at her, lost in thought—

“Isn't it done yet?”

Ren peered at what I was cooking and asked.

I turned my eyes back to the pan. The chicken was burning.

I hurriedly turned off the heat.

And so, after putting the worst of the burnt bits onto my own plate, I managed to finish preparing dinner without further incident.

Ren, who had gone back to her room, appeared at the dining table at just the right moment. As though it were perfectly natural, she sat down beside me. The fact that this was becoming an ordinary occurrence brought a smile to my face.

“What are you smiling about?”

Ren's voice was cold as she remarked on my expression.

“Sorry, it's nothing. Come on, let's eat.”

I sat down in my own place.

“Let's eat.”

Ren followed my lead, bringing her hands together, then gazed intently at me.

I smiled and offered her a piece of chicken. Ren opened her mouth and popped it in. As always, it was a tiny bite.

I was used to this by now. I fed Ren in an orderly fashion while eating my own meal. Of course, I made sure not to take my eyes off her for too long, or she'd look displeased, so every now and then our eyes met.

As we continued this rather unusual meal, Ren finished chewing and suddenly asked:

“You're a little strange today. Did something happen?”\
“Why do you ask?”\
“Because you burnt the food, and you keep looking at me. You're acting weird.”

I was doubly surprised by Ren's question. First, by how perceptive she was, and second, by the realisation that I'd apparently been staring at her so much without even noticing.

“Nothing happened.”

There was no way I could tell her that I'd learned Shimamoto-san's true feelings, so I looked away and tried to brush the matter aside. But Ren immediately pressed me further.

“Don't tell me that girl who confessed to you the other day said something to you again...”

As she spoke, Ren leaned sharply towards me. When someone as beautiful as her glared at you, the pressure was overwhelming. Combined with her figure, I felt as though I were being cornered.

Actually, she was very close.

Ren's long eyelashes and glossy lips filled my field of vision. My heart began racing reflexively.

Just as I found myself in this minor predicament, I heard the front door open.

Ren immediately moved away from me. A moment later, Mum appeared at the dining table.

“I'm home. Well, what's got into you two? Sitting next to each other and eating so companionably.”

Mum looked suspicious, and I hurriedly explained.

“Ren seems to have hurt her wrist, so I've been feeding her in her place.”

At my explanation, Mum noticed the bandage wrapped around Ren's wrist and hurried over.

“Oh, goodness! How bad is it? Are you all right? Let me have a look at how it is now.”

With the practised efficiency of a medical professional, she began unwrapping the bandage around Ren's wrist.

“Ah!”

For some reason, Ren let out a startled sound.

A moment later, her exposed wrist was revealed. It was white and just as beautiful as the rest of her skin. There didn't appear to be anything wrong with it.

“From what I can see, there's nothing wrong at all.”

Mum murmured in puzzlement.

“It's healed. I hadn't noticed.”

Ren said this, then began eating the rest of her meal at an astonishing speed. Gone were the tiny bites she'd been taking before. She deftly polished off everything on her plate.

“I'm finished.”

With that, she hurried back to her room just as quickly.

She tried to act as though nothing had happened, but her face was bright red.

For some reason, seeing that flush made my heart leap.
