## Chapter 8 – Sleep-Deprived?

\
"Forget what I said earlier."

I couldn't get those words of Ren's out of my head.

"Because Big Sis... is my big sister."

No matter how hard I tried, I just couldn't forget them. Yesterday's words and expressions were firmly etched into my mind, refusing to go away.

My thoughts kept going round and round in circles, preventing me from falling asleep. I drifted between shallow sleep and wakefulness, over and over again.

Lying in bed, I tossed and turned for no particular reason.

Whenever I couldn't get to sleep, it was usually because I was trapped in a dead end of my own thoughts, just like now. I tried changing direction and thinking about something else to escape that endless cycle, but inevitably my thoughts turned to the love letter.

In the end, there was no shortage of things to worry about.

Perhaps I already knew what my answer would be.

Right now, even if someone confessed their feelings to me, I wouldn't be able to accept them. After all, I couldn't yet think of dating or romance as something that concerned me personally. Even now, I was still not even convinced that someone like me could actually receive a love confession.

I thought it would be unfair to accept someone's feelings when I was in such an uncertain state of mind.

However, having reached an answer didn't mean the problem was solved.

For example, how could I turn someone down without hurting their feelings?

And, more fundamentally, wasn't my decision not to date anyone at least partly influenced by what Ren had said yesterday?

In the end, my thoughts always returned to Ren.

It seemed that a peaceful night's sleep wasn't going to happen for me today.

Perhaps I should just get up and make myself some coffee.

I climbed out of bed.

Peering outside through a gap in the curtains, I saw that the sky was already beginning to grow light.

The soft, pale blue of the sky blended into the orange glow on the distant horizon, slowly painting over the night.

I could hear crows calling somewhere in the distance.

Looking at the scene outside, I vaguely realised, through my heavy head, just how little sleep I'd managed to get.

For now, I decided to wash my face.

I left my room and headed towards the washroom.

Just then, the stairs creaked. A moment later, Ren came slowly descending them, her steps unsteady and uncertain.

When she noticed me, her body gave a sudden start.

"Good morning."

It was a greeting that usually went unanswered.

"...Morning."

But today, she replied.

Her voice was weak and unusually soft and floppy-sounding, quite unlike Ren.

I'd practically given up expecting an answer, so I couldn't help staring at her face in surprise.

"What?"

Noticing my gaze, Ren gave me a suspicious look and quickly recovered her usual stiff tone.

"Oh, nothing. I just thought it was unusual for us to run into each other this early."

By the time I normally got up, Ren had already finished getting ready for the day. Seeing her immediately after waking up was quite a rare occurrence.

She was still wearing her pyjamas, her hair was sticking up slightly, and her eyes were a little bloodshot.

"You're staring too much."\
"Oh, sorry."

After Ren pointed it out, I hurriedly looked away.

Then, belatedly, I remembered yesterday's exchange and suddenly had no idea what sort of expression I should wear when facing her.

We were both feeling awkward about this unfamiliar atmosphere when...

"Good morning! Ai, you're up early today too."

Mum emerged from the bedroom.

"Good morning. I couldn't really get to sleep."\
"Oh dear, that's unfortunate. Apparently it's going to be rather hot today for May, so if you start feeling unwell, make sure you tell your teacher straight away. And good morning to you too, Ren."\
"Good morning."\
"Hang on, what's happened to those dark circles under your eyes? Are both my daughters sleep-deprived?"

Until then, I'd been looking up at Ren, so I hadn't noticed the shadows beneath her eyes. But now that Mum had pointed them out, I could see that Ren did indeed have faint dark circles.

"That's... It's nothing. I'm perfectly normal."

Ren hurriedly disappeared into the bathroom.

"That girl really is as difficult as ever, isn't she?"

Mum laughed with an exasperated expression.

After that, the busy morning passed in a flurry, and I didn't manage to have another proper conversation with Ren.

I even asked her:

"Would you like me to make you some coffee?"

But she simply brushed me off.

"No."

It seemed that Ren was completely back to her usual cold self, as though yesterday's events had never happened.

Somehow, that made me feel just a little lonely.
