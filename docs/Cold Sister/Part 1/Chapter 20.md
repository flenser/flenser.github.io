## Chapter 20 - Seconds?

\
During the lunch break, amidst the relaxed atmosphere, Tomoka-chan and I sat with our desks pushed together, chatting as usual.

“How did things go with Shimamoto-san after that? She hasn't done anything unpleasant to you, has she?”\
“It's all right. There was just... a little misunderstanding with her confession, and she apologised for that.”

Even I thought it was a rather strained explanation, but there was no way I could tell Tomoka-chan that Shimamoto-san had actually been in love with Ren.

“I see. Well, that's good.”

Tomoka-chan accepted my explanation surprisingly easily. I breathed a sigh of relief, then took the opportunity to change the subject and ask something that had been on my mind.

“By the way, when did you and Shimamoto-san become friends? I was surprised when I heard that you'd be passing on a message from her.”\
“It's not like we've become friends, exactly. A few things happened, that's all.”

Tomoka-chan smiled as she said this. Unusually, she seemed reluctant to elaborate. I tilted my head inwardly, wondering what she meant, when the classroom door rattled open and a familiar commotion filled the room.

I turned towards the door. There stood Ren, carrying a plastic convenience-store bag.

“Every single day lately, huh?”

Tomoka-chan grinned teasingly.

“I wonder what's happened. I'll go and see.”

I got up and headed towards the door at the back of the classroom. My classmates, apparently never tiring of it, turned their attention towards me. I'm not particularly comfortable being the centre of attention, so I hurried over to Ren.

“What's wrong, Ren?”

Ren answered my question without the slightest hesitation.

“I've come to eat.”

She said it as though it were the most natural thing in the world. Her attitude was so different from the blushing girl I'd seen yesterday that it was hard to believe they were the same person.

“But hasn't your injury healed...?”

I voiced the question that had occurred to me, somewhat hesitantly.

“So what? Does that mean you won't eat with me unless I'm injured?”

Her tone remained firm, but she was clearly being clingy. I smiled and shook my head.

“Of course not. I'll just go and tell Tomoka-chan.”

Saying that, I rose onto my tiptoes and gently patted Ren's head, as though soothing a child.

Ren recoiled in surprise.

“Big Sis, we're in public.”

At Ren's reminder, I realised that the classroom had fallen into an oddly noisy silence. Heat rushed to my face. I turned on my heel and hurried back to my seat, head bowed.

“Showing off, aren't you?”

Tomoka-chan teased me with a mischievous grin.

“It's not like that. I did it without thinking.”

I muttered an excuse. Lately, Ren and I had been getting so close that I'd let my guard down.

“Well, it's nice that you two have become so much closer than before.”\
“Y-yes, I suppose.”

I nodded vaguely. I couldn't very well tell her that I was actually troubled by how close we'd become.

“So, you're eating with your little sister again today, right? I've already got plans with someone else, so don't worry about me. Go on.”\
“Okay.”

I nodded obediently at Tomoka-chan's suggestion, picked up the plastic bag containing my bread, and hurried out of the classroom as though escaping.

◇◇◇

As usual, there was nobody on the rooftop.

We sat down side by side in the shade of the same building as last time, munching away at our bread. I had a croquette sandwich, while Ren had a cream bun.

The excited voices of students playing on the sports ground drifted up towards us. Their noise only made the silence surrounding us more noticeable. Perhaps because of what had happened yesterday and earlier today, neither of us seemed quite sure what to say. But it wasn't simply awkwardness that filled the silence. There was also the excitement of having Ren sitting right beside me, and the restless fluttering in my chest. All those feelings, tangled together, were part of that silence.

Since we were both quietly concentrating on eating, we soon finished our bread. I discreetly glanced at Ren, wondering whether she would head back already.

But she was looking at me at the same time, and our eyes met naturally.

“What's wrong?”

I smiled and tilted my head.

Ren murmured something under her breath.

“More.”

I struggled to make sense of the mysterious word. Then I guessed she meant she wanted something else to eat.

“Sorry. I only bought the bread we just had.”\
“No.”

Ren shook her head. Then she turned towards me, took my hand, and placed it on top of her own head. Despite her cool expression, the warmth of her body enveloped my palm.

“Continue from earlier. One more time.”

Only then did I understand. I turned towards Ren, kneeling slightly as I reached out to her. Then I gently stroked her head.

Ren bent her back so that I could reach more easily, closing her eyes. Her expression seemed a little softer than usual, and a warm feeling spread through my heart.

“Ren, do you perhaps like having your head patted?”\
“It's not like I like it. You just stopped halfway earlier.”

She replied in a tone that made me admire how skilfully she managed to maintain her cold demeanour.

And yet, when I tried to take my hand away from her head—

“Don't.”

She caught my hand and placed it back on top of her head.

I thought that gesture was adorable. Perhaps this was what boys felt like when they wanted to tease a girl they liked.

“You're not allowed to stop until I say you can.”

Even that selfish demand seemed endearing, and I continued stroking Ren's soft hair again and again.

In the end, Ren never did say, “That's enough.” We carried on like that until the warning bell rang.
