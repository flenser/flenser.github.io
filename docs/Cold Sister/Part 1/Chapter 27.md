## Chapter 27 - The Day Before

\
A restless, excited feeling, like the day before a school trip. A pure white dress, shining brightly inside my wardrobe.

I rested my hand on the wardrobe door and gazed at its radiance.

Come to think of it, Tomoka-chan had been amazing today. She'd darted from one shop to another, searching high and low for clothes. I simply followed her recommendations, trying on all sorts of outfits.

“Usually I'd picture you in a skirt, but trousers look great on you too. It's a lovely change.”\
“An off-the-shoulder top adds a grown-up touch to your naturally graceful look. Really nice.”\
“Something with a bit of a jirai-kei feel is surprisingly good on you too…”

Every time I opened the changing-room curtain, I was greeted by a stream of praise like that. And it was true: the person reflected in the mirror had a completely different atmosphere each time. I could only marvel at Tomoka-chan's excellent taste. When I told her as much, she just laughed.

“That's because you're cute, Ai.”

I think that was Tomoka-chan's magic.

Of all the outfits she'd had me try on, though, the one that drew the strongest reaction from her was the pure white dress.

“Everything you've tried on so far has been great, and it's lovely seeing you in styles you don't normally wear. But, in the end, I think this sort of elegant, cute look really suits you best, Ai. The clean design gives it a mature feel, too. It brings out everything that's appealing about you, including that contrast with your usual look. Honestly, it's absolutely perfect.”

Tomoka-chan had praised it at such speed and with such enthusiasm that I was swept along and found myself heading straight for the till.

I turned my eyes once more to the pristine white garment in front of me. Of course, I thought it was incredibly cute, and I liked it very much. But I wasn't sure I could really pull it off.

It had been such a long time since I'd felt this excited and nervous about buying new clothes. And I didn't know quite what to make of the fact that Ren was probably the reason those feelings had returned.

Just because I had a date with my little sister tomorrow, I was this restless.

For now, I decided I should simply go to bed. It would be terrible if I overslept tomorrow, and I couldn't very well let Ren see me in such an embarrassing state.

With that thought, I closed the wardrobe door and headed towards my bed. On the way, someone knocked on my bedroom door.

I had a pretty good idea who it was, and opened it.

“Can I sleep with you again tonight?”

As expected, Ren was standing there. The moment the door opened, she immediately dropped those words.

“Of course.”

I sat down on the bed. Ren sat beside me, with the ease of someone who'd grown accustomed to doing so.

Ever since that day, Ren had come to my room every night. I hadn't asked her why, but given how often she'd recently had dark circles beneath her eyes, I suspected she was having trouble sleeping and was longing for the comfort of another person's warmth.

I'd wondered what would happen after that morning when I'd hugged Ren in my sleep, but she didn't seem particularly bothered. We'd continued sleeping in the same bed ever since. In fact, sometimes I'd wake up to find Ren holding me instead. It made me feel warm and nostalgic, remembering our childhood, but once I was fully awake, my heart would start pounding. It was quite a predicament. Still, compared with those days when Ren and I had been so distant, I supposed this was something to be happy about.

As I sat there thinking about Ren, she unexpectedly spoke first.

“Tomorrow, huh?”\
“That's right.”\
“I'm really looking forward to it. Are you, Big Sis?”\
“...Of course! I'm looking forward to it!”

Her unusually straightforward words caught me off guard, and I was a moment late in replying. Her face, higher than mine, remained as motionless as ever, like an ice sculpture. Somehow, that expression only made her words seem all the more sincere. I liked the way they reached me so directly.

The lively, expressive Ren from when she was little, and the Ren she was now. Both were my adorable little sister.

“It's an early start tomorrow, so shall we get some sleep?”\
“Mm.”

After watching Ren nod, we both lay down on the bed. I settled against the wall, and Ren nestled up against me. It was the arrangement we'd settled into over the past few days.

I picked up the light remote and pressed the button.

Darkness enveloped the room. In the faint glow of the night-light, the two of us gazed at each other for no particular reason.

“It's kind of like the day before a school trip.”

Ren murmured the words.

“I was just thinking the same thing.”\
“Then forget I said it.”\
“Why?”

Even that cold response from Ren felt comforting. But as I looked at her beautifully sculpted face, I suddenly felt an urge to tease her, so I brought up something from the past.

“But when I was in about second or third year of primary school, you cried the night before a school trip, didn't you? You said, ‘Don't go, Big Sis. I don't want you not to be at school with me.’”\
“...I don't remember.”

After a slight pause, Ren replied.

“Really?”\
“I really don't. Anyway, why do you remember something so trivial, Big Sis?”\
“Because back then you were always following right behind me. You were so, so cute.”\
“...And now?”\
“Eh?”

I hadn't understood what she meant, so I asked her to repeat herself.

“Am I not cute now?”

In the limited field of vision afforded by the darkness, Ren's lips seemed to be slightly pursed. Seeing her like that filled me with affection.

“Whether it's now or back then, you're my adorable little sister, Ren.”

I said that and gently stroked her head. I thought her expression softened a little.

“Then that's fine.”

Silence settled between us again. Without really thinking about it, I continued stroking Ren's head. Somehow, it was me who felt calmer, my consciousness gradually melting away.

Just then, Ren murmured something.

“Big Sis, do you remember our promise?”

Promise.

At those words, I remembered the sensation of linking little fingers with Ren the other day.

“Of course. I'm really looking forward to tomorrow.”

My words grew soft and unsteady as they mingled with sleepiness. I was so drowsy that it felt as though I might stumble headlong into sleep.

“...That's right.”

Despite agreeing with me, Ren sounded a little lonely. Before I could think about what that meant, the edges of my consciousness dissolved away.
