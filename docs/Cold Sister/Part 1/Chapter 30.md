## Chapter 30: Kiss Purikura

\
We eventually found ourselves at an arcade, having wandered there without any particular destination in mind. A jumble of unfamiliar sounds assaulted our ears. Amidst it all, Ren pointed towards a machine beside the crane games.

“I want to try that.”

It was a purikura photo booth.

“Sounds good!”

I'd never taken purikura photos before, but I'd been a little curious about them, so I accepted Ren's invitation.

We put our money in together and passed through the fluttering curtain into the booth. A bubbly, high-pitched mechanical voice greeted us.

“Choose your mode!”

Looking at the screen in front of us, I saw several options.

Friends Mode. Couples Mode. Party Mode.

Ren selected Couples Mode without the slightest hesitation.

“Right, let's start taking pictures!”\
“First, give us both a big, cheerful smile!”

Following the instructions, I smiled at the camera. Ren seemed to have trouble smiling, the corners of her mouth twitching slightly. In the end, she wore her usual expressionless face, but her natural beauty alone was enough to make the picture look good.

After the shutter clicked, the next instruction came.

“Now, make a big heart together!”

The difficulty suddenly shot up. I raised both arms and bent my body, following the example picture. Ren struck the same pose. But our heights and arm lengths were so different that the shutter went off before we could manage to make a proper heart.

Before we had time to reflect on our failure, the next instruction arrived.

“Next, give each other a big, tight hug!”\
“Eh?”

I couldn't help voicing my bewilderment. Ignoring my confusion, Ren moved towards me, bent down slightly, and wrapped her arms around me. Then, without changing her cool expression, she looked straight at the camera.

I felt as though the temperature inside the booth had suddenly shot up. I followed Ren's example, doing my best despite feeling utterly overwhelmed, and looked towards the camera too. The countdown continued, and we were enveloped in a flash of white light.

I wondered if my face would look terribly red in the photograph, when another instruction came.

“Finally, the girlfriend gives her partner a kiss on the cheek!”

The atmosphere froze.

Both Ren and I were speechless. Meanwhile, the numbers on the monitor continued counting down relentlessly. The example picture displayed beside them showed a short girl kissing a tall boy on the cheek.

The decreasing numbers seemed to hurry me along, and, without really knowing what I was doing, I stretched up on my toes, following the example picture.

The shutter clicked, and we were enveloped in white light.

Ren's very red cheek touched my lips.

◇◇◇

Exhausted by the photo session, I watched as Ren decorated the pictures.

Her fingers flew at a terrifying speed, adding sparkling embellishments and lettering.

“Lovers' Anniversary”\
“Ren & Ai”\
“Together Forever”

Those words sparkled alongside countless hearts and stars. I secretly peered at Ren's face. As usual, she wore the same expressionless look, making it hard to believe she was the same person writing all those messages. Apparently, she became a little mischievous when it came to words.

Come to think of it, she'd written letters in an equally excitable style when we'd exchanged them as children.

Remembering that, and finding the contrast between her expression and the decorations in front of me amusing, I couldn't help quietly laughing.

When the purikura prints came out, we used the scissors provided to cut them in half and share them between us. I couldn't bring myself to look directly at the kissing picture, but the others were cheerful, and, sparkling decorations and all, they had turned out beautifully.

Ren held her share of the pictures carefully against her chest.
