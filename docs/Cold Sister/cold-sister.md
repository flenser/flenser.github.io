## Description

### The Ultimate Sisterly Yuri Romance

\
Award: Kakuyomu Contest 10 – Comic Adaptation Award

A sisterly yuri love story revolving around two sisters:

- Ai Kawai – A gentle, kind-hearted older sister.
- Ren Kawai – A cool, aloof younger sister who struggles to express her true feelings.

A love story woven between these two sisters.
