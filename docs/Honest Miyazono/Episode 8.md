The small camera tucked away in the corner had been recording a certain space since that morning.

It was a changing room, slightly more cramped than an ordinary classroom. Wood-grain lockers lined the walls, and a bench stood in the centre. The lights were off, leaving the room dimly lit by the light filtering through the frosted glass. The camera's angle was remarkably low, as though it had been placed near the floor, looking upwards. From the corner of the changing room, it captured the entire space in a wide-angle view, including the entrance.

At one point, around halfway through a lesson, the door to the changing room, which should never have been opened at that time, swung open.

Two female students entered, their expressions serious. After exchanging a few words, they began opening the lockers one after another. Having confirmed that there was nothing inside, the girl with dark brown, semi-long hair peered into the ventilation fan and electrical sockets, while the black-haired girl stretched up on tiptoe to inspect the tops of the lockers.

Two minutes later, the semi-long-haired girl pointed towards the camera with a puzzled expression and said something.

The black-haired girl immediately turned towards her voice, furrowing her brows. Together, they approached.

They began picking up the stuffed toys lying nearby, one after another, examining them in turn. Then the recorded image suddenly lurched violently.

The camera, now lifted from its hiding place, faced the two girls head-on.

The black-haired girl must have noticed something strange about the stuffed toy at once, because she turned it upside down. The words A gift from another school. Do not touch filled the camera's field of view. A few seconds later...

*

“Found it.”

*

Chiharu unzipped the back of the teddy bear's neck, reached inside, and pulled out the miniature camera that had been filming the changing room through the bear's eye.

The device was connected to a portable battery by a modified cable. It was an ungainly collection of components with a lens attached. The recording unit, memory, and battery were separate, connected only by exposed wires. It had clearly been designed to sit in a stable position, and the cables looked as though they might snap if pulled carelessly.

There was no mistaking it. This was a camera intended for voyeuristic photography.

Chiharu had never doubted Yukino, but she still hadn't expected the camera to be real. She stared at it in stunned silence, cold sweat beading on her skin. Yukino, who hadn't been entirely certain it would be there herself, turned pale.

“Is this... really a surveillance camera?”

“‘Voyeur camera’ would be more accurate. Yes, I'd say there's a very high chance that's what it is. The portable battery is connected and supplying power, which means someone must be replacing it regularly. And the warning that it's a gift and shouldn't be touched would discourage anyone who knew the story behind it from getting suspicious. We can't check the footage right now, but... the only person who'd deny what this is after seeing it would be the culprit.”

As Yukino grew deathly pale, Chiharu took a calm, deep breath.

“Chiharu-san, y-you're... calm...”\
“Do I look calm? There's a trick to it. Count prime numbers. One, two, three, four...”\
“You're not calm at all! You're not calm! Please, calm down!”\
“I'm joking, I'm joking. Well, I am a little shaken, but I'm all right.”

She closed her eyes and took another deep breath. This time, the trembling in Chiharu's hands subsided.

Seeing that, Yukino calmed down as well. She felt reassured by the knowledge that, with Chiharu beside her, everything would be all right.

“I wasn't doubting you, Yukino, but even so, I didn't think we'd actually find one.”\
“Neither did I... Do you think Shinohara-sensei planted it?”\
“That's certainly possible, but we can't be sure yet. We need to examine what's inside before we can say anything definitive.”\
“The footage... Could we use a computer if we went to the audiovisual room?”

Yukino murmured thoughtfully, pinching her chin. Chiharu stared at her with her mouth hanging open, looking thoroughly exasperated.

“D-did I say something strange?” Yukino asked nervously.

Chiharu gave a wry laugh, almost bursting out in disbelief, then tapped Yukino on the forehead with her fingertip.

“You try to shoulder absolutely everything by yourself. You've got far too used to being alone.”\
“Ugh!”

Yukino blushed and covered her forehead with both hands.

Chiharu smiled ruefully as she returned the camera to the stuffed toy, then looked at Yukino.

“Listen. From this point on, this is clearly beyond what students should be handling.”\
“Ah.”

Yukino let out a rather foolish-sounding exclamation, her expression equally blank.

She had grown too accustomed to dealing with problems she'd discovered on her own, like the incident involving Iida Riko's handkerchief. Now that Chiharu pointed it out, Yukino realised she had completely lost sight of where her responsibilities as a student ought to end.

“...I'm sorry. I hadn't even considered that.”\
“I thought so. You looked like you hadn't. But don't worry. I'm here.”

Chiharu gave her an impudent, confident grin. Yukino found her utterly reassuring, and her own cheeks relaxed into a smile.

“What are you going to do?”\
“If someone has been taking the camera home every day, dawdling around here today would give them time to realise we've found it and cover their tracks. We need to act quickly.”

Yukino's heart began pounding for a different reason this time. Chiharu, however, continued calmly.

“First, I want to stop anyone else becoming a victim. We should close the changing room. Yukino, sorry, but could you lock the door from the inside for a while and make sure nobody comes in? Meanwhile, I'll fetch the teachers responsible for the changing room, preferably a female teacher, and the principal before the next lesson starts.”

As Yukino nodded thoughtfully, Chiharu pinched her chin and glanced at her.

“Sorry, Yukino. I think there's a very high chance Shinohara is the culprit, but...”\
“...You're worried that whoever you bring back might be the culprit, and I would have to listen to their voice, right?”\
“That's it. It might be painful. Can you manage?”\
“If it's someone whose voice I've heard a reasonable number of times, it shouldn't be a problem. Besides, it's necessary.”

Yukino hated hearing lies. But, strangely, when she used her ability for something constructive, the suffering came with less revulsion.

More than anything, she was happy that Chiharu was relying on her. She readily agreed with a smile.

Chiharu looked at her with an expression of concern, then took her hand and met her eyes as she said, “Thank you.” It was as though she wanted to ease Yukino's suffering, even if only a little.

Yukino savoured the gentle warmth pulsing through their joined hands.

“It's nothing,” she replied with a smile.\
“Once we know we can trust those two, the rest should be straightforward. Judging by how this camera is built, recording probably started as soon as it was planted. If we handle things properly, we should be able to identify whoever installed it. We can call the police and have them examine the footage with us. That should take care of things. Ideally, we'd catch the culprit in the act of retrieving the camera, but I'll leave that decision, along with everything else, to the teachers. ...That's roughly how I'd plan it.”

*

Things rarely go as one imagines they will.

At least, that was what Yukino believed, and it was how things had always been for her.

But differences in ability, shaped by one's life experience, could make a tremendous difference. If Yukino had been on her own, she would probably have run into difficulties somewhere along the way. With Chiharu handling things so smoothly, however, everything proceeded without a hitch.

“Th-this... It really does look like a camera. I can't believe it.”\
“How could something like this be here...? H-how long has it been there?”

The two people they had summoned, the middle-aged, plump principal and a tall, sturdily built female PE teacher, turned pale as they examined the camera inside the stuffed toy.

Chiharu glanced at Yukino. Yukino had been listening closely to what they said, but she sensed no taste of lies. Instead, she detected a bitterness that conveyed their intense disgust. She nodded to Chiharu with a trusting look.

She hesitated over whether she should nod or shake her head, but decided the nuance of her expression would get the message across.

Chiharu nodded once, then gestured for Yukino to cover her ears.

She was about to lie.

Yukino didn't think that was unfortunate. She hated lies, but she didn't believe they were evil.

Even a hamburger patty wouldn't cook properly without a little binding ingredient. Sometimes, covering your ears was the sensible thing to do.

“[When Hasekura-san used the changing room some time ago, she apparently heard a strange noise coming from inside. Ever since then, she couldn't shake the unpleasant suspicion that something was wrong.] During class, she started feeling sick, so I went with her to find out what was going on. That's how we discovered this.”

Chiharu's explanation would fit the accounts from the people in the classroom as well as the footage from the camera.

She gestured for Yukino to uncover her ears, then turned to the principal.

“I'm sorry we left class, but...”

Of course, aftercare was part of a professional's repertoire. Chiharu asked whether their absences could be recorded as attendance.

“Oh, of course. I'll mark you both as present. But what on earth are we going to do about this...?”

Having made that promise, the principal folded his arms and looked troubled. Behind him, Chiharu proudly gave Yukino a thumbs-up. Yukino could only return a wry smile, as though to say, You didn't have to do that.

“In any case, we should notify the police. This is exactly the kind of thing professionals should handle.”

The female PE teacher suggested the quickest solution without attempting to cover anything up. The principal murmured, “...Yes, I suppose you're right...” and seemed to consider whether there might be some way to quietly smooth things over. Eventually, he gave up on that idea as impractical.

*

Chiharu's ideal outcome had been for police officers to lie in wait and arrest the culprit red-handed when they came to retrieve the camera.

But she had apparently decided that suggesting such a thing would be overstepping her position as a student.

Yukino, for her part, wanted to take responsibility rather than leave everything to others. At the same time, she recognised that the best course of action was probably not to meddle any further. She watched and listened for lies, leaving the rest to the adults.

Three hours after they had reported the camera, the principal summoned the two girls to his office over the school intercom. They had stayed behind, chatting in the library.

Since they were the ones who had reported the discovery, the principal had promised to share the minimum amount of information necessary to put their minds at ease.

“To get straight to the point, the footage showed the face of the person who installed it.”

Sitting on the luxurious sofa in the principal's office, he immediately patted his receding hairline with a troubled expression.

The first words out of his mouth dispelled the anxious thoughts that had been weighing on the two girls. They exchanged bright smiles.

“We've already asked the suspect to accompany us voluntarily for questioning. An arrest should follow before long, and you'll receive an official report at a later date. Please keep this confidential until then. You've both worked hard staying here so late. And thank you for finding it.”

There wasn't a single lie in his words, so events must really have been progressing just as he described.

Chiharu, who seemed to have had a similar concern, glanced at Yukino. Yukino returned a deliberately gentle smile. That appeared to reassure her completely.

“Thank goodness,” Chiharu murmured.

They still didn't know who the culprit was. It might not even have been Shinohara.

But their goal hadn't been to uncover the truth for its own sake. It had been to prevent further harm.

If the culprit had been found and the offending behaviour stopped, they didn't need anything more.

“You must both be exhausted by all this. We'll need to arrange for the school counsellor to come in at a later date, but would you like to take a few days off school, with your absences officially excused, to give you some time to recover psychologically?”

*

“Are you sure you don't want to take the time off?”

They were on their way home. It was five in the afternoon, and the sky had turned a deep orange as they walked together.

They followed the pavement alongside a guardrail, gazing at the orange-tinted residential streets and stepping through the long shadows cast by the trees. White clouds stretched across the sky, glowing as though they held orange stars. It was beautiful.

Then a pair of eyes, just as beautiful, turned towards Yukino, catching the reddish light.

“Mm... Is that because... you think it would be hard on me, given my condition?”\
“That's right. Your classmates might ask you all sorts of questions at school tomorrow. And with your condition, wouldn't it be easier to avoid going to school as much as possible? Besides, given how the camera was discovered, the teachers probably think your health deteriorated because of the distress caused by the voyeuristic photography. It seems like a perfectly reasonable offer to me.”\
“Ahaha... Well, I can't deny that. But... without the social lubricant of lies, I think I'll always create friction in my relationships with people, and end up living alone.”

Yukino gave a slightly self-deprecating laugh. Chiharu glared at her in displeasure.

Accepting that stern but gentle gaze, Yukino opened up a little more.

“So I thought I'd at least increase the things I can do for myself. I've already started saving a little of my part-time wages, and I want to take as many lessons seriously as I can, especially the ones that might be useful in the future.”

Yukino wasn't as academically gifted as Chiharu, but whether she was good at something or not wasn't going to determine whether she made the effort. She wanted to work hard so that she could survive on her own.

Now it was Chiharu's turn to purse her lips, not out of habit or uncertainty, but to show her displeasure.

“Hmm...? Alone, you say. Even though I've told you I'll make you happy as your friend?”

Chiharu sounded almost frustrated, and Yukino cast her gaze into empty space.

“I'm happy about that, you know? But relying on that and living off it would be unhealthy, wouldn't it?”\
“That may be true, but...”\
“I still don't think I can be happy. But I don't want to give up on living, either. So I thought I'd keep doing my best, even if I have to eat dirt to get by...”

Having said that, Yukino lowered her head a little, suddenly embarrassed.

She rarely paid attention to her appearance, yet for some reason she fiddled with her fringe and confessed honestly.

“...But lately, because you're here, Chiharu-san, I enjoy going to school a little more.”

Chiharu's eyes widened in surprise.

Yukino's cheeks flushed just enough that she could blame it on the early summer warmth.

“I want to go to the school where you are, tomorrow, too. That's eighty per cent of the reason. Is that all right?”

For Yukino, attending school had always felt like an obligation, even if it wasn't compulsory education for her. Even if she were offered officially excused absences, she believed she ought to go for the sake of her future.

In other words, she had no particular reason to want to go. She went because she felt she should.

But lately, school had become a little more enjoyable. And the reason was the girl standing right in front of her.

Yukino looked up at Chiharu hesitantly. Chiharu responded with a rather curt, “I see,” and looked away. But even she seemed embarrassed. Her lips moved as she brushed a hand through her hair, then she murmured:

“Then... shall I make you a bento again tomorrow?”

Yukino's heart leapt.

She fought to keep her cheeks from relaxing into a grin and narrowed her eyes.

“I'd be happy if you did, but... wouldn't that make it seem as though I'm only interested in the food?”\
“I don't mind. You're a little too thin, anyway.”\
“But I do mind. I can't possibly let you go to that much trouble for me...”\
“Then give me the money you'd spend buying those savoury bread rolls. I'll use that amount to cover the ingredients.”

It was an appealing offer. Yukino's face brightened, but then she reconsidered and groaned.

“B-but what about the labour?”\
“Honestly, you're so stubborn. Fine, then. On days when I make you a bento, give me a shoulder massage after you eat.”

Chiharu shook her head in exasperation, took out her phone, and began tapping away at something.

A few seconds later, Yukino's phone vibrated in response. She took it out and saw a notification from the messaging app. Chiharu had sent her a friend request.

Now that she thought about it, she belonged to the same group chat as Chiharu, but they'd never exchanged contact details directly.

Yukino gazed at the request, savouring it as proof that she wasn't merely one among a crowd, but a special friend. Then she smiled and accepted it.

“I-I'll do my very best.”\
“Good. See that you do.”

Chiharu teased her with a playful expression as Yukino responded with such determination.

After walking a few more steps, Chiharu folded her arms and changed the subject.

“Still... being able to tell when someone is lying. If you could switch it on and off, it'd be pretty useful.”\
“Indeed. If only it were that easy, my life would be so much simpler...”\
“By the way, would you mind if I asked you more about it? I'm getting curious.”\
“N-not at all! You've done so much for me, Chiharu-san. Ask me anything, I'm ready!”\
“Anything?”\
“Fists are the one thing that are don't-come-near territory. Please stop clenching yours.”

Having delivered that perfectly timed retort, Yukino watched Chiharu unclench her fist and abandon the joke.

Yukino had only recently realised that Chiharu was rather fond of making jokes.

Now Chiharu rubbed her chin thoughtfully, glancing at Yukino as they walked.

“So it's not that you know what the lie is about, exactly. You can tell that the words are lies, right?”\
“That's right. More precisely, it's that I can taste emotions. When I was little, the doctors just labelled it ‘synaesthesia’, but after living with it for over ten years, I think it's a combination of an ear that can detect subtle changes in someone's voice caused by their emotions, and a condition that connects those changes to my sense of taste.”\
“Hmm.”

Chiharu murmured thoughtfully, her eyes lowered. Then she looked at Yukino, curiosity glimmering in her gaze.

“Can I ask you something? Do you hate all lies? Do you not want to hear even a single one?”\
“Oh, no. If it's the kind of lie where I don't mind if someone finds out, it doesn't bother me much.”\
“Would it be all right if I tried something, partly as an experiment?”

One of Chiharu's good qualities was that she always made sure to ask permission. Yukino smiled and nodded.

“Go ahead.”\
“This morning, I ate rice. I ate bread. I ate udon. I ate pasta. I ate soba.”\
“Did you have tempura with your udon?”\
“...I got up at four today. I got up at five. I got up at six. I got up at seven.”\
“Four o'clock is early. Was making the bento difficult after all?”

Since Chiharu's intention was simply to test Yukino's ability, there was hardly any emotion mixed in beyond the lies themselves. That actually made them easier to detect.

Yukino calmly identified the lies, and Chiharu stared at her with her mouth hanging open in astonishment.

“Wow... That's practically a superpower. At this point, it really is. Sorry for testing you. Was it unpleasant?”\
“No. Even when we call something a lie, what I actually taste is the emotion of wanting to hide something. A little lie like that is about as unpleasant as having a teaspoon of sewage placed on my tongue.”\
“...Oh. I'm really sorry. Are you sure you're all right?”

Apparently, having sewage placed on one's tongue was generally considered an extremely unpleasant sensation.

Realising how differently they perceived things, Yukino hurriedly reassured Chiharu that it was nothing.

“I-I'm fine! Something playful like that is really, really mild!”\
“I'm almost afraid to ask, but... well, only if you're comfortable talking about it. What does a bad one taste like?”\
“A lie someone is genuinely trying to conceal... Let's see. The worst one I've ever experienced was like mixing vinegar and vomit in equal parts, dissolving potato starch in it and heating it over a flame, then topping it with the raw, green smell of uncooked cabbage and peppers, and sprinkling chilli powder and sugar over the whole thing...”

Back in primary school, a teacher had tried to cover up a serious mistake of their own by blaming it on a student. As they kicked the teacher's desk and shouted angrily, that was the taste of their words. Immediately afterwards, Yukino had vomited all over her desk.

A thoroughly humiliating memory.

At the horrifying description of Yukino's experience, Chiharu clutched her own arms where they emerged from her blouse and shrieked.

“Of course you'd throw up! Of course you would! Ahhh! That's impossible! Just hearing about it is unbearable!”

“Eeeek!” Chiharu cried, rubbing her arms as goosebumps rose on her skin, while Yukino laughed.

“I hate remembering it, too. But honestly, it's easier when people laugh.”

Rather than being treated as though she were made of glass, or having someone thoughtlessly trample over her feelings, it helped when people understood her to a reasonable degree and then reacted honestly to how awful her suffering was.

It reassured her that her suffering wasn't unreasonable. That she was allowed to find it painful.

At least, that's how it felt to Yukino now.

“Chiharu-san.”

Yukino called to her, and Chiharu tilted her head.

“Hmm?”

Yukino felt a little embarrassed and stumbled over her words. After a few seconds of silence, she smiled and spoke.

“...Thank you for pulling me out of there.”

The sun was setting a little later than usual on their walk home that day.

And yet everything seemed much brighter than usual.

Because the sun was walking right beside her.

Chiharu received Yukino's heartfelt words with a faint smile, her eyes turned away. Yukino began to worry that she'd said something wrong, something Chiharu didn't like. But Chiharu closed her eyes and spoke.

“Yukino.”\
“Y-yes?”

Yukino answered nervously. Chiharu turned an absorbing gaze towards her.

“...Thank you for working so hard for everyone's sake, even when it hurts.”

Yukino stared at her in astonishment, her mouth slightly open. She hadn't expected that answer at all.

Chiharu hadn't said, “You're welcome.” She had said, “Thank you.”

Yukino couldn't know exactly what Chiharu meant. Perhaps she wanted to acknowledge that Chiharu's own actions had been made possible by Yukino's goodwill. Or perhaps she thought that if Chiharu deserved praise, then Yukino did, too.\
But one thing was certain. Even if Yukino's actions had seemed obvious to her, even if she'd endured a taste so foul it made her vomit, she'd still stood up because she couldn't ignore the possibility that the voyeuristic photography was real.\
And being praised for something she had considered no more than an ordinary, necessary act made her happy.

Yukino's grateful smile transformed into a bashful one, unable to conceal her delight.

“You're welcome.”
