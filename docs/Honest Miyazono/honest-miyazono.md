## Original Title

幸せになれない私と正直者の宮園さん

## Description

### Can a girl who can tell when people are lying ever be happy?

Hasekura Yukino, a second-year high school student, has a constitution where other people’s emotions come through to her as taste. Anger is spicy, sadness is sour, and disgust is strongly bitter. Lies are complex, unpleasant and foul, like vomit. Along with that dreadful taste, she sees through every “lie” people can’t help spilling out to keep communication running smoothly. This is the story of a girl who probably can’t be happy.
