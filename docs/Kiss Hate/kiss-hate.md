## Description

### A slow-burn office romance that starts with a kiss, with the calculating junior coworker I hate most at work.

**A sometimes-perfect-prince, sometimes-hopeless senior x her cocky, boy-crazy junior.**

Asami Nagi is a top-tier sales rep, sharp and efficient at everything she does. But there's exactly one person at the office she can't handle: the new hire, Hanamori Chisa.\
A honeyed voice. Upturned eyes. A "calculated cuteness, full-on flirtatious" mode that only ever activates in front of the male staff.\
And yet toward Nagi, her female senior, she's all blank stares, icy responses, and not a flicker of life.\
That gap infuriates her. And infuriatingly, she can't stop thinking about it.

Then one night, Nagi happens to find Hanamori dead drunk, and can't just leave her there. She takes her home.

And that's when it happens.

Bewildered by how much she's noticing a woman she's supposed to hate, Nagi finds herself running into her more and more at work as things develop.

A slow-burn, heart-fluttering office comedy about a romance that starts from hatred.

*Note: given the nature of the story, a fair number of men appear in it. My apologies to readers who prefer their yuri without male characters.*
