## Episode 28: Orange

\
Without a moment’s delay, [Strawberry Pink] activated the forced-awakening programme.\
She had learnt her lesson from her battle with the [Witch of Pisces].

Magical girls dreamed of the Witches they killed.\
She never wanted to see something like that again.

And yet...

*

“An offensive firewall of my own design. Don’t imagine you can counter it with the hacking techniques of some [Red] working for the government.”

An overflow of black light, black M-particles. The *dream* swallowed [Strawberry Pink].

＊

She had always loved tinkering with machines.

“From [Strawberry Pink] to [Chihuahua Yellow]. I’ve been had.”

*

A room full of mechanical junk.\
A room belonging only to herself and her *friends*.\
A room where she repaired old games she’d picked up, so everyone could play them.

“I’m temporarily withdrawing from the front line.”\
“Hey! [Orange]!”

Her *friends* had come to visit again today.

“Don’t pursue them too far. They’ve installed something in their own hearts, their Magus Engines.”\
“Let’s play Street Fighter 8! I’ve practised a whooole lot!”

Sure, [Blue].

“They’re *crazy*.”

Hang on, where were the [Green]s?\
If they were going to play games, they’d have to invite those girls too, or they’d get in a mood...

“They’re fighting, apparently.”

Oh, dear.

“Oh, dear.”\
“...Tampering with the source of M-particle generation, their own hearts... They’re not even adults yet.”

Well, the [Green]s were always fighting. That was nothing new.

“... [Orange], [Blue].”\
“Oh, [Brown]!”\
“It wouldn’t be surprising if you blew yourself to pieces, you... [Witch]...!”

How unusual. It wasn’t every day that [Brown] came to her room.

“Lend me a game console.”\
“What!? The [Brown] wants to play games!?”\
“Stop it.”

It would snow tomorrow.

“The [Green]s’ fight is getting serious. To bring the situation under control, I hereby declare a gaming tournament!”\
“Woohoo!”\
“What a load of useless information...”

[Brown] was clever.

“You’re just all idiots.”\
“Your [Emotions], your [Thought]...!”

Even as she said that, [Brown] looked rather pleased with herself.\
[Blue] was carrying as many game consoles as she could hold in her arms.\
Behind her, [Orange] hurried after them, laden with cables.

*

“Don’t pour it into me...!”

*

The [Green]s broke.\
[Brown] died protecting her.\
[Blue] was dead, Sere-chan had told her.

*

“...There’s no way I don’t know what you’re suffering through.”

*

The rain striking her skin brought [Strawberry Pink] back to reality.\
The heart she had stabbed with her magical cyberdeck staff... The [Witch of Cancer] had apparently escaped somewhere.

The accumulated logs invaded her brain.

“From [Chihuahua Yellow] to Senior [Strawberry Pink]. I failed.”\
“From [Chihuahua Yellow] to Senior [Strawberry Pink]. Please respond.”\
“From [Chihuahua Yellow] to Senior [Strawberry Pink]. Run! Please, run!”

Suppressing her headache, [Strawberry Pink] glared up at the rain-darkened sky.

“[Chihuahua Yellow] to Senior [Strawberry Pink]. The [Witch of Gemini] has been completely revived!”

*

The [Witch] spread her wings.\
Grotesque wings made of iron blades.\
A jet-black mask. A black dress. Two torsos.

*

Two Magus Engines, both beating with light.
