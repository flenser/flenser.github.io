## Original Title

厭世の魔法人形屋～隠居希望の最強魔女、ゆっくり暮らしたいだけなのに、いつの間にか英雄になってました～

## Synopsis

### The magic doll shop's job is to bring back the dead — for one day only.

In the royal capital stands the magic doll shop, "Lux."

It's a shop run by Fiine Fellner, a genius witch.
The magic doll shop's job is this: to bring back the dead — for one day only.

People carrying all kinds of circumstances come to Lux, asking, "Please, let me see the dead again, just for one day."

"...I believe there are people who can find salvation by having a second chance at saying goodbye."

This is the story of a witch carrying her own trauma, offering others a chance to redo their farewells.

And—

it's the story of her finding her own way to face forward again.
