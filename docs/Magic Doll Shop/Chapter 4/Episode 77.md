## Episode 77: The Invitation

\
The knock came not at the shop's front door, but at the back door that led only to the living quarters upstairs.\
Very few people used that door.\
So Fiine, tense, opened it slowly.

"W-welcome, Mr. Burkhardt..."\
"Yes. It's been a while... Though not really."

With a slightly awkward smile, he handed Fiine a large basket.\
Inside was a great quantity of fruit: apples, strawberries, grapes, mandarins, and more.

"A gift."\
"A gift, you mean?"\
"Yes. ...When I told my subordinates I was going to my daughter's house, they told me to at least bring some fruit."

The moment he said "daughter," Burkhardt's face went so stiff that Fiine couldn't help laughing.\
Seeing her, Burkhardt sighed.

"Sorry. Anyway, let me get us something to drink."\
"I'll do that, so Fiine, you sit and relax!!"

Yuria popped her head out and shouted.\
She still remembered the time Fiine had made an outrageously awful pot of tea.

Taking Yuria up on it, she and Burkhardt moved to the sitting room.\
Having him look around the house so intently made it hard to feel settled.

"Um... how have things been since then? Have the higher-ups said anything?"

After defeating the black dragon, Fiine and Yuria had returned to the royal capital without delay.\
Burkhardt had come back to the capital the very next day, too, but he'd had a lot to report to the higher-ups, including the fact that some kind of drug had been administered to the black dragon, and had been busy.

"Lately, incidents of monsters attacking people have been happening more and more. If they've been deliberately engineered, it's a serious problem. Discussions are continuing among the higher-ups about treating it as a matter the whole country should investigate."\
"I see..."\
"Yes. For now, things are moving in the direction of investigating and countering monsters more actively than before. Checking whether there are other monsters that have been given drugs, as in this case, is the top priority."\
"...I see..."

At present, partly because of a shortage of personnel, the Magic Corps' policy had been to respond after a problem occurred.\
But if it stayed that way, a major incident might eventually happen.\
If there was a possibility that monsters were being made to attack people on purpose, they would need to act quickly.

"His Majesty is quite concerned about it too, and sometimes takes part in the discussions himself. I had a chance to meet him the other day, and... he asked me to give you this."

Burkhardt took a letter from inside his coat. It was a plain white envelope, but the wax seal bore an emblem she recognised.

(But surely, I must be mistaken...?)

With trembling hands she took the letter and checked the emblem again.\
However many times she blinked, it didn't change.

"Th-this, the, the royal..."\
"Yes."\
"W-why me!?"

The only person in the country who could use this emblem was the king alone.\
In other words, this was a letter from His Majesty the King.

"I wrote about you in the report on this mission, too. I can't lie. On top of that, word of your exploits in Mond has already reached the capital. And apparently the 'magic doll shop' had already been a minor subject of gossip among the nobles for some time."\
"E-even so, why would His Majesty himself...!?"\
"You might not know this, but the current king is keen on recruiting talented people. He maintains that positions should be given according to ability, not social rank. Because of that, he doesn't get along well with the old guard..."

Burkhardt sighed.\
As a general of the Magic Corps, he couldn't stay clear of the world of politics either. He must have had a lot of trouble.

(Come to think of it, two years ago the Crown Prince took the throne and became the new king...)

She didn't know much about it, but she'd heard that the first and second princes, who were the same age, had split into two factions and fought each other.\
In the end, the one chosen as heir and enthroned had been the second prince.

"Go ahead and open it. I don't know what's inside, either."\
"Y-yes."

Fetching a paper knife seemed like too much bother, so she carefully opened the seal with wind magic.\
Inside was a short letter and an invitation.

> *Fiine Fellner*\
> *I have something to discuss. Attend the next ball.*\
> *Alexander von Alfheim*

(Th-this is, no doubt about it, an invitation to a party, isn't it...!?)

She looked up fearfully and met the eyes of Burkhardt, who had been peering at the letter.

"Um, Mr. Burkhardt, this...!"\
"...I see. Wanting to speak in person is just like His Majesty."\
"C-can I say no...?"\
"Of course not. You wouldn't lose your life for it, perhaps, but he might well revoke the magic doll shop's operating licence on the spot."\
"R-right..."

In the first place, Fiine was legally Burkhardt's adopted child, which made her a noble.\
Nobles were a class that served the king and couldn't disobey the king's commands.\
If Fiine stubbornly turned down the invitation, it would cause trouble for Burkhardt.

(I can't say no. I know, I know that, but...)

She hated attention, and she would certainly get attention.\
In fact, getting attention was exactly what had led to this. And on top of that, she'd never liked parties to begin with.

"...Something to discuss, he says..."\
"Eight or nine times out of ten, they'll demand that you join the Magic Corps."

Burkhardt put a hand to his forehead and sighed deeply.

"...Fiine."\
"Yes..."\
"I'll be at the venue too. ...There's nothing I'll be able to do, though."\
"...Yes."

What on earth was she going to be told?

(I don't want to go...!!)

Fiine's wish didn't reach the heavens.\
A week later, she would be heading for the castle where the party was being held.
