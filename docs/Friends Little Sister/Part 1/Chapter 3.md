## Chapter 3 – If She Finds Out, It's All Over...!

\
Ever since that day, I'd been doing my best to carry on with my life as normally as possible.

Marika and I still laughed about stupid things, traded ridiculous jokes, made snarky comments at each other, got smacked for it, laughed some more, and then went right back to making snarky comments.

I made sure everything looked exactly the same as before.

But the truth was, I was constantly on edge. I had to be careful not to let anything slip, whether it was the slightest change in my expression, an awkward wandering of my eyes, or even a moment's hesitation.

Above all, I couldn't let Marika find out that I'd fallen in love with Manami-chan. Absolutely not.

It wasn't just that I was afraid Marika might dislike me or be creeped out by my feelings. More than anything, I didn't want to see her looking hurt. She was a best friend who trusted me wholeheartedly, and admitting my feelings felt almost like betraying that trust.

So whenever Marika asked, "Wanna come over to my place today?", I'd turn around and invite her to mine instead.

Because I was afraid of seeing Manami-chan.

I had a feeling that if I saw her again, all those feelings from that day would come rushing back. No, worse than that... What if they'd grown even stronger? I wasn't sure I'd be able to keep myself together.

That's how deeply Manami-chan had taken root inside me.

Then, one day, after the afternoon bell rang, I was packing up my things as usual. Marika was absent today, so I'd be going home alone. Apparently, she wasn't feeling well, which did worry me a little.

I stuffed my notebooks and textbooks into my bag and zipped up my pencil case. Just as I was wondering whether I should stop somewhere on the way home today...

"Hey, Miyanomori. Could you come here for a moment?"

"...Yes?"

For a moment, I panicked, wondering if I'd done something wrong. But when I went over to the teacher, he held out a sheet of paper.

"This was handed out this morning, but Ayase was absent today because she wasn't feeling well, right? It's an important document that needs to be delivered to her family straight away. Could you take it to her?"

"Um, couldn't you...?"

"I'd love to do it myself, believe me. But I've got a mountain of work to get through, and I've got a meeting coming up now. And naturally, it has to be done before the end of the working day. Honestly, I'd rather not have to work overtime."

The teacher shrugged apologetically as he spoke.

"...Riiight. I seeee."

"You're good friends with Ayase, aren't you? And you live nearby, too. You're about the only person I can ask."

"W-Well, I suppose we are good friends..."

Right. There wouldn't be any problem with simply going to Marika's house. I could call out, "Get well soon!" through her bedroom door, hand over the document, and come straight home. Easy.

The problem was that I was in love with Manami-chan.

What if she came home while I was there? Worse, what if she answered the door herself and I ended up face-to-face with her?

That alone would send my heart spiralling out of control all over again. My rational mind wouldn't stand a chance. I suppose that's what falling in love does to you.

...But if I was only delivering a document, I could just ring the doorbell and say I'd leave it in the letterbox. Yeah, that would work. I wouldn't even have to open the front door. I could send Marika a get-well message afterwards.

"...All right. I'll deliver it."

The teacher smiled with relief at my answer and handed me the paper.

"Thank you! That's a huge help! I really appreciate it. I'll leave it to you!"

I watched him hurry back towards the staff room, and felt a thin sheen of sweat forming on the hand holding the document.

"Guess I've got no choice..."

I opened my bag slightly and carefully slipped the paper inside so it wouldn't get creased. The sound of the zip closing seemed strangely loud, lingering in my ears.

After taking a deep breath, I finally left the classroom.

Normally, the walk to Marika's house was nothing special. We'd walk side by side, chatting about whatever trendy snacks we'd discovered or laughing as we imitated videos we'd seen online. It was always such an easy, enjoyable time.

But today was different.

Every time I turned a corner, my heart gave a nervous thump. The scenery was familiar, but somehow it seemed to have changed colour. It was probably just my nerves playing tricks on me.

"Haah... I wonder how Marika's doing."

The words escaped my lips in a quiet murmur, almost fading into the sky.

Manami-chan had been occupying practically every corner of my mind, but the fact that Marika was unwell was still weighing on me.

She'd seemed perfectly fine yesterday. At lunchtime, she'd even helped herself to the tamagoyaki from my lunchbox and complained that it wasn't salty enough!

*(I know I should properly visit her and see how she's doing...)*

But what if Manami-chan was there? What if my heart was stolen all over again, just like last time, and I completely lost my composure?

And what if Marika noticed...?

If she found out I was in love with her own little sister, there was a chance our friendship would fall apart.

*(...If that happened, Marika and I would never be able to...)*

I didn't even want to imagine such a horrible outcome. That's why I'd been doing everything I could to avoid seeing Manami-chan, desperately trying to keep my feelings hidden.

And yet, here I was, standing right in front of the very house where that worst-case scenario might come true.

I looked up. Marika's house stood before me, looking exactly as it had that day. The windows were shut, the curtains drawn, and there was no obvious sign of anyone being home.

But that didn't mean Manami-chan wasn't there. She could be watching television in the living room, or having a cup of tea in the kitchen.

"Okay... I'll ring the doorbell, and whoever answers, I'll just say I'm leaving it in the letterbox and go..."

I took a deep breath in front of the doorbell. My finger was trembling more than I'd expected.

As though trying to hide that tremor, I clenched my hand into a fist and gave the doorbell button a little fist bump.

The moment I did, my heart gave a tremendous thump.

That girl might be inside this house.

That smile might appear right in front of me again.

I might hear her voice again.

I wanted to run away. And yet, somewhere deep down, there was also a tiny part of me that was actually looking forward to seeing her.

Those contradictory feelings kept welling up inside my chest.

*(...Oh no. What am I going to do?)*

Five seconds...

Ten seconds...

Twenty seconds...

I strained my ears, but all I could hear was the sound of my own heartbeat, absurdly loud. Thump, thump, thump. My heart was practically shouting. The air outside was a little chilly, and everything around me was quiet.

Then thirty seconds passed.

Still, there was no response. No answer, not even the slightest sound from inside.

...I suppose Marika was asleep, and there really wasn't anyone else home.

The moment I thought that, all the tension drained out of my body.

"—Haaah... Thank goodness..."

I let out a long, deep breath, as though I were emptying every last bit of air I'd been holding in my lungs.

My shoulders rose and fell as I breathed, and even I was surprised by how relieved I felt. Calling it a sense of relief hardly did it justice. It was more like... *I'd been saved!* That was how liberating it felt.

I wouldn't have to face anyone. I wouldn't have to worry about running into that girl... Manami-chan.

*(Right... Good. That's that, then.)*

I quietly took the document out of my bag and carefully slipped it into the letterbox. Once I'd watched it disappear inside, I pulled my phone out of my pocket.

"Let's see... 'I left an urgent-looking document in your letterbox. Get well soon!' There."

I tapped away at the screen and typed out a quick message. All that remained was to send it.

And with that, today's mission would be complete.

Or so I thought...

"—Um, did you need something?"

The moment I heard that familiar voice right beside me, I genuinely thought my heart was about to leap straight out of my chest.
