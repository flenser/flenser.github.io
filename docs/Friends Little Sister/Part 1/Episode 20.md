## Episode 20: God, Buddha, and Lady Marikaaaaaaaaa!!

\
The following afternoon, I casually picked up my phone. The moment I saw the notification glowing on its screen, I felt as though my heart had leapt right out of my chest.

*“Manami says she's fine with going with you, Yuu. Sorry to dump this on you, but I'm leaving her in your hands until I get back that evening.”*

――Huh?

The instant my brain fully processed the message, I let out a strange little squeak.

“Eep!”

Still clutching my phone in both hands, my knees suddenly gave way and I sank to the floor.

“W-w-w-wha...!?”

The words escaped me like the incoherent muttering of someone delirious.\
My body was getting hot, my heart felt ready to explode, and yet some part of me still couldn't believe it. But this was definitely happening. It was real.

――God.\
――Buddha.\
――Lady Marikaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa!!!

I pressed my phone against my chest as though I were worshipping it, then promptly collapsed onto the floor.

The ceiling of my bedroom was all I could see, but my vision was dazzling, blurred with tears. The whole world seemed to sparkle.

This wasn't a dream. I'd been entrusted with something. Time alone with Manami-chan.

I suddenly noticed another message from Marika, sent immediately afterwards. It contained the meeting place and time. Seeing everything laid out so precisely made it feel even more real, and my heart gave another enormous thump.

“...All right!!”

I sprang to my feet and rushed straight over to my desk, opening my computer and starting to search.

Information about the fireworks festival, how crowded it usually got, the locations of the food stalls, the best viewing spots and hidden-away spots, routes for avoiding the crowds, even when to take breaks for refreshments.\
I concentrated so intensely that you would have thought I was preparing for university entrance exams, committing every last detail about the festival to memory.

Because this was...

“...A date.”

I whispered the words to myself, and warmth spread gently through my chest.

Of course, as far as Manami-chan was concerned, she might simply think she was going to the festival with “Big Sister's friend”. It was probably too soon to call it a date.

But, but...

I was serious about this.

It would be our first special outing together this summer. We might get to stand side by side and look up at the fireworks blooming in the night sky. We might laugh together, gasp in surprise together, her yukata swaying in the breeze. Perhaps, when we got home, we might even play with sparklers.

A dreamlike evening like that was now on the verge of becoming reality.

There was no way I couldn't be excited. I straightened my back. As the older one, I had to be someone she could rely on. I had to keep calm, avoid getting flustered, and escort her properly. That was my responsibility as a “reliable older girl”!

◇

And then, at long last, the day of the fireworks festival arrived.

The weather had been clear since morning. The humidity was a little high, but the forecast said a cool breeze would probably blow in by evening.

As soon as I woke up, I checked the sky. Seeing the clear weather, I gave a small triumphant fist pump towards the heavens.

*(Thank you, sky...! I'll make sure today is absolutely wonderful...!)*

With that determination tucked away in my heart, I got ready, carefully calculating how much time I needed before leaving home. I'd already rehearsed what to wear over and over, and the previous evening I'd laid out and prepared absolutely everything.

A neat, feminine blouse and a long skirt that showed a little ankle. I'd lightly tied my hair back to keep cool, and applied just one spritz of perfume to my wrists so I wouldn't overdo it.

*(Yes, this should be fine... Probably...!)*

I checked my appearance in the mirror repeatedly, turning this way and that, and finally left home in the late afternoon.

The journey to the meeting place felt so long that I wondered if it would ever end. Gradually, I began seeing more and more people dressed in yukata, and my heartbeat grew even faster.

*(I wonder what Manami-chan will be wearing... Probably ordinary clothes. Unless... could it possibly be a yukata? No, no, surely not... I shouldn't get my hopes up. Manami-chan is still in middle school, and she doesn't seem like the sort of person who's particularly used to dressing up for things like this...)*

I went round and round those thoughts until I finally arrived near the meeting place. The whole area was already filled with the excitement of a festival about to begin.

I arrived a little before our appointed time, but there was no way I could sit still. I checked my phone, fixed my hair, straightened my posture, and repeatedly checked the contents of my bag for absolutely no reason.

But no matter how much I tried to distract myself, my heart just kept pounding away.

After a little while...

“Sorry to keep you waiting, Yuu-san.”

The moment those words drifted gently towards me from behind, the sounds of my world seemed to fall silent.

I turned around instinctively.

And there, standing before me, was Manami-chan, dressed in a pale wisteria-coloured yukata.

“...!”

For a moment, I stopped breathing. No, I'm pretty sure I actually stopped for several seconds. I forgot how to draw air into my lungs and could do nothing but stare at her.

Her pale wisteria yukata was decorated with delicate little white and light-blue flowers, giving her an impression of such gentle refinement. Her hair was loosely gathered to one side, and she had a different kind of cuteness from the one I was used to seeing in her school uniform.

The line from her shoulders down to her arms was slender, and the skin I could see was so white and seemingly soft it was almost translucent. When I shifted my gaze, I noticed that her obi was sitting at a slight angle, which somehow made her look as though she wasn't quite used to wearing it.

My entire field of vision had become Manami-chan.

“...Um, Yuu-san?”

Oh, no! I'd been standing there silently this whole time! I'd been staring at her like crazy!

“Ah! S-sorry! My brain kind of froze when I saw how you were dressed!”

“Eh...? Does it look strange? My mother suggested that I should try wearing a yukata for the first time, so...”

Strange? There was absolutely nothing strange about it! In fact, thank you so very much, Manami-chan's mother! I'll bring you a proper gift of appreciation next time I visit!

“No, not at all! It really suits you. You look lovely!”

“Ah, thank you very much. I'm glad to hear that.”

――Ahhh, I... I...

I'd come all this way today just to say those words to her. The day had barely even begun, and it was already worth everything.

Thank you, fireworks festival. Thank you, summer.

But afterwards, I had no idea what to talk about, and fell silent again for a little while. I wished I were someone who could chat more naturally at times like this. But with the way I was now, all I could picture was myself saying something awkward and making a complete mess of things.

I panicked, searched for something to say, but couldn't find the words...

“S-so, shall we go?”

I finally managed to squeeze that out, then turned forwards and took a step.

At that very moment...

“...Um, Yuu-san.”

She called out to stop me again.\
Then she gently tugged at my right sleeve.

“I'm not very used to wearing geta, so they're a little difficult to walk in... Would it be all right if I held on to you?”

“Y-yes, of course!”

Heat rushed through my entire body in an instant. Manami-chan's fingers were gently holding my sleeve.

It wasn't my hand. It wasn't my hand, but...

We were connected. We really were.

That warmth slowly melted my heart. Even through the fabric of my sleeve, I could feel something passing between us. As we began walking slowly, matching our steps, my heart went wild inside my chest.

It's all right. I have to take the lead. As the older one, as someone she can rely on, I have to do this properly.

But...\
Would it be all right if just a little of my pounding heartbeat reached her, too?
