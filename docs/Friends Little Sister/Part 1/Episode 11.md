## Episode 11: I Actually Went and Said It!?

\
With the end-of-term exams fast approaching, I found myself heading to the bookshop near my house one day.

“Now then, where are those end-of-term exam revision books... Oh, there they are!”

Something thin and lightweight. I'll rule out the thick ones because they just put me off reading them. And one with concise explanations... Right, this should do it.

Recently, I'd been avoiding going over to Marika's house. The thing was, with both of us having exams coming up, putting two people who were easily distracted by messing around together was a recipe for getting absolutely no studying done.

Which meant I hadn't been able to see Manami-chan, either. That was the hardest part of all...

...Well, it's not as though we'd be able to play together even if I did see her. Middle-school students must have exams around this time, too. In fact, I could only imagine Manami-chan looking at me in exasperation and saying, “What are you doing at a time like this?” if I tried to play with her now.

Thinking about that, I happened to glance up at a nearby shelf. What caught my eye was the section of red-covered books for “High School Entrance Exam Preparation”.

There was a past-exam book for my own high school, and for some reason I found myself picking it up.

“Ahh... I feel like I remember solving problems like these.”

I'm really the sort of person who forgets absolutely everything as soon as an exam is over.

It had only been about six months, but it already felt like the distant past. I idly flicked through the pages, reminiscing about that time, when...

“Ah, excuse me. Sorry.”

A voice suddenly came from beside me, and I hurriedly put the exam book back. I reflexively stepped to one side, and someone else's hand reached for the very same book...

“Ah, sor... ry... AAAAAAAAAH!?”

“...Oh, Yuu-san. It's been a while.”

Standing there was the very person responsible for making my heart leap into overdrive: Manami-chan.

Dressed in her sailor uniform, she looked even more refined and dazzling beneath the shop lights. I almost forgot to breathe right there on the spot.

“W-w-w-what are you doing here, Manami-chan!?”

“If you're at a bookshop because exams are coming up, wouldn't we have the same reason for being here? Although... I'm surprised to see that Yuu-san is thinking of taking the high-school entrance exams all over again.”

“N-no, that's not it! It's not as though I've dropped out or am planning to sit the entrance exams next year! I was just feeling a little nostalgic, that's all!”

“Hehe, I know. I was joking.”

That bashful little smile of hers. What was that supposed to be!? It was unfair! She was so cute that I was running out of oxygen!

And what's more, Manami-chan was wearing her summer uniform today, with short sleeves. The last time I'd seen her, she'd still been wearing long sleeves. What was with that white short-sleeved sailor uniform!? The whiteness of her upper arms, those delicate lines... wasn't it just perfect? ...Could I touch them? No, obviously not! I didn't even need to ask!

“Anyway... Exams are coming up, and you're already thinking about high-school entrance exams? You're only in your second year of middle school, Manami-chan. That's impressive.”

“Do you think so? I'm not particularly good at managing my time, so I need to make sure I don't fall behind.”

...She's so diligent... So admirable... I have nothing but respect for her.

A girl this responsible was already thinking seriously about her future and working towards it. And if that future might involve the very high school I attended...

...Hm? Wait a minute. Isn't this an amazing opportunity!?

That's right! If I offered to teach her, we'd have a reason to spend time together! And, what's more, by the time I was in my third year of high school, Manami-chan would be enrolling, wouldn't she!? Then I could casually call out, “Manami-chaaaan!” in the corridor, and she'd call me “Yuu-senpai”! We'd walk around the school festival together, swap side dishes from our lunch boxes, and maybe even study together in the library after school!

Uwaaah, that would be too precious...!!

No, wait! I shouldn't be thinking about two years from now! If I let this opportunity slip away, I'd regret it for the rest of my life! I had to make the most of this miracle that the god of chance had bestowed upon me in this bookshop! Go, Miyanomori Yuu!

“U-um, Manami-chan!”

My voice cracked. It sounded like all my nervousness, excitement and agitation had been thrown together into one chaotic mess.

Manami-chan quickly turned towards me and tilted her head ever so slightly.

“Yes? What is it?”

Aaaaaaaaaah, that gesture!! Why are you always able to make such unconsciously adorable movements!? Do you have any idea how weak I am to that little head tilt? Of course you don't, do you!? Or... are you doing it on purpose!? Stop... No, don't stop! Do it more!

Desperately trying to suppress the chaos inside my head, I took a deep breath.

Right. I can do this. Come on, me!

“If you're aiming for the same high school that Marika and I go to, then! W-would you like me to... um... teach you some of the school subjects once summer holidays start...? I-I was just thinking... maybe I could...”

I actually went and said it!? What was that supposed to mean!? Where had my ability to speak gone!?

But I'd already said it. There was no taking it back now. Perhaps because my heart was pounding so hard, the palms of my hands had started to sweat.

Manami-chan's eyes widened for just a moment, then she smiled softly.

“Well, if it wouldn't be too much trouble for you, Yuu-san, I'd be delighted.”

“Right, I see. I suppose you wouldn't want to... Yeah, I understa... Hm?”

Wait, wait, what did she just say? Delighted? ...Delighted!? Does that mean she'd actually like me to do it!? Rather than turning me down, hadn't she just accepted my offer without hesitation!?

“? It's not too much trouble.”

“I-is that really okay!? Are you sure!?”

“? Yes. I don't particularly have any reason to refuse. I don't think I'd feel very motivated studying with my big sister, but with you, Yuu-san... I feel like I ought to take it seriously.”

Ah. I've just died. I've died from the sheer preciousness of it all.

How could she say something like that!? Now I feel like I have to take this seriously, too! I might not have any qualifications, and I'm just an ordinary high-school student, but I'll give it everything I've got! Once the end-of-term exams are over, I'll dig out all my old middle-school textbooks, go over everything again, and cram my head full of knowledge so I can teach her!!

“Thank you! I'll do my absolute best to teach you! Ah, let's talk again once the end-of-term exams are over! See you!”

I fired off a string of thanks and farewells, carried away by the moment, and started to leave. But then Manami-chan's calm voice called out from behind me.

“...Oh? Aren't you going to buy anything?”

“Ah...”

...That's right!! I'd come here to buy a revision book!!

Feeling as though my face was literally on fire, I turned around. Manami-chan had a hand over her mouth and was laughing softly.

“Hehe.”

...Wow. That might have been the most natural smile I'd ever seen from her.

Usually, there was something almost excessively polite about her, and she often seemed more mature than her age. But the Manami-chan standing in front of me now was unmistakably a second-year middle-school girl, and... cuter than anyone in the world.

Just seeing that smile made something deep inside my chest give a little squeeze.

(*I'm so happy. I love her. I really do love her...!*)

I waved goodbye once more, bought my revision book, and finally left the shop for real.

All the way home, my heart kept pounding. My steps felt as though I were walking on clouds. The scenery passing by seemed more colourful than usual.

I don't think I've ever looked forward to summer holidays this much in my entire life.

“...All right! I'm feeling motivated now!”

I'm going to work hard at my studies, all for the sake of a rose-coloured summer holiday!
