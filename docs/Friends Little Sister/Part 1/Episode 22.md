## Episode 22: A Special Time, Just for the Two of Us

\
“By the way, Yuu-san. Where are we going to watch the fireworks today?”

As we walked along, Manami-chan suddenly asked me that question. I puffed out my chest, as though I'd been waiting for her to bring it up.

“Hehe. I've done my research!”

With the rather smug smile of a child who'd just got the right answer to a little quiz, I smoothly took out my phone. I opened the map app with a flick of my finger and tapped the spot where I'd placed a pin.

“That's a little further ahead, and apparently there's a hidden viewing spot there! That's what I read online!”

I lowered my voice and gave her a mischievous smile. I'd spent who knows how long diving into the depths of the internet to prepare for this.

So, as proof of all that hard work, I held the screen out in front of Manami-chan. I was hoping she'd be impressed by my perfect planning.

But...

“...?”

Manami-chan stared at the map with a puzzled expression, then tilted her head slightly.

Eh? What's with that look...? Did I say something strange...?

“Um, Yuu-san...”

“Y-yes!?”

“If it's already been posted online... doesn't that mean it's not really a hidden spot?”

“...Ah.”

The moment she said that, my thoughts ground to a halt. It felt as though time had frozen for just an instant.

O-oh, I see...! Of course...! She's right...! If it's written about online, that means someone knows about it, someone has read about it, and someone has probably been there. Which means...

It's not hidden at all!!!

“I-I'm such an idiooooooot!!”

Holding my phone in one hand, I started patting myself on the forehead with the other, then practically crumpled on the spot. My carefully prepared trump card had been demolished in an instant. All the effort I'd put into researching it crumbled away like a sandcastle.

*(Wait... What am I going to do...?)*

What I'd really wanted was to watch the fireworks from somewhere quiet, just the two of us, rather than trying to catch glimpses of them through a crowd. Somewhere a little removed from all the noise, where we could gaze at the same sky side by side and share a comfortable silence...

That alone would have been enough.

But at this rate, we might end up watching the fireworks from within a crowd, craning our necks to see over everyone else. I'd wanted to give Manami-chan a special memory.

*(I wonder if she's disappointed...)*

Just as the gloom seemed about to settle over me, I heard her voice.

“There's nothing we can do about that.”

A gentle warmth brushed against my shoulder.

Startled, I looked up to see Manami-chan smiling softly.

Her eyes were gently narrowed, and her smile was so warm it seemed to say, *Don't get so down over something like that.*

“Eh? M-Manami-chan?”

“Come this way.”

With that, Manami-chan gave my sleeve a light tug. Her fingers were gentle, but there was a definite intention to guide me somewhere.

Unable to say anything, I simply followed. Together, we stepped away from the flow of people and turned into a narrow side street. We had only taken one turn off the main road, but I could already feel the clamour fading into the distance.

Before long, we came to a narrow slope without any paving.

Grass was growing here and there through the dirt, giving it the feel of a little animal trail, the sort of path you might easily walk straight past without noticing.

“Please watch your step. It's a little uneven.”

“R-right...!”

The slope was steep enough that we might easily stumble if we weren't careful. Manami-chan made her way down in her geta, taking each step cautiously despite wobbling a little. I followed just behind her, keeping an eye on her as she went.

We walked for a while, and just as I thought we'd reached the top of the little slope, the view suddenly opened up before us.

“W-wow...!”

The sound escaped me before I could stop it.

We were on top of a genuinely tiny hill. It wasn't particularly high, but the ground rose just enough to give us a view stretching into the distance.

The atmosphere was completely different from the riverside path we'd left behind. There wasn't another person in sight. Far below us, I could see the lively lights of the plaza everyone called the best viewing spot. Little points of light flickered as though floating in the air, surrounding us with the strange sensation of looking down into another world.

And then...

A golden flower suddenly blossomed in the night sky. A moment later, a deep *BOOM...!* reached us, just slightly delayed, like a thunderclap splitting the heavens.

Each tiny point of light shimmered delicately before slowly drifting downwards. Their glow fell softly across Manami-chan's profile beside me, gently illuminating its outline.

“...This place wasn't mentioned online at all!”

“My sister and I found it when we were little. It's our secret place.”

There was a hint of pride in her smile.

But more than that, there was a gentle light in her eyes, something that seemed to carry a touch of nostalgia.

Then, gazing up at the sky, Manami-chan murmured:

“But I'm sure someone will discover this place someday, too.”

There was something in the way she said it, a mixture of wishing that nobody would ever find it, and accepting that there was nothing she could do about it.

But at least for now... At least in this moment, this was a special time, just for the two of us.

“So this is the Ayase family's... VIP viewing spot?”

I said it lightly, with a hint of playfulness, and Manami-chan nodded.

Then, suddenly, she turned to look at me...

“...Please don't tell anyone about it, okay?”

She whispered those words and gently placed her index finger against her lips.

The gesture was so unbelievably cute that my chest seemed to tighten.

*(Oh, come on... You can't look at me like that...)*

“Of course... I promise I won't tell a soul.”

I answered softly, as though making a solemn vow, and the two of us sat down.

The sound of little blades of grass swaying spread quietly around our feet. The grass felt just a little cool against my skin. It was slightly chilly, but pleasant.

Sitting side by side, we watched the fireworks burst into the sky one after another.

Blue, red, green, purple, and then gold again. Each colour transformed the night sky, changing its expression with every burst. And each time, the light painted Manami-chan's profile in a different hue.

Her hair swaying in the breeze. Her eyes reflecting the light of the night sky. Every now and then, she'd lift her face and point towards the sky, saying, “Ah, that one was beautiful.”

*(No...)*

There was just one honest thought I wanted to whisper to myself.

*(Those fireworks were beautiful, too, but... Manami-chan is so much more beautiful.)*

Of course, I didn't say those words aloud. There was no way I could.

Even so, sitting beside her like this, with our shoulders just barely touching, looking up at the same night sky... That alone filled my heart with a gentle warmth.

We're not lovers yet. But just for today, even if only a little, I wanted to be allowed to stay by Manami-chan's side.

The fleeting flowers blooming in the sky quietly lit a flame in my heart.
