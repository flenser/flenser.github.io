## Episode 4 – She's Sooooooo Incredibly Cute!

\
"—Um, did you need something?"

The moment I heard that familiar voice right beside me, I genuinely thought my heart was about to leap straight out of my chest.

"Whoa?! W-Waaah!"

My breath caught, and I instinctively jumped half a step backwards. I hurriedly turned around, only to find—

A girl in a sailor-style school uniform.

The white collar was softly illuminated by the setting sun, while her black hair caught the same light, taking on the faintest reddish hue. Her silky hair swayed gently in the breeze, softly framing her face.

It was Manami-chan.

There was no mistaking her. The very first girl I'd ever fallen in love with was standing right there in front of me.

"Ah... U-Um..."

My throat felt as though it had closed up, and I couldn't get the words out properly. This wasn't just surprise or nervousness. I was so completely overwhelmed that I'd practically forgotten how to breathe.

How could I put it...?

She was just—

SOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO CUTE!

My mind went completely blank in an instant. Every thought other than *cute*... every last scrap of my vocabulary, had been blown away without a trace.

Just looking at her and trying to burn her image into my memory was already more than I could manage. My heart was pounding like a runner approaching the finish line at full speed, my lungs felt as though they'd shrunk, and I could barely breathe.

Seeing me like that, she tilted her head slightly and asked:

"...Um, have we met somewhere before?"

"...!"

At that question, I nearly crumpled to my knees.

Guh...! No way... Had she really not remembered me properly?!

For me, that day had been a turning point in my life. Without a doubt, it was the most important moment I'd experienced in my entire life so far. It had been my first love, a fateful encounter, and the sort of meeting I was certain I'd never forget for as long as I lived.

But for her—

It had probably just been an entirely unremarkable moment when she'd exchanged greetings with one of her older sister's friends.

...Yeah. I knew that. We'd only exchanged a few words that day, and as far as she was concerned, I was practically a complete stranger. Quite a bit of time had passed without us seeing each other again, so it was only natural that her impression of me had faded.

But, but, but, BUT! What was I supposed to do with these feelings of mine?! How was I supposed to process the overwhelming impact of this first love that had taken hold of my entire being?!

Just imagining how many people must approach her every day because she's so cute made my chest tighten.

"U-Um... I..."

I desperately tried to force out some words, but my throat tightened and the sound seemed to shrink away along with my breath. Nothing coherent would come out.

She blinked at me, looking puzzled.

I wanted her to say, *Of course I haven't forgotten you! I'm just teasing!*

But reality was cruel. Right now, my existence in her eyes seemed about as substantial as a faint mist.

*(...This hurts... But she's still so cute...)*

My emotions were in an absolute mess, and yet I simply couldn't take my eyes off the girl standing in front of me.

"Ah, um, I'm Marika's friend—"

Just as I finally managed to squeeze out something resembling a proper introduction, Manami-chan gave a little "Ah!" and tilted her head.

"Oh! You're the person from the other day. Um, Miya... Miya... Miyaura-san...?"

"...Miyanomori, thank you very much."

I managed a strained little smile as I corrected her.

But...

*(S-She remembered me! I'm unbelievably happy...!)*

She'd at least remembered me vaguely. I was honestly surprised that getting my surname wrong could make me feel so incredibly happy. Even though she'd only got the first part of it right... just *Miya*... my heart gave a little flutter.

And then there was—

The way she narrowed her eyes slightly as she tried to remember. The way she looked upwards, her lips unconsciously pressing together a little. The way her slender fingertips fidgeted absent-mindedly with the cuff of her sleeve.

—Oh, honestly... Everything about her was unfair!

*(Who looks that cute just trying to remember someone's name?! Even the tiniest little mannerism of hers is picture-perfect. That's just cheating!)*

She was doing nothing more than trying to remember my name, yet simply watching her was enough to fill my heart with happiness.

"I'm sorry for getting your name wrong... Um, my big sister has caught a cold today, though..."

"...Ah, yeah. I know. I, um, had a document I needed to deliver, so that's why I came over."

I somehow managed to disguise the trembling in my voice, forcing a smile despite the way the corners of my mouth were twitching.

"Oh, I see. Thank you for taking the trouble to bring it all the way here."

"Well, I've already put it in the letterbox."

Manami-chan gave a little nod and reached towards the letterbox to retrieve the document.

Her fingers moved so delicately. I could tell she was being careful not to bend the corners of the paper.

*(Ahh... Her fingers are so pretty... I want to touch them... Wait, WHAT am I thinking?!)*

Inside, my mind was a complete storm. On the outside, though, I like to think I was somehow managing to keep my composure.

Manami-chan then walked a few steps towards the entrance. Her slender ankles, her straight posture, the way her sailor uniform's skirt swayed with each step... My eyes were drawn to every little detail, completely against my will.

Then, suddenly, she turned around.

"Um, would you like to come in and visit my big sister?"

That casual question made my heart leap.

Wait, what? What did she just say? Was that... an invitation to come inside?

H-Hang on. Seriously?!

If I did, there was a good chance I'd end up alone with Manami-chan. I'd get to spend time close to her, hearing that voice, seeing those expressions, watching those little gestures. Not from a distance like this, but much... much closer.

Until just a moment ago, I would've hesitated. No, there's no doubt about it. I would've turned her down.

But now—

I'd seen her face again. I'd heard her voice again. I'd been captivated all over again by those gentle little gestures.

*(...No. I can't. I just can't. There's no way I can say no. I want to be close to her.)*

At that point, the outcome had already been decided.

"I'd love to!"

The answer slipped out of my mouth before I'd even had time to think about it.

And then Manami-chan gave me a soft, gentle smile.

That alone made it feel as though the entire world around me had changed. Even the scent of the breeze and the colour of the sky seemed different from just a moment ago.

I hadn't intended to go inside with Manami-chan there. I'd agonised over this exact situation, imagining what might happen, getting nervous, and doing everything I could to avoid it.

And yet, right now, my heart had completely forgotten all about that.

I wanted to stay close to Manami-chan for just a little longer. I wanted to exchange even one more word with her. I wanted her to look into my eyes with those eyes of hers, and to hear her call my name in that voice.

Those feelings came flooding out, like a dam had finally burst, and my feet naturally carried me towards the inside of the house.
