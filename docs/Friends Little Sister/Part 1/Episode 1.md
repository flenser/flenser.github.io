## Episode 1 – The Beginning of Love

\
A little over a week had passed since the high school entrance ceremony, and I, Miyanomori Yuu, was visiting the home of my closest friend in class, Ayase Marika, a self-proclaimed believer in the supremacy of two-dimensional characters.

At first, we'd been talking about going somewhere after school. But then Marika casually mentioned, "My place is just around the corner. Wanna come over if you're free?"

"Sure, absolutely!"

I jumped at the offer, and before I knew it, I was stepping through her front door.

"U-Um... excuse me!"

"Huh? Why are you so nervous?"

"Well, I was just wondering if your parents were home. I should probably greet them and all that, right?"

"...Sorry, but I don't remember marrying you, Yuu."

"What the heck are you talking about?!"

My first encounter with the ever-jovial Marika had been much more formal.

On the first day of school, during our self-introductions, she'd been sitting near me. So I'd given her a perfectly proper greeting.

"I'm Miyanomori Yuu. Nice to meet you."

Back then, we'd even addressed each other by our surnames with the polite honorifics.

But that sort of relationship didn't last even two days. Apparently, we'd clicked surprisingly well. Little things had brought us closer: whispered comments during class, the things we talked about, our shared taste in manga. Before long, the distance between us had practically vanished.

In barely a week, we'd gone from strangers to being practically old friends.

We joked around, traded playful insults without holding back, laughed, and generally acted like idiots. Being together was easy. I never had to pretend to be someone I wasn't.

Honestly, I felt incredibly lucky to have met someone like her so soon after starting high school.

...Actually, maybe it was fate.

...Okay, calling it fate might be pushing it a little. But I'm allowed a little exaggeration, right?

When I entered the living room, I spotted colourful coasters and a slightly battered TV remote on the table. There was a calendar featuring a popular anime on the wall, and the shelves were crammed full of manga. The place was a little cluttered, but somehow, it felt strangely cosy.

"Yuu! Want something to drink? Got any requests?"

Marika's voice rang out from the kitchen. I could faintly hear the low rumble of an electric kettle boiling as I sank deep into the sofa.

"Hmm... How about some barley tea?"

"Sorry, we've only got roasted green tea."

"That's pretty old-fashioned! And what was the point of asking for requests, then?!"

I immediately fired back, and I heard her giggling from the kitchen.

"Well, I'm willing to listen to your wishes, at least."

"Your idea of kindness is seriously messed up!"

Honestly, I never got tired of talking to Marika. Whenever we were together, we'd exchange the most ridiculous remarks, and before I knew it, we were laughing again.

If only she were a guy...

The thought crossed my mind, and I found myself chuckling.

"Hey, Marika."

"Hmm? What's up?"

"If you were a guy... I might've fallen for you."

"Whoa, where'd that come from? But sorry, I'm not interested in three-dimensional people."

"Wow. You're hopeless."

"Coming from someone who's never had a boyfriend in her entire life..."

"Marika! Boomerang! That's a boomerang! And don't just assume I'll be single forever!"

"Well, I have a boyfriend, so it doesn't really count as a boomerang for me."

"He's a two-dimensional character!"

It was rare to find someone I could joke around with like this. Laughing, trading snarky comments, and casually messing with each other might sound simple, but relationships like that weren't easy to come by.

We were in the middle of another such ridiculous exchange when I heard light footsteps coming down from the second floor.

Tap... tap...

With each step descending the staircase, for some reason, my heart seemed to jump along with them.

"Hm? Marika, didn't you say your parents weren't home?"

"My parents aren't, but my little sister is."

"Oh, you have a little sister? I didn't know that."

"Yeah, sorry about that. She's not a handsome older brother or younger brother. I'm reeeeeally sorry."

"I didn't say anything!"

Wait, wait, wait. What was that supposed to mean? Did she think I'd hear the word *little sister* and go, "Aw, it's not a boy? What a shame"?

Sure, I'd like to have a boyfriend someday, but that didn't mean I'd take just anyone! Besides, love wasn't something you could be so casual about!

The footsteps stopped right outside the living room. Then, without even a knock, the door slowly opened.

"Hey, big sis! Do you know where my earphones are— Oh... Um, hello."

Standing in the doorway was a girl who looked to be around middle school age.

Her black hair was tied up in a high ponytail, the ends reaching her shoulders and swaying gently.

She had big, bright eyes, but her expression seemed a little uncertain, somewhere between embarrassment and surprise.

She wasn't wearing a school uniform, just a casual T-shirt and shorts that looked comfortable enough for lounging around at home.

And I couldn't take my eyes off her.

Time stopped.

No, that wasn't quite right.

It was more like everything had shifted into slow motion.

A low-pitched ringing, almost like tinnitus, echoed somewhere deep inside my head. The only thing that remained clear in the centre of my vision was the outline of that girl.

At that very moment, my heart gave a tremendous thump.

Before my mind could even catch up, my heart screamed:

*—Oh no. This girl... She's ridiculously cute.*

"H-Hello! Nice to meet you!! I'm Miyanomori Yuu! Um, I'm... in the same class as Marika!"

"Um... Yes, hello. I'm Marika's little sister, Ayase Manami. I'm... in my second year of middle school."

Despite my sudden, overly enthusiastic introduction, the girl, Manami, introduced herself in a surprisingly composed manner for someone younger than me, though she seemed a little embarrassed.

Second year of middle school...

In other words, she was about two years younger than me.

...Wait, why was my heart beating so ridiculously fast?!

Actually, I already knew the answer.

The moment our eyes met, the world around me fell silent. I was so completely captivated that I'd forgotten everything else—not just that I'd been laughing moments earlier, but even that I was at Marika's house.

Everything had been swept away in an instant.

It was love at first sight.

My heart had been moved the moment I laid eyes on her, in a way I couldn't resist, no matter how hard I tried. It was the sort of textbook, drama-worthy first love you only ever heard about in stories.

...No, no, no! Calm down, Yuu. First things first, have some tea. Let's get a grip, okay? Marika's roasted green tea... I know I called it old-fashioned earlier, but right now, I should be grateful for it. Actually, it's got a lovely roasted aroma. Very soothing, really.

...Seriously, though, what am I supposed to do? Is my first love completely doomed?!

She's younger than me, and on top of that, she's my friend's little sister?!

No way. Absolutely no way. Isn't this morally and ethically unacceptable?!

This was so far beyond anything I'd ever expected.

But maybe that's just how love works. The moment you think, *This is the one*, all your carefully constructed reasoning goes flying out the window.

My heart had already made its choice.

And just like that...

My love had begun.
