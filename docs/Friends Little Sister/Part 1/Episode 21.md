## Episode 21: So This Is What They Call Happiness Weight Gain!

\
With Manami-chan holding onto my sleeve, we walked along a little path beside the river towards a spot where we were supposed to get a good view of the fireworks.

Beneath the gradually darkening sky, people in colourful yukata passed us in both directions, while the lights of the food stalls flickered like swarms of fireflies.

But honestly, the person walking right beside me was so dazzling that the scenery in front of us barely registered.

*(A yukata... Could it really be this devastatingly powerful?)*

The nape of her neck peeking out from beneath her hair, the curve of her neck, her slender wrists visible through the openings of her sleeves... Why did seeing just a little more skin than usual make my heart race so much?

And then there was Manami-chan herself, gently holding my sleeve. She seemed to be having a little difficulty walking, but she stayed close to me, taking one step at a time in time with my pace.

Well, technically, I was matching hers.

Even so... We were so close...

Oh no! At this rate, my thoughts were going to be completely swallowed up by Manami-chan! Right! To distract myself, I should focus on the food stalls around us!

“Ah, Manami-chan! They've got yakisoba over there! And there's cotton candy! Candy apples, baby castella, takoyaki, too! What shall we try first!?”

I knew my voice had gone a little high, but I decided to power through it. Pointing at the stalls like a tour guide, I rattled off the menu one item after another.

“Um, Yuu-san...”

“Oh, if you're worried about money, don't be! I've already decided I'm paying for everything today! I brought loads of pocket money, so you can choose whatever you like!”

“That's not what I mean...!”

Manami-chan tugged at my sleeve a little more firmly than before.

“Hmm? What is it?”

I stopped walking and turned towards her. She looked at me with an expression that suggested she wasn't quite sure how to bring something up, but her gaze was perfectly direct.

What could it be? She's suddenly looking so serious that my heart's skipping a beat. Well, it's been skipping beats nonstop since we met, but still!

“Yuu-san, are you... trying to make me――”

Oh, no! This is bad! Has she figured out how I feel about her!? Have I been staring too obviously!? Or is it the way I've been standing so close!?

I could practically hear several flags of impending doom falling over simultaneously inside my head, when...

“...Make me put on weight?”

...Huh? Wh-what? What did she just say?

“E-eeeeh!? Wait a minute! How did you arrive at that conclusion!?”

“Well, whenever you come to our house, you make all sorts of dishes and ask me, ‘Would you mind trying this?’ Before I knew it, I'd been eating quite a lot every time...”

“N-no, no, no! It's not like I'm trying to force you to eat anything! It's just... I wanted you to taste things without being biased, and, well... I really like it when you eat the things I make...!!”

“But I feel bad refusing. And when I stepped on the scales recently, I'd gained a kilogram...”

As she said this, Manami-chan shifted her yukata obi slightly and made a little gesture as though pinching the tiniest bit of flesh around her stomach.

“W-wait! That's far too alluring! Please stop!!”

I practically shouted it out, and Manami-chan looked up at me in bewilderment.

“Eh? What is?”

Gaaaaah! Why would you do that to me!? Why are you casually unleashing such devastatingly innocent lines!? That little gesture of pinching her stomach, that slight upward glance... Everything about it is unfair!

Besides, that little bit of softness can't possibly be anything significant! If anything, she's much too slender already!

“N-nothing!”

I hurriedly looked away, desperately trying to calm the panic alarms blaring inside my head.

Honestly, what on earth does she have to worry about with that slim figure!? One kilogram!? What's one kilogram going to change!? Meanwhile, since meeting Manami-chan, I've somehow put on three kilograms myself! And I haven't even changed my eating habits! How!?

Ah... Could this be what they call happiness weight gain...?

“D-don't worry! One kilogram is well within the margin of error! Manami-chan, you're already incredibly slim and lovely! You don't need to diet at all! You're a middle-schooler, so you need to eat properly and grow!”

I ended up giving her quite a passionate lecture. Unusually for me, the words came pouring out smoothly, without a single stammer.

At that, Manami-chan gave a little laugh.

“...You sound a bit like my mother.”

That smile was just... just...

It was so beautiful that my heart let out another anguished scream.

I've lost count of how many times it's happened today, but I feel as though my life expectancy has been shortened by at least three years.

But... it was worth it. Just seeing that smile made the whole trip worthwhile. In fact, this was the sort of day that would go down in my memory as one of the happiest days of my entire life.

“Then... since you've gone to all this trouble, I'd like to have just a candy apple.”

“Got it! Leave this to your big sister!”

All right! This is my chance to show off my reliable older-girl skills!

I swung my arm enthusiastically and dashed towards a nearby candy apple stall.

Weaving between the people in yukata, I made a beeline for the stall, where rows of gleaming red candy apples were lined up.

*(All right, this one... The shiniest one!)*

I chose one without hesitation, handed the man behind the stall some money, and received a large, glossy candy apple. It was firmly attached to the end of a plastic stick, its smooth, bright-red coating reflecting the light.

Just this one little thing might be enough to make Manami-chan smile again. Thinking that, I felt a warmth spreading through my chest even as I held it in my hand.

“Sorry to keep you waiting!”

I waved as I returned. Manami-chan brought her hands together neatly and accepted it with a slightly bashful expression.

“Thank you very much.”

That gesture was so cute, so polite, so modest...

Honestly, it was unfair. Manami-chan was just too cute today. Completely unfair.

Then Manami-chan gently brought the candy apple to her lips and took a small bite.

There was a delicate *crack* as the sugary coating split slightly.

“...It's sweet, but quite refreshing.”

She said this with her eyes narrowing slightly, and the sight of her profile was so defenceless, so unbearably beautiful, that I found myself staring.

The breeze brushing against my cheeks, the hubbub of the food stalls, the distant sound of festival drums... Everything seemed to fade away.

At the centre of my vision, there was only one person.

Manami-chan.

The world around me was growing blurry, but her figure alone remained perfectly clear and vivid.

*(I love her. I love Manami-chan so much!)*

The words rang out inside my heart, making my chest tighten.

But I still couldn't say them aloud. I didn't yet have the courage to throw these feelings straight at her.

So at least...

At least today, I wanted to stay by her side like this. Until the very moment the fireworks ended, I wanted to quietly savour this happiness with all my heart.

Because that was the very best I could do right now.
