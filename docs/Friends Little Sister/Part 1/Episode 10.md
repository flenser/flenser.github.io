## Episode 10: The Big Sister Who Comes Over All the Time

\
Manami-chan had joined our game, sat down beside me, and started calling me by my given name. At this rate, our relationship was bound to take a huge leap forward...

Or so I had thought.

And yet...

Thirty days.

Seven hundred and twenty hours.

Forty-three thousand two hundred minutes.

That much time had passed since that day, but in reality, my relationship with Manami-chan hadn't moved forward by even a single step. Even so, the love inside me had continued to accumulate, quietly but surely.

Every time I breathed, every time I closed my eyes, some part of my heart was searching for Manami-chan. Her smiling face, the way she tilted her head, every little movement when she was absorbed in a game: all of it remained vividly etched in my memory.

These days, she called me “Yuu-san” quite naturally. The first time I heard those words, I was so happy my heart practically leapt out of my chest.

But now that I'd grown used to hearing them, even the sound of her calling me that had started to feel like our distance had settled into place.

The days stayed the same. Our relationship went nowhere.

Of course, she never asked me out on a date. We hadn't even exchanged personal contact details, and she rarely spoke to me of her own accord unless Marika was there.

Sometimes, I could spend time with the two of them in Marika's living room, playing games as usual and laughing together as usual.

But once it was over, Manami-chan would always bow her head and say, “Well then, if you'll excuse me,” before returning to her room. All I could do was watch her go, following her with my eyes, unable to say a thing.

The distance between Manami-chan and me hadn't grown any closer than when our shoulders had touched that day. The love was growing entirely within me. It had grown so much that it felt ready to overflow, yet not a single bit of it was reaching the person it was meant for.

A sense of being left further and further behind, of being left all alone, was slowly sinking into my chest.

(*Am I really going to go into summer holidays like this...?*)

The moment I imagined that future, a heavy feeling settled deep in my stomach.

Summer holidays, along with winter holidays, are the magical time of year when romantic events are most likely to happen. Wearing yukata together, going to a fireworks festival, eating shaved ice, scooping goldfish, our hands accidentally touching as we watch the little sparklers... Or going to the pool, getting all flustered at the sight of her in a swimsuit, splashing water at each other, and accidentally brushing against one another...

I mean, those are classic romantic scenarios, aren't they? Of course I'd dream about them. I'd always longed for that sort of thing.

But...

(*In reality, I'm still stuck in the role of the big sister who comes over all the time.*)

That's right. I'm a first-year high-school student, and Manami-chan is in her second year of middle school. That mere two-year difference felt heavier than the numbers suggested. As far as Manami-chan was concerned, I was simply “Big Sis's friend”, and nothing more.

I wasn't family, a relative, or even a friend of hers. I wasn't anything special to her.

(*A fireworks festival? She'll surely go with her friends. Or maybe just the two of them, her and Marika. There's no reason whatsoever for her to go with me.*)

I'd spent the past month imagining it over and over.

The two of us walking side by side in yukata. The moment we'd look at each other and laugh because I couldn't catch a single goldfish. Our fingertips accidentally touching as we watched a sparkler burn.

But in reality, I couldn't even see the possibility of any such future. It was all down to my own cowardice, my inability to work up the nerve to make a proper move.

“...Sigh.”

I was turning the pages of my notebook with a sigh in the study room, but I wasn't making any progress with my revision. English vocabulary simply wouldn't enter my head. All I could think about was Manami-chan's smile. With exams coming up, there was no way I could concentrate.

“Yuu's been worrying about something all the time lately. Think you're going to fail your exams?”

The familiar voice came from beside me, spoken quietly. It was, of course... Marika.

“No, I'm... probably fine on that front.”

“Then what's not fine?”

“...My plans for the summer holidays, I guess.”

“Are you going somewhere? Without me...?”

“I'm not...”

“Your comeback was weak. Anyway, what plans?”

“Well...”

I wanted to spend even a little more time with Manami-chan. I wanted to find some excuse, however small, to increase the number of days we could see each other.

Otherwise, even when summer arrived, wouldn't I just end up standing by and watching without doing anything? Under the blazing sun, I'd be left gazing at Manami-chan's back from a distance, and that would be the end of it.

And then, one day, Manami-chan would fall in love with someone else. Someone other than me. She'd hold their hand, smile at them, kiss them...

The moment I imagined it, something deep inside my chest seemed to grind painfully.

No. No, no, no, no, no, no!!!

“Gwaaaaaaaaaah!”

“Eek!? Yuu, what on earth's gotten into you all of a sudden!?”

Marika jerked backwards, scraping her chair across the floor, and the mechanical pencil slipped from her hand and rolled away.

The other students in the study room all turned to look at us, but right now, I couldn't care less.

“I just... I can't take this any more! At this rate, summer's going to end without anything happening...!”

“It hasn't even started yet!? And keep your voice down! This is a study room!”

Just as Marika's panicked whisper reached my ears...

“You two! Keep it down!”

A teacher's sharp reprimand came flying towards us.

““Sorry...””

The study room fell silent once more, and the sound of mechanical pencils diligently scratching away began to fill the room again.

But inside my heart, it was as though a typhoon had just swept through, leaving nothing but devastation behind.

(*...I want things to move forward.*)

I wanted to close the distance between Manami-chan and me, even just a little. One step beyond her calling me by my given name. ...No, even half a step would do.

But...

(*I still can't tell Marika, “Please help me out here!”*)

Because she's your little sister, Marika. She's your precious family, someone irreplaceable to you. And I'm genuinely in love with that very girl.

“Marikaaa...”

I murmured her name, and a quiet “Hm?” came from beside me. I hesitated for a moment, but the words were too heavy to swallow back down. They slowly seeped out from the back of my throat.

“...No matter what kind of person I am, will you always stay friends with me?”

“Hm? Where'd that come from? What do you mean?”

“...Even if I'm a little creepy... or a little too intense...?”

Even as I said it, something hurt deep inside my chest. The thought that Marika might realise what lay behind those words made it hard to breathe.

But...

“I don't really get what you're on about, but unless you turn into some kind of tentacle monster, I'll stay your friend. Don't worry.”

Her tone was so casual that I nearly burst out laughing.

“Thanks.”

Marika had said it jokingly, but even so, her words were almost unbearably kind.

A tentacle monster, and a best friend in love with her little sister. If I seriously tried to compare which was more alarming, there probably wouldn't be any point. But even so...

Marika had said she'd stay my friend. She'd continue to see me as her friend, just as before, even knowing I was the sort of person I was. That fact alone was a lifeline.

A lifeline, yes... but not one I could rely on when it came to this love.

Because the moment I asked Marika for help, what if she rejected me? What if she told me, “Don't go near Manami any more”?

That was the one thing I couldn't bear.

And that's precisely why this was my own problem. I had to close the distance between Manami-chan and me with my own hands. I had no choice but to move forward on my own two feet, one step at a time.

Even if it took an incredibly long time.

I had to work up the courage to speak to her, little by little. Read her expressions, think of topics that might interest her, and gradually close the distance, even if it was only half a step at a time.

I wasn't expecting us to start going out straight away. But if I didn't do anything, I'd be... “Big Sis's friend” forever.

And that was something I absolutely couldn't accept.

(*Summer is already... just around the corner.*)

Looking at the calendar, our end-of-term exams were next week. Once they were over, summer holidays would begin.

If I took action, something might change. I might even be able to make it change.

With that tiny hope alone held close to my heart, I tightened my grip on my mechanical pencil.
