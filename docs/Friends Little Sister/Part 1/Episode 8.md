## Episode 8: Getting Closer to Manami-chan!?

\
Whenever I stepped into Marika's house, my eyes instinctively checked the shoe rack in the entrance hall. It had become something of a routine whenever I came here… almost a ritual, really.

Among the rows of shoes, my eyes would immediately seek out two particular pairs: Manami-chan's school shoes and her usual, understated trainers.

…Yep, they're here. Both pairs, right where they should be. Which means Manami-chan is somewhere in this house right now. All right! Another perfect score for today's luck…! Wait, remembering her shoes like this makes me sound like a complete stalker.

I was a little disturbed by my own thoughts. But I couldn't help it. Whether or not the person you love is nearby can determine the course of your entire day.

“Yuu! What shall we play today?”

Marika turned around and called out to me, so I hurriedly pulled myself together and answered.

“Hmm, we played a shooting game last time… How about another racing game?”

“Sounds good! If you think you've got what it takes to beat me, that is.”

“That's my line. Today, I'm crushing you!”

I puffed out my chest confidently, but inside, my heart was racing for an entirely different reason.

Manami-chan was somewhere in this house. Was she studying in her room? Or perhaps taking a break? I had no idea, but just knowing she was *here* was enough to make my heart rate gradually climb.

We went into the living room, started up the game with practised ease, and entered our player names on the character selection screen.

Mine was “Yuu”. I thought writing my real name in hiragana and adding a little flourish made it look rather cute. Marika, naturally, went with “Marika”. Just as usual.

Once I had a controller in my hands and the game had started, I could at least forget about reality for a little while. Although, knowing Manami-chan was somewhere nearby meant I wasn't exactly managing to escape reality, either.

◇

“Hey, Yuu! You're only using your items against me!”

“Of course I am! You're in first place, idiot! Good! Stay down there in second or worse!”

“Oh, you've got some nerve! Just you wait. I'm going to make you regret it with the item I just picked up!”

“Hmph, as if! I've got a defensive item ready, so you can—”

“Yoink! Stole your item, and now here's a shell. So, what was that about having a defensive item? Didn't quite catch it. Mind repeating that?”

“Mariiikaaaaaaa!”

The living room was filled with our laughter, frustrated cries and excited cheers. Before we knew it, the computer-controlled characters had been left trailing far behind. It had become a full-blown one-on-one battle between Marika and me.

We traded first and second place again and again, sparks flying between us, until finally…

“And that's another win for me!”

“Damn it…! If I'd got a mushroom instead of a shell back there…!”

“Yeah, yeah. Sour grapes, sour grapes~... Oh, I just love the way you get all worked up. I could listen to that all day... Come on, encore♪ Encore♪”

“Y-you little…!”

I was frustrated, but I couldn't help laughing because I was having so much fun.

And then…

For just a moment, when my attention wandered, my gaze drifted towards the living room door.

I wondered if Manami-chan might suddenly appear from the other side of it, peeking her head around the door. I wanted her to scold us, saying, “You're being noisy. You're disturbing my studies.” I wanted to be told off by her…

…No, even hearing her voice for just a moment would be enough.

(*…I'd like to see her at least once a day.*)

That thought kept playing in the back of my mind, like the background music in a game.

I was just about to choose the next race course when it happened.

“Hmm? What's up, Marika?”

Beside me, Marika put her controller down on the floor and slowly got to her feet.

“…Well, I was just thinking. Playing the same way all the time gets a little boring, doesn't it?”

“Then… how about an online match? We could get more people involved and make things more chaotic…”

“No, hang on a second. I'll go get someone.”

…Go get someone? Who? Did she have some neighbour or friend she could invite over that casually? Or perhaps—

…No. Surely not. No, no, that couldn't possibly be it… Could it?

While I sat there dumbfounded, Marika strode out of the room. Her footsteps faded down the hallway, leaving silence behind in the living room.

(*Huh… Huh? Really? No, surely not… But…*)

I could feel a little sweat gathering in the hand holding my controller. Just moments ago, I'd been locked in an intense battle with Marika, but now every nerve in my body was focused on the other side of that door.

(*Could it be Manami-chan…? No, no, but… no, but…!*)

My heart suddenly began pounding at a frantic pace. The nervous excitement of the race we'd just played seemed trivial by comparison. My heartbeat was so loud now that it drowned everything else out.

Then I heard the living room door click open…

“All right! I've brought her!”

And as Marika spoke, another figure appeared from behind her.

“Um, I've finished studying, so I thought I'd come and play for a little while.”

The girl standing there wore a slightly shy smile, but she was looking right at me…

“M-M-M-MANAMI-CHAAAAAAAAAN!?”

The moment I screamed, the controller slipped from my hands and dropped to the floor with a clatter. I tried to follow it with my eyes, but I couldn't even manage that.

My gaze was completely fixed on Manami-chan, who was peeking out from behind Marika's shoulder.

(*…She's real… She's actually here…! This isn't a hallucination, is it!?*)

I was only saying it inside my head, but my voice felt as though it might burst out loud.

“Huh? What's with that reaction? Are you that surprised? Manami said she wanted to play games once in a while, too. That's okay, right?”

“…Y-yeah, of course… Ahaha… Ahahahaha…”

I could feel the corners of my mouth twitching. I knew my expression was probably a mess, but I had no room to fix it. I wasn't nearly calm enough for that.

Who could have imagined that a sudden close-quarters encounter event would happen like this…?

I'd thought I was having fun playing a game with just the two of us, and then Manami-chan suddenly showed up. Who could possibly have predicted that? Well, I'd certainly wished she'd come, but now that she actually had, there was no way I could stay calm!

My heart was beating much faster than it had during the race. This wasn't just the nervous excitement of approaching the finish line. I'd already floored the accelerator before the starting line!

My heart was no longer in the game.

Of that, there could be absolutely no doubt.
