## Episode 2 – I Had Fallen Completely in Love

\
"Ah, sorry, Manami. I had your earphones with me at school."

"Honestly... They're mine, you know? Make sure you give them back properly next time, okay?"

As they exchanged those words, Marika took a pair of earphones out of her bag and handed them to her little sister, Manami-chan.

Manami accepted them, then slowly made her way towards the living room door. Just before leaving, she turned around, gave me a little bow, and quietly disappeared.

"......"

I couldn't say a word. My mouth was hanging open as I stared blankly at the door, watching it slowly close.

That impeccable politeness. That clear, transparent voice. The gentle sway of her ponytail. Those innocent yet somehow resolute eyes. And, finally, that fleeting glance she'd given me before leaving.

I was done for. Utterly defeated.

So this was what first love did to you... It stole your heart just like that...

Still in a daze, I reached for the teacup on the table and took a sip of my hojicha.

—Huh? Why is it sweet?

It had a faintly roasted aroma, but even more noticeable was the sweetness lingering at the back of my throat. Almost as though someone had added sugar...

No, wait. That's not it. This isn't the taste of the tea.

Maybe... this was what they called the taste of love.

...Ugh! What am I even thinking?! That's ridiculously sappy!

"Well? Isn't my little sister Manami cute?"

Marika's sudden voice from beside me brought me back to reality.

"...Yeah. Hard to believe she's your sister, actually."

"Huh? What the hell is that supposed to mean?"

As she spoke, Marika grabbed my head and started vigorously rubbing it around.

...This is strange. Normally, I'd be making a fuss and shouting, "Hey! Cut it out!" But today, I couldn't even feel the pain. Actually, I couldn't find the energy to resist at all.

That was how completely my attention had been stolen by someone else... by Manami-chan, who was now on the other side of that door.

After rubbing my head around for a while, Marika apparently grew bored with my lack of resistance. She let go and sat down beside me.

"...What's up, Yuu? You've got this look on your face like the whole world has turned upside down."

"Yeah... It kind of is right now."

"Wait, what? Has the world actually ended?"

"Yeah... Pretty much."

"Don't drag me into it, okay?"

Even Marika's teasing remarks sounded distant today. My head was so full of Manami-chan that everything else seemed to fade into the background.

—Seriously... What am I supposed to do?

"...Sorry. I think I'll head home now."

"Huh? You've only been here for about five minutes. Are you seriously feeling unwell?"

"Well, it's not that I'm sick... It's more like everything's spinning around and around."

"What? Isn't that a hangover?"

"I'm underage... That's not allowed..."

"Wow. Your comebacks have completely lost their edge... This is serious."

Serious... Yes, that was exactly what it was. I'd probably caught a nasty case of the *first-love virus*.

Marika looked at me with an expression that was a little bewildered, but also genuinely concerned. Her tone was casual as always, but I could tell she really was worried about me.

And that was precisely why I couldn't tell her.

There was no way I could tell her that the person I'd fallen in love with was her little sister.

"Want me to walk you partway home?"

"No, I'm okay... Thanks. See you tomorrow."

Marika nodded, then accompanied me to the entrance and opened the front door.

The air outside was a little chilly. As the early spring breeze drifted gently around me, I clenched both hands into fists and walked with my head lowered, wondering:

—Why did a girl like that have to appear in my world? Why does my chest hurt so much?

I'd never felt my heart race like this in my entire life.

◇

As soon as I got home, I kicked off my shoes at the entrance and bolted upstairs without even responding to Mum's "Welcome home!" from the living room.

I flung open my bedroom door and immediately launched myself onto my bed. Hugging my pillow tightly, I buried my face in it and let out all the feelings that had been building up inside me.

"WAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAH!!!"

I screamed at the top of my lungs.

My chest felt as though it were burning. I was so frustrated, and I had absolutely no idea what to do. All those feelings burst through my throat and came flying out as a scream.

"HOW DID IT COME TO THIS?!"

Of course, screaming wouldn't make someone appear with an answer. But I couldn't help it. There was no way I could keep all these feelings that had welled up inside me bottled up any longer.

I'd never imagined that girls could become the object of my romantic feelings. Not once in my life had I ever felt my heart flutter over another girl.

And yet, today, when I met that girl, when our eyes met, when my heart leapt and my mind went completely blank...

I had fallen completely, utterly in love.

"...B-But... But still...!"

Still clutching my pillow, I started flailing my arms and legs. I kicked at the covers, buried my face in the pillow, and desperately swallowed back the urge to scream again.

Eventually, all the strength drained out of my body, and I collapsed limply onto the bed, practically melting into the mattress.

Because... the person I was in love with was Marika's little sister.

In other words, she was younger than me. And not just that, she was still in middle school.

Right. She was someone with whom there was absolutely no chance of anything happening between us. Maybe something could happen if one of us were a third-year high school student and the other a first-year, but the reality was that I was a first-year high school student and she was only in her second year of middle school.

And, on top of that, we were both girls. The chances of her ever looking at me romantically were overwhelmingly slim.

"Haah... What am I even doing...?"

I took a deep breath, closed my eyes, and tried to push my thoughts away, almost as though I were attempting to escape reality.

But it was no use.

That black ponytail, that clear, beautiful voice, those eyes she'd turned towards me just before leaving... They kept replaying in my head, over and over and over again.

"No, no... I'm sure it's just a passing fancy...!"

Yeah, that had to be it. She was just so cute that I'd gotten a little flustered. That sort of thing happened, right? Like when you see a celebrity and think, *Wow, she's adorable!* Just a temporary admiration, that's all!

Okay! Let's calm down and imagine a completely impossible scenario. For example, what if that girl called my name and tried to kiss me...

*'Yuu-san.'*

—!!!!!!!

*'Would you... like to kiss me?'*

"Ah... N-No! No, that's not allowed!! Auuuuuuugh!!"

I reflexively smacked my pillow.

My face was burning. I could practically feel how red my cheeks and even my ears had become. I don't think my heart had ever beaten this wildly before.

"Ahh... I really... I really do like Manami-chan..."

The moment I said those words aloud, something tightened painfully around my chest.

"...But... there's probably no chance of it ever coming true, is there...?"

Manami-chan probably liked boys. Some cheerful, kind boy in her class, the sort who was popular with girls and played sports. Maybe she was already going out with someone.

—No, no, no, no, no!!

Why am I getting jealous of some boy I don't even know?!

Getting worked up over someone who might not even exist is just so pathetic!!!

I clutched my head and rolled around on the bed. Then I buried my face in my pillow again, letting out a soundless groan.

"...Ughhh..."

My sigh felt unbearably heavy. And then the most painful reality of all came pressing down on my chest.

What if Marika found out...?

What kind of expression would my best friend make if she discovered that I was in love with her own little sister? Would she be disgusted? Angry? Would she laugh and cheer me on?

I had no idea. There was no way I could know.

And so, for now, I couldn't tell her.

"...Am I going to end up getting my heart broken before anything even happens...?"

I murmured the words while staring blankly at the ceiling.

Was love really this bittersweet, this hopeless, this maddening? I'd always imagined it would be something much more sparkling, sweet and lovely...

But whenever I closed my eyes, as though trying to run away from reality, Manami-chan's smiling face would appear.

It hurt.

And yet...

It made me happier than anything in the world.
