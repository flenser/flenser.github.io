## Episode 25: Precious as Ever...!

\
About a week later, on a sunny afternoon.

Sunlight streamed through a gap in the curtains, falling softly across the blank space in my notebook. The room was quiet, and just as a gentle breeze from the electric fan threatened to turn a page...

My phone gave a little buzz. I glanced at the screen and saw Marika's name.

*(...Which means――!)*

My heart gave an instinctive leap, and my hopes began swelling at an alarming rate.

Marika calling me at this time? It's almost certainly something to do with Manami-chan, isn't it? Please, please... Let this be a chance to see Manami-chan! Come on!!

Without a moment's hesitation, I pressed the call button――

“Hello, Yuu? Manami wants you to come over and help her with her stu――”

“I will come!!”

My answer burst out reflexively. There was a moment of silence on the other end.

“...Why are you being so formal, and why did you interrupt me so quickly? Well, whatever. Come whenever you like.”

“Thank you! I'll be right over!”

The moment I ended the call, I was already on my feet.

I dashed in front of the mirror and checked my hairstyle, light make-up, clothes, posture... everything. Things I'd normally glance over and decide were good enough received a thorough inspection today.

Because I was going to see Manami-chan.

I dabbed on a little of the same perfume I'd worn on the night of the fireworks, just around my neck and wrists. Not too much, but enough for her to notice. I even indulged in the slightly greedy hope that the scent might remind her of the continuation of that wonderful night...

After one final check in the mirror, I headed out.

The summer afternoon sun beat down relentlessly on the asphalt. I made my way through it with a light step, though I was walking a little faster than usual.

*(Come to think of it... we still haven't exchanged contact details.)*

The little pang of frustration that surfaced seemed to mingle with the sweat in the blazing sunshine.

*(Right! Before summer ends, I'm absolutely going to exchange contact details with her! That's my goal!)*

I made the declaration firmly in my heart, as though swearing an oath to myself, and quickened my pace once more.

When I finally reached the Ayase house and pressed the intercom, Marika's voice came through a little later.

“Come on in!”

Her relaxed tone was so familiar that it eased my nerves just a little.

I gently opened the front door, and――

“Yuu-san, thank you for coming today.”

“Ah! Y-yeah! Thank you for having me!”

Standing there was Manami-chan, dressed in her own clothes.

Her pale blue blouse fluttered softly in the breeze. The pure white skirt she wore beneath it caught the summer sunlight gently. Her hair was neatly gathered, and depending on the angle of the light, it seemed to glow faintly.

And above all else――that modest little smile.

*(Aaaaaah... precious as ever...!)*

She was so dazzling I thought my eyes might burn. Then my heart got burned, too. How can someone be so precious simply by existing?

Manami-chan stood there smiling pleasantly, completely unaware of the commotion inside my head. Meanwhile, my gaze naturally drifted down towards her feet.

Yes――the foot she'd injured on the night of the fireworks.

“Is your foot all right now?”

I asked without thinking. Manami-chan looked straight at me and smiled.

“Yes. I had it checked, and it was only a mild sprain. I can move around normally again now.”

And then, in the very next moment――

“See?”

Boing, boing, boing! An angel was hopping lightly on the spot.

*(E-eh? Wh-what is that...? Isn't that just unbearably cute!?)*

My self-control was wobbling dangerously. At that very moment, an endangered-species-level display of cuteness was unfolding right before my eyes.

My heart's bouncing around like crazy, too!! But go on, bounce some more! You can keep bouncing forever, for all I care!!

I silently prayed as though worshipping her, completely lost for words at the dazzling sight before me.

Unfortunately, the hopping session came to an end all too quickly. Manami-chan said, “Come in”, and I followed her guidance towards the living room.

When I opened the door and stepped inside, I found that all the study materials had already been arranged in neat, orderly rows.

The edges of the notebook pages were perfectly aligned, the pencil case had been opened and placed neatly to one side, and several sticky notes marked the relevant sections of the workbook, making it clear which exercises we were going to tackle today.

*(...Ooh. She's really... raring to go, isn't she?)*

I was a little overwhelmed by her meticulousness, but another feeling was quietly simmering in my chest.

*(Still... the living room, huh? I was kind of hoping for something else.)*

Just a little... No, to be honest, I'd been hoping quite a lot.

I'd been entertaining the faint... extremely faint... possibility that, instead of studying in the living room where Marika was, we'd be alone together in Manami-chan's bedroom.

It wasn't as though I had any improper intentions just because we'd be alone together. I simply wanted to enter her personal space. That was all. The books on her shelves, the little ornaments, the knick-knacks and posters on her desk... I wanted to get even the tiniest glimpse of a place filled with all the things Manami-chan liked.

*(Wait, is that an ulterior motive? Maybe it's too soon, yeah. But if the day ever comes when she invites me into her room, that would mean she considers me someone special, wouldn't it?)*

I let myself imagine that future, then steeled my resolve. For now, this living room was my battlefield.

I casually adjusted the cushions, securing a spot beside Manami-chan.

Sitting directly opposite her would mean making eye contact too often, and diagonally opposite would leave too much distance between us. Beside her... yes, this was the perfect position. I repeated these excuses to myself over and over.

...By the way, where was Marika? She'd answered the intercom, so I'd assumed she'd be relaxing in the living room, but looking around, I couldn't see her anywhere.

“By the way, where's Marika?”

When I asked, Manami-chan smiled a little before answering.

“Big Sister said, ‘I want to organise my merchandise today, so I'll leave the rest to you’, and went straight up to her room after answering the intercom.”

“I-I see.”

*(...Which means, huh!?)*

I'd already noticed it at the front door, but there weren't any other shoes besides ours. Marika was upstairs, completely absorbed in her fandom activities.

On this summer afternoon in the Ayase household, with the quiet hum of the air conditioning barely audible, only Manami-chan and I were present.

In other words――

*(We're completely alone together right now, aren't we!?)*

My heart gave a tremendous leap.

Oh no, my face must be burning. But I can't let her notice how flustered I am.

*(N-no! Today is a day for teaching her how to study! Romantic-girl mode is taking a break! Private-tutor mode, switch ON!!)*

I steeled myself, picked up a pen, and focused on the workbook.

“Right, shall we get started?”

I did my best to sound natural, as though everything were perfectly normal, but inside I was still restless.

A soft, shampoo-like fragrance drifted over from beside me. We weren't quite close enough to touch, but the fact that Manami-chan was right there next to me was impossible to ignore.

“Um... I'm having a little trouble with this problem.”

“Sure, let's have a look... Ah, this one. So, what you need to do is――”

I lowered my eyes to the notebook and began explaining, but honestly, about half my concentration had already deserted me.
