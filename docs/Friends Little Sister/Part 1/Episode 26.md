## Episode 26: Today Gets Full Marks!

\
As I continued teaching Manami-chan, one ordinary little sound after another found its way into my heart.

The scratch of her pencil as it moved across the notebook. The faint creak of the desk. The thoughtful little “Hmmmm...” that occasionally escaped her lips. The soft rustling of paper as she turned a page.

Every single one of them made my heart flutter.

I was doing my best to appear composed, keeping my eyes on the workbook, but my ears couldn't help following Manami-chan.

*(Oh no... Even her little “hmmmm” is unbearably cute...!)*

I nearly let the words slip out, but hurriedly pressed my lips together.

No, no! This is study time. ...But there was simply no helping the fact that every little sound I heard made my heart beat faster.

Perhaps because the weather was relatively cool today, the air conditioning wasn't on. Instead, a fan in the corner of the room swung from side to side in a steady rhythm. Its breeze made Manami-chan's hair flutter softly.

It was such an ordinary moment. So why did she look so beautiful?

Her hair gently lifted by the breeze. The little movements of her fingers as she turned the pages. The faint rustle of her shirt against her shoulders when she stretched up a little...

Every one of those sounds belonged to Manami-chan alone. Sounds that existed nowhere else in the world.

*(Oh, honestly...)*

Before I knew it, I was letting out a quiet sigh.

My eyes naturally drifted towards her profile. Her gaze was fixed straight ahead on her notebook. Her lower lip was caught ever so slightly between her teeth as she concentrated on the problem. Her hand held the pencil carefully, and her fingers turned each page with deliberate precision.

Everything seemed to enter my eyes in slow motion. My eyes, my ears, my heart... every part of me was filled with Manami-chan.

A faint fragrance lingered in the air, gently tickling my nose. It wasn't too sweet, but had a certain gentle quality that seemed to wrap me in its warmth. The memory of her innocent little hops at the front door came floating back along with that fragrance, making my heart flutter all over again.

I never knew I could feel so happy. I never knew the world could seem so soft and gentle just because someone was sitting beside me...

But then.

Reality suddenly came rushing back.

This was study time. I was essentially acting as her tutor, and Manami-chan was concentrating on her lessons. She wasn't feeling happy just because I was sitting beside her. At most, she probably thought I was dependable, or that having me explain things was helpful. That was about it.

But for now, even that was enough for me. If my feelings ever reached her and made her uncomfortable... this time together, this closeness between us, would surely fall apart.

So for now, I'd quietly tuck this happiness away in my heart.

I was thinking just that when Manami-chan's voice suddenly drifted down to me.

“Yuu-san, you seem happy. Has something nice happened?”

...!

My shoulders gave a little jump, and I could feel my ears growing hot.

“N-no! It's nothing!”

My voice came out rather high as I hurriedly spun the pen in my hand, trying to play it off.

Oh no. It was as though she'd seen straight through me. But the fact that she could ask me so innocently meant...

Manami-chan really had no idea how I felt about her.

...That was a little reassuring, but at the same time, a little lonely.

Still, it was precisely because she didn't know that I could be sitting here beside her like this.

I put on a smile, doing my best to look composed, and peered at Manami-chan's notebook.

“Ah, Manami-chan. Be careful with this bit. It's an easy place to make a mistake. I got it wrong loads of times myself.”

“Oh, really? So this is a case of the expert speaking from experience?”

She gave a little chuckle, and I couldn't help laughing too.

“That's right! I'll talk your ears off about it! I'm really good at telling stories!”

“...But if you talk too much, we won't get much studying done, will we?”

“R-right, true! Sorry, sorry!”

I apologised with a sheepish smile, and Manami-chan gave a little laugh too.

That playful little exchange made me strangely happy.

For as long as we were laughing together like this, I felt as though I could become a part of Manami-chan's everyday life.

And right now, that was more special to me than anything else.

Come to think of it, Manami-chan had told me at the bookshop that she wasn't particularly quick on the uptake, but she certainly didn't seem that way to me.

Today, she was completely absorbed in her studies. She sat upright at her desk, listened attentively to my explanations, and carefully organised the important points in her notebook in neat handwriting. Even when faced with difficult-looking problems, she didn't give up. She worked through them one by one, doing her best to understand.

*(She could get into my high school without breaking a sweat at this rate...!)*

When that day came, Manami-chan would become my junior. Manami-chan in our school uniform... She'd absolutely suit it.

The soft sleeves of her blouse. The ribbon at her collar. Her skirt swaying gently as she walked, her back straightening a little shyly.

*(Uwaah... No, stop! She's adorable even just imagining it...!)*

We'd walk through the same school building, and go to school together. On the way home, we'd buy a few snacks at the convenience store and chat on a park bench. We'd enjoy all the school events together, too, like the cultural festival and sports day.

And then, we'd become lovers――

*(...If a year like that ever came, I'd be so incredibly happy.)*

Before I knew it, I was gazing vaguely at Manami-chan's profile, imagining a future that felt like a dream.

“Manami-chan, why did you decide you wanted to go to my high school?”

The question slipped out naturally before I even realised it. It was rather sudden, but I wanted to know the reason. No... Truthfully, I was hoping that maybe, just a little, I might be part of that reason.

“I don't have any particular reason, but if I had to choose one, I suppose it's because my big sister goes there...”

“...O-oh, I see. Right, that makes sense.”

...Of course. Marika.

It was perfectly natural to choose a school that a family member already attended. There was absolutely nothing strange about it.

Still, somewhere in my heart, I'd been harbouring the rather presumptuous hope that maybe it was because she wanted to see more of me.

“Besides, Yuu-san goes there too. I don't think I'll be bored.”

“Eh...? Ah, th-thank you!”

I desperately tried to stop my voice from cracking, and just barely managed to resist the urge to slam my hands down on the desk.

*(I didn't mishear that, did I!?)*

She didn't think she'd be bored. Maybe that was all she meant, but even so, it was enough. I really did exist somewhere in Manami-chan's thoughts.

And that made me so, so happy.

*(...That's it. Today gets full marks!)*

I couldn't help feeling a little exasperated at how easily pleased I was, but I was happy all the same.

“Right! Let's try this next problem, then. If you can solve this one, you'll have done everything you need for today!”

“Yes. I'll do my best.”

Manami-chan adjusted her grip on her pen. Her hand seemed just a little tense, but there was a firm determination in it all the same.

As I sat beside her while she tackled the problem with such concentration, I quietly made a little vow in my heart.

*Your entrance exams are still a long way off, but I'll make absolutely sure you pass. And then we'll start high school together.*

And when that day came...

I hoped that, to Manami-chan, I would be something much, much closer than simply “Marika's friend”. I hoped we'd be able to wear the same uniform, hold hands, and walk side by side down the hill after school.

I gently pushed those flighty thoughts aside. But deep in my heart, I allowed myself to picture that dreamlike future just a little longer.

Then I turned my attention back to Manami-chan's notebook. I wanted to give her everything I could in this moment, so that I could help her future in even the smallest way.
