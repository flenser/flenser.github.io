## Episode 7: This Love Is on Nightmare Difficulty…

\
Ever since my second reunion with Manami-chan, I'd become a completely different person from the one who'd been avoiding visiting Marika's house. Every single day, I couldn't stop thinking about how desperately I wanted to see Manami-chan.

The moment I woke up in the morning, she was the first thing on my mind.

Walking along the road to the station, I'd find myself wondering, *If I don't get to see her today, how am I going to distract myself?* Even during lessons, I'd write Manami-chan's name in the corners of my notebook, only to erase it again.

Whenever I had a little free time, I'd end up pressing my cheek against my desk and imagining things like, *What would it be like to walk somewhere together?* or *What would she smell like if she sat beside me?* I was going on endless imaginary dates, all inside my own head.

…Even I had to admit I was seriously lovesick.

But that didn't mean I could tell Marika.

“Hey, do you think Manami-chan will be home today? If she is, I'd love to go over and hang out~”

There was no way I could say something like that. If I did, I felt as though my friendship with Marika, and the peaceful everyday life I had now, would both come crashing down.

So every time, I'd simply say, “I'm bored. Mind if I come over?” and visit Marika's house. Each time, as I took hold of the front door handle, I'd find myself hoping, *Will she be there today…?*

But even when I did get to see her…

“Hello, Miyanomori-san,” she'd say with a polite smile, before immediately returning to her room with a, “I have studying to do.”

Well, of course. To her, I was her big sister's friend. And beyond the age difference, there was the fact that she was in middle school and I was in high school. There were so many things we didn't know about each other, and hardly any common ground for conversation. From her perspective, she might even find being around me awkward.

Besides, middle-school studies were important. It wasn't as though she had time to spend playing around. I understood that perfectly well.

…Not that I was any less in need of studying myself.

But no matter how much I understood all those practical considerations intellectually, my heart simply refused to listen.

(*Haaah…*)

Sighing, both aloud and inwardly, was becoming something of a habit.

And just like that, a whole month passed. My relationship with Manami-chan had made astonishingly little progress, while my feelings for her simply continued to grow.

Then, one day, I was lying face-down on my desk in the classroom, letting out a deep sigh.

“Ugh… What am I supposed to dooooooooo…?”

At the sound of my voice, Marika, sitting beside me, leaned over with an amused expression.

“What is it, Yuu? Were you trying to figure out how to meet a two-dimensional character?”

“That's pretty much exactly what it feels likeeeee…!”

I cried out, banging my forehead against the desk with a thud. Honestly, that was exactly how it felt.

Someone just beyond my reach, on the other side of a screen. I suppose there was at least a chance of physically meeting Manami-chan, unlike a fictional character, but…

“Oho? So you've finally come to understand how I feel! There, there.”

Marika grinned and gently patted my head. Her touch was surprisingly tender, and it did make me feel a little calmer. But…

(*Haaah… If only that were Manami-chan…*)

If only Manami-chan were the one stroking my head and telling me, “There, there.” If I could lean against her shoulder and have her ask, “What's wrong?”, then quietly murmur, “I've fallen in love with you…” How wonderful would that be?

And then she'd say, “Well then… shall we go out?”

…No, that was getting a little ahead of myself.

“So, what are you actually worrying about? Come on, out with it.”

Well, of course she'd ask. I'd been sighing and slumping over my desk all this time.

But this was one thing I absolutely couldn't tell Marika.

After all, the person I was in love with was her little sister. What would she think of me? I couldn't blame her if she gave me a look that said, *What on earth is wrong with you?* Worse still, she might even look down on me and say, “Yuu… that's really not okay.”

If I just told her I'd fallen for a younger middle-school girl, perhaps I could somehow gloss over it. But the moment I added “my best friend's little sister”, well… that would be a whole different level of trouble.

So, even if I opened my mouth, this was about as much as I could say.

“I can't go into the details, but I've got a problem that's wider than the universe and deeper than the Mariana Trench…”

“Compared with the universe, the Mariana Trench is practically shallow water.”

“What kind of comeback is that?”

I couldn't help laughing, but at the same time, it brought me back to reality, and my spirits sank again.

There really were so many obstacles for me and Manami-chan to overcome.

For starters, I needed to graduate from being “Miyanomori-san” to “Yuu-san”.

Even that seemed like an enormous distance from where we were now. And beyond that, there were things like exchanging contact details and having a conversation with just the two of us.

And if I dared to dream even bigger, there were the incredibly difficult missions of holding hands and going somewhere together, just the two of us.

(*Haaah… This love really is on nightmare difficulty…*)

I gazed vaguely out of the classroom window. A lone crow perched on a distant telephone pole spread its wings and took off into the sky. I wondered how wonderful it would be if my feelings could fly as freely as that.

But reality wasn't nearly so kind. I had absolutely no idea where or how to begin approaching her. I couldn't even imagine whether a day would ever come when Manami-chan saw me as something other than “my big sister's friend”.

Ahh, she's so far away. The day my first love comes true really is… unimaginably distant. How many years would it take before I could reach her, even if I stretched out my hand? No, there wasn't even any guarantee I'd ever reach her. The chances of failing were far greater.

And yet, if I didn't reach out, there was absolutely no chance of ever getting there.

“Well, if you've got such a monumental problem, why not forget about it by having some fun? How about coming over after school? We can play some games or something.”

Marika said it in her usual carefree manner. It was such a simple suggestion, but somehow, it made my heart feel just a little lighter.

I lifted my face from where it had been resting sideways on the desk and gave her a small smile.

“Yeah, let's.”

That was about all I could do for now. Any visible progress was probably still a long way off, and perhaps trying to come up with elaborate strategies would only end in frustration.

All I could do was keep visiting Marika's house, increasing my chances of seeing Manami-chan, even if only by a little. Seeing her face, exchanging a few words, and gradually, ever so slightly, closing the distance between us.

It might be progress at a snail's pace, but it was still better than standing still.

After all, my first love had only just begun.
