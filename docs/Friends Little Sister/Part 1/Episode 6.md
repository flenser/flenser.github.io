## Episode 6: There Might Be a Chance…!

\
When I quietly opened the door to Marika's room, the first thing that caught my eye was the wall, which was covered from end to end with anime and manga posters. And judging by the characters, there was no particular preference for either men or women. Every single one of them had an absurdly handsome or beautiful face, and their poses were all ridiculously cool.

(*…Whoa. This is something else…*)

I suppressed the urge to blurt out my honest opinion and cautiously stepped into the room.

On the floor were boxes of figures, neatly arranged. The shelves were lined with large books that looked like art collections and character design reference books. Evening sunlight streamed through the gap in the curtains, illuminating the characters' profiles on the posters with an almost divine glow.

(*As expected of you, Marika… You really do live up to someone who can declare that she's not interested in three-dimensional people…*)

And as for Marika herself, the person I had come to see… she was lying in bed, cuddling a body pillow.

And not just any body pillow. It was one of those so-called *life-sized body pillows*, printed with a handsome male character. She had her face buried in it, sleeping peacefully and looking utterly content.

“Well, this is… quite something.”

The words slipped out before I could stop them, and I couldn't help chuckling a little.

No, no. I shouldn't criticise other people's hobbies. I'm hardly one to talk, considering I'm seriously in love with my best friend's little sister.

Still… I'd come here worried that she was suffering from a fever, only to find this sleeping face and this devastating body pillow. She seemed much healthier than I'd expected. In fact, she looked as though she were having the sweetest dream imaginable.

“Marikaaa, are you awake?”

When I called out, Marika murmured something in her sleep, still wearing a dreamy little smile.

“Mmm… Wait, Lord Schwarz… You mustn't, not that…”

…Who the hell was Schwarz?

Maybe he was one of the characters on the posters. Probably some handsome butler type. I had no idea. Or perhaps he was the character printed on the pillow she was currently hugging? Either way, what a ridiculous thing to say in her sleep.

“I came all this way because I was worried about you, and this is what you're dreaming about!? Honestly!”

I couldn't help flicking her forehead with my fingertip. Marika let out a startled “Wah!?” and woke up.

“…Huh? Where's Lord Schwarz? I'm sure he was just here…”

“Sorry to disappoint. It's me.”

“Huh? Why are you here, Yuu?”

“I'm visiting you because you're ill, in case you'd forgotten. Can I go home now?”

Marika stared at me blankly for several seconds before finally scrambling upright.

“S-sorry! I'm really sorry! I was genuinely feeling awful a little while ago, you know? I was shivering and sweating in my sleep…”

As she offered what sounded suspiciously like excuses, I looked at her face. Her cheeks did indeed still seem a little flushed. I picked up the thermometer from the nearby shelf and held it out to her.

“Here. Take your temperature properly.”

“Okay…”

The way she slipped the thermometer under her arm was rather sluggish. She clearly wasn't quite back to normal yet.

After a short silence, it beeped.

“37.5… You've still got a slight fever. You should probably get some more sleep.”

“Maybe your temperature went up because of the dream you were having.”

“Huh? What dream?”

“…Who knows?”

I wasn't kind enough to go out of my way to recreate it for her. If she found out she'd been murmuring things like “You mustn't, not that…” in that sweet, breathy voice, it'd be the sort of thing she'd never live down.

…Although, knowing Marika, she might actually be delighted to hear she'd had a dream like that.

…Speaking of which, what about me? Had I ever accidentally said Manami-chan's name in my sleep…?

No, no, surely not… Surely?

I could feel my cheeks growing faintly warm. I was just turning away from Marika when she called out to me.

“Hmm? What's up, Yuu? Your face is red. Did I give you my cold?”

“If I caught your cold in this short a time, that'd be one seriously powerful virus.”

“Hehe. Fair enough. Anyway, thanks for coming to see me.”

Marika gave me an embarrassed smile and snuggled under the covers, body pillow and all. Seeing her like that reassured me a little, and I suddenly remembered that I'd forgotten to give her the handout.

“Oh, right. The handout… Huh?”

I searched my pockets, but it wasn't there. It wasn't in my bag, either. Then I remembered.

“Ah, I had a handout to give you, but I ended up giving it to Manami-chan.”

“I see. Then I'll ask her to show it to me later.”

“Okay. Well, I shouldn't keep you any longer, so I'll get going. Get well soon!”

“See you at school!”

I waved back, pretending it was nothing, and left the room.

All I had to do was go down the stairs, put on my shoes, open the front door, and leave. It should have been that simple. But as I descended the stairs, a little flicker of hope began spreading through my chest at the thought that I might see Manami-chan again.

(*…Yeah. Since I've come all this way…*)

My feet were already carrying me towards the living room. I wanted to see Manami-chan just one more time. It wasn't as though I had anything in particular to say. I just wanted to see her face.

Besides, it would be rude not to at least let her know I was leaving. If she wasn't in the living room… well, I'd just say “Thank you for having me!” from the entrance and go home.

I gave the door a light knock, called out, “Excuse me!”, and opened it.

And there, sitting at the table with her reference books spread out in front of her, was Manami-chan.

(*…Haaah…*)

My heart suddenly grew warm, and I felt my breath catch just a little. She was wearing an unassuming navy sweatshirt and jeans. She leaned slightly forward as she moved her pen across the page. Such simple, unadorned clothes, just what you'd expect of a middle-school girl. Perhaps she wasn't all that interested in fashion yet. But somehow, that only made her unbearably cute.

(*Ahh… I just love her.*)

I sighed inwardly as I whispered the words to myself. Over and over, in a voice only I could hear, I repeated, “Cute…”

There was no way I could stop feeling this way now.

“…Oh, Miyanomori-san. How was my big sister? Was she feeling better?”

Manami-chan noticed me, looked up and gazed straight at me. Just thinking that my reflection might be visible in her eyes made my heart rate shoot up.

“Um… She seemed well enough to be cuddling a body pillow and talking in her sleep.”

“Ah, that's my big sister as usual. Then she should be all right.”

“…Ah. So it's a family-approved hobby, then.”

…No, wait. Of course it was. They lived together, after all. Naturally she'd know about it. But that wasn't what mattered right now…

(*This conversation… Could this be my chance?*)

Something inside me gently nudged me forward.

(*I can ask her now. Just make it sound natural. Nothing too serious, just a casual little question…!*)

“H-hey, Manami-chan…”

“Yes? What is it?”

Manami-chan turned towards me with a puzzled expression, and my throat tightened.

(*Calm down… Just be casual. Make it sound natural…!*)

“D-do you have anyone you like, or… well… a favourite character, like Marika? Or maybe a boyfriend?”

…I'd done it. I'd actually asked. My heart was pounding so hard I thought I might lose my mind.

“…No, not really. I do get confessed to sometimes, but it's usually by people I hardly know, so I turn them down.”

“…………I see.”

(*WHO THE HEEEEELL WAS IT!?*)

I screamed inwardly, with enough force to overturn a desk, while doing my best to maintain a perfectly calm expression.

Well, of course. Someone as cute as her was bound to get confessions from people. I don't know who you were, but thank you for turning them down, Manami-chan…!

My love wasn't completely over yet! There might be a chance…!

…Although that chance might be about as likely as winning the lottery jackpot.

“Um… Would it be all right if I got back to studying now?”

“Ah! S-sorry for interrupting you! Maybe we could talk again sometime… if you're free!”

By this point, I was already floating on air.

“? Um… Yes. If you don't mind talking to me.”

I barely managed to suppress the urge to pump my fist in the air.

“If you don't mind talking to me”… Wait, does that mean she might be interested in me!? No, no, perhaps she was just being polite…! But! But, but, but! She doesn't have anyone she likes! And she'd be willing to talk to me if she's free! This is an enormous haul!

“Right, then! Thanks for having me!”

I gave her one last little bow and left the living room.

When I opened the front door, the slightly chilly evening breeze brushed against my skin. But my heart was positively glowing. I walked home with a spring in my step, even humming a little tune to myself.

And so, that day, I left Manami-chan's house as the happiest girl in the world who was hopelessly in love with someone who didn't love her back.
