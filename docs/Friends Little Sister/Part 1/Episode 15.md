## Episode 15: W-What Kind of Relationship?

\
And so the study session began. Contrary to my expectations... no, unfortunately, it went remarkably smoothly.

I'd imagined scenes where someone would get stuck on a difficult problem, or one of the girls would whine, “I don't get it～”, or Manami-chan would look up at me anxiously and ask, “What should I do?”

But reality was different.

“Couldn't you do it like this?”\
“Mm, I think that's right.”

Manami-chan calmly opened her notebook and began writing, her hand moving steadily as she worked through the problems.

Beside her, the other girls were just as quiet, diligently solving their own questions and occasionally checking answers or helping one another.

(*Umm... aren't they all rather advanced? Haven't I got a lot less to do than I expected?*)

They did ask me questions, but they were mostly along the lines of, “This is the right way to solve it, isn't it?” Little more than asking me to confirm their answers. Manami-chan and her friends were perfectly capable students. What on earth had I been doing back then, constantly struggling to finish my homework?

“Yuu-san, you're really good at explaining things!”

A girl with glasses sitting opposite me suddenly smiled and said that.

“Huh? O-oh. Thank you.”

The unexpected compliment made my cheeks grow a little warm.

Ah, but wait. Isn't this a chance? Perhaps Manami-chan would notice that I'd been praised, even just a little. Maybe she'd get jealous and think, *I wanted to be the one to say that...*

With that hope in mind, I sneaked a glance in her direction.

...Yep. No reaction whatsoever. No “I think so too” or “Well, of course she is”, either. Manami-chan was simply continuing to work diligently through her problems.

(*Uuugh... this hurts... And being called Yuu-san by every single one of them is doing surprisingly serious damage, too...*)

Do you have any idea how much effort it took before Manami-chan would call me “Yuu-san”? How carefully I had to close the distance between us, how much I had to watch what I said, how much trial and error it took...!

Well, in the end, she called me that quite casually thanks to the way the game went.

(*And now\... every single one of them starts off calling me Yuu-san from the moment we meet!*)

While I was brooding over that, the clock crept towards noon, and it was time for a break.

I took a breather with some cold tea. Just then, one of the girls sitting around the table turned towards me.

“By the way, Yuu-san, what exactly is your relationship with Manami-chan?”

“Huh? Oh, um...”

(*W-what kind of relationship? Well, I'm friends with Marika, Manami-chan's older sister, and that's how we met. But lately, Manami-chan and I have become something more than acquaintances, but not quite friends. Or, well, practically friends, I suppose. Except that I actually love her so much I can hardly stand it... But there's no way I can say any of that!!*)

“...I'm friends with Manami-chan's older sister, Marika. We got to know each other through her, basically.”

“Ohh, I see.”

“But you two seem really comfortable with each other. Like you're quite close.”

“Eh? Do we...?”

I gave an uncertain smile, hoping somehow to get through the conversation, but the next question turned out to be sharper than I'd expected.

“Hey, Manami-chan! What kind of person is Yuu-san, from your point of view?”

“Huh?”

Manami-chan, taken by surprise when the question was suddenly directed at her, looked momentarily puzzled before turning her eyes towards me.

When she looks straight at me like that without warning, my heart skips a beat. I practically forget how to breathe.

“Umm... Other than being my big sister's friend... I'd say she's an interesting and unusual person.”

“Oooh!”

“What do you mean by that?”

“Umm... Just what it sounds like...?”

“I think I get it! She suddenly seemed to get all nervous just now, too!”

(*You've noticed!?*)

Apparently, every little fluctuation in my emotions was on full display for everyone around me.

But, but listen. Being called an interesting and unusual person, just like before, probably wasn't a bad thing. It was certainly better than being called annoying... probably.

It's just that I wanted a little more! Something like “someone I'm curious about”! Or, if not that, “kind” or “reliable”! Come on!!

(*Well... at least we got to talk. Even if it was only a little, like this...*)

Just as I was trying to look on the bright side, someone suddenly dropped a bombshell.

“Well, since we've got a high-schooler like Yuu-san here, why don't we have a little romance talk?”

“Sounds good!”

“Yeah, yeah!”

“I want to hear about what dating is like in high school!”

“Yuu-san, what about you? Are you in love with anyone?”

(*Huh? Huh? Can we not? Let's change the subject right now!? Hey, why don't we do some more maths instead!?*)

The atmosphere suddenly grew charged with excitement, and my face stiffened. From the corner of my eye, I saw Manami-chan tilt her head.

The moment it looked as though those eyes might turn directly towards me, I prepared myself for death.

(*Oh no! They're going to ask me! If they find out I'm in love with Manami-chan, I'm dead! Instant death, right here and now! I have to think of something! Some perfectly natural-sounding romance story...!*)

“Umm... Well, I'm not really very good at this whole love thing... Ahaha...”

As I forced a dry laugh, my mind was already constructing every conceivable fake romance story.

- A boy who was popular with girls confessed to me in middle school, but I turned him down.\
- Someone confessed to me after I started high school, but I didn't even realise what was happening.\
- I fell in love at first sight once, but I never even found out their name, and that was the end of it.

(*Which one sounds the most believable while having absolutely nothing to do with Manami-chan!?*)

Even as I desperately racked my brains, the romance conversation continued to spread.

“Come to think of it, Manami-chan, do you have a type? I don't think I've ever heard you say!”

“...I've never really thought about it.”

“Ehhh? There's got to be someone!”

“Well... maybe someone kind?”

(*Someone kind!? Does that mean... me!?*)

My heart gave an involuntary leap, and I looked at Manami-chan's profile. But her expression remained utterly calm, and I was once again reminded that, to her, I probably wasn't that sort of person yet.

(*Seriously... this study session is doing a number on me emotionally...*)

And yet, even so...

Even so, I still wanted to be here. Even if she didn't think of me that way, simply being able to sit beside her was enough to make my heart feel as though it had been saved.

And so, while desperately maintaining a harmless smile, I continued to run my brain at full capacity, trying to cobble together a perfectly ordinary, perfectly innocuous romance story.
