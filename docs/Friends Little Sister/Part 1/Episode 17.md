## Episode 17: Please Teach Me How to Cook!

\
And so, by the time the romance talk, otherwise known as my public execution, finally came to an end, the cheerful atmosphere filling the room had begun to settle back into its original purpose.

“Right, shall we get back to it?”

At someone's suggestion, everyone turned their eyes back to the study materials in front of them. I couldn't help admiring how quickly they'd switched gears. It was hard to believe these were the same girls who'd been squealing excitedly over romance just moments ago.

(*Wow\... They're really good at concentrating. Was I this diligent when I was in middle school...?*)

Even as I admired them, though, my mind was full of something else entirely.

Manami-chan's words, *a complicated feeling*. They'd been lingering deep in my chest, and I couldn't quite shake this restless feeling.

Still, this was a study session, and that conversation could wait until another time. I told myself that, looked over my worksheet again, and tried to concentrate as seriously as possible. But then...

A small sound echoed through the quiet room. It had come from my stomach.

(*...Oh no. Did everyone hear that!?*)

I cautiously raised my eyes and checked the others. Fortunately, everyone was absorbed in their problems, and nobody was paying attention to me.

――Phew...

But just as I relaxed, another question occurred to me.

(*Hang on... What are we doing about lunch today?*)

I was pretty sure I'd heard that the girls' mother was out for the day. Did that mean we'd be eating out? But going anywhere in this heat... Then perhaps they'd ordered delivery? Had they arranged lunchboxes? Who was paying?

While I was turning all this over in my head, the living-room door swung open with a clatter, and a bright, familiar voice rang out.

“Right, time to make lunch! Keep working hard until it's ready!”

The person who appeared, of course, was...

Marika.

“Yaaay! Marika-san's cooking!”\
“Yesss! I wonder if she'll make that dish again this year!”

Manami-chan's classmates all erupted with excitement, like primary-school children delighted at the prospect of school lunch.

(*Huh...? Huhhh...? Is Marika actually that good at cooking!?*)

I was surprised. No, honestly, it was a bit of a shock. I'd heard she was reasonably good at housework, and I'd always had the impression she was quite capable of looking after herself, but I hadn't realised she was this popular with the others.

“Marika, you can cook!?”

“Huh? You didn't know, Yuu? Never underestimate me. I am a woman who can handle every aspect of housework. I'm prepared to master absolutely everything so I can spend my future days with my favourite fictional character.”

“...Right. Give me back the moment when I was thinking, ‘S-she's so cool...!’ You ruined at least half of it with that last bit.”

“Heh. Even so, my cooking skills are the real deal. You can look forward to it!”

She was saying the usual ridiculous things, but her expression was full of confidence. And, more than anything, the way she tied on her apron was so natural.

She was supposed to be the same age as me...

No, perhaps that was exactly why I felt a little jealous. Right now, Marika genuinely looked like an adult.

“Marika... Can I help?”

“Of course. How about you try making one of the dishes?”

“Sure. ...By the way, how many explosions am I allowed?”

“Oi! What are you planning to do in my kitchen? At least make sure the ingredients come out alive!”

Marika laughed, but her hands never stopped moving. She took ingredients out of the fridge, arranged the necessary seasonings, and picked up a chopping board and kitchen knife. The whole thing looked like a scene from a cooking programme.

“Hey, Marika.”

“What is it, walking disaster?”

“I'm not a walking disaster! ...Anyway, do you usually do the cooking? What about your mum and Manami-chan?”

Marika turned her head slightly towards me as she chopped the vegetables, smiling.

“Hmm, Mum and I basically split it between us and take turns. As for Manami, she's pretty clumsy in the kitchen. Whenever she picks up a knife, I get so nervous watching her that I can't relax.”

“Ohh... I see...”

Marika's words somehow seeped into my mind in a rather peculiar way.

――Which meant that if I ever ended up going out with Manami-chan, I'd be the one in charge of cooking in the future...?

(*Oh no...!*)

Until now, I hadn't given much serious thought to the prospect of living together with Manami-chan someday. But suddenly, it seemed to be taking on a rather startling degree of reality. And at the same time, my imagination started running all by itself.

Manami-chan eating a breakfast I'd made. Perhaps she'd give me a slightly embarrassed smile and say, “I didn't know you could cook too, Yuu-san.” And then I'd puff out my chest and reply, “Heh, this is nothing!”

And then there was the possibility of a lazy weekend afternoon, the two of us standing side by side in the kitchen wearing aprons...

(*N-no! I can't! My face is going to give me away! I'm going to start grinning...!*)

My reason was barely managing to keep a smile from spreading across my face, but my mouth had already started moving on its own.

“Lady Marika! Please teach me how to cook!”

“...Hmm? Sure, but why?”

Marika turned towards me with a slightly furrowed brow, a faint smile playing around her lips.

“Well, you know... I thought it might be difficult in the future if I couldn't cook.”

“Hmm? So you've finally accepted that you're destined to live alone, then?”

“That's not what I mean!!”

“I'm joking, I'm joking. ...But you're right, cooking is certainly a useful skill to have. All right, then. In exchange for helping me with my summer homework, I'll teach you a thing or two.”

She lightly tapped the handle of the frying pan with a clack and smiled, looking quite pleased with herself. She might have been teasing me, but she was still willing to give me a push in the right direction. That was just like her.

“Right! Let's have you make some rolled omelette, then.”

“Leave it to me! ...It won't suddenly explode, will it?”

“Unless you put it in the microwave, it won't explode. Relax.”

As she handed me the eggs, I felt a quiet flutter of excitement in my chest.

(*If I learn how to cook like this...*)

Someday, I might make Manami-chan a homemade cake for her birthday, or prepare a lunchbox for her. Or perhaps, on some ordinary Sunday morning, the two of us could fry eggs together, smile at each other and say, “Good morning.”

All of those things were futures that might actually come true.

(*I've gained another reason to come here, besides the study sessions...*)

Of course, that reason might exist entirely on my side. Even so, just having a reason made me feel a little more at ease.

When you're in love, there's always a part of you that feels anxious. A reason to meet, an excuse to start a conversation, some pretext for sending a message. Every little thing feels as though it might accidentally damage the relationship.

But if, through cooking, I could gradually become part of this household... If I could simply be here, naturally, laughing together with everyone, and naturally growing closer to Manami-chan...

Then perhaps, someday, I could become someone special to her.

――Right! Starting today, I'm going to become a reliable older girl who knows her way around the kitchen!

With that vow firmly planted in my heart, I faced the eggs in front of me.

...Taking absolutely every precaution to make sure they didn't explode.
