## Episode 18: ...She Li-kes It...!?

\
“Right, everyone! Lunch is ready!”

The moment Marika's bright, cheerful voice rang through the room, Manami-chan's classmates put down their pens and pencils with little clatters and cheered, “Yaaay!”

I could see their faces light up after concentrating so hard on their studies. Watching them, I somehow felt a little proud. *Ah, they really have been working hard...* Though I'm not sure how much of that was thanks to me.

Following behind Marika, I quietly made my way from the kitchen into the living room.

In both hands, I was carrying a plate of rolled omelette that I'd made with every ounce of effort I could muster. I could feel a faint sheen of sweat on my palms from all the nerves.

The shape was uneven, some parts were slightly burnt, and the layers had collapsed in places. It was hardly what you'd call perfect. But I'd worked really hard, following each step under Marika's watchful eye. When I'd tried to turn it over with my chopsticks, I'd got so nervous that I'd nearly ruined it. Marika had advised me that sometimes you just had to be decisive, so I'd summoned all my courage and firmly rolled the final layer into place.

Marika had tasted a piece earlier and given me a little smile.

“This is fine in its own way.”

It wasn't exactly lavish praise, but at least she'd certified that it was edible. That made me happy.

(*Yeah... I think that counts as a pass, right?*)

Marika began arranging plates of chilled Chinese noodles one after another. The colourful toppings were beautifully arranged, making them look just like a restaurant lunch. Ham, cucumber, tomato, finely shredded omelette... The eggs on those plates were so much more delicate than my own, and sat there proudly in all their glory.

(*Ahhh... Now that's professional...*)

Was this the difference that came from years of cooking experience? It was frustrating, but I had to admit it.

Beside her, I nervously placed my own plate of rolled omelette in the middle of the table.

The moment I did, everyone's eyes turned towards it. And the reactions were... well, a veritable storm of expressions that said, *What's that?* It wasn't so much *anticipation* as *curiosity*... and not in a good way.

The room fell just a little silent.

(*Eeeek... My stomach... My stomach's all tied up in knots...*)

Just then, Marika called out brightly, though there was a hint of kindness in her voice.

“This is the rolled omelette Yuu made. It looks a bit... well, you know, but it tastes like rolled omelette, so don't worry.”

“Oi! You said it looks a bit ‘well, you know’!”

Regardless of her choice of words, if she hadn't said something, I might genuinely have fled from sheer nervousness.

And then, at last, one of the girls cautiously reached out with her chopsticks, picked up a piece and popped it into her mouth.

*Chew... chew...*

“...Hmm. It's normal.”

Another girl followed, then another.

“Yeah, it's rolled omelette.”

“I thought there might be eggshell in it, but it seems fine.”

Their comments were hardly glowing reviews. But that word *normal* was more than enough to make me happy.

(*Normal...! She said it was normal...! Nobody called it delicious, but nobody said it tasted awful, either! Thank you, chickens. Thank you, supermarket eggs. Thank you, Marika, for watching over me...*)

I secretly pressed a hand against my chest and smiled, making sure nobody noticed.

Now, there was just one problem. No, just one person...

What did Manami-chan think?

No matter how many people told me it was “normal” or “surprisingly decent”, the only opinion that really mattered to me was hers.

She was quietly gazing at my rolled omelette. My nerves were on the verge of reaching their absolute limit, and my heart was still beating wildly inside my chest.

(*I-is she... not going to try any...?*)

Just as anxiety began swirling around inside me, Manami-chan gently picked up her chopsticks and took a piece without hesitation. I thought I noticed her fingertips tense ever so slightly, but perhaps that was just my imagination.

She slowly brought it to her mouth.

(*...Well? H-how is it!?*)

I fought the urge to lean forward and held my breath, waiting for her reaction.

One second... two seconds... three seconds passed, and Manami-chan's expression gave absolutely nothing away.

But then, in the next moment, just a little... only a tiny bit, the corners of her mouth softened into a faint smile.

(*Huh...? D-did she just smile!?*)

It was over in an instant. But my eyes had definitely caught that little change.

Immediately afterwards, Manami-chan's quiet voice fell gently onto the table.

“I... think I like this taste.”

Like.

That one word echoed over and over in my ears.

(*...Li-ke...? She li-kes it...? She said she likes it!? Where's my phone!? I want to cut out just the part where she said ‘like’, put it on a ten-hour loop and listen to it every night as I fall asleep...!*)

“Ehhh? Manami-chan, isn't this just ordinary rolled omelette?”

One of her classmates said with a mischievous grin.

Well, yes, of course it was. It wasn't anything particularly elaborate, and the seasoning was just the basic combination of sugar and soy sauce...

“Yeah, I suppose it is. But I like the slightly burnt bits.”

(*SHE SAID SHE LIKES IT AGAINNNNN!! WHICH BURNT BIT!? WHICH ONE!? I'll never forget that particular bit for the rest of my life... I'll do my absolute best to recreate it next time...!*)

Just as my emotions were about to explode, Marika's voice came flying in from beside me.

“Oi, what's gotten into you, Yuu!? Are you having another emotional breakdown!?”

“It's fine, Marika... Just let me cry over this in peace...”

My clenched fist trembled in my lap with happiness. I never imagined that such an ordinary comment could make me this happy.

But I suppose the word *like*, coming from Manami-chan, had made it something special.

And then Manami-chan went on to take another piece of my omelette, even taking some that had originally been intended for the other girls. Every time she did, I silently punched the air in triumph.

The living room filled with laughter, while the cool, colourful plates of chilled Chinese noodles added a summery brightness to the scene. Sitting beside Manami-chan, I quietly immersed myself in that gently warm moment.

This was a small happiness on a summer afternoon. Just one little word, *like*, could make someone feel so wonderfully light and giddy.

――If I could keep accumulating little moments of affection like this, one by one... It didn't have to happen every day. Even occasionally would be enough. But if, someday, that affection could turn into her liking me, too...

The thought made something deep inside my chest grow pleasantly warm.

And by the time the afternoon study session resumed, my motivation gauge hadn't merely reached MAX. It had broken right through the limit.

“Right! Let's try solving this problem! Anyone not understand it?”

“Eh...? Yuu-san seems really energetic all of a sudden... Is she a different person from earlier?”

“Are you YU? Get it? YU?”

“So this is the famous Interesting Yuu-san, huh?”

“Hey, you lot! Stop messing around with my name and concentrate!”

――I couldn't tell anyone, but a single little expression of affection had made my heart unbelievably strong.

This summer, I was going to get a little closer to Manami-chan, one step at a time.

With that resolve quietly taking root in my heart, I made my decision.
