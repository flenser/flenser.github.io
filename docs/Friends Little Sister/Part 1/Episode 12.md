## Episode 12: An Interesting and Unusual Person

\
The day our end-of-term exam papers were handed back had finally arrived.

The moment the last test was returned, I could hear voices here and there around the classroom muttering things like, “Oh no…” and “I completely messed that up.” But I was different.

Every single paper spread out across my desk had a score in the eighties. Row after row of numbers I wasn't accustomed to seeing.

(*Amazing…! I really did this well…!?*)

I could hardly believe it, and checked them over again and again. But no matter how many times I looked, the answers matched what I remembered writing.

There was no doubt about it. These were the results I'd earned myself.

“Hehehe…”

The corners of my mouth curled up of their own accord. I was probably grinning. No, I was definitely grinning far too much. There was no stopping it.

Because I'd only achieved these results thanks to getting to talk to Manami-chan.

That chance encounter at the bookshop, the smile she gave me when she accepted my suggestion. When I thought about doing it for Manami-chan, I found myself able to concentrate. I was motivated, and I even started enjoying studying…

(*Love… really is amazing…*)

I felt it deep down in my heart.

The meaning of the questions seemed to sink naturally into my head. Vocabulary and equations, somehow I could understand them all without difficulty. Rather than running out of motivation, I found more and more of it welling up inside me. I wasn't getting sleepy either. There were even nights when I'd suddenly realise it was already the middle of the night.

I'd never enjoyed studying so much in my entire life.

If this wasn't just an unrequited crush, and we actually started going out or something… I wonder if I'd start getting perfect scores one after another? Maybe I'd even come top of the year. And then, if Manami-chan and I were dating, we could have lunch together in the school cafeteria, study together in the library, and walk home on dates while looking down on all the students who'd failed their exams…

(*…Wait, oh no! I can't stop grinning just from imagining it…!*)

I couldn't keep my cheeks from lifting, and even though I tried to hide my face with my hands, I felt like I was about to burst out laughing.

Seeing me like that, Marika leaned over from the desk beside mine.

“Ooh, look at you, Yuu! That's impressive. Your midterm results were pretty ordinary.”

“Fufun! This is what you'd call my true potential! The awakening of my latent abilities!”

I puffed out my chest as I declared it, but to be honest, the only thing behind these results was sheer desperation.

Even so, I was simply happy that I could laugh and joke about it now.

“Whatever happened to the Yuu who was screaming, ‘I'm doomed!’ just a little while ago?”

“That was all in the past! Most of my worries have blown away! The Yuu standing before you has achieved full awakening!”

I puffed out my chest a little more dramatically, and Marika gave me a wry smile.

“…It's like you've finally gone back to being your usual self. I'm glad you're feeling better.”

Her words made something warm spread through my chest.

No matter how strange I acted, Marika never pried any more than necessary. She simply carried on being herself, just as she always had. Whatever feelings I might be harbouring, she never tried to force them out of me. She accepted me as I was.

I was truly grateful for that.

“Yeah! I'm going to keep this momentum going through the summer holidays!”

I flashed her a big smile as I said it.

The summer holidays. In previous years, I'd just let the days drift by, and before I knew it, they'd be over. But this year was different. This was going to be the time when I took even one step… even half a step closer to going out with Manami-chan.

Just as these exams had given me confidence, I wanted to gradually close the distance between us. I wanted to see that lovely smile of hers more often, right there beside me.

“Speaking of which, what happened to those summer holiday plans of yours?”

Marika suddenly asked. I puffed out my chest a little proudly.

“Well, first things first, I've promised to help Manami-chan with her studies, so that's my top priority!”

“Eh? When did you arrange that?”

“Hehehe, well, I happened to bump into her at the bookshop the other day… and I was the one who suggested it! That we'd study together once the summer holidays started!”

“Ooh, I see. Manami's quite attached to you, isn't she, Yuu?”

That casual remark struck my brain like a bolt of lightning.

“Really!? What do you mean, attached!? In what way!? How much!? How much!? Tell me all the details, Marika!!”

I'd completely forgotten even to worry about Marika discovering my feelings for her sister. I grabbed Marika by both shoulders and shook her with all my might.

“W-whoa! H-hey, what's gotten into you all of a sudden!? Calm down, Yuu!”

“How am I supposed to calm down!? You're her big sister, Marika! You know better than anyone how Manami-chan reacts to things, don't you!? S-so, what is it!? What does she say about me!?”

Marika looked at me with wide eyes, seeming a little taken aback. She tilted her head thoughtfully and went, “Hmm…” before finally answering.

“Let's see… She said you're an interesting and unusual person.”

“…An interesting and unusual person…”

The moment I repeated those words, my thoughts ground to a halt.

Could this… Could this possibly mean…!?

“Well, I don't think she meant it as an insult.”

“Guhuhuhu…”

“Y-Yuu!? Eh, wasn't that a really weird laugh just now!? H-hey, what's going on!?”

An interesting and unusual person. In other words, someone who'd made an impression on her, right!? Am I winning!? Could I actually be winning here!?

An ordinary person, someone completely unremarkable, wouldn't stick in her memory. But interesting and unusual? That had to mean there were all sorts of nuances to it. Maybe I said unexpected things, or had exaggerated reactions, or my expressions changed every other second. Maybe I was a little clumsy, but somehow the sort of person you couldn't help looking out for…!?

“Hehehe… I can see it… I can see my future…!”

Marika rested her cheek against one hand, looking thoroughly exasperated.

“Yuu, you're kind of scary. Are you emotionally unstable or something?”

“No, Marika! This is the face of someone who's certain a bright future awaits her! My future is going to be rosy!”

“Wow… I don't really know what you're on about, but you're pretty far gone. …But honestly, you really are an interesting and unusual person, Yuu.”

“Right!?”

“So you admit it…”

For some reason, having even Marika confirm it made me feel rather proud. My excitement shot straight through the roof and kept on climbing.

If Manami-chan thought I was an interesting and unusual person, that surely meant she found me at least a little intriguing, didn't it? If I was memorable, then that meant I'd caught her eye. That she'd taken notice of me as a person…

(*I've practically been promoted to the ‘someone I'm curious about’ category already… haven't I!?*)

My face was burning. My ears must have been bright red, too.

But I didn't care. At this very moment, something inside me was burning hotter than the sun.

“Hehehehehe… I'm starting to get SOOOOO excited for the summer holidays…!”

“Yuuuu, come back to your senses! You've got a mountain of homework to do, you know!”

Marika's protests didn't reach me.

Summer was coming. I'd be studying with Manami-chan. There would be smiles, conversations, and time spent together. And little by little… little by little… our hearts would grow closer.

(*All right! This is where the real battle begins…!*)

Our relationship wasn't something that could even be called a relationship yet. But something was definitely beginning to move. I existed in Manami-chan's heart. Just being able to believe that was enough to make me look ahead with such optimism.

This summer might just become the summer that changed my destiny.

So, for now, all I had to do was give it everything I had!
