## Episode 23: ...I'm Such an Idiot

\
“It's over, isn't it?”

Manami-chan murmured softly, gazing up at the night sky as though reluctant to look away. Her voice quietly settled over the hilltop, where all the commotion of the fireworks had completely faded.

“Yeah...”

I found myself looking up at the sky, too.

The sky, which had been so lively just moments ago, was now eerily silent, as though all that excitement had never happened. The only sound reaching us was the murmur of the crowds beginning to move down below.

But up here, it was still peaceful. The wind gently brushed past the grass as it swept by. That was the only sound drawing us, just a little, back into reality.

*(It was like a dream...)*

Only a little while ago, I'd been sitting beside her, watching the fireworks bloom across the night sky. Every time our shoulders or hair brushed against each other, my heart had leapt, and I'd almost forgotten how to breathe. It had been that kind of evening.

To be honest, I don't think I'd properly watched most of the fireworks. I'd been looking forward to them so much, but before I knew it, I'd spent nearly the whole time watching Manami-chan's profile instead.

But that was fine. Being able to spend such a special time with her was more than enough.

Next year, in this same place... No, before then, I wanted to tell her how I felt. And if my wish came true, I wanted to return to this hill as her girlfriend.

Such a fantastical, almost dreamlike wish naturally welled up from deep within my heart.

I slowly stood up. I was brushing the grass and dirt off the hem of my skirt with my palms when...

“...!”

Manami-chan, who was standing beside me, seemed to lose her balance as she tried to get up. Her body tilted sharply towards me.

“A-are you all right!?”

I instinctively reached out and caught her firmly by the shoulders. She quickly regained her balance, then gently moved away from my arms and lowered her head, looking a little embarrassed.

“I-I'm sorry, Yuu-san. I'm all right...!”

――But I hadn't missed it.

The brief moment when her expression had twisted slightly. The faint trembling of her lower lip. The way her body had tensed, ever so subtly.

“Manami-chan, sit down for a moment.”

“Y-yes...”

When I spoke to her, she obediently lowered herself to the ground. Even that little movement looked as though she were trying to endure some pain, and my heart tightened again.

I crouched down and looked at her feet.

“...Sorry, just a moment.”

I reached towards her feet and gently slipped off her geta.

The skin where the sandal straps had rubbed against her was red and chafed.

That wasn't all. Her ankle also looked slightly swollen.

“...How long has it been like this?”

“Um... I think I twisted my ankle a little on the slope when we came here. At the time, it didn't really hurt, though...”

Her voice sounded somehow apologetic.

But at the same time, I could sense a kindness in it. She hadn't wanted to make me worry...

“But it hurts now, doesn't it?”

I asked quietly. Manami-chan looked away slightly, then gave a small nod.

“...Yes, a little.”

That tiny nod landed heavily in my chest. She might have been enduring the pain the whole time we'd been watching the fireworks.

Pretending everything was fine, smiling as though nothing were wrong...

*(Why...? You didn't have to push yourself so hard...)*

I was frustrated... Frustrated with myself for not noticing.

What on earth had I been looking at all this time? I'd been watching Manami-chan's profile, thinking how cute she was, how beautiful she was, and nothing else...

But what I really should have been watching was something entirely different. The pain lingering beneath her expressions, the subtle changes in the way she walked. I'd wanted to notice those things straight away.

“...I'm such an idiot...”

I murmured softly, then gently placed my hand near her ankle. Taking care not to touch the swollen area, I carefully wrapped it in my handkerchief.

There was no way I could let her keep walking. Making her push herself with an ankle like this was out of the question.

But we couldn't stay here forever, either. We needed to get moving before the way home grew even darker.

――In that case, there was only one thing left for me to do.

“Manami-chan.”

I called her name, then turned my back to her and crouched down, kneeling on the ground.

“Yuu...san?”

Her voice reached me from behind, tinged with confusion. Even so, I didn't turn around. I spoke in a calm, gentle voice.

“Come on, hop on. If I let you walk around in that condition, Marika will seriously tear me to pieces.”

Of course, that wasn't my only reason. There was something inside me that I couldn't hold back. If I could be completely honest, I wanted to say something like this:

*I'll carry you. I'll carry you as far as you need to go.*

If someone accused me of having ulterior motives, I couldn't exactly deny it. I'd be able to feel her warmth against my back, her slender arms wrapped around me. It wasn't as though I was likely to get another opportunity like this. My heart was already pounding so hard it felt ready to explode.

But more than anything, I wanted to protect her.

After a brief silence...

“...I'm sorry. Please excuse me.”

Hesitantly, her arms came around my neck. Then her weight settled softly against my back. Her breath brushed lightly against the nape of my neck, tickling my ear, and my back straightened instinctively.

“A-all right! Let's get going!”

I drew in a breath, summoned my courage, and stood up.

She's heavy... Much heavier than I'd imagined.

“...Am I too heavy?”

Manami-chan whispered into my ear. There was a faint trace of anxiety in her voice. She sounded hesitant and worried... but I could also sense that she was trusting me enough to let herself lean against me.

Without turning around, I kept my eyes straight ahead and answered.

“Not at all, not at all. So don't go on a diet, okay?”

I tried to make it sound light, laughing as though it were just a joke.

But the feelings deep inside my chest were considerably heavier. They were gentle, painful, and filled me with pride.

Because the person on my back right now was...

The girl I'd fallen in love with for the first time. My friend's little sister. The special girl who, tonight, had quietly shared with me a secret place she'd never shown to anyone else.

Of course her weight mattered less than nothing.

Holding her close, cherishing every bit of that weight, I began to walk. One step, then another.

The slope was dark. It wasn't paved, and the grass had grown thick enough to make it difficult to see where I was putting my feet. So I proceeded carefully.

The warmth against my back, the little breaths that occasionally brushed my ear, the feeling of her slender arms resting gently around my neck as she balanced herself. Every one of those sensations made me feel ticklish, breathless, and happy...

My heart took in all of it, pounding away with every beat.

There was nobody else on this narrow path. Just the two of us, descending through the quiet summer night with Manami-chan on my back.

Someday, let's come back here again. Next year, and the year after that. And if possible, next time, I'd like us to walk side by side, holding hands.

With that wish tucked away in my heart, I carried Manami-chan home.
