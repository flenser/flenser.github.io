## Episode 13: I Want to Exchange Messages with Her…!

\
The following day, with Manami-chan still in the middle of her end-of-term exams, I decided to get on with some things I could do at home.

And that meant… digging out my old middle-school textbooks and exercise books. I'd thought I'd never need them again, but they'd been sleeping in a cardboard box at the back of the storage cupboard.

“Hmm, hmm… I see, I see. Whoa, this notebook brings back memories…!”

What greeted me inside were my own terrible handwriting from years ago, ridiculously over-decorated headings, and a teacher's comments in red pen saying things like, “Pay attention when I'm talking!” It was so painfully embarrassing that just looking at it made my stomach hurt from laughing.

“No, no, no… There's absolutely no way I can let Manami-chan see this…!”

But now, even this embarrassing relic of my past could become a weapon.

“Just because I did well in my high-school exams doesn't mean I've mastered all the middle-school material.”

As I flicked through the notebook, I started imagining it in my head.

Manami-chan was sitting beside me, looking at a question with a slightly troubled expression. Then I would lean over casually and say, “Oh, that's how you do it…” and explain it to her. Manami-chan would smile and say, “I see…!”

(*Hehehe… The dependable older-girl route is steadily taking shape…!*)

I was so happily carried away that I could almost hear background music playing in my head.

But there was something just as important as preparing for our study sessions. I stopped what I was doing, picked up my phone and tapped the screen.

“All right, studying preparations are going well, but I need to think about this, too.”

I opened my phone and searched for local summer events.

—〇〇 River Summer Fireworks Festival, confirmed for July 〇〇!\
—〇〇 Leisure Pool, summer season now open!

“The fireworks festival… Manami-chan in a yukata… I want to see her. I absolutely have to see her…!”

Among the images displayed were fireworks illuminating the night sky and silhouettes of couples gazing up at them. Holding hands, standing close together, visiting food stalls in their yukata…

Without thinking, I sank to the floor and cupped my cheeks in both hands.

“Uwaaaaaah…!”

Just imagining myself there with Manami-chan was devastatingly powerful. She'd be wearing a yukata, asking me rather shyly, “How does it look…? Does it suit me?” But there was no way it wouldn't suit her. I'd probably be so overwhelmed that I wouldn't even know what to say…

Wait a minute. Wouldn't that basically be a date!? But she hadn't invited me, and I hadn't asked her either… And we weren't even going out.

For now, we'd reached the point where I was the person teaching her and she was the person being taught. The biggest challenge was figuring out how to grow closer once our study sessions began.

“If I'm going to invite her somewhere, I can't be all flustered about it. A capable older girl needs to have a proper plan ready and take the lead naturally!”

If I was going to ask her, I needed to make it sound casual and smooth. I'd start by lightly asking, “Manami-chan, are you interested in the fireworks festival?”

Then we'd walk along the riverbank together at night, with Manami-chan in her yukata, and I'd make sure we didn't get separated in the crowds…

*Come over here. Don't let go of my hand.*

I'd say that as I gently held out my hand…

*With you, Yuu-san… I feel safe.*

——

————————

“Gyaaaaaah!”

Overwhelmed by the sheer emotional impact, I writhed in agony. I threw myself onto the bed, hugged my pillow and rolled around, kicking my legs wildly. My heart felt as though it was about to explode… Actually, perhaps it had already burst. Love is terrifying… It's absolutely brutal.

(*Calm down, me! Nothing has even started yet…!*)

Once my excitement had subsided a little, a more practical problem suddenly occurred to me.

“…Well, before any of that, we need to exchange contact details, don't we…?”

That's right. No matter how much I prepared, no matter how perfect a plan I came up with, without a way to contact each other, everything would remain nothing more than a fantasy. We couldn't discuss dates or arrange meeting places smoothly without exchanging contact details.

And besides… Besides…!

(*I want to exchange messages with Manami-chan…!*)

The moment that wish crossed my mind, my thoughts went off the rails all over again.

For example, I could send her a simple message before bed saying, “Good work today.” A few minutes later, my screen would light up, and there'd be a reply: “Thank you. Good night.” Then, the following morning, I'd discover a message from her, sent just a little earlier than I'd woken up: “Good morning.” I'd hurriedly reply, “Good morning! Let's do our best today!”

That alone would send my mood soaring first thing in the morning. I'd be so happy I'd want to dance around, skipping washing my face and brushing my teeth altogether.

(*Maybe we'd even exchange stickers…*)

Cute little rabbit stickers, or slightly weird, surreal ones with funny expressions. I wonder what sort of stickers Manami-chan would use. Perhaps she'd unexpectedly be fond of those soft, laid-back designs.

(*Maybe she'd even send me a long message every now and then…*)

Something beginning with, “There's something I couldn't tell you at the time…” A long message, written with just a hint of embarrassment. It would surely be carefully worded, with only a few emojis, but every single sentence would be filled with feeling…

And so that fantasy continued to blossom endlessly inside my head.

“Haahhh…”

Feeling as though my whole body were floating in mid-air, I lay on my bed and gazed blankly up at the ceiling.

“All right… I'd better practise asking her to exchange contact details…!”

I suddenly sat up, phone in hand, and moved to a corner of my room.

“Hey, Manami-chan. I was thinking it'd be better if we could message each other about the study sessions and things from now on, so… Um, if you don't mind, could I… have your contact details…? Just kidding.”

By the time I'd finished, my voice had dwindled to practically nothing.

“That's so lame!!”

I hurried back to the bed, buried my face in it and writhed around, kicking my legs.

But if I casually brought it up by saying something like, “Why don't we exchange contact details in case you have any questions?”, then… that probably wouldn't be weird. Ideally, I'd make it feel like the most natural thing in the world, as though exchanging contact details were simply the obvious next step.

But I didn't just want to talk about studying. I wanted to talk about more personal things, too. What had happened that day, little worries, our favourite foods… I wanted us to become the sort of people who could casually chat about ordinary things together.

“I want to go from being an interesting and unusual person to being someone special…”

The words slipped out in a quiet murmur, with just a hint of loneliness in my voice.

But what if it didn't go well?

What if Manami-chan looked uncomfortable?

Just imagining it made something deep inside my chest tighten painfully.

“…Hmm…”

Yesterday, when I was with Marika, I'd felt as though I could do anything. But now that I was alone, I found myself going round and round in circles like this.

With the summer holidays, which should have been something to look forward to, just around the corner, my head was full of a combination of anxiety and romantic fantasies.

“Ahhh, no! This won't do!”

I suddenly cried out and hugged the pillow beside me tightly.

Then I closed my eyes and took a deep breath. I remembered the time we'd all been playing a game together. The three of us had been sitting side by side, holding our controllers and completely absorbed in the screen. When I'd leaned forwards a little, Manami-chan, sitting beside me, had shifted slightly…

And at that moment, our shoulders had gently touched.

Sometimes it had only lasted an instant. Other times, we'd remained like that for a little while. But that sensation had stayed with me ever since.

(*That softness… That warmth…*)

Her body had been just a little cooler than mine. And yet there was something so reassuring, so gentle about that warmth. Just remembering it made my whole body feel ticklish, and the corners of my mouth curled upwards on their own.

“Nnhehe…”

I knew perfectly well that I was being pretty ridiculous. I understood that, in my own head, I was becoming a little creepy.

But I couldn't help it. I couldn't stop. Perhaps that's just what being in love was like. It made people strange. It made them act irrationally.

But I didn't mind.

Because, when I thought about Manami-chan and became this ridiculous version of myself, I found that I actually liked myself just a little bit more.
