## Episode 16: That's Not What I Meant!!??

\
The romance talk continued to gather momentum, and the atmosphere in the living room had become thoroughly saturated with girly chatter.

Favourite celebrities, ideal dates, the handsome teacher they'd spotted at school who apparently had a girlfriend... The conversation just kept picking up speed.

Well, that's only natural when you get a group of girls at that age together. All it takes is for someone to casually bring up romance, and you've got more than enough fuel to get everyone excited.

But then, amidst all that chatter, a particular topic suddenly caught my ear.

“Come to think of it, did you hear that Ishii-san from the class next door confessed to Gotou-san the other day?”

“Wasn't that the pair everyone was saying were a same-sex couple?”

“That's the one! And apparently, they really did start going out!”

“Ooh! Those two always seemed so well suited to each other. I'm glad it worked out!”

As everyone chattered excitedly, one girl leaned forward with an animated expression, another covered her mouth and giggled, while someone else nodded enthusiastically along...

Just outside that little circle, I sat alone, listening to a story that somehow didn't feel entirely unrelated to me.

(*Two girls... going out with each other...*)

My heart gave a small but sharp thump.

I didn't know Ishii-san or Gotou-san at all. All I knew was that they were classmates of these girls. But the moment the word *same-sex* entered the conversation, the protective barrier I'd somehow built around a certain part of my heart shattered with an audible crack.

It was such a direct topic, and it struck so close to home.

“W-wait! Can I ask something?”

Before I knew it, my voice had gone all squeaky. I hurriedly tried to cover it up, but it was too late. Three girls turned towards me, looking at me with puzzled expressions.

“? What's wrong, Yuu-san?”

“Um... Are same-sex couples really not that unusual!?”

The moment I said it, I surprised even myself. It was as though a thought I'd buried deep inside had suddenly burst out as words before I could stop it...

The atmosphere in the room shifted ever so slightly. It wasn't quite silence, but everyone exchanged glances for a brief moment before a gentle wave of laughter spread through the room.

“It's not that unusual, is it?”

“Yeah, yeah. I mean, they might stand out a little, but there's nothing weird about it. Honestly, I think it's lovely when two people have mutual feelings for each other. I'm kind of jealous!”

“Exactly! I think it's wonderful when people cross the boundaries of gender like that.”

Their reactions were so natural that I almost felt taken aback. Nobody was surprised or seemed uncomfortable. If anything, they were positively supportive...

I could feel something that had been tightly wound inside me slowly beginning to loosen. Perhaps these girls would even be happy for me if I ended up going out with Manami-chan.

But then...

“Actually, Yuu-san... could it be that...?”

The moment I heard those words, every nerve in my body stood on end.

One of the girls leaned towards me with a little giggle. Her eyes were fixed on mine, and there was something frighteningly knowing about her expression.

“Are you the type who likes girls but worries about what people will think?”

Gah!! Oh no, this girl... She's way too perceptive! Is she some kind of romance-detecting device? Has something inside my head been leaking out? I don't have *MANAMI-CHAN I LOVE YOU* written across my forehead, do I!?

“N-no, it's not like that...!”

And just as I hurriedly tried to deny it...

“Ah! Wait! Does that mean you like Manami-chan's—”

“Wha—!?”

“—older sister!?”

GAAAAAAAH!! THAT WAS CLOSE!!

I nearly screamed, but managed to deny it with every fibre of my being.

“THAT'S NOT WHAT I MEANT!!??”

And it wasn't just a little outburst. Calling it merely raising my voice would be an understatement. I denied it with such force that even my body jolted as though I'd been struck by something.

For one brief moment, the atmosphere in the room went taut... but then the girls, everyone except Manami-chan, laughed and reacted cheerfully.

“Whoa, that's one emphatic denial!”

(*No. This isn't something to laugh about. I was just fighting for my life over here...*)

A wave of sweat broke out across my skin. My heart still wouldn't settle down. I secretly clenched my trembling fingertips together in my lap.

Among all the laughing faces, I was the only one who couldn't laugh.

These girls didn't think there was anything wrong with two girls going out together. But I had no idea how Manami-chan felt about it. I couldn't let my feelings for her come out here, and if she got the wrong idea and thought I liked Marika, who knew what that would do to our relationship from now on?

Of course, I did like Marika. She was surprisingly kind and considerate, and above all, I never got bored when I was with her. She always had a comeback ready whenever I teased her, and when I was feeling down, she'd somehow find a way to cheer me up.

But that was as a friend.

“Denying it so desperately makes you look suspicious～. Hey, Manami-chan, what would you do if Yuu-san really did like your big sister?”

The atmosphere in the room shifted ever so slightly. The laughter that had been filling it until then faded, and everything grew just a little quieter.

It only lasted a moment, but somehow it felt strangely long. I could almost hear someone swallowing, the sound seeming far louder than it should have been.

Then Manami-chan raised her head. The movement was very slow, but her clear, unwavering eyes settled on me.

(*Urgh...!*)

It wasn't a piercing glare. She wasn't angry, and she wasn't smiling, either.

But her eyes were clear, serious and utterly calm.

She looked much more mature than usual.

There was a strength in her expression, as though she were carefully weighing something, quite different from the soft, delicate look I was used to.

My heart gave a painful throb.

A little while later, Manami-chan quietly opened her mouth.

“It's... kind of complicated.”

Her voice was soft, but I heard every word clearly. It felt as though time had stopped for me alone.

“Yeah, I get that! I think I'd probably feel a bit conflicted too, if I were in the same position.”

“Having a family member get a partner is a little complicated in itself, isn't it?”

“Exactly. And even more so if it's someone you know.”

The other girls immediately responded, and the room once again filled with lively chatter. But inside me, the world had fallen silent.

(*...A complicated feeling...?*)

Those words kept circling around inside my chest.

――Complicated.

What a vague, inconclusive thing to say. And yet, perhaps precisely because of that, I felt as though those words contained countless different emotions beneath their surface. I couldn't look away from them.

What was it that Manami-chan found complicated?

Was it that Marika might get a partner and become more distant from her, making her feel conflicted? Was it that, if I liked Marika, she'd feel awkward being caught between the two of us?

Or was it...

(*Because I thought she liked someone who wasn't... herself...?*)

No, no. Surely not. That was just wishful thinking on my part.

And yet, there was a part of me that had wondered, even if only for an instant, whether that possibility might exist. Its voice echoed somewhere deep inside my chest.

(*...No, no. There's no way I could ask her that.*)

That's right. There was no way I could ask. If I pressed her with something like, “What do you mean by that?”, I felt as though everything might fall apart.

This atmosphere, this distance between us, even this moment we were sharing right now... It felt as though all of it might disappear.

So I simply smiled.

“Wahaha! Seriously, don't get the wrong idea! Marika and I are just friends!”

I deliberately made my voice as bright as possible and waved my hands, trying to brush the whole thing aside.

The others laughed along.

But Manami-chan's gaze was just a little different.

She wasn't smiling. She wasn't angry or confused, either. She simply looked straight at me, and yet... there was something about her expression, as though she were holding something back.

I thought about looking away. But somehow, I just couldn't.

The question that had taken root inside me was no longer something I could easily dismiss.

――Hey, Manami-chan. That complicated feeling... what did it mean to you?

A small voice continued to ask the question deep inside my chest. But for now, it could only echo within me, unanswered.
