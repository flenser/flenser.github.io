## Episode 29: I Honestly Thought She Was an Angel

\
As we made our way towards the changing rooms, I spotted Manami-chan holding a swimsuit of her own.

“Oh, have you finished choosing yours too, Sis?”

“Yep. Oh, I know! Since we're here, why don't the three of us change together and show each other? Yuu, you're okay with that too, right?”

“Huh? Ah, y-yeah...!?”

My voice cracked. My throat was completely dry, and a pitch came out that I hadn't intended at all. But I didn't even have the presence of mind to worry about that.

Show each other? Our swimsuits... after we've put them on...?

M-Manami-chan would see me wearing a swimsuit... and I'd get to see Manami-chan wearing one...!? I can look? I'm allowed to look at that!? Really!? Are you absolutely, positively sure!?

No, wait! This is just trying on swimsuits! That's all! Calm down! Think about it rationally! Showing each other is simply a perfectly wholesome way of checking the designs and how well they fit! It's a perfectly ordinary thing for girls to do when shopping together!! It's completely normal between friends!!

But it's not normal for me!

My brain went into overdrive, conjuring up images of Manami-chan in her swimsuit. Just imagining it was enough to make my knees tremble. I could barely breathe. My heartbeat was pounding so loudly that I could hear it in my own ears.

Completely oblivious to my turmoil, the other two casually disappeared into their respective changing rooms.

“Right, once everyone's changed, we'll all come out on the count of three!”

Marika's carefree voice reached me through the curtains.

Still in a daze, I somehow managed to slip into a changing room of my own and snap the curtain shut.

*(C-calm down... This is just trying on swimsuits. We're only showing each other so we can judge them objectively. If I let any improper thoughts creep into this, I've lost...)*

Why did something so perfectly ordinary have to feel so incredibly special?

I hurriedly took off my clothes and pulled the one-piece swimsuit on over my underwear. Standing in front of the mirror, I peered at my reflection. It was the same familiar face I saw every day, but somehow, I didn't quite look like my usual self.

“I... I have no idea...”

A groan escaped me.

I couldn't tell whether it suited me or not. It wasn't flashy, but perhaps it was a little too safe. The longer I stared, the more my reflection seemed to blur.

*(...But maybe Manami-chan prefers something understated like this...? Or should I have been a little more daring...? But if I show too much skin, she'll become conscious of me in a weird way... No, actually, there's a much greater chance she won't think about me at all...)*

My thoughts kept going round and round until I couldn't tell what the right answer was any more.

“Manami! Yuu! You two ready?”

Marika's voice rang out, and my entire body stiffened.

“I'm ready, Sis.”

Ugh...!

The moment had finally arrived.

The other two seemed completely prepared.

I was the only one still flailing around inside my changing room, all on my own.

...But there was no escaping now.

“...I-I'm ready too!”

At least my voice sounded a little more normal than it had earlier. That was my one small consolation.

“Right, then! I'll give the signal. Ready? Three, two, one!”

The sounds of curtains opening rang out together.

With trembling hands, I gripped the edge of the fabric...

And flung it open in one quick movement.

The first person I looked at was on my left.

Marika was wearing the one-piece she'd been looking at earlier. But it wasn't just any old one-piece. It had a refreshing blue design, with flowing, wave-like lines running across it. They seemed to shift gently as she moved, giving the swimsuit a slightly different impression from every angle.

And, of course, her chest was making its presence very firmly known.

Grrr...

“Oh, Yuu! That looks good on you!”

“D-do you think so? Yours looks really well balanced too, Marika. And... you've got a great figure.”

It was an honest compliment, but somehow it sounded like sour grapes. That annoyed me. I mean, it really did suit her! I had no choice but to admit it.

But more importantly...

The moment I turned to my right, my entire field of vision seemed to sparkle.

Standing there was Manami-chan, wearing a swimsuit decorated with frills in white and pale pink.

It looked as though someone had taken the petals of early spring and fashioned them into a swimsuit. The design was soft and gentle, and the little frills on her shoulders accentuated how slender her arms were. There wasn't much exposed around her chest, but there was still a natural, delicate curve to her figure. The light fell on her fair skin, making it seem as though she was surrounded by a faint glow.

I drew in a breath. No, that wasn't right. I stopped breathing altogether.

In that single instant, my brain completely froze.

Without having time to think about anything, I simply... simply stood there, mesmerised.

“Manami, that really suits you. You look cute.”

Marika's voice sounded as though it were coming from far away, and somehow it brought me back to reality.

“Thank you, Sis. ...Yuu-san, what do you think?”

The sound of Manami-chan saying my name made my heart leap.

*(I honestly thought she was an angel.)*

I couldn't possibly say something like that out loud. Actually, if I had, I probably would have died on the spot.

“Huh? Ah, um... It suits you incredibly well. Really, you're so cute, I mean...”

My head was spinning. I couldn't arrange my words properly, and I couldn't come up with a single clever thing to say.

Even so, Manami-chan smiled at me.

“Thank you. You look lovely too, Yuu-san.”

That smile...

It pierced straight through my heart.

She was only smiling. So why did it have to affect me this much...?

She looked directly into my eyes and said it to me, gently, calmly, but unmistakably meant for me.

Her kind voice, her soft gaze, the glimpse of her ear through her swaying hair...

Every little thing. Every single thing about her was so cute, so overwhelming, that I just couldn't take it any more.

“R-right, then! I'll go with this one!”

Unable to hold it in any longer, I blurted that out, yanked the curtain of my changing room open and practically threw myself inside.

I was running away. No, more than that, I just wanted to get away. Before Manami-chan noticed how wildly my heart was beating.

When I stood in front of the mirror, my reflection was bright red. Not just my cheeks. Even my ears were burning, and I could feel the heat spreading through them. My mouth was stuck in a ridiculous grin. There was absolutely no way I could make myself look composed again.

It was pathetic, but I couldn't stop it.

“...Oh, honestly, I love her so much... I want to go out with her...”

The words came out in a mutter, almost as though I were spitting them out. Even I was surprised by how quiet my voice sounded.

But I couldn't help saying them. I wanted to let just a little of the feelings swirling inside me escape, at least somewhere nobody else could hear.

I took several deep breaths. Filling my lungs right to the bottom, breathing out, then drawing in another breath... I wanted to cool my head and bring my racing heartbeat down, even just a little.

Beyond the curtain, or perhaps on the other side of this wall...

The girl I loved was there. She was sharing the same time and the same space with me. She was smiling and being kind, completely unaware of how I felt, and she was right there beside me.

For now, that was already enough. I wanted things to stay just as they were for a little longer, so that this distance between us wouldn't be broken.

“Right... Right. I'm okay. Everything's fine. Just act normally, like always...!”

I told myself that in the mirror. I consciously relaxed the muscles in my face, practised putting on a smile, and took one more deep breath.

Then I finished changing, straightened my clothes, fixed my hair and gently placed my hand on the curtain.

*Please let me be back to my usual self when I step outside. Please let my smile look natural...*

I quietly made that wish in my heart, then opened the curtain.
