## Episode 28: A Girl in Love with Manami-chan

\
Once we reached the swimwear section, nobody particularly took the lead. We simply said, “Well, I'll have a look around over here,” and naturally drifted off towards the areas that caught our interest.

I'd imagined the three of us excitedly agonising over our choices together, or standing side by side with Manami-chan, saying things like, “Oh, that would definitely suit you!” and sharing a quiet laugh between ourselves.

I'd been hoping, just a little, that this might be a chance for us to grow closer.

But right now, as far as Manami-chan was concerned, I was nothing more than a high-school girl who was good friends with Marika. This was just another ordinary day in the middle of a short summer holiday.

If I suddenly tried to close the distance between us and she recoiled, things might become awkward between us afterwards. I might even put Marika in an uncomfortable position.

Thinking about that worst-case scenario, I realised that I'd somehow ended up choosing to stay beside Marika.

“...Hey, Marika, did you have to buy a new swimsuit because you'd put on weight too?”

I meant it as a light-hearted tease, and Marika, predictably, played along.

“Honestly, yeah. I tried on the one I wore in middle school, just to see, and it was ridiculously tight!”

“O-oh, really? I see...”

Looking at Marika's chest, it was obvious hers was considerably bigger than mine. My gaze was drawn to it almost of its own accord, before dropping down to my own.

...Yep. Nothing. A void. An abyss wider than the universe itself. In other words, I had infinite potential... or so I wanted to believe.

“Why... Why is God so unfair...?”

The complaint slipped out in a quiet mutter.

I was going to be wearing a swimsuit in front of the person I liked, you know? Sure, we're both girls, so there wasn't much point worrying about my chest, but still... Couldn't I have just a little... the tiniest glimmer of hope...?!

“Hm? Yuu, does that mean you can still fit into your middle-school swimsuit?”

“Huh? Ah, no... Mine's getting a bit tight too, actually. Well, in my case, it's more... my stomach...”

I was certainly having trouble resisting the temptation of snacks, but I'd also... well... put on a little happy weight, so I was getting a bit softer around the middle...

I turned my attention to the swimsuit racks, as if to distract her from my gaze.

Bikinis were immediately out of the running. I didn't have anywhere near enough confidence in my appearance, or the courage, to wear one of those. Which left one-piece swimsuits. Preferably a subdued colour, but with enough design detail not to look too plain. Something that might make my figure look even a little better...

Did such a perfect piece of equipment even exist?

I glanced to the side and saw Marika looking at the same rack. I couldn't resist making another cheeky remark.

“Marika, with assets like yours, you should just go for a bikini.”

“No way. I'd rather not have guys hitting on me. And I'm not interested anyway.”

“...What if it was a two-dimensional character?”

“That depends on the circumstances and whether I like their design.”

“You really don't mess about, do you...”

I gave her an exasperated look, and she immediately fired back.

“What about you, Yuu? If you want people hitting on you, why not wear a bikini?”

“No chance. With my measurements, all a bikini would do is bring misery.”

“I know.”

“Hey! You little...!”

Well, I was a girl in love with Manami-chan now. Even if some handsome guy came along and hit on me, I wouldn't go out with him. Of course I wouldn't. I wouldn't even consider it.

...But if Manami-chan were to tell me that she really, really wanted me to wear a bikini...

Then I might at least think about it. Yes, I'd be willing to give it serious consideration... perhaps. But would a situation where she actually told me that to my face ever exist in this world...? No. Definitely not. A miracle like that would be on the level of a natural disaster.

As I wandered back and forth between such ridiculous fantasies and reality, I felt a sudden sense of comfort from Marika walking beside me. This easy familiarity, where we could trade teasing remarks and laugh together without holding back or feeling embarrassed, was just like us. It felt good.

And perhaps that was exactly why I sometimes found myself afraid.

*(If I tell Manami-chan how I feel, and things actually work out between us... will Marika and I still be able to carry on just as we always have?)*

Reaching out to my best friend's little sister. I understood perfectly well what that meant. Marika might tease me, but she's kind-hearted, and I didn't think she'd fly off the handle straight away... Still, there might be some lingering discomfort deep down.

...But now wasn't the time to think about that.

For now, I needed to gather up the little moments of happiness right in front of me, one by one.

“By the way, when are we going to the pool?”

I deliberately put a bright note into my voice and changed the subject. Marika promptly shared the plans she'd already worked out.

“I was thinking the day after tomorrow. And the place is that one that opened last year. You know, the one with all sorts of things, like water slides and a lazy river. Have you heard of it?”

“Oh, yeah, I know it. It's pretty big, right? It's become quite a popular spot lately.”

“That's the one. Manami said she's free the day after tomorrow, too, so let's go and cool off properly!”

“Y-yeah!”

I somehow managed to stop the happiness from spilling out of my mouth.

Yes...! Manami-chan's coming...! Will my heart even survive another two days? Choosing a swimsuit today might be one of the most consequential decisions of my entire life.

All the colourful swimsuits lined up at the edge of my vision suddenly looked like beacons of hope.

Perhaps my destined swimsuit was somewhere among them. The one that would give my love a little push in the right direction.

“...Yuu, aren't you looking a bit excited?”

“N-not at all! I'm perfectly normal. Oh, hey, isn't this one cute?”

In a panic, I picked up a one-piece swimsuit in a subdued navy, decorated with a small floral pattern. It had understated frills and a design that left just a little of the back exposed.

It wasn't too flashy, but neither was it too plain. It seemed to strike a balance between maturity and cuteness...

*(I wonder what Manami-chan would think of this...?)*

I was thinking about it with a seriousness that surprised even me. But that was just how much it mattered.

This trip to the pool wasn't simply a bit of summer fun. It might be a chance for Manami-chan to see me, even just a little, as an individual in my own right.

“...Thanks for this, Marika. Really.”

The words slipped out under my breath, and Marika turned to look at me with a puzzled expression.

“? You're welcome. All I did was invite you to the pool.”

Her casual reply made me laugh softly.

That's right... Marika was a precious friend who had brought me into Manami-chan's life, and who continued to give me little pushes forward in the course of our everyday lives.

I didn't want this summer to end up being just another season.

I wanted these feelings to move forward, even if only by a single step.

This summer, I was going to make memories I would never forget.

With that vow in my heart, I held on tightly to both my courage and my swimsuit.

Then I set off alongside Marika towards the changing rooms.

My steps were filled with nerves and anticipation. They felt almost weightless, and yet they were carrying me, without a doubt, towards the future.
