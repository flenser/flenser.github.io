## Episode 24: What Was That? Is That a Good Sign!?

\
“...Hah... heave... there.”

With a little gasp, I planted my foot on the final step and finally reached the bottom of the slope.

Even if Manami-chan was light, my legs were starting to tremble. My thighs were twitching, and my lower back felt as though it was screaming, *Please, no more!*

And yet, the weight of Manami-chan resting against my back had somehow become as familiar as a part of my own body.

Besides, the warmth seeping through her yukata had been keeping my back nice and warm all this time. Her faint breaths against my ear were gentler than the breeze, wrapping themselves around me.

That was what kept me going.

“...Um, Yuu-san. Are you really all right? It feels like you've slowed down quite a bit...”

The little voice coming from behind me carried such obvious concern for my condition that my heart gave a warm little squeeze.

“D-Do you think so? M-Must be your imagination!”

I tried to put some force into the end of my sentence, but even I could tell. She'd seen right through me. My breathing was shallow, and my legs were heavy. But even so, I wanted to keep walking because...

“Yep, yep. Just your imagination! If you tell yourself it's all in your head, you can overcome anything! Positive thinking is important!!”

I said it brightly as I took another step.

At that very moment, Manami-chan's body shifted slightly against my back, and her cheek brushed my neck.

...I swear, that one little touch made half my muscle pain disappear. My heart gave a sudden thump. I wasn't sure whether I was disappointed that only I could hear it beating, or relieved.

“...Oh, that's right.”

Just then, Manami-chan suddenly spoke up.

“There's something I need to apologise to you for, Yuu-san.”

“If it's about today, don't worry about it, okay?”

Taking me to that secret place, the two of us sharing it together, putting on a brave face even though she was struggling.

And watching the fireworks all the way to the end, despite being in pain.

Every bit of it was a precious memory to me.

“No, it's not about today...”

Her words made me tilt my head inwardly.

Not today? Then what was it? I couldn't think of anything. All those days when I'd been falling for her, getting flustered and indulging in my own fantasies. Not once had I felt that Manami-chan had done anything wrong by me.

“Um... Do you remember when we made that promise at the bookshop? That you'd help me with my studies?”

“Ah, yeah. I remember.”

Back then, I'd practically done a victory dance inside. Finally, a chance to spend some time alone with her and have a proper chat! My heart had been practically dancing with anticipation...

But when the study sessions actually began, things hadn't quite worked out that way. Even so, they were good memories.

“...But then I went and invited my classmates without asking you first. I'm sorry. When I told them I was going to study, they all said they wanted to join us, so...”

Her voice seemed to grow a little quieter, and I almost laughed.

She was apologising for something like that? That was so like Manami-chan. So terribly conscientious.

“Oh, is that all?”

A little laugh escaped me.

Sure, I'd been a little disappointed at the time. Just a little.

I'd been so excited, looking forward to studying with Manami-chan one-on-one, and then I'd arrived to find three of her middle-school classmates waiting there.

The moment I'd seen them, my brain had frozen for a few seconds.

But looking back now, there was something about Manami-chan's expression that day that had become a rather special memory to me.

When she was talking to those girls, and when she looked at me... I felt there was a slight difference in the softness of her gaze, and even in the tone of her voice. Just a tiny difference. Or maybe I was imagining it.

But it had made me happy enough to want to believe it.

“Don't worry about it. I had quite a good time, actually.”

As soon as I said that, a little giggle fell from behind me.

“...Well, that's good, then.”

Her voice sounded just a little brighter. A little embarrassed, perhaps, and a little relieved. That alone was enough to fill my chest with a gentle warmth.

And then, just afterwards, she said:

“Next time, I won't invite anyone else. So, if you don't mind, I'd like you to help me again.”

...Huh? Huh? HUUUUH!?

An incredible noise went off somewhere deep inside me, and my brain simply couldn't process what I'd heard.

Does that mean it'll be just the two of us next time? Wait, what? Is that a good sign!? Or is she just being considerate!? Or, or, am I getting completely the wrong idea!?

While I was having an absolute meltdown inside, my mouth somehow started moving perfectly smoothly.

“Of course! I'll teach you as much as you like! I could even be your private tutor forever!”

The moment I'd finished speaking, I wanted to clamp my hand over my own mouth.

That was a bit much. I sounded like someone who'd got carried away with herself...!

Just as I'd expected, Manami-chan paused for a moment before replying in a gentle but firm voice.

“No, forever would be too much. Please make sure you leave time for yourself, Yuu-san.”

Aaaaaah...! Of course she'd say that...! But! But still! Couldn't she at least have said something like, *I wouldn't mind if it were forever*, or *Then please stay with me forever*!?

“Ugh, you're so cold...”

I wasn't sure whether she'd heard my muttered complaint, but I thought I felt Manami-chan give a little laugh against my back. That alone was enough to make all my flustered panic melt away.

I hope that someday, I can be with her without having to put on an act or pretend to be someone I'm not. I want to be able to just be myself.

“Please help me with my studies again.”

“Leave it to me! Maths, science, Japanese, anything you like!”

“Then... social studies, please.”

“Got it! I'll brush up on that!”

I was so happy that this conversation could simply carry on, as though it were the most natural thing in the world.

It would probably take another fifteen minutes to reach Manami-chan's house. In just a little while, the warmth against my back would be gone. The faint fragrance of Manami-chan, and the feeling of her arms wrapped around me, would disappear too.

Just fifteen minutes.

So I walked a little more slowly. Slowly, one step at a time, cherishing each moment... as though I were trying to commit it all to memory.

I wanted this time to last just a little longer. I silently whispered that wish somewhere deep inside my heart.

And as though answering it, Manami-chan said nothing, simply continuing to rest her weight against my back.

Even without exchanging words, I felt as though we understood each other. That feeling filled my chest with something warm and wonderful.
