## Episode 14: Now's My Chance to Make an Impression!

\
And so, the first day of our summer holiday study sessions arrived, and I was hurrying towards the Ayase household.

The arrangements had only been finalised yesterday.

*Manami wants to have a study session tomorrow. Starting at ten in the morning. Can you make it?*

That was the message I'd received from Marika. The moment my phone chimed and I saw it, I'd replied instinctively, without even checking the time.

*I can!*

Actually, “I can” wasn't quite right. The option of saying no had never existed in the first place.

I didn't care what time it was. Five in the morning, three in the middle of the night, either would have been fine. Just tell me when, and I'd be there. I'd even be willing to stand by from the previous day if necessary.

I would gladly give my life to improving Manami-chan's academic performance.

…And, well, I wouldn't even mind staying overnight. We'd study until late, and then, quite naturally, someone would say, “Why don't you stay over?” And then we'd have a pyjama party, maybe take a bath together, and spend the night alone with Manami-chan—

“No, that's not happening!!”

I didn't actually say it aloud, but I was already right on the verge of losing control. I forcibly shut down the fantasy cinema that had started screening its own feature-length production inside my head.

And then, realising that I'd also been trying to erase Marika's existence from the picture altogether, I felt a deep pang of guilt.

(*No, no, no… Marika, I'm so sorry for falling in love with your little sister…*)

The faint sweat gathering on my forehead wasn't entirely due to the summer heat.

Dragging that little burden of guilt along with me, I finally arrived at my destination: the Ayase household.

I pressed the intercom, feeling a little nervous, and someone answered almost immediately.

“Hey, Yuu! It must've been hot getting here. Come on in!”

Marika opened the door and greeted me. I gave her a little nod and stepped into the entrance hall.

(*Phew. So far, so good. Just act natural… Natural…*)

But then something caught my eye. Something seemed a little off.

(*…Aren't there rather a lot of shoes here?*)

There were three pairs of shoes I didn't recognise.

I was actually pretty confident about the shoe situation in this house. I'd visited so many times that I knew more or less who owned which pair.

I mean, I knew it was pretty creepy that I'd memorised all that in the first place. But right now, that knowledge was proving useful.

I'd definitely never seen these shoes before. Someone else was visiting today. Three people, in fact.

(*N-no way… Surely not…*)

My heart gave a heavy thump.\
My fingertips began to grow damp with sweat.

Forcing an awkward smile onto my face, I made my way towards the living room.

I gently opened the door, and—

“Ah, Yuu-san. Thank you for coming today.”

That gentle voice sank straight into the depths of my heart.

Manami-chan was sitting with a notebook resting on her lap. She looked up at me and smiled.

(*…Manami-chan… You're so cute today, too…!*)

But surrounding that angelic smile was something far more startling than a mere sense that something was out of place. The entire scene was practically a whirlwind of shock.

“““We look forward to studying with you!”””

Three cheerful voices rang out together.

Manami-chan wasn't the only one there. Sitting around her were three other girls, all around her age.

Notebooks, textbooks, reference books and pencil cases were spread across the table. They were already seated, with drinks set out in front of them. The whole room had the unmistakable atmosphere of a study session about to begin.

…Well, it's not as though anyone had promised we'd be alone together. I'd simply assumed that was how it would be. I'd been dreaming of that sweet time when the two of us would sit facing each other, lean over the same notebook, and occasionally catch each other's eyes, our hearts fluttering…

(*Ugh… I feel as though my innocent little hopes have been trampled…*)

No, it wasn't as if anyone had deliberately played a trick on me, or lied to me.

It was just that the tiny dream I'd been harbouring inside me, that faint hope of having a study session alone with Manami-chan, had been thoroughly crushed underfoot.

Even so, with a little thorn-like ache lodged deep in my chest, I put on a calm expression and walked towards the middle of the room.

(*…No, that's not right. It isn't like that…*)

A voice inside my head tried to reason with me.

(*Manami-chan probably thought it'd be more efficient to study together as a group. She didn't want to put all the responsibility on me, so she asked the other girls to help out, too. Yes, that's it! She wants us to share the teaching between us…*)

That's right. It had to be.

Manami-chan was a kind, considerate girl. She always thought about the people around her and acted with their needs in mind, rather than just her own. She really was… really was such a good girl, but…!

Still! I was just a little bit disappointed! Just a tiny, tiny bit! I'd been looking forward to it so much!!

Just the two of us in the living room. Manami-chan would spread out her workbook and say, with a slightly troubled expression, “I'm having a little difficulty with this part…” Then I'd explain, “Well, here's how you do it,” and we'd naturally grow closer, until our shoulders happened to touch. My heart would race, but I'd pretend to be perfectly calm…

And then, during a break, we'd have some tea and I'd casually ask, “Are you going anywhere during the summer holidays?” If she replied, “I don't really have any plans yet…”, I'd say, “Then would you like to go somewhere together? Maybe the fireworks festival?” And then… and then…!

(*Aaaaaahhhhhh!!*)

The continuation of that dream just wouldn't stop playing in my head, and I was on the verge of quietly collapsing inside.

That was when it happened.

“Yuu-san, please sit here.”

A gentle voice reached my ears. I looked up to see Manami-chan smiling warmly at me, indicating an empty seat with a small gesture of her hand.

(*Ah…*)

That seat was…

Right next to Manami-chan.

(*…I-I love you…!*)

My heart leapt in an instant. It was pounding so loudly in my chest, my face felt hot, and I could barely breathe. It was as though I'd been forcibly strapped into a roller coaster of love.

Where had all my disappointment from a moment ago gone? With just one little comment and one simple gesture, my heart was soaring high into the sky again.

(*W-wait a minute!? What's this!? Is this fate!? Has God finally decided to do some work!?*)

But I couldn't let myself get carried away just yet.

(*No, calm down, me. If I get too conscious of her and start acting suspiciously, all my efforts so far will have gone to waste…!*)

I took a quiet, deep breath and sat down, doing my best to look natural.

And right beside me was Manami-chan.

We were only a little over ten centimetres apart. The hem of her skirt swayed beneath the table, and it felt as though the slightest movement might bring us into contact.

(*This distance… This is exactly the distance where opportunities happen…!*)

I took another look around the room.

Manami-chan and the three other middle-school girls had each opened their notebooks and workbooks. My job was to teach them…

(*All right. Let's do this…!*)

I took my favourite mechanical pencil out of my pencil case. My fingers tightened ever so slightly around it.

The number of students had increased from one, Manami-chan, to four, but that didn't change what I had to do. If anything, this was my chance to make an impression! I was going to play the part of the ideal older girl to perfection: good at explaining things, easy to understand, and someone they could rely on!

I'd make all of them think, “Yuu-san is amazing!” And among them, I'd make sure that Manami-chan, in particular, thought, “Maybe this person really is special…”

A determination began to burn steadily deep inside my heart.\
Only by balancing love and studying could I become a true big sister!

And so, the hottest and brightest operation of the summer was about to begin…
