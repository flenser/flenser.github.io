## Chapter 5 - Oh No… I’m in Love…

\
When I took off my shoes in the entrance hall, I found myself neatly lining them up without even thinking. Normally, I’d just shove them off to one side, but today, for some reason, I was being unusually meticulous.

I was about to step inside the home of the person I loved. Perhaps the thought of taking such a huge step was what made me behave that way.

Then, as I followed Manami-chan further inside, my back suddenly straightened.

Because walking in front of me was Manami-chan, in her sailor uniform…

Her swaying black hair, the occasional glimpse of the nape of her neck, her slender, straight legs. Every little thing caught my eye and sent my thoughts into a spin. Honestly… every step she took made my heart beat louder.

“S-so, by the way, where’s Marika’s room?”

Trying to distract myself, I struck up a conversation. Manami-chan stopped dead and turned to face me.

“Miyanomori-san, before that, please make sure you wash your hands properly. The washroom is over there.”

“…Oh, yes!”

I was so startled that my voice nearly squeaked.

She just called me “Miyanomori-san”…! She said my name…! She finally remembered it!! And I think there was something rather gentle about her tone…! Wooooooah…!!

…For a moment, my thoughts threatened to explode.

But then, a realisation hit me like a bucket of cold water.

Wait, hold on. “Wash your hands”… that was a telling-off, wasn’t it!? “Please wash your hands”, that’s what she meant, right!? In other words, I was about to stroll into a sick person’s room without even washing my hands first, as if it were perfectly normal. Oh no, I’m the worst…! And a middle-school girl had to point it out to me… I’m so, so embarrassed!

“P-please excuse me…”

I gave a small bow and headed to the washroom as instructed.

When I opened the door, I found myself in a spacious, immaculately tidy room. The washbasin was white, without a speck of dirt along its edges. The mirror was sparkling clean, and I caught sight of my reflection. I had to do a double take.

(*Whoa… I look so lovestruck…*)

My cheeks were probably red because I’d been flustered out of my mind this whole time. Even my eyes looked feverish. It was painfully obvious that, despite pretending to be calm, I was anything but.

My gaze happened to fall on a cup that looked like a toothbrush holder. Inside were two toothbrushes, one pink and one light blue, standing side by side.

(*Could one of these… belong to Manami-chan…? Wait, what am I looking at!? What is wrong with me!? Idiot! Pervert! Don't go getting all excited over toothbrushes!*)

“Haah… No, honestly, I need to calm down.”

I shook my head vigorously and, in an attempt to collect myself, pressed the pump on the hand soap dispenser with all my might. A mound of foam bubbled out and spread across my palms. I focused on its texture, carefully rubbing between my fingers, over the backs of my hands and beneath my nails.

(*Calm down… Calm down… If you start acting weird in front of Manami-chan, it's all over…*)

I was in the middle of giving myself that stern lecture when it happened.

“Excuse me.”

“!!!!!!!!”

A small but clear voice came from right beside me, and my hands stopped moving for an instant.

I looked to the side. There she was, right next to me. So close that our shoulders were practically touching. In the mirror, I could see the two of us standing side by side.

(*Eeeeeh!? Why!? Was there even room in here for two people!?*)

Her profile entered the edge of my vision. Smooth, pale cheeks. Long eyelashes casting shadows, a beautifully straight nose, and a tiny little beauty spot that somehow caught my attention.

The sheer closeness made my head spin, and my breathing grew shallow.

…Then a sweet fragrance drifted softly past my nose.

Shampoo? Fabric softener? Or was it Manami-chan herself? What was that smell…? Oh no… I love it…! There's no way I can stay calm like this!

Then my eyes fell on her fingertips. Manami-chan's slender, long fingers were gently working the foam into her hands.

…Beautiful… So delicate… No, stop it! I told myself not to! But how can I not look!? She's right in front of me! Aaaaaah, I love her so much…!!

“Um… Aren't you going to rinse them?”

“Huh? Ah, ahhh! S-sorry!!”

Manami-chan gave me a puzzled look, and I snapped back to my senses, hurriedly rinsing the foam from my hands.

Once I'd finished washing them, I took the towel hanging beside the washbasin, murmuring, “I'll borrow this,” and dried my hands. It was soft and fluffy, with a faint scent of soap. Even that was enough to set my heart fluttering again.

(*Could this towel be Manami-chan's favourite…? No, no, no! I told you not to think about things like that!*)

I covered my face with my hands and took a deep breath. This time, I really would get a hold of myself. I was just about to leave the washroom when…

“Oh, you don't know where my big sister's room is, do you?”

“N-no. The last time I came over was my first time…”

I answered honestly, and Manami-chan smiled.

“Go up the stairs and turn left for my big sister's room. Mine is on the right. There are nameplates on the doors, so I don't think you'll mix them up.”

(*Nameplates!? Does that mean… the kind with their names written on them!? Maybe Manami-chan's name is on hers!? Perhaps in some sort of pretty cursive lettering, or a cute font, with little hearts or something!*)

“Thank you!”

I somehow managed to squeeze out the words, forcing my panicking brain back under control as I gave her a little bow.

My heart was racing again. My face felt hot. My head was spinning.

Oh no, oh no, oh no!! This atmosphere is way too much!! At this rate, I'm seriously going to say something weird! I need to get out of here before that happens… But, but…!

(*I want her to say my name one more time…*)

And just like that, the angel and devil inside my head launched into a furious argument.

(*Yuu, calm down. You're here to visit Marika, remember?*)

(*Don't be silly, Yuu! You have to seize this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity!*)

(*Manami-chan thought your name was Miyaura, remember?*)

(*But she still called you Miyanomori-san! This is your chance! Make your move! Fortune favours the bold!*)

(*And what exactly are you going to do if it doesn't work!? Besides—*)

Oh, shut up already!

I shoved the bickering angel and devil into a corner of my mind and started up the stairs at a brisk trot.

(*All right, Manami-chan's room is on the right♪ Manami-chan's room is on the right♪*)

I repeated it over and over in my head like a mantra, desperately trying to make sure I wouldn't get the rooms mixed up. Actually, since I was liable to wander over there without even thinking, I needed to put a stop to that in advance.

At the top of the stairs, I saw two doors facing each other.

Without thinking, I naturally made my way towards the one on the right and reached for the doorknob—

“…No, no, no! Wait, wait, wait!!”

That was close…! I'd nearly walked straight into Manami-chan's room! I'd been so busy repeating “Manami-chan's room is on the right” in my head that I'd completely forgotten she'd said it was the left!

What an idiot I was… No, actually, I really was an idiot.

A cute little nameplate hung on the door to the right. It had a pink border scattered with tiny hearts, and the name “Manami” was written in pale blue lettering.

(*Whoa… Her name really is on it…*)

Just seeing it made my heart tighten. Even imagining the world waiting for me on the other side of that door was enough to make my cheeks burn.

“I wonder if I'll get to go in there someday…”

I let the words slip out in a tiny murmur, then caught myself and hurriedly shook my head.

(*What are you saying!? It's not as if you can just walk in there! Idiot, idiot, idiot!*)

I gave myself a thorough scolding in my head, and it helped me regain at least a little of my composure.

Standing in front of the door on the left, I saw that it had a little nameplate of its own. “Marika” was written in a bright, playful font. Somehow, it felt very much like her.

Pulling myself together, I called out softly, as though knocking.

“Marika? It's me. I'm coming in, okay?”

I slowly turned the doorknob and gently pushed the door open.

It felt as though my first love had taken another unexpected step forward.
