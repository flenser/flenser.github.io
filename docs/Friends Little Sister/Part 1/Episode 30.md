## Episode 30: A Blank Chat Room

\
On our way back to the station after finishing our shopping, the sky, which had been clear just moments earlier, suddenly began to cloud over. And I don't mean it merely became overcast. In the blink of an eye, it turned pitch-black, and at the same time...

“Huh!? You're kidding!”

“Oh no! I don't have an umbrella!”

The sound of raindrops hammering the ground spread all around us. People nearby cried out in alarm and hurried beneath shop awnings and the sheltered entrances of convenience stores. The sky grew so dark that it was hard to believe it had been broad daylight just moments before.

“It'll take us a little longer, but let's take the bus home. Even with umbrellas, our shoes would probably get soaked.”

It was Marika who suggested that. Manami-chan and I nodded, and the three of us made our way along the covered walkways towards a nearby bus stop.

When we arrived, several other people were already sheltering beneath the roof. We naturally lined up behind them, standing together.

We left just enough space between us that our shoulders didn't quite touch. But every now and then, Manami-chan's sleeve would softly brush against my arm. Each time, I felt something inside me about to explode, and had to remind myself, *This is dangerous... Calm down.*

*Actually, I wouldn't mind if it rained like this every single day!*

That's how happy I was with the situation, even though I knew how utterly impure that thought was.

Eventually, the bus arrived. We climbed aboard and found an empty row of three seats towards the back, so we naturally headed there.

Marika sat in the middle first, with me on her left and Manami-chan on her right.

...I let out a tiny sigh inside my head.

If only I'd sat in the middle first, I could have been beside Manami-chan. It was such a tiny thing, but I was frustrated. Incredibly frustrated.

The bus pulled away and slowly began climbing the hill. Each time the vehicle swayed slightly, the vibrations of the seat travelled gently through my body. It was strangely soothing, and I found myself relaxing. Outside the window, raindrops struck the glass at an angle, leaving thin trails of water as they trickled down.

That was when something suddenly settled softly against my shoulder.

“M-Marika?”

I turned to look. Marika was fast asleep, her head bobbing gently as she breathed.

I couldn't imagine today's shopping trip had been tiring enough to make her fall asleep like this, though...

Beside her, Manami-chan quietly spoke.

“My sister was watching television until quite late last night, so I think she's probably sleep-deprived.”

“I see... Well, I suppose I'll just have to be her pillow, then.”

I said it lightly, as though making a joke. But inside, I was thinking...

*If only it were Manami-chan instead.*

If Manami-chan were to lean gently against me like that, I could stay perfectly still and be her pillow for an entire night.

...She was sitting right next to me, separated only by Marika, and yet that little distance felt as vast as the universe.

It was still raining outside. The rhythm of the raindrops against the bus window sounded soft and gentle, almost like a lullaby, and my heart gradually began to settle.

And then it happened.

Manami-chan suddenly leaned just a little towards me and turned her gaze in my direction. Her long eyelashes and beautifully formed profile made my heart skip a beat. The next moment, her lips moved softly...

“By the way, Yuu-san, would you like to exchange contact details?”

...Huh?

My thoughts came to a complete halt, as though someone had slammed on the brakes.

What... did she just say? Contact details...? Exchange them...? With me? Manami-chan...?

“...Pardon?”

It was a completely vacant response. I'd been caught so off guard that my brain couldn't keep up. But Manami-chan didn't seem at all fazed by my reaction. In the same calm, gentle voice, she continued.

“Whenever you help me with my studies, we've always arranged things through my sister, haven't we? I thought it might be quicker if I contacted you directly. But only if you don't mind, of course.”

*...Oh, I see.*

A perfectly sensible reason. A practical decision. There was no romantic implication, no suggestion that she had any special feelings for me.

But...

Even so, I was happy.

At last, I'd be able to communicate with Manami-chan directly. It might have been a tiny change, but to me, it felt like something that could turn my entire world upside down.

“It's no trouble at all! That would be... yeah, much more convenient!”

I could hear how giddy my voice sounded. I hurriedly covered my mouth, but it was already too late. I couldn't stop myself from grinning either.

We each took out our phones and exchanged contact details.

Even though she was sitting right there beside me, the simple act of tapping away at our screens felt strangely ticklish. The reality of exchanging contact details with Manami-chan was gradually taking shape right there in front of me, through the screen, and I was simply, overwhelmingly happy.

Trying to hide the trembling in my fingers, I finished adding her contact and glanced down at the screen.

The new name displayed there was **Ayase Manami**.

When I tapped it, I found an empty chat room, not a single message in it yet.

And just that was enough to make something deep inside my chest give a little squeeze. My heart was pounding with nervous excitement.

I wanted to ask, but I was a little afraid of the answer. Even so, I gathered my courage and spoke.

“H-hey, Manami-chan. ...Would it be all right if I sent you messages about things other than studying, too?”

It was a question I'd finally managed to voice, mixing a tiny bit of hope with a whole lot of anxiety.

Manami-chan tilted her head slightly, looking a little puzzled, then gave me a soft smile.

“? Yes, that's fine. I don't check my phone very often, though, so I might not be able to reply straight away.”

“N-no, that's completely fine! Really! Just whenever you have some free time! Whenever you feel like it!”

Ahhh, my voice cracked again...!

But that was all right. Because she'd given me permission. She'd actually told me herself that it was okay to send her messages.

That alone lit a tiny flame inside my heart.

*Right! I'll send her something. I really will! But not so much that I become annoying, okay? I'll start with something light. Maybe, 'Thanks for today.' Should I avoid emojis to seem a little more mature? But what if that makes me sound cold?*

*Then again, a heart at the end of a message is probably too soon... Or is it perfectly normal between girls? Oh, and what about polite language? If I become too casual, will I seem overfamiliar? But I've always been inconsistent about whether I use polite language with Manami-chan or not...*

My thoughts kept spinning round and round, refusing to stop. Meanwhile, the bus slowly descended the hill, and thin streams of rain continued running down the windows.

The world outside, which had looked grey just moments ago, now seemed somehow a little brighter. It was the same familiar scenery, but it was amazing how differently everything could look when your feelings changed.

It was almost like magic.

Marika was still asleep beside me, her head resting against my shoulder. Feeling its weight, I quietly glanced towards Manami-chan.

Today, I'd certainly taken one step closer to her. It might only have been a single step, but it was a definite one.

Exchanging contact details didn't mean that love would suddenly begin. But I'd gained the possibility of making it begin.

The name displayed on my phone screen. The chat room where we hadn't exchanged a single word yet.

It felt like a ticket to a future that was still completely blank. Just looking at it made me happy.

*Perhaps, someday, we might even become a couple.*

For now, it was enough simply to dream about it. This racing heartbeat was the greatest treasure I had.

I gently adjusted my shoulder to support Marika's head, then turned my gaze towards the rain outside the window. It was still falling, but inside my heart, a little light had undoubtedly begun to shine.
