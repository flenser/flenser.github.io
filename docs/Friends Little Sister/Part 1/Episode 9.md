## Episode 9: The Best Day of My Life

\
“Then, if you’ll excuse me.”

With those words, Manami-chan gave a small bow and slipped down into the space between Marika and me without the slightest hesitation.

(*Wait, what? There!? In the middle!? Of all places!?*)

I was on the left, Marika on the right, and right between us, sitting smack in the middle, was Manami-chan.

Her petite frame settled into the space with such casual ease, as though there were nothing at all unusual about it.

(*No, wait, she just plopped herself down! No, no, no, no, I can't! I'm so happy, but still! ...Actually, am I even alive right now? Or have I already ascended to heaven!?*)

It might have looked like the most ordinary little movement, but to me it was a momentous event. Because from this very instant, Manami-chan's shoulder was ever so slightly touching mine.

“Yuu, hurry up and pick your character!”

Marika's voice came flying towards me, but I couldn't respond. Of course I couldn't. I could hear her, but I wasn't in any state to do anything about it.

Right now, a grand volley of celebratory fireworks was exploding inside my head. My heart was pounding so hard it was practically a fireworks festival. I could almost hear the background music playing, too.

The racing game's characters were lined up on the screen, waiting for us to choose, but I couldn't bring myself to press a single button.

(*I mean, come on! How am I supposed to operate anything in this situation!? Our shoulders are touching... They're touching!!*)

And then it happened.

“? Is this ‘Yuu’ here your name, Miyanomori-san?”

Manami-chan suddenly turned to look at me.

The way she tilted her head was already unfair. Those innocent eyes gazed up at me from close enough to touch, like some adorable little animal, and my breathing stopped for a moment.

“Ah, y-yeah! It's Yuu, with the character for freedom and the one for universe...”

“I see. Since we're here, would it be all right if I called you Yuu-san from now on? That is, if you don't mind, Miyanomori-san...”

She paused for a moment, then smiled gently. My entire field of vision turned white.

(*S-S-S-SHE'S GOING TO CALL ME BY MY NAMEEEEE!?*)

My ears were hot. My eyes were hot. At this rate, my heart could explode at any moment. Not Miyanomori-san, but Yuu-san. Just that tiny change made it feel as though the distance between us had suddenly closed.

“N-no, of course I don't mind! Call me whatever you like!”

“All right, Yuu-san.”

(*Uuugh... Thank you... Thank you, Mum and Dad... Thank you so much for giving me a short, easy-to-pronounce name...*)

I'd never felt such heartfelt gratitude before, but right now I wanted to thank my parents with all my heart. I wanted to send them a handwritten letter of appreciation. Seriously... I'd write one as soon as I got home.

Oblivious to my emotional upheaval, Manami-chan turned back towards the screen and gripped her controller firmly. Even her serious profile was adorable, and inside my head, the emotional roller coaster was already making more laps than I could count.

Perhaps having finally given up on waiting for me to choose a character, Marika reached over and pressed my button herself.

“Right, we're starting with random, then!”

And so the race began, but I couldn't settle down from the very first moment. Because it turned out that Manami-chan was the sort of person who moved her body along with the game.

“Ah, an item... Oh! Big Sis, move! That's mine!”

“Too bad, it's mine! Here, have a banana.”

“Uuugh... Honestly, Big Sis!”

Manami-chan's reactions were far more expressive than I'd ever have imagined from her usually composed demeanour. When her character jumped, she gave a little hop herself. When the track took a sharp turn, she leaned her body into it, and whenever someone stole an item from her, she puffed out her lips ever so slightly in a sulk.

(*S-She's so cute...!! She's cute from every possible angle...!!*)

But there was an even bigger problem. Manami-chan moved around a lot more than I'd expected.

Whenever she leaned into a sharp turn, her body would swing sharply to the side, bumping right into mine.

At that instant, it felt as though an electric current shot through my entire body, and my heart leapt violently.

(*Aaaaaaaaaaaaaah...!!!*)

While my thoughts were exploding like that, my hands were shaking just as badly.

I couldn't keep the controller's stick steady. When I should have been pressing the accelerator, I somehow hit the brake instead. At the jump ramp, I sailed straight off the track.

“Oi, Yuu!? What happened to that racing prodigy from earlier!?”

“Marika... Seriously... thank you so much...”

“Why are you thanking me!?”

As Marika's laughter rang out, my consciousness had already drifted completely away from the game.

After that, Manami-chan continued to sway from side to side, completely absorbed in the screen. She leaned desperately into every turn, touching me without the slightest hesitation.

Sometimes it was just a light bump. At other times, she practically leaned her entire weight against me...

(*No, no, no, no, no!!!*)

Every time it happened, my heart rate shot up, my vision went all wobbly, and operating my character became the least of my concerns. I fell off cliffs, failed to collect items properly, and sank steadily down the rankings.

“Ah, Yuu-san fell off.”

Manami-chan's voice rose in surprise. But there wasn't the slightest hint of reproach in it. If anything, she sounded rather amused.

“This is fun, isn't it, Big Sis?”

“Right? This game is great when you play against other people. The races tend to be really close, so you can't let your guard down until the very end. Yuu was pretty strong earlier, but now she's practically a shadow of her former self...”

“I see. ...Ah, Yuu-san fell off again.”

Manami-chan was smiling. Even as I fell hopelessly behind, she was still wearing a smile that showed just how much she was enjoying the game.

(*I mean, she's too cute... How am I supposed to withstand this...?*)

And on top of that, little by little... really, only a few millimetres at a time, but I felt as though Manami-chan was gradually edging closer to me.

I wanted to believe I was imagining it. But I was pretty sure I wasn't.

Because right now, the entire side of our bodies was pressed together.

(*I can't... I really can't... I'm so happy... Maybe I could just pass out right here and be perfectly content...*)

“Hey, Yuu, what place are you in? How are you feeling right now?”

“...I-I don't care...!”

“You're dead last, aren't you!? That's hilarious!”

Marika was laughing her head off, but her voice sounded as though it were drifting towards me from somewhere far away.

All of my attention was focused on the girl beside me. The fact that our shoulders were touching. The fact that she'd called me by my name. The fact that she was smiling. Every little thing was blossoming inside my chest like a flower.

(*Could it be... that this means... we're practically going out already!?*)

Well, I knew perfectly well that this was a fantasy with absolutely no basis in reality. But when my heart was this full, I felt as though I could be forgiven for thinking absolutely anything.

And then, at last, the race came to an end and the results appeared.

Marika came first, Manami-chan came third, and...

I came last.

But I didn't care about the rankings any more. Today, I'd gained something far more precious than winning or losing a game.

And finally, Manami-chan smiled and said:

“Hehe, that was fun. Let's play again sometime, Yuu-san, if we get the chance.”

Those words remained in my heart like a precious jewel.

She'd smiled and called me “Yuu-san”.

She'd promised that we'd play together again.

(*Ah... Today... This day is, without a doubt, the best day of my entire life...*)

I carefully tucked those words away in my heart, treasuring them like a good-luck charm, and gently hugged the controller to my chest.
