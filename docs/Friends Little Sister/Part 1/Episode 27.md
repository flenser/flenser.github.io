## Episode 27: That's Such an Angelic Summer Outfit!?

\
Under the pretext of teaching Manami-chan, I've been savouring the greatest reward imaginable day after day. But no matter how many times we spend time together, the distance between us never seems to shrink quite as much as I'd hoped.

Even so, I'm happy just to have time together with her... Wait, does that mean my brain has completely turned into a field of flowers? Well, it's true, so what can I do!?

It was a summer holiday afternoon, a little past lunchtime.

Relying on the air conditioner, which wasn't doing a particularly good job, I was trying to get on with my homework. Thanks to it, the words in my workbook were gradually blurring before my eyes, and sweat was gathering on my forehead. I just couldn't concentrate.

Just then, my phone gave a little buzz on the desk.

“Hmm?”

As usual, the name displayed on the screen was “Marika”.

“Hellooo?”

“Yuuuu! It's too hot! I can't take it! I'm melting! Tell me something that'll cool me down~. I'm seriously drying up here...”

“Don't spring such an unreasonable request on me out of nowhere. Let's see... How about this? Imagine you've just come home from your summer holiday trip, Marika.”

“Mhm, mhm.”

“You walk into your room, and your favourite character's figurine has melted in the heat――”

“...!! That gave me chills! Forget cooling me down, I've got goosebumps all over!”

I thought I heard something fall on the other end of the phone. Perhaps I'd gone a little too far.

“Don't do that to me... It's bad for my heart. I genuinely turned into an empty shell just now.”

“Sorry, sorry. All right, then. Why don't you come up with something that'll cool me down?”

“Hmmm... Then let's cool down in the real world! Let's go to the swimming pool! The pool!”

“Ooh! Now that's something that'll physically cool us down!”

Floating in the cold water, diving under, then bobbing back up again... Ahh, just thinking about it sounds wonderful!

But then, a certain image suddenly appeared in my mind: Manami-chan in a swimsuit.

I'd love to see that... No, no! I mustn't let my desires run wild!

But I couldn't exactly casually suggest, “Let's invite Manami-chan too!” If I asked, “Are we going just the two of us?” and Marika replied, “Is there anyone else you'd like to invite?”, I'd be in trouble...

Hmmmm.

Well, never mind. Even if it was just the two of us, Marika and me.

“So, what do you say? Coming or not?”

“I'm coming, I'm coming! Let's cool off to our hearts' content!”

“Great! Then let's go swimsuit shopping first! Meet me outside the station in thirty minutes. Let's have ourselves a nice, cool date!”

“That's sudden! But I'm free, so roger that!”

After ending the call, I put my phone down and stared blankly up at the ceiling.

A swimsuit... A swimsuit, huh? There's no way I can wear the one I had in middle school any more. I've grown a little taller, and I've put on a bit of weight lately. Ah, but that's just happy weight gain from being in love...! Yes, perfect excuse!

...By the way, what kind of cruel joke is it that my clothes size goes up when my chest hasn't grown at all? What on earth is wrong with the world's sense of balance?

“Well, whatever... I wonder what sort of swimsuit I should get~”

Something sporty would be nice, but I couldn't rule out something a little cute, either... It's not as though I'll be wearing it in front of Manami-chan, but maybe I might show her some photos or something... Wait, there's absolutely no plan to show her any photos!

As I fixed a stray lock of hair in front of the mirror, a fashion show was already beginning in my head.

What colour would suit me? White? Light blue? Or should I play it safe with navy? Aaaah, so many choices!

Still, it had been a while since I'd felt this excited while getting ready to go out. Summer always seemed to promise so many fun things.

With a little anticipation and a few fantasies tucked away in my heart, I picked up my bag and left the house.

◇

“Yuu! You're late!”

The moment I arrived outside the station, Marika's familiar, energetic voice rang out. There she was, waving her arms enthusiastically. And standing beside her was――

“Sorry, Marika, did I keep you wai―― Eeeeeeeeeh!?!?”

My voice shot up in surprise.

Because the person standing next to Marika was――

“Manami... chan!?”

This wasn't a dream, was it? I wasn't hallucinating, was I? Manami-chan was right there. Standing in the sunlight of the station plaza, dressed in her own clothes, looking just a little bashful!?

“Wh-wh-wh-why is Manami-chan here!?”

“Well, she seemed to have nothing on today, so I thought I'd invite her.”

“Hello, Yuu-san. I'm sorry for intruding on your outing together.”

“N-no! You're not intruding at all!”

That was close. I nearly blurted out, “In fact, you're more than welcome! Thank you so much! Are you a goddess!?” but somehow managed to swallow the words before they escaped.

Calm down, me. Don't lose your composure. ...But how am I supposed to do that!?

Just look at what Manami-chan is wearing today! A white sleeveless top and a light beige skirt. Isn't that an angelic summer outfit!? Her shoulders are bare! Her upper arms are dazzling! Her hair sways softly in the breeze, catching the sunlight until it seems almost translucent... Uwaah, the sheer destructive power of seeing her in her casual clothes is too much...!

“Hmm? What's wrong, Yuu? Your eyes are all watery.”

“It's just something getting in my eye, that's all! Yep!”

The impact of seeing Manami-chan's smile had nearly made my tear ducts explode. That was a close one. Seriously.

But then I remembered where we were going, and my heart leapt all over again.

A swimsuit department.

She might try some on.

Manami-chan... in a swimsuit...

Nooooooooo!!! I can't take it!!!!

This is a trial. The goddess is testing me...! Testing whether I can withstand seeing Manami-chan in a swimsuit! ...No, wait! What am I thinking!? I can't let my mind wander into such depraved territory!

I have to keep my overwhelming love for Manami-chan completely off my face, out of my behaviour, and out of my voice. Full defensive mode! Otherwise, she'll think I'm some kind of weirdo... No, worse, she'll start to dislike me...!

“Right! Now that Yuu's here, let's get going!”

“That's right. ...Yuu-san? Aren't you coming?”

“Y-yes! I'm coming!”

Manami-chan spoke to me, and I jolted in response.

Oh, honestly! I'm acting so suspiciously. Why did my voice jump up like that? But from now on, I'll get to walk around the shopping centre with Manami-chan, be there while she chooses a swimsuit, and maybe... just maybe... there's a chance she'll let me see her trying one on...!

No! My face is starting to grin! Aaaah, if only I'd at least done some sit-ups yesterday! I curse myself for eating two ice creams!

“Is my stomach... getting flabby...?”

“Hm? Did you say something, Yuu?”

“N-no, nothing! Yep, let's go, let's go!”

I covered it up with a smile, while inside I was desperately tightening my abdominal muscles.

Pull it in! Pull it in! My dignity and my love life are on the line!

And so the three of us set off together. Manami-chan moved just a little closer to me and smiled gently.

“It's actually the first time I've gone out with both you and Big Sister together like this. It feels rather new.”

That one little comment made my heart spark.

Manami-chan was beside me, walking in the same direction, smiling.

...Yes. Today might become one of my happiest memories ever. No, I'll make absolutely sure it does!
