## Episode 19: So She Trusts Me with Her Future, Too!?

\
The two weeks that followed were a whirlwind of activity.

I started visiting Marika's house regularly, and spending the mornings working through our summer homework together had become a completely established part of my daily routine.

Then, at lunchtime, it was off to the kitchen. My assignment was to prepare one of the side dishes for lunch. Marika would stand beside me, carefully teaching me everything from how to cut the ingredients to controlling the heat and knowing when to add the seasonings.

“When you're frying onions, you want them to become translucent. Just don't let them burn.”\
“Huh? But how far can I go before they're considered burnt...?”\
“Yuu, that's something you judge by the smell, not the colour.”\
“But you literally just told me to judge by the colour!”

We repeated exchanges like that over and over, and little by little, ever so gradually, my hands became more accustomed to holding a frying pan.

But there was something even more special than cooking that was driving my motivation.

And of course, that was Manami-chan.

Naturally, I didn't get to see her every day. It was summer holidays for Manami-chan, too. She had friends from our previous study sessions to go out with, and surely she had her own precious time that she wanted to spend doing things for herself.

I was the one who kept showing up uninvited. For Manami-chan, I might have been someone whose presence made no difference at all.

Even so, whenever she happened to be home, I'd ask, “Would you mind trying something I've made?” And every time, she'd agree to eat it. At first, my hands trembled with nerves, but the moment Manami-chan put a piece into her mouth became my daily reward.

“...It's not very good.”\
“It's not bad.”\
“It's something I'm not keen on eating, so...”\
“Ah... It's delicious.”

Even the briefest comments like these made my heart flutter. Every little change in her expression sent me soaring or crashing. Whether things went well or badly, I treasured every single reaction and remembered it all.

Before I knew it, I'd even learnt which foods Manami-chan liked, which vegetables she disliked, and what sort of seasoning she preferred.

It was almost as though I wasn't visiting every day to practise cooking, but to get to know Manami-chan.

...But.

No matter how often I visited, or how well I learnt her tastes, my position never changed. I was still nothing more than “Big Sister's friend”.

My relationship with Manami-chan remained exactly the same. The way she looked at me so directly wasn't anything special. Even when she said something was delicious, it was probably just a kind compliment for “Big Sister's friend who was doing her best”.

――Probably.

But that wasn't a reason to give up.

For now, it was fine if I was Marika's friend. But someday, surely...

It was all right to believe that, wasn't it?

Holding on to that feeling, I continued learning to cook.

Secretly trying to disguise the peppers I'd burnt to a crisp, while searching once again for the flavours Manami-chan liked.

◇

Then, one day, shortly before noon, with the sun beating down on our skin, I found myself walking along a scorching-hot road with Marika.

There was only one reason we'd ended up in this situation. We'd been queuing together since morning so Marika could get her hands on some limited-edition merchandise she'd absolutely had to have.

“Ahhh, finally got them! Thanks, Yuu. I managed to get two because of you!”

Marika said with satisfaction, swinging her shopping bag back and forth.

“Don't mention it. Still, it's ridiculously hot today...”

“Apparently, today is the hottest day of the week. That's what the weather forecast said.”

“Oh? What about next week?”

“It's going to be even hotter than today.”

“Ehhhh...”

The sky was impossibly blue, and the cicadas were screaming as though they'd gone mad. Wiping the sweat sticking to my face, I zigzagged along the pavement in search of patches of shade. We'd already finished our drinks, and the heat was so intense I felt as though I'd melt and evaporate within the next five minutes.

As the heat began to make my thoughts grow hazy, my imagination naturally started wandering.

On a day like this, I'd love to go to the swimming pool with Manami-chan. Just the two of us. I wonder what sort of swimsuit she'd wear. A school swimsuit? Or perhaps... something like a frilly one-piece, or maybe she'd wear a rash vest to protect herself from the sun...?

Uwaaah, no, I can't! Just imagining it has made me even hotter! This isn't heatstroke. It's Manami-chan fever!

So this is what they mean by the sickness of love...

I was busy thinking such ridiculous thoughts when a poster stuck to a telephone pole caught my eye.

*“○○ River Summer Fireworks Festival! This Saturday!”*

It featured a nostalgic-looking background, with an illustration of fireworks blooming brilliantly across the sky.

“Ah... So it's this Saturday. That's a problem.”

Marika stopped and murmured as she gazed at the poster.

“Hmm? What's wrong?”

“Well, there's a limited-edition merchandise release in central Tokyo that day. It's my favourite character's swimsuit version, so there's no way I can miss it. I'd never forgive myself if I died without getting it.”

“It's tough being so devoted to your favourite character, huh?”

“But there's a bit of a problem... Oh, hey, Yuu.”

“What?”

“Could you go to the fireworks festival with Manami that day?”

“...Hwaaaah?”

My brain stopped functioning for a moment. The cicadas, the heat radiating from the asphalt, everything seemed to recede into the distance.

What did she just say...?

“W-w-wait! Why would that happen!?”

“Well, Manami looks forward to it every year, but I can't go this time. On the other hand, I'd be worried about letting her go alone, so I thought I'd feel better if you went along with her.”

“Does that mean... you trust me to look after her!? That I'm someone you can rely on for the future, too!?”

“I'm not sure what future you're talking about, but yeah, I do trust you.”

“Really!? You're sure!? It's okay!?”

“I'm the one asking you for a favour here, remember? I'll check with Manami first, just to be sure, but I doubt she'd mind going with you.”

You trust me to look after her. As your replacement, as someone to accompany her...

Even so, I'd get to go to the fireworks festival with Manami-chan.

This could only be described as a miracle sent down from heaven.

“I'LL GO!! I'D BE DELIGHTED TO!! I'll escort her with all my might, protect her from danger, and do absolutely everything I can to make sure Manami-chan says she had a wonderful time!!!”

“...Right. You're certainly enthusiastic. Did you really want to go to the fireworks festival that badly?”

“Yes, Lady Marika! Thank you so much!! I promise I'll repay this kindness!! I'll buy Manami-chan yakisoba, cotton candy, absolutely anything she wants from the festival stalls!!”

“R-right. I mean, I'm the one who suggested it, but do keep an eye on your spending, okay? And don't buy a stall. Buy something *from* a stall. Yakisoba, for instance.”

Marika laughed with a slightly bemused expression, but my head was already completely occupied with thoughts of my fireworks date with Manami-chan.

Or rather, what I was choosing to interpret as a date.

Her wearing a yukata, holding cotton candy, the two of us walking through the crowds and perhaps even holding hands because it was so busy...

No, no, that's too soon! Get a grip, Yuu...!

But I couldn't stop smiling.

...The heat? The sun? What's that? Right now, Manami-chan's existence is far more dazzling to me than any of that!

Drenched in sweat, but walking with a lighter step than anyone else, I set off alongside Marika once more.
