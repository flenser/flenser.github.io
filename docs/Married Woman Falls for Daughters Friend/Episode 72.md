## Chapter Four: The Snow Melts into the Clod of Earth - Part Sixteen

\
And then.\
After what might have been longer than half a lifetime.\
When I got home after dropping Kai off at the hotel, my daughter had already returned. Apparently unaware even of my arrival, she sat in a chair, resting her cheek against one hand and staring vacantly into space. Then she murmured, looking upwards, “Sora…”\
The sky? I followed her gaze upwards, but all I saw was the familiar ceiling.\
And on that ceiling, I saw the scene from when I'd said goodbye to Kai today.\
I ran the pad of my finger over the lips she'd stolen from me.

‘That date was fun.’\
‘It wasn't a date.’\
‘Then what was it?’\
‘An outing.’\
‘If you translate that into English, it becomes a date.’\
‘Idiot.’\
‘That outing was fun.’\
“…………………………………”\
In the bedroom at night, still lying there alone, I gazed at my phone as though looking up at a planetarium.\
What on earth had I done? I regretted it.\
At the same time, the area around my chest seemed to be trying to recall the sensation, so I hurriedly brushed the thought away.\
‘It was all right.’\
‘You should be a little more honest with yourself sometimes, Yuki-san.’\
I nearly replied, You're the last person who should be telling me that, but lately Kai had been showing a more honest side of herself, too.\
She made no attempt to conceal her feelings for me. She was completely straightforward about being in love with me.\
The long, lingering kiss we'd shared before parting came back to me whether I wanted it to or not, and I looked away.\
Kissing Kai was like soaking in a bath for too long. It made my consciousness grow hazy, and the kiss seemed to go on without end.\
‘I'll invite you on another date.’\
“I told you, it wasn't a date…”\
‘Goodnight.’\
I was secretly enjoying these little exchanges, hiding away and carrying on behind everyone's backs.\
Is this really all right? I asked myself, a question whose answer was already decided, as though trying to prove to the world that I was still in my right mind.\
I knew what I was doing.\
‘Goodnight.’\
I turned my phone face down, then opened and closed my mouth repeatedly, like a goldfish gasping for air.\
My husband would be coming into the bedroom soon.\
Tonight, too, I would sleep beside him, pretending nothing was amiss.\
“………………Wouldn't it be better if I just died?”\
As my mind drifted, I remembered Kai's index finger and closed my eyes, as though trying to escape.
