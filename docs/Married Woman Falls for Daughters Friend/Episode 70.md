## Chapter Four: The Snow Melts into the Clod of Earth - Part Fourteen

\
“Hellooo. Kai? If it’s about what we discussed before, I’m always available.”\
“This is Amamiya. Please don’t teach Kai things that are bad for her education.”\
“Hm…?”\
“I said, Amamiya.”\
When I introduced myself again, she responded, “Oh, the designer.” It was quite clear she’d completely forgotten who I was.\
“Kai, um…” The nipple-guessing game. I couldn't bring myself to say it aloud. “Don’t teach her indecent games.”\
“W-which one, I wonder…? I’m sorry, could you explain which game you mean?”\
“Is that really all you ever teach her?”\
“Well, yes. I’m rather ashamed to admit it.”\
You really ought to be.\
“So, which one are we talking about?”\
“I just told you…”\
I began to suspect she was deliberately pretending not to understand, just to make me say it.\
It was entirely possible, coming from someone who had raised Kai. I tightened my grip on the phone.\
I wouldn't give in. I steeled myself, ready to stand my ground. And then, inevitably, I lowered my head.\
My face was itching from the heat building up inside me, as though I were about to breathe fire.\
“The… nipple-guessing… game…”\
Out of the corner of my eye, I saw Kai laughing uproariously.\
“Oh, that one! I recommended it to Kai as a basic form of communication.”\
“You don't need to teach Kai anything else. Not a single thing.”\
I delivered my ultimatum, drawing a line between them. Was this what it felt like to be a parent storming into a school to confront the teachers?\
“I’ll take care of her myself.”\
This woman, who had remained so indifferent despite knowing what was being done to Kai at home, was an enemy to me, too.\
My enemy laughed, as though she found the whole thing amusing.\
“Have you become Kai’s mummy, Amamiya-san?”\
“That would be fine, too. So, please look after yourself.”\
I shot back, matching her provocation, and hung up. Honestly.\
Or so I thought, until the phone rang again almost immediately.\
“Yes?”\
“Just one thing, for what it’s worth.”\
“What?”\
“Kai is a wicked woman, so be careful.”\
“You’re hardly one to talk.”\
“Quite right.”\
I hung up again.\
Then I glared at the girl beside me, who had been listening and laughing all along.\
“Kai, listen.”\
“What is it, Mummy?”\
“Let’s put ‘Mummy’ on hold. I understand that she’s the one who looked after you. And even now, she’s paying for your hotel, so… I know my own attitude towards her isn't exactly fair. But there’s no doubt she’s been teaching you nothing but rubbish. So stop believing everything adults tell you without question.”\
“All right?”\
I squeezed both her cheeks between my hands, trying to make her agree.\
“Does that include what you say, Yuki-san?”\
“You should listen to me.”\
“Strong ideological convictions, I see.”\
“Kai, are you not enjoying spending time together like this today?”\
I peered into her eyes, still holding her cheeks slightly squashed. I wanted to look into those deep eyes, the sort that seemed capable of swallowing me whole, leaving no way back to consciousness.\
“Having lunch together… Doesn't that make you happy?”\
There had been a time when my feelings had certainly sent ripples across Kai’s eyes.\
I wanted to believe in the tears I’d seen in her eyes. This girl had a heart that could still return to something healthy.\
“Of course it’s fun.”\
She stuck out her tongue, trying to lick my finger, and I hastily moved it out of reach.\
“What about you, Yuki-san?”\
Kai’s voice as she asked me in return was gentler than usual, like the surface of a calm sea.\
It was unusual for her voice or manner to contain no hint of an attack. This question wasn't an attempt to invade my space.\
I answered her quiet question.\
“...It’s fun. I enjoy being with you, Kai.”\
Once I admitted it, I felt as though I’d stepped down one stair from the landing where I’d been holding myself back. I had a premonition that the momentum would carry me down the rest, that I wouldn’t be able to stop. Even so, my true feelings spoke up before my reason could intervene.\
Who would go out of their way to see someone if they weren't enjoying themselves?\
“Then I’m glad.”\
Even with her cheeks squashed, Kai somehow managed to form a soft smile, one that seemed to express relief.\
I found something in that exchange that reminded me of a clear river flowing peacefully, and tried to take another step towards Kai.\
“So… Kai. From now on, too…”\
“Although, I do still want to play the nipple-guessing game normally…”\
She made the request with such modest, apologetic earnestness that I nearly felt dizzy. Perhaps education had nothing to do with it. Maybe she was simply a pervy little brat by nature. I might have been wrong all along. My hands went slack, sliding down from her cheeks.\
Kai stroked her own face as though smoothing out its shape, then looked straight at me and asked:\
“Can you do it?”\
“Huh?”\
“Can you enjoy the sort of wholesome fun you’re talking about, together with me, out in the open?”\
There was something utterly direct in her words. Reality itself took shape around them, its edge so sharp that an unwary touch from the side would slice your fingers open.\
Kai was holding that reality up in front of me. Her hand reached out at the same time, and I slapped it away.\
For one brief moment, I’d thought she was asking whether I could play the nipple-guessing game. I wasn’t about to admit that. As if!\
“If you can do that, then fine. But you can’t.”\
As Kai gazed at me with earnest eyes, her hand quietly crept forwards again. I slapped it away.\
“...Perhaps not.”\
Even if we stripped away every improper motive, there was no way I could openly spend time being affectionate with my daughter’s friend without attracting strange looks. Who would approve of seeing us holding hands? My family would never accept the idea of us sitting here in the car, gazing at each other like this.\
Kai’s index finger began to move again, stealthily, as though trying to slip past my notice. I slapped it away.\
“But that doesn't mean…”\
I slapped away her left hand, which had circled round to attack from another angle.\
“I want to find happiness in whatever time I can share with you, even if it’s secretly, just the two of us… even if it’s in a small, closed-off world that belongs only to us. I want you to find it worthwhile, even if only a little.”\
I grew tired of it and grabbed both her hands as they came lunging towards me.\
“Kai…”\
“And that’s where the nipple-guessing game comes in.”\
“WHY DOES IT ALWAYS COME BACK TO THAT?!”\
Still holding both her hands, I prevented her from starting the game.\
“Originally, the game is about seeing whether you can touch the other person’s nipple accurately in one go.”\
“I don’t need to know. I don’t need the rules explained. Put it away.”\
“You can go first, if you like, Yuki-san.”\
“…………What? I’m supposed to play, too?”\
“It wouldn't be much fun on your own, would it?”\
It wouldn't be fun with two people, either. It would only leave us sitting in an awkward silence.\
Kai struggled to free her hands, but she simply didn't have the strength. Kai was… a girl I could easily overpower if I decided to restrain her. …What on earth was I thinking?\
Using force to hurt Kai would make me no different from the people in that house.\
She would hate me.\
“Very well. Then this time, I’ll guess without touching.”\
“Please, really, stop doing such vulgar things.”\
Perhaps it was ridiculous for a woman who was secretly staying away from home without telling her husband to lecture anyone about propriety. Maybe that was why my words carried so little weight. Kai certainly wasn't listening to a thing I said.\
“I’ll guess without touching, all right?”\
“...I honestly can't understand why you're so enthusiastic about this.”
