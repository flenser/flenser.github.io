## Chapter Four: The Snow Melts into the Clod of Earth - Part Five

\
Leaving a gap of two weeks might have been a mistake.\
What I felt towards Kai had clearly grown worse. I had come to understand just how much she meant to me. I had grown far too accustomed to being with her, to having her in my life.\
I stood once more in the hotel I had arrived at early that morning, taking in the familiar combination of the sea, its surface scorched by the rising sun. A sigh escaped me.\
Perhaps I would never be able to escape this hotel.\
As usual, I went through the lobby and took the lift up to the fourth floor. Then I knocked on Kai's door. Just as always, it opened almost instantly, and Kai appeared.\
At least today, she didn't leap on me the moment she opened the door.\
“Good morning.”\
Perhaps she'd noticed how wary I was, how I stood with my weight shifted back, ready to retreat. A faint smile played across Kai's lips.\
“...Morning.”\
“We still have a little time.”\
“And?”\
Kai smiled brightly and took my hand. She invited me inside, then drew me over to the bed and made me sit down, as though she intended to push me right onto it. Until now, Kai had sat on the bed while I watched from the sofa, keeping that distance between us. Today, she pulled me along without giving me any say in the matter and sat me down on the edge of the bed. Naturally, she sat right beside me, close enough for our bodies to touch.\
“What are you doing?”\
Kai didn't answer. She merely smiled, looking satisfied.\
Then she put a hand on my shoulder and leaned towards me. I realised what she was about to do, placed my hand against her forehead, and stopped her. Watching Kai's lips purse in frustration at her failed attempt to kiss me, I nearly found my own lips sticking out in a pout. Honestly, this girl, first thing in the morning... And night-time was out of the question, too.\
“Will you finally say something? If you don't, I'm leaving.”\
I should have left anyway. The part of me that criticised me as though I were some unrelated third party, what was it? Was it the part of me that wanted to be a proper member of my family? There was only supposed to be one me, yet I was trying to protect myself by separating off some other part and blaming it instead. I was nothing but a cowardly cheat.\
With my hand still holding her forehead in place, Kai finally parted her thin lips.\
“May I kiss you?”\
“No.”\
“That's a shame. Well, I did plenty of that yesterday, so I suppose it's fine.”\
“...It is not fine.”\
I was still angry about yesterday, too.\
...I was angry.\
Honestly, though, all that remained was the relief of being here with Kai like this again.\
“More importantly, why don't you reply to my messages?”\
Kai tilted her head as if she had no idea what I was talking about. She seemed to know perfectly well, which made me furious. And yet, infuriatingly, I had no choice but to play along.\
“Look.” I took out my phone and pointed at the screen.\
Kai looked at it, then finally broke into a wide, sly grin.\
“So Yuki-san doesn't like it when she doesn't get a reply straight away, either.”\
Was this her way of getting back at me for laughing at her about it before?\
How childish, I thought, scratching my head. Then again, she was a child, and that made it difficult to argue with her reasoning. It was true that when someone read your message and left you hanging, you grew restless, anxious and irritated. You started to develop a sort of expectation, a sense of entitlement towards the other person. But it wasn't as though I'd deliberately ignored her.\
Still, I was an adult. An adult. I admonished myself, restraining the urge to argue with her as though we were equals.\
“...Fair enough. I do feel uneasy when I don't get a reply. So from now on, you reply straight away, too.”\
When someone made an effort to respond calmly, Kai couldn't quite hide the softness in her voice at having got exactly what she'd wanted.\
“Honestly, what a pervy old lady...”\
“...What does that have to do with being pervy?”\
Was it terminally hopeless that I found her endearing when she looked so utterly delighted?\
Kai, encouraged by her success, slipped a hand around my waist.\
“What are you doing?”\
“I want to touch Yuki-san.”\
Kai drew me closer by the waist. Or rather, she couldn't actually move me with those slender arms of hers, so she moved herself around me instead, pressing her body against mine. Her legs fitted snugly against mine, and her shoulder bumped against my upper arm.\
Her fingertips weren't merely resting on my waist. They kept shifting in small, exploratory movements.\
“Your hands are...”\
“What about them?”\
I hesitated to call them lewd. Saying it aloud would only make the atmosphere even more awkward. Knowing Kai was moving her fingers suggestively precisely because she knew I would hesitate made her an utterly infuriating little brat.\
“Hey... that's not my waist any more...”\
Kai's hand slipped a little lower, her fingers straying below my waist, and I hurriedly warned her.\
But Kai let out a short, startled squeak. She had moved the whole of her palm there now.\
“I've been wondering about this for a while.”\
“Move your hand, you pervy little brat.”\
“Is your bottom on the larger side, Yuki-san?”\
“I'm leaving. Go to school by yourself, like a good girl.”\
“Goodbye.”\
I stood up and hurried to make my escape.\
But the very next moment, I felt a tremendous impact from behind, as though someone had shoved me with all their might.\
The difference in our builds meant I didn't actually fall over, but the suddenness of it startled me.\
Kai had thrown her arms around me from behind.\
I could tell from the way she held me just how desperately she was clinging on. I hadn't expected such genuine intensity after the teasing that had just taken place, and my feet stopped before I could think.\
Pressing her mouth against my back, Kai pleaded with me.\
“Please... stay with me.”\
“...You don't have to be so desperate.”\
It made me sound like some villain abandoning a lonely child.\
When in reality, the other party was just a middle-school girl who was a sexually harassing little pervert.\
“Oh, what, were you joking?”\
“No, I meant it.”\
“Then I'm afraid I can't let you.”\
Kai hugged me even tighter.\
...Was this girl not simply taking advantage of a good opportunity to cling to me without holding back? Her chin ground insistently into the area around my shoulder blade, tormenting it.\
“Stop messing around.”\
“I couldn't help myself.”\
“...Honestly.”\
The childlike side she occasionally showed me gave my feelings towards Kai their alternating rises and falls. Perhaps that was the secret to keeping someone's interest from growing stale.\
“Besides... well, it's not as though I'm that big.”\
“Huh?”\
Don't tell me you're actually serious. And don't just go around touching me so casually. It's more that you're just ridiculously skinny all over.\
“I'm sagging, I'm not young any more, I'm an old lady. There, happy now? Satisfied?”\
I gave up trying to defend myself, shoved Kai away, and sat back down on the bed.\
“Woooow!”\
Kai, who had been pushed away, spun round and round without changing her expression, corrected her trajectory, then returned to sit beside me. She peered into my face.\
“What?”\
“You don't look like a sagging, not-young-any-more old lady to me.”\
“...I appreciate you trying to make me feel better, but...”\
Couldn't she have phrased it a little better?\
“A beautiful, pervy old lady.”\
“Are you actually trying to compliment me?”\
“May I kiss you?”\
“...No.”\
Kai's small, stubborn hand came to rest against my face. Her own fresh, youthful cheeks had flushed red, making it clear that she wasn't teasing me from a position of perfect composure. Her eyes were serious.\
They weren't the mysterious, beautiful eyes that seemed to draw you into a swamp, as they usually did.\
They were clear, shining with light.\
“Didn't you say it was fine because we'd already kissed so much yesterday...?”\
“I'm sorry. I was putting on a brave face.”\
“Hey...”\
Right to the very end, my mouth offered what little resistance it could.\
And when that mouth was finally sealed shut...\
I offered no resistance at all.
