## Chapter Four: The Snow Melts into the Clod of Earth - Part Thirteen

\
Eventually, a signal came to let us know our order was ready, and I went to collect it. It’s been a while since I’ve done this, I thought, carrying the tray. What exactly this was, I couldn’t have said.\
“This one’s yours, Kai.”\
I returned to our seats and handed her the fish burger. I set the corn soup and the orange juice she’d asked for in front of her, too. While Kai watched the steam rising from her soup, I sorted out my own order: a teriyaki chicken burger and oolong tea. And, since looking at the menu together with Kai had made me fancy some, I’d ordered onion rings and chips as well.\
“Right, let’s eat.”\
“Go ahead.”\
Kai unwrapped her burger and took a bite of the fish burger, now half exposed.\
Her first bite was small, as though she were testing it. Then she quickly moved on to a second.\
“Mmm. Mm-hmm, mm-hmm.”\
She chewed and nodded. I found it a little funny, and smiled at how strange she was being.\
“What’s your verdict?”\
“The mayonnaise really comes through, even though it’s fairly well concealed.”\
“Nothing’s being concealed.”\
I wiped the mayonnaise already clinging to the corner of Kai’s mouth. “Thanks,” she said, thanking me rather more readily than I’d expected. Perhaps she’d been hungry, because she immediately took another bite. I watched her, pleased that she seemed to like it.\
The light streaming into the restaurant overlapped with the state of my heart.\
Ah, this is bad. I kept smiling, even as the shadows within me grew increasingly alarmed.\
Just looking at Kai as she was now was enough to make my heart feel as though it might overflow with affection.\
We’d been in such an awkward atmosphere only a little while ago, and now this. My emotions couldn’t keep up with such drastic changes in temperature.\
Sometimes Kai looked like my daughter.\
Sometimes she looked like a young friend.\
Sometimes she looked like some inexplicable creature.\
And then…\
“………………Here, you can have an onion ring, too.”\
“You shouldn’t be a fussy eater, Yuki-san.”\
“Do you have no concept of consideration?”\
“I’m joking.”\
Kai bit into the onion ring, and I enjoyed watching her small mouth and jaw move as she chewed. The little movements showed how much she was savouring the taste. I found myself affirming that coming here had been the right choice.\
I’m glad.\
Even though there was no way I should be glad.\
“What have you got, Yuki-san?”\
“Teriyaki chicken. …Want to try some?”\
I tore off a small piece and held it out, but Kai didn’t take it. Instead, she approached with her little mouth hanging open.\
“...Again?”\
“Ahhh.”\
“I told you, it’s not particularly alluring.”\
Just as I had at Christmas, I brought the food to Kai’s mouth. It wasn’t anything like what Kai might have imagined. I felt more like a parent bird delivering food to its chick.\
After she’d finished eating, I dampened a paper napkin with a little water and wiped Kai’s hands and the area around her mouth.\
“Perhaps you really are a mum…?”\
“Not yours, though.”\
When Kai thought about her home, she probably didn’t even have a clear idea of what parents were supposed to be.\
There were so many people in that house, yet no family. An abnormal state of affairs that I simply couldn’t comprehend had somehow become normal there.\
That said, I wasn’t sure I could be her mummy, either… Though perhaps I might think of her that way if our positions were reversed.\
Just as I felt so many different things in relation to Kai,\
Kai might also see me in all sorts of different ways.\
As a mother, an older sister, a friend, and then…\
That last possibility was something we must never give shape to.\
I put the parking ticket through the payment machine, and after we returned to the car, Kai thanked me.\
“Thank you for the meal.”\
“You’re welcome. Did you enjoy it?”\
“Hmm…” Kai’s eyes wandered.\
“What? Didn’t you like it?”\
With all those soft little reactions of hers…\
“I wanted to say something clever, but I couldn’t think of anything.”\
“Just say it normally.”\
“I shall never forget the kindness you have bestowed upon me.”\
“That’s quite enough.”\
It turned out to be a much bigger deal than I’d expected.\
Before setting off, I took a little break and sat there, staring into space.\
We’d already had lunch, and if we kept driving in this direction, there weren’t any places we could reasonably stop at on a date. Not that it was a date. We could turn back and go to the aquarium… No, an aquarium on a weekend would mean far too many people. It wouldn’t be at all surprising if one of my daughter’s classmates, or their family, happened to see us. Kai in particular was wearing her school uniform, so she’d be easy to recognise.\
I was seriously considering where to take her on an outing. I was the sort of person who couldn’t do things by halves once I’d committed to them.\
Perhaps I should simply ask where Kai wanted to go… Or maybe somewhere further away?\
Just as I was thinking all this, Kai’s silent message arrived, distracting me. This pervy little brat was staring intently at my chest. She wasn’t even trying to hide it. Her eyes were positively gleaming.\
“Kai, honestly… You’re being far too blatant about staring.”\
Even though we were both girls, she was looking far too openly. Especially considering that she had openly declared her romantic feelings for me. …Yes, Kai was in love with me. And I was meeting up with a girl who loved me, spending time alone with her in a confined space like this car. Not on a date, of course, but still.\
What had I thought was permissible about all this, when I’d set out and driven here?\
“Do you usually do this to other people, too?”\
“I’m only interested in yours, Yuki-san.”\
“...Oh. Is that so?”\
Why did I feel as though a single knot in a tangled web of threads had just come undone?\
“I’m only interested in big boobs.”\
“Hey, that really is sexual harassment.”\
“Yuki-san.”\
The sudden, deliberate way she said my name put me on guard. The fact that I reacted at all showed how thoroughly I’d become accustomed to being called “Yuki-san”.\
“There’s this harmless little game my older sister taught me. It’s called the nipple-guessing game.”\
“I want her to die just for coming up with that name.”\
Kai’s index finger slid towards something as though it had a destination in mind. I slapped it away.\
“Give me your phone. I want to have a word with your sister.”\
“She probably won’t answer.”\
“Give it here.”\
“...All right.”\
This might have been the first time I’d ever left Kai with no room to argue.\
Still furious, I dialled the number without letting my momentum die down. After a short wait, the call connected.\
She answered after all.
