## Interlude: The Story of the Daughter of a Happy Family Who Falls Head over Heels for a Blonde Beauty - Part 1

\
It was a chance encounter on a day off, when I was heading to the convenience store to buy a prepaid card.\
As I waited at the traffic lights on the main road, I raised my head, and a light shone down on me from an angle different from that of the sun.\
A golden field of wheat swaying in the breeze, soft waves of lustrous gold.\
It was the hair of a young woman waiting at the lights on the opposite side of the road.\
Even from a distance, its brilliance showed no sign of fading. It was like another sun.\
The lights changed. The pedestrian signal on this road stayed green for only a short time. I had to cross, so I hurried along. The young woman coming towards me from the other side looked as though she was about to pass me, but instead she turned back and fell into step beside me. The wooden teeth of her geta sandals struck the asphalt with a light, cheerful clatter.\
“Yo!”\
“...Huh? Wh-what?”\
The blonde woman, wearing geta and a haori in a colour like the pale light before dawn, greeted me with such familiarity that I was taken aback. Whoa, what was with that hair? Seeing it up close was even more breathtaking. The fine, silky strands of blonde spilled and swayed with the slightest movement of her head, captivating me all by themselves.\
“You were staring at me so intently that I thought we might know each other. ...Hm? Feel like I've said something like that before.”\
“Ah, um, sorry for staring.”\
“It's fine, it's fine. I love being looked at by cute girls. And I love gazing at cute girls, too.”\
She praised me without the slightest embarrassment, and my cheeks burned as though the sun had been shining directly on them.\
Cute. That was the first time anyone had ever said it to my face. I nearly started frothing at the mouth.\
More than anything, what amazed me was that someone like her could say such a thing without it sounding the least bit insincere.\
When she looked straight at me, I felt as though my soul had become transparent. As though it were fading away from inside my chest.\
There weren't even any flowery metaphors left to describe her.\
Her face was just that good.\
She was unbelievably, outrageously beautiful.\
“B…”\
“Hmm?”\
“Y-you're, like, ridiculously beautiful, ma'am.”\
I was so nervous that my shoulders had gone rigid, making them feel almost square. I thought I must have developed a rather impressive shoulder width.\
The young woman laughed, seeming to enjoy my predicament.\
“Thanks. I get told that sometimes.”\
Aaaaah! She was used to hearing it! A storm of silent screams erupted inside me.\
Sometimes was definitely a lie.\
“You're…” Her gaze travelled unabashedly from my head to my toes, then back again.\
“A middle-schooler, huh? Such a fresh, innocent sort of cuteness.”\
“H-how did you know?”\
I wasn't even wearing my uniform, since it was a day off.\
“Well, let's just say I've cultivated a keen eye for observation. Oh, by the way, I usually pull a rickshaw around here. Have you ever seen me?”\
“Ah!”\
Now that she mentioned it, I remembered. Her hairstyle and clothes were different, but this was the same blonde woman.\
“I'll be pulling it again from the afternoon onwards. Today I've left things to one of the new recruits, so I've got the morning off.”\
“Though I suppose they're not exactly new recruits any more?” she added, tilting her head at her own words.\
“What about you? Got some free time?”\
“Ah, um, well, I suppose you could say I'm free…”\
I was planning to go home and laze around for the rest of the day, after all.\
“Hmm… Well, even if you are free, following an adult you don't know isn't something I'd recommend. You need to be careful, all right?”\
“Ah, um, yes…”\
It wasn't so much that I was following her. She was the one who'd followed me.\
Before I could voice that objection, the young woman flashed a wonderfully pleasant smile.\
“So, how about I become an adult you do know? Whatchyourneinmat!”\
“Pardon?”\
“Whatchyournein… mat!”\
She wasn't giving up. This time she exaggerated the pause and her peculiar pronunciation even more. Whatchyour… Ah! She was asking my name. I finally understood.\
“Your English is very good.”\
“Hahaha. Was that sarcasm?”\
“I'm Amamiya.”\
“Ah, I see. Amami-chan. That's a lovely name. We might be quite compatible.”\
She'd shortened my name into something like a nickname in an instant. What did compatibility between names even mean?\
“And may I have your name?”\
“Hoshi Takasora. Oh, Takasora's my given name.”\
“Takasora…”\
It was an unusual-sounding name. High sky?\
Just saying it seemed to lift my spirits, as though the name itself had the same airy quality as the sky.\
But if it was stars in the sky and rain, weren't our names actually rather incompatible?\
“Most people I get to know call me Sora, so if you feel like it, you can call me that too, Amami-chan.”\
“Um… For now, I'll just call you Hoshi-san.”\
“Nice.”\
She seemed so frivolous that I suspected she would have given the same answer whatever I'd called her. Hoshi-san took out her phone, checked the time, then immediately tucked it back inside her haori.\
“Perfect timing. So, how about it?”\
“...How about what?”\
She casually swept back her sun-bright hair, then leaned towards me, her beautiful face approaching without the slightest hesitation, so dazzling that it might even have eclipsed the sun itself.\
Her voice seemed to take hold of the very tips of my lips.

“Would you like to spend some grown-up time with me?”\
“………………Zweh!”\
I was so flustered that I stumbled backwards.\
Grown-up time?\
Just adding the word grown-up somehow made everything sound indecent.\
“Zweeeeeeeen!”\
“You're crying!”\
“H-homa!”\
“Oh, is that so? That's a good answer.”\
I wonder which way she'd interpreted my strange noises.
