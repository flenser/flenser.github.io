## Interlude: The Story of the Daughter of a Happy Family Who Falls Head over Heels for a Blonde Beauty – Part Four

\
“Ah, Amami-chan. Hello! On your way home from school?”\
“H-hello...”\
My face was already going into all sorts of contortions.\
“You look lovely in your school uniform, too.”\
“Th-thank you.”\
I'd stumbled over the words so badly that I'd practically turned into a cow. Hoshi-san found that absolutely hilarious.\
“Did you come to see me?”\
“N-no, just by chance.”\
“By chance.”\
I awkwardly shook my head and hands from side to side. “I see,” Hoshi-san replied, looking pleased.\
Why?\
I panicked, desperate to say something. But then again, we hardly knew each other yet. We had barely had any contact at all. I was at a loss. Oh, but there was one thing. Maybe there was. That was all I could think of. I bowed my head.\
“Thank you for buying me lunch the other day.”\
I'd forgotten to thank her for treating me, probably. I'd been far too busy looking at her face.\
“You came all this way just to tell me that?”\
“W-well, that was the only thing I could think of for the time being...”\
If I'd actually planned to talk to her, I would have thought of plenty of other things to say during lessons.\
It was an idea that would probably get me told off by Mum if she found out.\
Hoshi-san gently stroked her shoulder, as though setting down a heavy burden, and her expression softened.\
“Your modesty shines brighter than the evening sun.”\
Hoshi-san swept her fringe back. My chest tightened at the gesture.\
How could something so small make me imagine particles of light dancing through the air?\
“It's difficult to tell someone not to lose that sort of innocent freshness, but I'd like you to remember it, even if only as a pinch of salt.”\
“A-a pinch of salt...?”\
Hoshi-san continued to tease me with her usual carefree attitude.\
And then...\
At the moment she happened to notice Chitaira-san waiting in the distance, Hoshi-san's eyes, which had been so gentle until then, suddenly widened.\
Astonishment gathered deep in her pupils, giving them a dull gleam.\
At the same time, Chitaira-san seemed to narrow her eyes in response.\
“Um...?”\
Their reactions crossed like those of people who knew each other. Wondering whether something had happened, I looked from one face to the other.\
“Ah... No, it's nothing. Take care on your way home.”\
“Okay...”\
She waved goodbye, her earlier smile returning, and I hurriedly waved back.\
Her smile seemed to shine even brighter than her blonde hair.\
With my heart and senses filled with the feeling and fragrance of holding a bouquet of flowers against my chest, I ran back to Chitaira-san.\
And she immediately asked me:\
“Did you manage to pick her up?”\
I hadn't managed to.\
“I-I said it wasn't like that.”\
Apparently, Chitaira-san had only been teasing me. Her shoulders were shaking with laughter.\
But that aside...\
“Chitaira-san, do you actually know her?”\
“No. That was our first meeting.”\
But...\
Chitaira-san's eyes turned towards the distance.\
“She reacted as though she knew me.”\
“What do you mean?”\
“Don't worry about it. More importantly, I think blonde hair might be rather nice after all.”\
“What?!”\
I reacted far too sensitively to Chitaira-san's remark. Was being too beautiful really a problem after all?\
“Ah, don't worry. It has nothing to do with your woman, Amamiya-san.”\
“I-I don't think you should call her that!”\
To begin with, she wasn't my woman. And what did she mean by *woman*? It wasn't as though I was... well, Hoshi-san and I...\
I did want to become closer to her, though.\
“I'll support you, Amamiya-san.”\
“Huh?”\
“Because...”\
Chitaira-san's smile spread across her face, revealing her teeth as though baring her fangs.\
It was an expression that seemed to promise an attack, enough to make a cold sweat break out on my back.\
She kept walking with that smile frozen in place, then suddenly returned to her usual expressionless face and looked ahead.\
“Hmm?”\
“No... I tried building up the suspense as though I had a reason, but I couldn't actually think of one.”\
“What was that supposed to be?”\
Chitaira-san had her own peculiar sense of timing, which sometimes made her unexpectedly funny.\
“Then I'll support you too, Chitaira-san. I hope things go well with your... boyfriend?”\
I could only offer her my moral support, of course. I didn't even know who the other person was or what their circumstances were.\
At first, Chitaira-san turned towards me, her eyes widening.\
“...................Ah...................!”\
For an instant, she puffed out her cheeks.\
Then, as though swallowing down something she had been trying to hold back, her smile suddenly burst open.\
“Thank you.”\
It was the brightest expression I'd ever seen on Chitaira-san. Her face, her voice, everything about her was more radiant than ever before.\
Against the evening light, her lips seemed to tear through the shadows.\
Her gratitude was so utterly sincere that I found myself thinking, rather rudely, *So she can actually react like that?*\
Then, just as suddenly, she returned to her usual expression.\
“Oh, and the reason I'll support you is because we're friends.”\
“Bit late for that!”\
She murmured it so casually, wearing her usual expression, as though pretending not to have said anything particularly unusual. That was Chitaira-san's particular charm.\
The wind changed direction, pushing gently against our uncertain backs.\
Amid the sound of the wind passing over the ground, I heard someone mutter:\
“Chitaira.”
