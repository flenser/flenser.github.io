## Chapter Four: The Snow Melts into the Clod of Earth - Part Eleven

\
“There it is, you little liar.”\
“Would you look at that? I was sure I’d brought it with me, but I couldn’t find it.”\
Thank goodness I’d noticed it now. With heartfelt gratitude, I hurriedly pulled away. It was as though the stagnant air had finally escaped, and all the tension that had been building up relaxed at once. Kai had been cultivating such a suggestive atmosphere that I’d let myself be drawn in by a moment of weakness. What had come over me? All from touching Kai just a little.\
…Why was touching her face so different from touching her body?\
When I picked up the phone and switched it on, a familiar person, and her photograph, appeared boldly on the screen.\
“Honestly, who does something like this? You idiot.”\
“I thought I ought to put it on display, since you went to the trouble of giving it to me.”\
“Change it to something innocuous.”\
“No.”\
“...Please change it. I’m begging you.”\
I handed the phone back, lowering my demand to a request rather than an order. With my eyes cast down, I confessed why.\
“I don’t want you to get caught out over something like this…”\
If someone else found out about our relationship, the chances were that I would lose the opportunity to see Kai like this.\
And that would be for the best. Absolutely.\
Thinking back over the time I’d just spent with Kai, a part of me desperately wished I could put an end to it before it was too late.\
Then what was it, exactly, that I’d just said I didn’t want? Lately, my contradictions had become pronounced, my thoughts utterly incoherent.\
Kai laughed at me.\
“It’s nice, getting caught out, isn’t it?”\
“Huh?”\
“It feels like we have a secret between us, you and me.”\
That almost carefree remark turned my mind scarlet. Not white, but red with blood rushing through it.\
“I’m the only one taking this seriously. I feel like an idiot. Forget it.”\
“Don’t sulk.”\
“I said forget it.”\
I knew I was throwing a childish tantrum, but I couldn’t stop myself. I turned away, trying to shut Kai out, but she reached up and touched my face as easily as anything. There was none of the conflict or hesitation that arose within me. Kai simply leapt over the wall.\
As I turned my face towards her, Kai’s lips came straight down over mine.\
As though refusing to let the last flicker of light within me go out.\
She kept her lips pressed against mine, gazing at me from close enough to see every detail.\
As though waiting for those yellow-green eyes to be burned into mine for eternity.\
I forgot to breathe. My vision and consciousness were stolen away.\
Or rather, I simply started to run short of breath, so I tapped her back and asked her to pull away.\
Kai finally parted her lips, seemingly reluctant to let go. Her expression remained unchanged, but her cheeks had coloured.\
“I’m sorry.”\
“What part of that was an apology…?”\
Even as I was speaking, she pressed her lips against mine again, silencing me. The back of my head struck the window on the driver's side, and I wrapped my arms around Kai’s back to steady her.\
Don’t steady her.\
Kai’s hair tickled my neck, sending a shiver through me.\
“You think you can get away with anything by covering it up with a kiss whenever things get awkward?”\
“When I feel more strongly that I love you, I just can’t help wanting to.”\
“That’s not a reason.”\
Another baseless kiss was added to the tally. Every time she kissed me, did that mean she was thinking about how much she loved me? …Was that what kissing meant? It was. That was what the kisses I’d shared with my husband had meant.\
Then why was I kissing Kai?\
Why was I losing myself in it, in a place where one glance through the car window could bring my life to an end?\
When I touched Kai and let her touch me, I wanted to cast aside all those complicated considerations. It was dangerous.\
After that, we kissed about ten more times.\
“That’s a lot.”\
My mood had been more or less appeased, but not a single problem had been resolved.\
“Kai, listen to my request, too.”\
I looked straight at her. Kai lowered her gaze to the phone, gripping it tightly.\
“If you feel that strongly, Yuki-san, I’ll change it.”\
“...Please do.”\
“I’ll change it, all right?”\
“Don’t sulk.”\
We’d taken an awfully roundabout route just to get that far.\
I’d been led in a direction I should never have gone, until I found myself peering into a yellow-green swamp.\
“Then… could you take off the ring on your left hand?”\
Holding up her phone, Kai smiled at me, utterly untroubled.\
Hm? Hmm… Hm?\
“Why?”\
“If I take a close-up of your left hand without your wedding ring, surely no one will be able to tell whose hand it is.”\
“...What? Wait, you want to take a picture of my hand?”\
“My hand? What do you mean?”\
“I want to keep you as my wallpaper, Yuki-san.”\
“...So you think people won’t be able to identify me just from my hand?”\
Kai nodded enthusiastically, as if pleased that I’d finally understood.\
I hadn’t.\
Why on earth did I have to take off my wedding ring for something like that? I wondered, but reluctantly slipped it off all the same. Wasn’t I far too indulgent towards Kai? Well, it was a little late to ask that now. Or was it?\
“A hand…”\
I still had absolutely no idea why, but I went along with the photoshoot.\
To begin with, if all she wanted was a picture of my hand, she could have taken my right one. Then I wouldn’t have had to remove my ring. …Perhaps she’d only wanted to make me take it off? Was that reading too much into it?\
“Make-up is all set. We’re ready to shoot!”\
“Who are you talking to? What crew?”\
I rolled up my sleeve so that not even the cuff of my clothes would appear in the picture. In the end, all that was visible was the back of my hand. Even though I was looking at my own hand, I could barely recognise it as mine. It was hardly as though my fingers were visible, either, so there hadn’t even been any need to remove the ring.\
And yet, the wallpaper was now an extreme close-up of the left hand of someone I didn’t recognise.\
“This might make people suspicious for a different reason.”\
Of Kai’s tastes, perhaps. Kai herself was gazing at my left hand with great satisfaction, so I decided to compromise. Well, whatever.\
These days, perhaps someone could analyse even such a tiny scrap of information and identify the person behind it. But the risk of being exposed that way seemed far lower than the risk of someone finding us here, out on a drive together.\
We’d stopped and used the car park, so I took the opportunity to get out and buy two bottles of tea from the convenience store.\
I silently held one out to Kai. She warned me, “When you’re kind to me, I fall even more in love with you.”\
“...I’ve told you over and over. I’m not going to fall in love with you. It’ll never come to anything.”\
“That may be true.”\
Her words, as though she could see right through me, irritated me. I put my ring back on.\
“But love isn’t something you give up just because the other person doesn’t love you back.”\
“...Quite the philosopher for a fourteen-year-old.”\
“There's something in a novel by one of my hotel friends that says, ‘Perhaps our desperate insistence that the passage of time brings us closer to the truth, rather than merely wearing us down, is just one more delusion we refuse to abandon.’”\
“...Perhaps.”\
So even if Kai understood the nature of love at her age, it wasn't something to laugh off.
