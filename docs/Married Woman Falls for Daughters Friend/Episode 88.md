## Interlude: The Story of the Daughter of a Happy Family Who Falls Head over Heels for a Blonde Beauty – Part Three

\
“Chitaira-san, are you the sort of person who can talk about love and romance?”\
Once again, Chitaira-san had skipped PE, and she hadn't even shown up during the lunch break. Yet she was sitting in the classroom with the air of someone who had diligently attended every lesson that day. Perhaps it was just the way the light fell, but she seemed a little listless.\
“Leave it to this woman of many romances.”\
Without changing her expression, she pointed proudly at herself, leaning forward with considerable confidence.\
“What a reassuringly suspicious lie.”\
“Anyway, what is this about?”\
Chitaira-san, who always tried to leave as soon as school was over, looked up at me with her bag in hand.\
When she looked at me with those strangely coloured eyes, I could understand why the other girls in class felt intimidated by her.\
There was a pressure to them, as though they were issuing a warning.\
“Although, perhaps I haven't actually had that many romances.”\
“Don't worry, I don't believe you anyway. Actually... well, how should I put it?”\
“Have you fallen for someone?”\
She saw straight through me. Well, I suppose I was being rather obvious. Though I wasn't even sure whether I liked her yet.\
“It's more like... an idol, maybe? You know, an idol who's on the other side of a screen, except this one's local. There's not really much difference, is there? Do you understand?”\
“Not at all.”\
“I'm thinking of going to see that idol today, just for a little while... And, well, I was hoping you might come with me, Chitaira-san.”\
I tried to fill the awkward silence with an indistinct, giggly laugh.\
“I'd feel a little nervous going by myself.”\
“Why me?”\
The movement of Chitaira-san's eyes seemed to ask why I wasn't going with one of my many other friends.\
“It's just that, well... I thought you probably wouldn't spread rumours about this to the other people.”\
It wasn't that I didn't trust my friends, but school was the sort of place where rumours spread much further than you expected.\
“Ah. That's because I don't have any friends.”\
“I-I wasn't saying you had to go that far!”\
I gestured emphatically, as if to say, *Look, I have friends! See?*\
Chitaira-san casually took out a phone, despite the fact that bringing them to school was technically prohibited, and checked something on the screen.\
“If it won't take until tonight.”\
She smiled.\
And so, in a very unusual turn of events... no, perhaps for the first time ever, I left school with Chitaira-san and headed towards the main street.\
On the way, we even managed to have a proper conversation about romance.\
“Chitaira-san, do you have someone you like?”\
“I do. Actually, I'm dating someone.”\
“WHAT?!”\
It was a shocking revelation from someone who seemed completely uninterested in romance.\
“The other person isn't honest enough to admit it, but we're practically already going out.”\
“O-oh, really...”\
Was that all right? If Chitaira-san was simply interpreting things that way on her own, it wasn't exactly a peaceful situation. Perhaps she was actually quite prone to jumping to conclusions.\
As though peering into my thoughts, Chitaira-san gave me a mischievous grin.\
“Someone in our class?”\
“Maybe.”\
I couldn't remember ever seeing Chitaira-san talk to anyone in the classroom, but perhaps they met in secret. Maybe during PE or the lunch break... No, if that were the case, the other person would have to be skipping lessons too, so perhaps not.\
“S-so, are you two all lovey-dovey?”\
“More like... all hot and heavy.”\
“EHH?!”\
Even though she wasn't talking about me, her bluntness made my heart clench. Once the tension released, my heart suddenly started pumping blood around my body, and my cheeks prickled with heat.\
Chitaira-san's expression remained perfectly blank.\
“Y-you're in middle school, aren't you?”\
Well, we were in the same classroom, after all.\
“Sometimes people mistake me for an elementary-schooler, too.”\
“R-right...”\
She was so far ahead of her time that I couldn't understand her at all.\
Overwhelmed by our conversation about romance, we headed towards the large gate on the main street. It wasn't long before we spotted Hoshi-san, pulling her rickshaw. Neither the many tourists nor the fading sunlight could obscure her radiance.\
Even from a distance, my eyes were drawn to her in an instant.\
“Hm. A blonde beauty, is it? You've got good taste.”\
“N-no, well, the blonde part... She's beautiful, though.”\
She had her hair tied up while working, which made her look even taller than the last time I'd seen her. More mature, somehow. Her appearance was different from the beauty she possessed when her hair was down. It was as though a single change of hairstyle could completely transform her impression. Beautiful women really did have far too many different faces.\
As soon as the foreign tourists riding in the back had disembarked, Chitaira-san gave me an unceremonious push between the shoulder blades.\
“She shouldn't be busy now, so go on and try to pick her up.”\
She supported my back without the slightest hesitation, urging me forwards so that I couldn't retreat.\
“P-picking her up wasn't part of the plan!”\
“Then what did you come here for?”\
“To admire her from a distance.”\
“Wait, are you a stalker?”\
“N-no, that's just what being an idol is like...”\
“There's no law against making a move on an idol.”\
“Th-there ought to be!”\
“I have no interest in idols you can't fall seriously in love with.”\
“That's quite a strong philosophy you've got there.”\
“So, do your best. I'll do the watching from a distance.”\
“Y-you're outsourcing the stalking?”\
I had been thrown into the fray as an emissary of the die-hard romantic fan faction, without ever agreeing to the role.\
“Good luck!” came a half-hearted cheer from somewhere behind me.\
With that voice at my back, I timidly approached the rickshaw and the blonde beauty. I hadn't planned on meeting her, so I had absolutely no idea what to say. My mind was completely blank as I found myself drawn towards her.\
Hoshi-san noticed me too, still holding the rickshaw, and gave me a broad, beaming smile.\
It was such a transformation in her expression that I almost imagined a golden flower, one that didn't even exist, suddenly blooming before my eyes.
