## Interlude Two

\
After finishing physical education and changing back into our uniforms, I returned to the classroom to find Chitaira-san already sitting at her desk. She had a fairly thick book open in front of her, apparently one she'd borrowed straight from the library, and was quietly absorbed in reading it.\
It was always like this during PE. I hadn't paid it much attention before, but now that we were friends, I'd grown a little curious.\
After splitting off from the friends I'd walked back to the classroom with, I headed over to Chitaira-san's desk before getting ready for the next lesson, while there were still relatively few people in the room. As soon as I approached, Chitaira-san noticed me and looked up.\
Unlike me, she didn't have a single bead of sweat on her forehead. Her skin was pale, almost bluish.\
She looked as though someone had taken a shadowy corner of the classroom and given it human form.\
“Hey, hey. Why do you skip PE? You are skipping it, aren't you?”\
As I spoke, I began to wonder whether there might be some other unavoidable reason, and made my question sound a little less certain. She'd been absent from school for a couple of days last week, so perhaps there was a health issue involved this time, too. I didn't want to upset her if I'd got it wrong, so I checked.\
Chitaira-san closed her book and gave a faint smile.\
“Yeah, I'm skipping it.”\
She admitted it without the slightest hesitation.\
“And the reason is that I don't like PE.”\
“Ah, that's exactly the straightforward reason I expected.”\
It seemed a rather flimsy excuse, but was she really allowed to get away with avoiding lessons for that? If I announced, “I hate maths! Goodbye!” I couldn't imagine that prayer reaching the heavens. My father might let it pass, but if my mother heard, she'd certainly call me an idiot and slap me. And then, after slapping me, she'd sit down and help me study anyway.\
“I'm small, as you can see. Even if I took part in PE, I don't think I'd be able to beat anyone.”\
“Wow, you're really competitive, Chitaira-san.”\
I'd hardly ever brought a competitive spirit to PE. I usually went outside with the attitude that I was there to sunbathe, and I was beginning to feel more like a plant than a person. Even if Chitaira-san bared her fangs, it would probably take her quite a while to defeat that plant.\
“Since you're such a highly motivated person, Chitaira-san, are you free after school? Do you have club activities?”\
Since I had the opportunity, I casually asked about her plans for the afternoon.\
“Club activities… What was it again?”\
Chitaira-san's expression and downcast eyes remained frozen, as though the answer really had vanished into the distant reaches of her memory. Her reaction alone was enough to tell me that she had nothing to do with club activities.\
“Oh, right. The kendo club.”\
“Wait, you're in a sports club?”\
“They told me I had to belong to something, so I picked one. I've never actually attended, though.”\
“Ohhh…”\
I looked at her, silently asking whether there was some particular reason she'd chosen kendo when she could have picked anything.\
“Because I thought the character for ‘sword’ in its name was cool.”\
“…Really?”\
Her expression hadn't changed at all, so it was difficult to tell whether she was serious. But if she was, I thought that was a rather easygoing sense of style.\
We'd had quite a few study sessions at my house by now, and I'd come to think that Chitaira-san was actually a pretty decent person. Incidentally, when it came to studying, she was clearly better at it than I was.\
That was why I suspected she came over to my house because she didn't want to go straight home…\
With that in mind, I thought I'd invite her to come somewhere with us after school if she was free.\
“If you'd like, after school, you could come along with everyone…”\
“I'll pass. Even if you don't mind, the other girls won't like it.”\
That was true. But since she had refused so calmly, I decided to push a little.\
“No, I mean, we could all get to know each other better, you know?”\
I thought that if everyone actually talked to her, they'd probably find that they didn't care about her family after all. Wouldn't that work?\
Chitaira-san put the library book away in her bag and closed the conversation with a polite, “Thank you for inviting me.”\
It was a thin wall, built from a smile and impeccably courteous manners. Much like the atmosphere Chitaira-san always seemed to carry around with her.\
“But I've got things to do after school.”\
“Like what?”\
“Go home, study, and prepare for the night.”\
“A model student, are you?”\
It was a perfectly exemplary answer, although the second half contained something that warranted a little suspicion.\
“The night?”\
The phrase prepare for the night had a rather ominous ring to it. Anything involving the night tended to sound a little dubious. Chitaira-san at night. Sounds gloomy. Me at night. Sleepy. Dinner at night. Delicious.\
“When night comes, guess what happens?”\
Chitaira-san drew out her words, deliberately creating a dramatic pause. I found myself getting swept up in the atmosphere, almost leaning backwards in anticipation.\
“What do you think comes?”\
She asked me, again making it sound as though she were hinting at something.\
What comes at night…? At my house, perhaps my mother, coming home from work…? No, that definitely had nothing to do with it.\
Right.\
So I made one last desperate attempt.\
“D… drowsiness?”\
“Correct!”\
I didn't know the answer to the quiz, so I just filled in the blank on my answer sheet. During exams, I made a point of never leaving a single space empty. You never knew what might happen.\
Chitaira-san smiled with such breezy cheerfulness that I almost felt certain she was lying.
