## The Clod of Earth Encloses the Snow

\
Whether in the classroom, at home, or in some little room somewhere.\
I had never felt that I was at the centre of any of those places.\
Everyone’s eyes passed straight over me without ever settling on me. Perhaps because of that, my sense of myself was faint. It was as though I were borrowing someone else’s eyes to look at the world around me.\
And yet, when I was subjected to all manner of abuse, pain still hurt. Sometimes it hurt so much that I wasn't even allowed to scream, and it was hardly unusual for me to be left unconscious.\
Looking back now, I really do wonder how I managed to survive. The bruises in places hidden by my clothes only ever grew more numerous with each passing year.\
If that person hadn't happened to speak to me, and if I hadn't fallen in love, I doubt I would ever have established any sense of who I was. Love gave me a reason to live. It made me want to try living. I wanted to live, and I wanted, with all my strength, to have a relationship with that person.\
It wasn't enough simply to see her. Nor was it enough simply to talk to her.\
All that mattered to me was making my love come true.\
And now, I felt a sense of superiority over every single one of my classmates in this classroom.\
You don't know the beauty of that person's naked body.\
You don’t know the feel of her breasts. You don’t know their taste.\
And you certainly don't know what it is like to be so close to her.\
Especially you, her daughter.\
The daughter she gave birth to by opening her legs for someone other than me.\
I wonder what expression her daughter would make if she heard that her mother was suckling the breasts of a classmate of hers to regain her energy. She still speaks to me as though she feels a sense of closeness between us, but honestly, simply because she was raised receiving that person's affection, I want to tell her to give it back.\
If she picked up all the love that I was supposed to have to myself before I could claim it, then I want her to return it.\
“Amamiya-san, do you like your mother?”\
I tried asking her a question whose implications she would have absolutely no chance of understanding. It was a provocation, and a display of my superiority. Hidden beneath it was the desire to assert that her mother liked me more than she liked her. Of course, she wouldn't read any of that into it.\
It would be troublesome if she found out just yet, so I wanted her to remain oblivious.\
“Hmm... Yeah. Of course. I think we're pretty close.”\
“I see...”\
Come to think of it, she was her daughter. She must have suckled at her breast at some point too. ...Hmm.\
Well, she'll never have another opportunity to do so.\
“What about you, Chitaira-san? I've heard your house is pretty enormous.”\
“Oh, me? I can't say whether I like or dislike my mother. I've never met her.”\
That's right. I don't know which woman is my mother. I've never spoken to her. There has never even been a conversation to speak of. There are very few creatures in the Chitaira household capable of holding an ordinary conversation. Most of them are nothing more than something else wearing the shape of a human being. I am something other than human, born between those things that resemble humanity but are not quite human.\
“Oh... I see. Sorry.”\
“Don't be.”\
I wondered whether, if I had been born as that person's daughter, I might have grown up like this.\
Kind-hearted, cheerful, bright, friendly. Someone who could only live in places filled with light.\
And yet, I was certain that even if I had been born that way, I would still have fallen desperately in love with her.\
Even if she had been my own mother.\
“So, what have you been muttering about beside me all this time?”\
“Oh, well, I'm just wondering what to do about Valentine's Day chocolate...”\
“Valentine's Day... Oh, right. That was coming up, wasn't it?”\
I was so unfamiliar with seasonal events that, apart from Christmas, I barely remembered any of them.\
Last Christmas had been wonderful. The only thing I regretted was missing the chance to take a photograph.\
“I'm sure Hoshi-san gets loads from other girls already, so I was wondering if giving her even more chocolate might just be a nuisance...”\
“I think you should tell someone how you feel without worrying about what anyone else thinks.”\
That's what I do, after all. In fact, I don't merely refrain from holding back. I'm perfectly willing to push others aside.\
I'm going to take all the affection that person gives her daughter for myself, too.\
“F-Feelings... I mean, it's more like... I support her, or... she's someone I admire, and she's so close to me...”\
“Hmm... Someone you admire, huh?”\
What on earth was there to admire when you were leaving your own love unattended?\
I couldn't understand the feeling. Rather than admiring something beautiful from afar, I wanted to take it into my hands.\
But why was she telling me all this in the first place? I asked with my eyes, and she replied:\
“Because you're the only person I can talk about love with... about Hoshi-san.”\
“That's because I don't have any friends.”\
I used the same self-deprecating joke as before to get a laugh. Watching the daughter of that person smile awkwardly, I felt like warning her.\
One day, you won't be able to smile like that.\
I could have a little love talk of my own, too, I thought, almost baring my teeth.

The journey from school to the hotel was quite a long one. Once I entered the room, I threw my things aside and collapsed onto the bed. Lying face down, I let out a deep breath. My ribs pressed painfully against the mattress.\
That person wasn't coming today. Just knowing that made the room feel as though its heating had been switched off.\
My heart had been cast out into a wasteland, and I was acutely conscious of my loneliness.\
Once I had stopped, I discovered how painful it was to be alone.\
*Do you know about Valentine's Day?*\
I sent that person a message. After a little while, a reply arrived.\
I couldn't help imagining what expression she was wearing, or what she was doing as she secretly typed a reply to me.\
*Do you want chocolate?*\
*If Yuki-san loves me, then yes.*\
That person stubbornly refused to say aloud that she loved me. Perhaps she was afraid that, once she said it and made it clear, there would be no going back. Wasn't it a little late to be worrying about that now?\
But that earnestness of hers was precisely what I found so endearing.\
And the fact that she would set that earnestness aside and seek me out anyway was truly, unbearably wonderful.\
*I'll think about it.*\
I smiled, imagining that she would probably hide her phone again straight away.\
If her family ever caught sight of an exchange like this, there would be no way she could explain it away.\
But she had replied promptly, just as she'd promised. I loved her, I loved her so much that my legs started kicking against the bed.\
“Mutual love really is wonderful.”\
The other person looked at me. I could gaze steadily back at them without being rejected.\
I wanted to share a deep, lingering kiss with her right away, one that would make it feel as though there were only the two of us in the entire world.\
And so, for that purpose, I picked up my phone again.\
I had been waiting for the right moment, but surely enough time had passed.\
From here on, the real battle would begin. Even for me.\
I would be staking my life, my entire future.\
After all the effort it had taken to claw my way this far through life, I wanted to choose for myself when to put it all on the line.\
The call connected.\
“...Ah, Shiho-nee-san. I'd like you to go ahead with what we talked about before.”
