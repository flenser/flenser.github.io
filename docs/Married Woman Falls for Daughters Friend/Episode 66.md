## Chapter Four: The Snow Melts into the Clod of Earth - Part Ten

\
We returned to the coastal road and drove in the opposite direction from town. If we kept going like this, we'd eventually reach the Hakone area. Last winter, we'd gone there as a family to soak in the hot springs. I could still remember how lovely it had felt when the three of us ate the ice lollies provided for guests after getting out of the bath.\
But that beautiful family memory was no longer something I could share with the people beside me.\
Kai had probably never been to a hot spring facility.\
She didn't like exposing her skin, after all.\
I'd never actually seen the reason for that myself, either. ...Well, of course I hadn't. I never would.\
It was unthinkable.\
“Why are you grinning?”\
I admonished her for the obvious gaze and expression coming from the passenger seat.\
“I'm happy you came to see me, you know. Even if I don't show it.”\
“...It's not that I came because I wanted to see you...”\
I couldn't honestly confess the reason I'd acted on my desire to see her. Given my position, that was only natural.\
Besides, if I turned her down, Kai might feel lonely.\
Was that, to begin with, already a sign that my thinking was beyond saving?\
“I think about you all the time, Yuki-san.”\
“............Wha... Oh, I see.”\
I'd almost asked what sort of things she thought about, but decided against it. That would be opening a can of worms. Knowing Kai, she might well tell me in excruciating detail. She ought to spare a thought for the person who'd have to listen. I might even lose control of the car.\
“Ah.”\
Kai suddenly exclaimed as though she'd remembered something, then tugged at my clothes while I was driving.\
“Stop that. It's dangerous.”\
“Could you come again wearing the Santa outfit next time?”\
“What? Why?”\
“I was so moved that I forgot to take a photograph. Please cooperate with a photo shoot.”\
“Absolutely not.”\
I'd already carefully folded that Santa outfit away, where it now languished in the costume room. I'd never wear it again. Just looking at it would inevitably remind me of what I'd done while wearing it, and I'd be overwhelmed by terrible self-loathing and regret.\
“Then if you put it on and send me a selfie...”\
“I'm not doing that.”\
“Please, you pervy old lady. Sounds like the title of a TV programme.”\
“Now you've made me absolutely determined not to do it.”\
“Pleeease, perrrvy ooold laaady.”\
“Changing your pronunciation won't help.”\
Kai let out an entirely unrestrained click of her tongue. Honestly, she'd become so brazen lately.\
Would it be hopelessly naïve of me to interpret that as her feeling comfortable enough around me to let her guard down?\
“Ah.”\
I, too, had just remembered something.\
“Speaking of phones, that reminds me. Show me your mobile for a moment.”\
Kai blinked. Her reaction was suspiciously innocent, and pointlessly cute, which only irritated me more.\
“I didn't bring it.”\
“That's a lie. You hesitated for a split second just now.”\
“I really didn't. If you like, you could search my body for it.”\
“...We don't need to do that.”\
There had been a time when Kai begged me for a selfie, and I'd reluctantly sent her exactly one.\
It wasn't an indecent selfie. Absolutely not. But if someone else saw that she'd set it as her phone wallpaper, there was no way I could avoid looking guilty. After all, to Kai, I was simply her friend's mother. At least, that's what I was supposed to be, according to the usual understanding of things.\
“Come on. Hurry up.”\
“I told you, I don't have it.”\
“....................................”\
I pulled into the convenience store I could see on my left and parked. When I silently held out my hand, Kai raised both of hers in a gesture of surrender.\
“I really don't have it. If you can't believe me, you can give me a body search.”\
For a moment, the space between us seemed to twist, as though Kai were deliberately inviting me.\
“...Kai, have you got something confused?”\
I didn't want to touch Kai.\
Unlike you.\
The fleeting, improper thought I'd had about how slender her wrists were... That had been nothing more than a momentary lapse of judgement, brought on by the rain.\
“I know. You don't feel anything for me, Yuki-san.”\
Kai smiled as she spoke, picking up on things I hadn't said, as though she could see right through me.\
She was right. As far as I was concerned, Kai was... I'll say it plainly: she wasn't a romantic interest.\
As a wife, I simply couldn't have such feelings for anyone other than my husband.\
“Then there's no problem with you searching me, is there?”\
“...Well, that's... true, but. If you'd just hand over your phone...”\
“I'm only proving my innocence because you're asking me to produce something I don't have.”\
If she really didn't have her phone, I could simply tell her we'd look for it when we got back to the hotel.\
But when I tried to say that, my throat dried out as though it were bleeding, and no words came.\
The dryness of the winter air suddenly seemed to intensify, conspiring to get in my way.\
“...You're nothing but lies, aren't you?”\
So she must have her phone even now. She was hiding it somewhere.\
The scene from Christmas night came back to me. That night when I'd fled, frightened by what had begun to stir inside me when Kai touched me.\
Back then, I'd discovered something fatal.\
In the course of my contact with Kai.\
And now it was about to be brought back to the surface.\
Which was why I shouldn't have come to see her.\
“........................................”\
I reached towards Kai, who still had both hands raised, and my vision began to sway.\
“This is only to look for your phone.”\
“Okaaay.”\
Why was I so easily drawn in by this sort of invitation?\
Why was I closing the distance between us?\
I found myself staring at Kai's body, at the vulnerable expanse of her chest.\
“Oh, but taking my clothes off or peeking underneath them is... not allowed.”\
“It's not as if I'd do that...”\
It was only a search.\
There weren't many places to hide a phone on Kai, dressed as she was in her school uniform. If I tried to search the pockets of her skirt, I'd inevitably have to touch her legs. I'd have to reach towards her waist, too.\
Kai simply smiled. Just as she always did.\
She was slender and slight everywhere. No one whose society would consider them acceptable could possibly find an immature body like hers attractive. Of course, I felt nothing. My vision had been unsteady the whole time, and I was feeling dizzy and sick, but that was merely because I wasn't feeling well. The ringing in my ears wouldn't stop either, but that was just another symptom of my deteriorating condition. Everything inside me had suddenly ground to a halt. My body had become so utterly absorbed in the sensations of my fingertips and palms that it was neglecting everything else.\
Just cautiously placing my palms against Kai was enough to make the daylight lose its sun.\
It was as though I'd been plunged into pitch darkness. Everything around me disappeared from view, leaving only Kai. When I placed a hand against her side, she let out a muffled, slightly nasal “Mm...” and my hand stopped instinctively.\
I gazed at Kai from close up.\
Ripples began forming of their own accord on the surface of my heart.\
“Sorry. Don't mind me. Please carry on.”\
“...All right.”\
Her legs, her waist, her sides, her arms.\
The sensation of Kai's body was so fragile, so utterly at odds with her usual provocative manner.\
And yet, the tautness and softness of her skin made her age unmistakable. It felt like touching a child's body.\
The moment I consciously thought of her as a child, something inside me felt even more cornered.\
As I touched her, tracing the contours of her body through her clothes, my fingers seemed so sinful that I almost imagined they were bleeding.\
“Have you found it?”\
“...No.”\
“Should you keep looking?”\
Every time she spoke, I felt as though I could hear the unspoken words pervy old lady in her voice.\
Between my paranoid imaginings and the sight before me, which hardly seemed real, my head was spinning. I was genuinely close to feeling sick. Even if I said I'd continue, there were hardly any places on Kai's body I hadn't already touched.\
We were inside a car, yet I could hear the sound of rain beating down on us. A storm of noise swirled in my ears.\
Something was kindling and wavering deep inside my chest.\
Just as I was about to respond to it—\
“Ah.”\
Out of the corner of my eye, I spotted a mobile phone lying on the back seat, its screen cracked at one corner.\
Instead of answering, I glared at Kai.
