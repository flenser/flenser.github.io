## Interlude: The Story of the Daughter of a Happy Family Who Falls Head over Heels for a Blonde Beauty - Part 2

\
“Which brings us to the adult world. Welcome!”\
As if to show off the place, Hoshi-san swept her gaze around the interior, accompanying the gesture with an expansive flourish of her hands.\
The place she'd brought me to had walls and ceilings whose age contributed to its classical atmosphere.\
“This is a bar, but lately we've started serving lunch on weekend afternoons, too.”\
“Ahh.”\
It was an adult place, certainly. But essentially, she'd invited me to have lunch with her.\
There was no doubt she'd phrased the invitation deliberately to suggest something more. She'd been teasing me.\
Come to think of it, this place was near where Mum worked. Oh, speaking of Mum...\
Hoshi-san watched me take out my phone, a faint smile playing on her lips.\
Whatever her expression, whatever the background, she somehow managed to stand out without being diminished by any of it.\
“Oh, I'm just messaging Mum to tell her I'll be having lunch out...”\
“Hmm. Are you close to your parents?”\
“Um, yes. I'd say we're pretty close.”\
“That's wonderful,” Hoshi-san said with a smile.\
Just looking at her face almost made me think, Ahh, yes, it really is. If this woman were a bad person, I felt I would slide smoothly into ruin.\
“So, what's your impression of the place?”\
“I-it feels like a bar.”\
There were bottles of alcohol everywhere, on the tables and on the shelves behind us. Just looking at all those bottles lined up made me feel almost tipsy, imagining the tastes of drinks I'd never tried. But the bottles themselves, perhaps I should call them that, glimmered in different colours in the bright light, like a distant city skyline at night. They were beautiful.\
When I shared that impression, Hoshi-san praised it as readily as she praised everything else.\
“A cityscape of bottles, huh? I see. That's a good way of putting it.”\
“Oh, no...” I lowered my head, embarrassed.\
Perhaps Takasora-san was the sort of person who could ruin you just by being nice to you.\
I'd left the ordering entirely to Hoshi-san. After a while, someone with conspicuously heavy make-up came out from the back carrying two trays. Seeing her alongside that woman made me wonder whether Mum even wore make-up at all.\
After placing the trays in front of us, Hoshi-san introduced her in the manner of someone showing a visitor around a famous landmark.\
“Allow me to introduce this woman. There's absolutely nothing to praise about her except her cooking.”\
“And this one is a woman who, despite that face, hasn't done anything particularly bad apart from playing with other women's hearts.”\
“Gwahahaha!”\
The two of them burst out laughing. The woman from the kitchen, presumably, turned to head back inside.\
“Oh, correction. Her fashion sense is terrible, too. What on earth is that hanten?”\
“Oi, you! It's a hand-me-down from an old lady in the neighbourhood. Anyway, has Rin contacted you at all?”\
“Of course not. She's probably deleted my number by now.”\
“Yeah, probably.”\
“Don't stay too long,” she said, leaving us with a farewell that hardly seemed appropriate for someone in the food-service business.\
“Well, she's a terrible mother, even by an outsider's standards. But she's a good cook. Go on, tuck in.”\
“Th-thank you for the food.”\
I'd met a beautiful older woman, let myself be swept along, and before I knew it, here I was having lunch with her.\
How strange.\
I wasn't exactly unhappy about it, though. I popped a little dish into my mouth along with that thought.\
The lunch was uniformly Japanese in style, from the tray and tableware to the food itself. Well, perhaps the pickles were an exception.\
I tried the pickles first.\
“Oh!”\
Then I compared them thoughtfully.\
“So, does it suit your taste?”\
I could almost hear someone from the back of the restaurant answering, Of course it does.\
“It's delicious, but...”\
“Oh? What's this?”\
“I think Dad's cooking is better. Ah, though, I'm biased.”\
But Dad's cooking really was delicious, whichever dish you picked. As for Mum's cooking... she won through sheer affection.\
“That's perfectly fine. Children ought to think their parents are the best.”\
Hoshi-san stopped eating, nodded solemnly, and then broke into a radiant smile.\
“In other words... the daughter of a happy family!”\
“Huh?”\
And so, we enjoyed a pleasant lunch together.\
“I invited you on a date, so I'll pay.”\
“Da...!”\
“Don't charge me for treating your bitten tongue, please.”\
“It's f-fine!”\
The taste of blood spreading through my mouth made it difficult to get the words out properly.\
“Still, I never learn.”\
After we left the restaurant, Hoshi-san narrowed her eyes.\
“What is it?”\
“Well, I ate far too much lunch, and now I'm terribly sleepy. Even though I have work this afternoon.”\
“Oh, you have work? That sounds tough...”\
“There are no adults who don't find work difficult. Well, see you.”\
“Ah, yes...”\
Like a dream, that almost comically weightless stretch of time came to an abrupt end.\
Or so I thought.\
“Amami-chan.”\
Hoshi-san came skipping back towards me, her geta clacking lightly against the ground. I watched the hair resting on her shoulder slip smoothly down, and lost what little vocabulary I had left.\
“Y-yes?”\
“Can I make a sexually harassing remark?”\
She asked with a perfectly clear voice and an unclouded smile. The question itself seemed like enough to qualify as sexual harassment.\
“G-go right ahead!”\
I braced myself, almost as though preparing to take a punch to the chest. Hoshi-san smiled sweetly at that very spot.\
“Amami-chan, you've got big boobs!”\
I witnessed my cheeks burst into flames in real time, accompanied by a sudden roar and a wave of heat.\
I don't know what it means to witness a sound and heat, either.\
“That was a much more direct case of sexual harassment than I expected! Sir, yes, sir!”\
“Nahaha! I absolutely love girls with big boobs. See you!”\
“…………Huh?”\
The sound of her geta echoed hollowly inside my head, like something rolling around in an empty container.\
Love...\
Me?\
No, no... There must be plenty of other girls with big breasts.\
Like Mum, for instance.\
“...M-my b...”\
In a fluster, I tried to cup my own chest, then realised where I was and hurriedly sprang away from myself.\
Even when I tried to run away from my own thoughts, they stuck to me like a loyal dog.

Even after I got home, light seemed to fill everything, as though I'd walked along a road just after the rain.\
I peered into the rooms of the empty house and found a note from Mum in the living room.\
I've just popped out for a bit.\
Perhaps she'd remembered something she needed to buy. Come to think of it, I hadn't seen her car in the garage.\
But that was the least of my concerns right now. I sat down on the nearest chair and rested my chin in my hands, supporting my face with both palms.\
My expression had grown soft, heavy, and utterly slack.\
I was gazing dreamily into space. Yes, dreamily.\
The exhilaration of having something wonderful happen to me was lingering for an almost unbelievable length of time.\
I hadn't known there could be someone I thought was so beautiful.\
I'd never seen hair that turned the world before my eyes golden.\
“Her face was so good... Wait, was it that good? Why was it so... huh?”\
She was an adult, and even among adults, her smile was dazzling. It seemed to make everything inside my chest transparent.\
We'd only just parted, yet remembering her made me twist and writhe so much that my sides hurt.\
Even the lingering pain in my tongue felt pleasant, the sensation itself a delight.\
I couldn't bear to wait to hear that voice calling me Amami-chan again.\
As I gazed at the light streaming into the living room from outside, I found myself murmuring before I realised it.\
“...Sora.”\
Each time I rolled that name around on my tongue, a flavour richer than candy spread through my mouth.
