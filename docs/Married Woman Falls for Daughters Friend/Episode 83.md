## Chapter Five: The Clod of Earth Is Sweeter Than Snow – Part Nine

\
I got into the car, closed the door and let out a deep breath. Finding relief in escaping other people's eyes. I really was a proper criminal.\
Shivering in the air-conditioned interior, I hunched over and fumbled with my phone.\
A little while later, Kai answered.\
*It's unusual for you to call.*\
“That's true.”\
I'm a wretched wife, after all, holding back for the sake of protecting myself.\
*You're calling to discuss what sort of naughty selfie you'd like, aren't you?*\
“You idiot.”\
*Why does covering the eyes make those pictures so much more indecent, I wonder?*\
“Idiot.”\
*I feel like you just insulted me from the bottom of your heart.*\
I certainly did.\
“That's not what I called about... I'm sorry about yesterday.”\
*You're still worrying about that?*\
Kai sounded surprised. Of course I was still worrying about it.\
It was difficult to peer into another person's heart. Perhaps, behind that smile, they were secretly sticking out their tongue at me.\
Nothing proved how impossible it was to know someone's true feelings more clearly than the way I treated my own family.\
*Yuki-san, you're quite the worrier, aren't you?*\
“I can't help it. I'm a coward.”\
Once, that anxious heart of mine had made me leave work early because I was worried about my daughter having a cold.\
Now, I was fretting over whether Kai hated me because I'd attacked her.\
As though I'd exchanged one person's affection for another's, I'd become a terrible mother.\
*I told you yesterday, too... You don't need to worry about something like that.*\
“It's not something like that... I know it might be presumptuous of me, but I want to...”\
Save you. Get through this together.\
And for that, I'd decided to destroy yet another part of the life I'd known until now.\
*Besides, I was happy.*\
“Happy?”\
*Because I finally got to meet the real naughty old lady.*\
“...I'm a naughty old lady...”\
A naughty old lady I could no longer deny being.\
I am a woman whose life’s goal is to do naughty things with Kai. I want to sleep with a fourteen-year-old.\
At the age difference, and at the very fact of our ages, a strange little laugh escaped me. My twisted lips wouldn't come together properly.\
*So, with that in mind, I'd like a selfie befitting a naughty old lady.*\
I hung up. Turning the car key, I told myself it was time to go to work.\
But something wasn't right.\
I'd developed a bad habit lately.\
There was a part of me that seemed to believe that if I did something deliberately, or simply brazened it out, that somehow made me innocent. Perhaps my mind couldn't accept that I was doing something wrong, and was twisting what little wisdom it had into knots in an attempt to protect itself.\
I was already doing something unforgivable, yet I was calmly setting it alongside my ordinary daily life.\
It was as though I were wandering through a dream, refusing to revise my previous assessment of myself: that I could never possibly do such a thing.\
Every time I came to my senses, I was driven to the brink of despair, to the point where I wanted to die.\
The more I did it, the stronger the backlash became.\
And yet, once again, I was about to go and see Kai.\
I was so impatient for the next opportunity that I was afraid I might become distracted behind the wheel.\
I was going to see Kai.\
I wanted to go and see her.\
I wanted to see her.\
Let me see her.\
I hated the world that stood in my way.\
Depending on how I phrased it, my attitude towards Kai could change into any number of things. For now, at least, it could all go to hell.\
“Drop dead.”\
With curses gathering inside my cheeks, I continued driving through the dawn.

---

I knew I was heading down the wrong road, but the headache kept getting in the way of sound judgement.\
Whenever I thought about Kai on my way home from work, I remembered my husband waiting for me at home and grew irritated. Then I remembered my daughter and grew irritated. But that made it sound as though I was irritated with my family, and that thought only further unsettled me. The stress kept bearing down on me, a constant, heavy burden. And so I was trapped in the worst possible vicious circle: using visits to Kai as an outlet for that stress.\
When I met Kai, the irritation that had been wearing me down became something else entirely... And because being with her made me feel better, I clung to it. I was a full-fledged addict.\
The cause and effect made no sense. Kai was the reason I'd ended up like this in the first place.\
And yet, in front of that very Kai, I could expose my wretched self and feel as though I was being saved.
