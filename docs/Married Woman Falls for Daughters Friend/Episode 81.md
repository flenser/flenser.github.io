## Chapter Five: The Clod of Earth Is Sweeter Than Snow – Part Seven

\
"I want to see you again already."\
"I love you."\
"Love you."\
"Love you."\
"I'll be waiting again tomorrow."\
Some time after I got home, a rapid-fire barrage of messages like these came from Kai.\
The date hadn't even changed yet.\
Without waiting for a reply, she just keeps piling on one-sided confessions of love.\
Looking down at them, the water level creeping steadily upward had already covered my mouth and reached my cheeks.\
"I love you too..."\
I started to type it, but stopped.\
I set the phone down and headed to the bedroom. Trying not to wake my husband, who had already gone to bed before me, I slid under the covers as quietly as I could. The inside of the futon was freezing cold, and my shoulders trembled the moment I touched it.\
I listened for his breathing, he seemed to be fast asleep. He used to fake sleep like that and then spring some prank on me the moment I let my guard down. Fifteen years since we built this house and moved in. Fifteen years of climbing into the same futon together. Objectively speaking, I think it was a relationship that worked well. There was real fulfillment in it, and there was joy in being held by my husband. A daughter was born as proof that he and I had exchanged love, and our happiness became all the more unshakable.\
All in past tense, I mock myself. Even lying still, neither the futon nor my body will warm up.\
This house, the things I belong to, I can feel them turning into past tense, one after another. Just as treasures I held as a child, now, only make me squint with the faintest nostalgia, I am stepping forward, ignoring the pace of my family. I'm leaving my family behind in the past.\
The present me, when I look back, was pathetic.\
I'd cried so much that I felt like I might start crying about that, too.\
Had there ever been a time in my life when I cried like that? Being teased for my blonde hair at school and crying quietly in hiding was the most vivid memory I had of my own tears, but today was probably going to overwrite it.\
Even now there's nothing but regret in my chest, and it's hard to breathe. The guilt and remorse over having hurt Kai don't even compare to the shame of continuing to betray my family. I even think I want to keep apologizing, like washing clothes over and over until not a single stain remains.\
When I remember how I desperately begged for forgiveness when Kai rejected me, I want to die. How ridiculous I must have looked to Kai, so frantically undone, clinging to her with tears in my eyes. "Desperate old pervert," she must have thought. "Way too desperate."\
Driven by paranoid delusions that I'm being laughed at by a girl the same age as my daughter, I still had no choice but to acknowledge it. I am desperate. I gave everything I had to loving Kai.\
For example, if Kai were holding hands with someone else, I would demand to know who.\
For example, if Kai had invited someone else into a hotel room, I would pound on the door loudly enough to bother the neighbors.\
For example, if Kai were being held by someone else, it would be a replay of the time I resorted to violence.\
To sum all of that up in one word:\
I love Kai.\
The moment the fire caught, the moment I wanted to hold Kai, I could no longer hide that feeling.\
It feels all the more like the worst possible judgment call, as if I'm using sexual desire to determine the truth of it, but—\
To me, Kai is that kind of person. I'm in love with a girl twenty-four years younger than me. With a husband, a daughter, and a beloved dog, I'm nurturing a romance outside my family. In other words: infidelity, adultery, an affair.\
A crime.\
The other party is an unmistakable minor, my daughter's classmate. If the relationship is exposed, every conceivable force will punish me, carve me up, cast me out of society and put my head on a pike. My husband might rage at me for my betrayal and kill me. There isn't a single reason for him to forgive me.\
If I go to see Kai, of course it will be fun. I'm crazy about that girl.\
The middle-aged woman drunk on love, charging headlong into an age-inappropriate romance, was, unluckily, or perhaps luckily, in a mutual love with the object of her affections. Neither Kai's feelings nor my love leave any room for doubt. We undoubtedly need each other, and that's exactly what makes it so terrible. If it weren't mutual, nothing more would happen. But because we've connected, all sorts of things will happen from here on out, and the damage will spread.\
I can't sleep. My exhaustion, physical and mental, is obvious, yet it won't let me rest.\
I dare, I dare to face my own desire.\
Having failed to suckle at Kai's breast, left hanging, an unfulfilled need had been building up.\
"........................... "\
No matter how many times I turned over, it wouldn't peel away, and I felt like I'd never be able to sleep. Inside my nightgown, tracing directly along my skin with my fingers, it took on that shape before I knew it, and remembering the feel of Kai's lips and hands, I writhed beside my husband, tormenting myself.\
Stifling my voice, careful not to move the futon, so no one would ever know.\
"...You idiot, aren't you..."\
Even with the Kai of my imagination as my partner, my body was responding, breath rising regardless.\
I despised myself from the bottom of my heart for getting heated at my age, my past self felt so far away.
