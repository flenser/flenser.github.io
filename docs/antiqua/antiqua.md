## Synopsis

### A yuri game of love, played out across the bar counter.

A proper, old-school bar dressed up like a girls' bar — Antiqua. Ordinary office worker Kurosu Mikoto finds herself locked in a romantic game with the enigmatic bartender known as "Shandy" — can she win?

This is the serialized version of a short story, "琥珀色恋愛遊戯" (Amber-Colored Game of Love), originally published in the anthology Yuri Marché, it's included here as the Prologue.
