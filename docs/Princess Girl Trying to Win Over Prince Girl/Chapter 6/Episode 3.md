## Chapter Six: "The Princess Screams in Jealousy" — Part Three

\
"You won't find another friend who'd help you make a lunchbox at the crack of dawn, you know."\
"…Thank you so much, Yuuri."

I'd only asked her the night before, and yet Yuuri had shown up at my house at five in the morning.\
This time, no amount of thanks would be enough.

"So, what are we making? Do you have a lunchbox?"

"Iori-senpai eats a lot, so I was hoping for something filling. You can use whatever's in the fridge, and there's a lunchbox my dad used to take to work."

*I see*, Yuuri said with a nod, and opened the fridge.\
She checked the ingredients inside, then checked the time on her phone.

"Himena, for now, mix some salt into the white rice and make rice balls. Barrel-shaped."\
"Barrel-shaped?"\
"Yes. We'll wrap them in pork and grill them later, so they become meat-wrapped rice balls. Meanwhile, I'll get the fried chicken ready."

With that, Yuuri rolled up her sleeves and started cooking.\
She was so efficient that I stopped to stare.

Yuuri really is amazing…!

Compared to her, I was hopeless.\
But she'd given me instructions I could manage. Shaping rice balls into barrels was something even I could do.

"All right! Operation: Appeal with a Lunchbox ♡ Make Her Imagine Our Lovey-Dovey Newlywed Life ♡, begin!!"\
"Still such a lame operation name…"

Yuuri muttered it with exasperation, but her face was so gentle that I felt a bit shy.\
And then Yuuri seemed to get shy too, and quickly turned her face away.

☆★☆★☆★☆★

"Wow, it looks like a lunchbox for sports day…!!"

With over eighty percent of the work done by Yuuri, the finished lunchbox was impressive enough that I couldn't help applauding.\
A large two-tier box, with meat-wrapped rice balls in the top tier.\
The bottom tier held the side dishes: substantial items like fried chicken and ginger pork, plus rolled omelette and cherry tomatoes for colour.\
On top of that, a soup jar full of pork miso soup packed with ingredients. And strawberries for dessert.

With this, Iori-senpai would definitely be happy!

"Thank you, Yuuri. Today I'm going to win Senpai's stomach for sure."\
"In that case, I'm the one who won it."\
"E-even you can't have Senpai!"\
"Don't want her."

Yuuri sighed and drank some of the leftover miso soup.\
Incidentally, our own lunches were made from what was left over after Iori-senpai's, so they looked a bit rough, whatever they tasted like.

"I hope Iori-senpai compliments it."\
"I hope she says something like *I love you, Himena-chan, let's get married.*"\
"You'd hate someone who was that easily won over by food."\
"If it's Senpai, it's cute."\
"Yes, yes."

I looked at the clock and found more time had passed than I'd expected.\
I wanted to greet Senpai after morning practice, so it was nearly time to head out.

I took a deep breath to settle myself.\
Whether Setoguchi-sensei was there or not, what I had to do didn't change.\
Appeal to Senpai again today and work toward becoming her official girlfriend. That was all.

☆★☆★☆★☆★

I wanted to hand her the lunchbox as soon as possible, so I decided to lie in wait for Iori-senpai after morning practice.

"Iori, you've really gotten good. Your shooting's more accurate than before, and your dribbling's faster too."

As I approached the gymnasium, I heard Setoguchi-sensei's voice.\
I peeked quietly inside, and there was Iori-senpai, holding a basketball and smiling shyly.

There was no one else in the gym but the two of them.

"…I don't like this."

They probably hadn't been doing morning practice alone together.\
Morning practice was for the whole basketball club. Iori-senpai had presumably just kept going longer than the others, and Setoguchi-sensei had joined her.

I understood that, but I didn't like it anyway.\
I couldn't bring myself to call out, so I quietly watched them.\
Iori-senpai was looking straight at Setoguchi-sensei.

"If we'd been one year closer in age, I could have been on the same team as you in high school too, Kaede-san."\
"True. But then I might have worked you to the bone, you know?"\
"That's fine. I'd be your kouhai."\
"You really are adorable, Iori."

Laughing heartily, Setoguchi-sensei ruffled Iori-senpai's head roughly.\
Even with her hair completely messed up, Senpai didn't complain at all.\
If anything, she crouched a little so it was easier for Setoguchi-sensei to reach.

No. No. No.\
Senpai, why are you letting someone else touch you? You're my girlfriend.

The bell rang. If I didn't get to class soon, I'd be late.

"…Let's go."

Without speaking to Iori-senpai, I changed direction and headed for the shoe lockers.\
If I faced her now, I wasn't confident I could greet her properly with a smile.

☆★☆★☆★☆★

"Himena. You look pale. Did something happen with Iori-senpai?"

When I got to the classroom, Yuuri, who'd arrived before me, jogged over.\
Apparently I looked terrible enough that she could tell from across the room.

"…Nothing. I couldn't talk to her. She was with Setoguchi-sensei."\
"Himena…"\
"I know. Senpai didn't do anything wrong. They were just practising basketball together."

What hurt was that I lacked confidence. As her "trial" girlfriend, I wasn't sure of Senpai's love.

"…Why not try telling Iori-senpai it hurts?"\
"I can't. Because I'm just a 'trial.'"

If we were proper girlfriends, maybe I could have been honest with her.\
But not yet.\
I had to put a lid on all the troublesome parts and show Senpai only the cute ones.
