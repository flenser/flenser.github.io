## Chapter Six: "The Princess Screams in Jealousy" — Part Two

\
As her girlfriend, I trusted Iori-senpai.\
Sure, it was still only a "trial," but that didn't mean I thought Iori-senpai was the type of person to act in bad faith.

But there was every chance Setoguchi-sensei liked Iori-senpai, right?!

For starters, Iori-senpai was so charming that it was only natural for all of humanity to fall for her. Which meant I might as well treat all of humanity as the enemy.\
To fight, you first have to know your enemy.\
And so I decided to begin observing Setoguchi-sensei.

☆★☆★☆★☆★

During lessons, she stood at the back of the classroom taking diligent notes. Every break, students surrounded her, and she responded to each of them with a smile.\
In PE, her demonstrations were excellent, and she also paid proper attention to students who weren't good at sports, which is to say, me.

After observing her all the way until after school, I'd found no flaws to speak of.\
Setoguchi-sensei was probably a good person and a good teacher.

"Which is exactly why I can't stand it…!"\
"And apparently she was really strong at basketball, too."

*Look*, Yuuri said, holding out her phone. It showed a news article from a few years back.\
The high school Setoguchi-sensei had attended had won the prefectural tournament, and the photo had her in it.

"An old acquaintance, a basketball friend, and a dependable older girl, huh."\
"Ugh…"\
"Well, she's probably just someone Iori-senpai knows, so try not to overthink it."

Yuuri put her phone away and said *see you* before leaving the classroom.\
She had home economics club activities after this.\
All I could do was wait for Iori-senpai's practice to finish.

"…Didn't Setoguchi-sensei say she'd help out with basketball practice?"

A sharp pang in my chest. I didn't want to cry since it would ruin my makeup, but I couldn't hold the feeling back.

☆★☆★☆★☆★

I peeked quietly into the gymnasium and spotted Setoguchi-sensei in her tracksuit.\
She was standing next to the basketball club coach, giving instructions to the members and helping with practice.

"…Lucky."

It couldn't be helped, but all I could do was wait outside the gymnasium for Iori-senpai.\
It was lively inside and quiet outside, and even though I was so close, it felt like another world.

Even so, I would wait for her. I wanted to be with her for every single minute and second.

Even after practice ended, Senpai always stayed in the gym until the very end. And today, she came out together with Setoguchi-sensei.

"Good work today, Iori-senpai!"

I greeted her with the biggest smile I could manage. After a tiring practice, she surely wouldn't want to deal with a gloomy me.

*Get home safely*, Setoguchi-sensei said, and headed off toward the staff room.\
The moment we were alone, Iori-senpai quietly took my hand.\
Normally we only held hands once we were off school grounds.

"You looked lonely, Himena-chan."

She smiled and laid her hand against my cheek. Her slightly sweaty palm was warm and comforting.

"I-Iori-senpai…"\
"I'm sorry for making you feel lonely. Let's have lunch together tomorrow."

I felt sorry for making her worry. I felt happy that she worried about me.\
The two opposing feelings mixed together, and I instinctively looked down.

"…Iori-senpai."\
"Yes?"

I like her. I love Iori-senpai. And that's exactly why I'm worried and anxious.

*You're not in love with Setoguchi-sensei, are you?*

I wanted to ask. I wanted Senpai to deny it and tell me no.\
If she did, I could relax.

But I couldn't ask. If she decided I was a troublesome girlfriend, I wouldn't be able to go on living.\
What I should be doing wasn't venting my anxiety, but appealing to Senpai.\
I had to make her feel that going out with me was fun.

"I'll make you a lunchbox tomorrow, Iori-senpai!"\
"Huh?"\
"I'll make you a lunchbox so delicious it'll shock you, so please look forward to it!"

I lifted my head and worked hard to produce a cute smile.\
So that she wouldn't have to worry about me any further. So that she'd want to see me again tomorrow too.

"…Yes. I'll look forward to it, Himena-chan."

As long as Iori-senpai smiled, I could swallow down any amount of anxiety.
