## Chapter Six: "The Princess Screams in Jealousy" — Part Four

\
"Iori-senpai! Let's eat lunch together!"

I'd burst out of the classroom the moment lunch started, to keep Senpai from coming to mine.\
It was my tearfully earnest attempt to reduce, even slightly, the chances of Senpai and Setoguchi-sensei running into each other.

Seeing me appear at breakneck speed, Senpai's eyes went wide.\
*You're fast*, she said with a delighted laugh, and for her sake, sprinting down the corridor at full speed had been worth it.

"Senpai, let's go to our usual spot."

I linked arms with her and looked up at her.\
It's fine. The me reflected in her eyes was smiling, just like always.

"Okay."

She nodded, and I led her by the hand to our usual courtyard.\
It was getting warm, so before long we might need to find somewhere else.\
The rainy season would make eating outside difficult too…

I was sure there were other places where we could be alone if I looked.\
But this one was our favourite, so it made me a little sad.

"Senpai. I made this for you!"

*Ta-da!* I pulled out the big lunchbox. Looking inside, Iori-senpai exclaimed, *wow.*\
I'd told her in advance that I'd bring a lunchbox, but she probably hadn't expected one this big.

"Did you make all of this yourself, Himena-chan?"\
"Yes! W-well, I did get a little, no, um, quite a lot of help from Yuuri…"

I'd thought about boasting that I made it all myself, but decided against it.\
That would be far too unfair to Yuuri.

"Thank you. It all looks delicious."\
"I-I hope it tastes good…!"\
"Himena-chan."

She handed me something: the disposable chopsticks I'd brought.\
I froze, unable to work out what she meant, and she tilted her head slightly.

"Don't you want to feed me?"\
"I do!!"

That teasing look, amazing…!\
And Iori-senpai was actually the one asking for it! Well, maybe she wasn't asking! Maybe I'd just had a longing look on my face!

Either way, the result was a win.\
I steadied my trembling hand and first picked up a piece of rolled omelette with the chopsticks.

"S-Senpai, say ah…"

She took the omelette from my chopsticks in one bite. The moment she chewed it, she smiled and said, *it's delicious.*\
Happiness… I really want to make her a lunchbox every day. Which means I want to marry her.

"Himena-chan, I'm sorry about yesterday. I know you look forward to lunch."\
"Iori-senpai…"\
"It was just that I hadn't seen Kaede-san in so long."

Hearing Setoguchi-sensei's name from Senpai's mouth hurt.\
But I also thought that her talking to me honestly like this meant she was facing me properly.

"…You and Setoguchi-sensei are close, aren't you."\
"Yes. There's a big age gap, so it's not quite like being friends. How should I put it… It's a bit embarrassing, but I think she's someone I look up to."

She tucked her hair behind her ear. A gesture she often made when she was embarrassed.\
It was cute, but right now it left me with complicated feelings.

"I said we were on the same basketball team in primary school, didn't I?"\
"Yes."\
"Kaede-san was captain then. She was great at basketball, of course, but as captain she watched everyone closely and thought about the whole team."

I'd never played a team sport, so I didn't really understand, but thinking about the whole team and not only yourself must be very hard.\
Maybe it was because she could do that that Setoguchi-sensei had wanted to become a teacher.

"I could only think about myself. But because of Kaede-san, I started to want to become a captain who could hold a team together. To win, my own strength alone isn't enough."

*I'm still nowhere near there*, she murmured, and she looked slightly troubled.\
Maybe Senpai was carrying lots of worries that I couldn't even imagine.

Now that I thought about it, Iori-senpai didn't really seem like the captain type…

She was often surrounded by people, but that was because she was popular.\
Iori-senpai herself, I thought, probably preferred acting alone over acting as a group.

"…Senpai, you're wonderful."\
"I'm glad, but where did that come from?"\
"Isn't it incredibly admirable that you're trying to take on something you're not good at?!"

Kaede-san might have influenced her, but it was Iori-senpai herself who'd decided to work at it.\
So I thought that decision was precious.

"Himena will always, for anything, cheer you on while you do your best, Senpai! With everything I have!!"\
"Himena-chan…"

Even now, the unease about Setoguchi-sensei was still there.\
I was jealous of the fact that she was someone Senpai admired and had been influenced by.\
But I didn't want to deny it. Her admiration for Setoguchi-sensei had been what gave Iori-senpai the drive to work hard.

"…Hey, Himena-chan."

Senpai's hand reached out and gently took mine. And in a lovers' grip, no less.\
My heart pounded at the suddenness of it and my face went hot.

"S-Senpai…?"\
"My girlfriend is only you, Himena-chan."\
"Ah, wah, aaah…"

I made a sound that wasn't quite a voice, and Senpai laughed softly.\
And then, just like that, she kissed my forehead.

Wait?! No, don't wait!! Keep going as far as you can!!

"Thank you for everything, Himena-chan."\
"…I've been exposed to a lethal dose of happiness."\
"That would be a problem. I need you to live a long time."\
"T-that means you want us to be together for life, right?!"

I stood up in my excitement.\
The dark mood from before felt like it had never existed.\
The world was shining. All the rainbows in the world had probably gathered right here.\
But Senpai was far more dazzling than all the world's rainbows put together.

"Iori-senpai. I'm so, so happy!!"\
"I'm glad."\
"This is all thanks to you, Iori-senpai. Himena is made one hundred percent of love for you!"

Happiness. Joy. Delight. If I gathered every positive feeling in the dictionary, it still wouldn't be enough to express my feelings.

That's right. Even if Setoguchi-sensei was a senpai she looked up to, I was Iori-senpai's girlfriend.\
Her one and only girlfriend!!

"Senpai. Himena loves you the most in the entire galaxy today, too!!"
