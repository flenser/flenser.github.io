## Chapter Six: "The Princess Screams in Jealousy" — Part One

\
"Um! D-do you two know each other…?!"

If something bothered me, I charged straight in. The word "wait and see" wasn't in my dictionary.

Because if I didn't ask now, I'd be stewing over it for sure! And besides, I'm her girlfriend, so I'm allowed to ask!

I asked while somehow holding on to my cutest face, and Iori-senpai nodded without hesitation.

"We were on the same basketball team when we were in primary school."\
"…The same team?"\
"Yes. There's a four-year gap, so we never overlapped in middle school or high school."

I glanced beside me, and Setoguchi-sensei was nodding too. Senpai didn't seem to be lying.

"And since it's been so long, we decided to have lunch together."\
"I-I see, so that's what it was…"

Which meant the reason I'd been turned down for lunch was that she was eating with Setoguchi-sensei.\
Frustration bubbled up inside me, and I dug my nails into my palm to hide it.

"I'm sorry, Himena-chan."\
"Ugh… you're as beautiful as ever, Iori-senpai, I love you…"

She pressed her hands together in front of her face and bowed her head slightly, apologetic, and she was as impossibly pretty as ever.\
I like her. I like her so much. I really do love her. I see her every day, and every day I can't stop liking her more…!!

"Iori. Are you sure about this?"

Setoguchi-sensei looked at me as if checking on me.\
The truth was, I wanted to shout *no!* and hold Senpai back.\
But the two of them hadn't seen each other in ages, they were senpai and kouhai from the same team, and Senpai had explained everything properly.

If I tried to restrict her here, she might start hating the idea of going out with me, right…?

"Iori-senpai. Please don't worry about me, and enjoy yourself!"

This was surely the right thing for a girlfriend to do.\
To be promoted to her proper girlfriend, I couldn't afford to be selfish.

"Thank you, Himena-chan."

Her smile was dazzling, and she looked so genuinely happy that I was frustrated enough to cry.

"…So in the end, you're not convinced at all."\
"Shh, Yuuri, quiet. She'll notice us!!"

We whispered to each other as we peered out from behind a tree at Iori-senpai and Setoguchi-sensei.\
I just couldn't stop thinking about the two of them, so I'd decided to keep watch, together with Yuuri and Kanami-senpai.\
This way, even if Senpai did notice us, we could say we'd just happened to be eating in the shade.

"…Iori-chan seems a little different from usual, doesn't she?"

Kanami-senpai, curled up as small as she could manage, murmured it as she watched Iori-senpai. Her observation was right.\
Perhaps because she was talking more than usual, Senpai was eating slowly.\
Her gestures as she talked made her look younger than she normally did.

What are they talking about?\
We were too far away to catch the details of the conversation.\
But from the scraps of words that drifted over, I could tell they were having a lively chat about basketball.

I love Iori-senpai, but I don't have much in common with her.\
Which is why I envied Setoguchi-sensei, who could get that animated with her.

"…Am I petty?"\
"Yes, you are."\
"Oh, Yuuri-chan, that's…"

Unlike Kanami-senpai, who encouraged me with a kind *that's not true*, Yuuri was cold.\
But I could tell from her eyes that she was thinking about me.

"Himena. You go out with just me sometimes too, don't you."\
"…Yes."\
"You're the one who's dating her, so have some confidence."\
"Yuuri…!"\
"Though it's only a 'trial.'"

Adding something unnecessary at the end was very Yuuri, but she had a point.\
I couldn't tell Senpai not to be close with other girls.\
Realistically, it wasn't even possible. Senpai had her own relationships and I had mine.

I know that. But still…

"You can't stop jealousy, can you…!!"

Yuuri let out a long sigh as I crumpled to the ground.
