## Prologue 2: "I'll Pick Up Your Bones"

A day like any other. A classroom like any other.\
But today was a special anniversary.

"Yuuri. Do you know what today is?"\
"No."\
"Today is Iori-senpai's summer uniform anniversary!"

Sakuraba High changed uniforms in May.\
Yuuri and I had already switched last week.\
That said, winter uniforms were permitted through the end of May, so it had been hard to predict when Iori-senpai would change.\
Which was exactly why, the moment I saw her this morning, I'd nearly screamed with excitement.

"Senpai's arms were so beautiful…! Beautiful enough that I wanted to latch my mouth onto them…!"\
"Gross."\
"Could you not use such a plain insult?!"\
"It's plainly gross, so that's on you."

Having said something outrageously rude to someone as cute as me, Yuuri started scrolling on her phone, completely uninterested.\
Honestly, if I weren't so generous, I'd be furious.

But I'm cute and kind, and I have a gorgeous girlfriend, so I wasn't going to get angry.\
Besides, there was something far more important to take care of.

I opened the couples app and entered *Summer Uniform Anniversary*, along with the photo of Iori-senpai I'd begged to take this morning after the flower bed watering.\
The app let me record every anniversary for me and Iori-senpai.

"…Aren't there a lot of anniversaries?"\
"With Iori-senpai around, every day is an anniversary."\
"I've never seen anyone log one every single day, and I mean that literally."

Yuuri sighed, clearly done.\
Honestly, no romance in her soul at all.

Today was summer uniform day, yesterday was the day Senpai forgot her umbrella, and the day before that was the day Senpai caught me when I almost fell.\
Every day with Iori-senpai was coloured with happy anniversaries.\
We were still only dating on a "trial" basis, but the relationship itself was going extremely well.

"By the way, Himena. Doesn't a student teacher start today?"\
"So I hear. …Wait, what?!"\
"What?"\
"I-Iori-senpai just sent me a message, and it says…"

Yuuri leaned over to look at my phone.\
On the screen was a message from Iori-senpai: *Sorry, I can't have lunch with you today after all.*

"W-why…"\
"There are days like that."\
"Ugh, I'm in despair…"\
"So annoying. What are you doing for lunch? If you don't want to be alone, I can ask Kanami-senpai if a third person is okay."

I grabbed Yuuri by both shoulders.\
She made a face of open displeasure, and I hugged her anyway.

"Please!! Don't leave me alone right now…"\
"Dramatic."

It was not dramatic.\
Iori-senpai was busy with club every afternoon, so lunch break was precious time together.\
Lunch was the longest stretch of the day we could actually talk.

Honestly, I hated the idea of not spending it with her.\
But I couldn't be selfish and get myself disliked…

I sighed. Right at that moment the student teacher walked in, but I couldn't summon the slightest interest.

☆★☆★☆★☆★

"I'm the student teacher, Setoguchi Kaede. I'll be teaching PE. Pleased to meet you!"

She bowed energetically, and she really did look the part of a PE teacher.\
A lean, toned body, with her ponytail tied high.\
Her cat-like eyes left an impression, and her face was good-looking enough that you could simply call her a beauty.

"I'd like to get friendly with all of you, so please talk to me a lot."

She finished her greeting and moved to the back of the classroom.

Well, she'd only be here a short while, so we probably wouldn't have much to do with her.

― Or so I predicted, until, a few hours later, that prediction was completely overturned.

☆★☆★☆★☆★

"Iori-senpai?!"

She'd said she couldn't have lunch with me today.\
And yet, the moment lunch break started, Iori-senpai came into our classroom.\
Plans changed! Great, I get a happy lunch with Senpai today too!

"Iori-sen—"\
"Kaede-san!"

What?

Iori-senpai walked in and went straight to Setoguchi-sensei.\
With her eyes sparkling, no less.

What is that expression, so cute… no, wait!! What is going on?!

"Oh, Iori. Long time no see. Did you get taller again?"\
"Yes. About five centimetres since I last saw you, I think."\
"You grow every time I see you."

Wait wait wait. What? They know each other? And aren't they standing awfully close?!

Unlike her teacher mode from earlier, Setoguchi-sensei faced Iori-senpai with a relaxed expression.\
Iori-senpai, in contrast, was practically bouncing like a big dog.

"I wanted to see you. When I heard you were coming to our school, I was so happy."\
"I'm happy too. I was looking forward to seeing you, Iori."

Huh?! And they're just naturally using first names, and isn't Iori-senpai a little bit flustered?!

This wasn't the usual breezy prince-mode Senpai, or the Senpai who got serious about club, or the Senpai who got lovey-dovey with me.\
A Senpai I'd never seen before was standing right in front of me.

"…Himena."

A hand landed on my shoulder. I turned around, and Yuuri said just one thing.

"I'll pick up your bones for you."\
"I-I'm not dead yet…"

My voice shook. Even so, I did want to see this different Iori-senpai.\
I gathered my courage and looked straight at the two of them.\
The moment Setoguchi-sensei lightly patted Iori-senpai's head, my own head exploded.
